         Ordinance               Provisions               and    Analysis

         The     Board            of    Commissioners'               decision               must        be     based       upon         the         criteria
         for     a     zone        boundary              amendment            set   forth          in     Section          303.01             (1)     of   the
         Zoning            Ordinance            as       follows:

         (a)         The  boundary adjustment   is consistent  with   the                                                    Comprehensive
                     Plan  Map designation  existing  on the  parcel;

         (b)         The        predominant               zoning     district               includes               at    least     55     percent
                     of the            subject           property,  and the readjustment   will not
                     reduce            in area           the predominant   zoning district which exists
                      on    the        subject           property;

         (c)         The        boundary           adjustment             shall        follow           existing           or     proposed
                     property               lines,        public     roads,            or    rivers;               and

         (d)         The  boundary                 adjustment    shall   result  in a single   zoning
                     district   for                the  existing    parcel   or proposed  parcels.

        As  stated    in Background                             Fact 1, the  Comprehensive    Plan  Map designation
        for  the   entire  property                             is Agriculture/Forestry    Large   Holding,  and
        because             this        plan       designation                applies        to     both           the    EF-40     and         AF-20
        districts,  the  proposed    adjustment    to make the  entire  property zoned
        AF-20  is consistent   with   the  Comprehensive   Plan  Map designation   on the
        property.   Therefore,    the   request  is consistent   with  Criterion 1
        above.

        As  stated    in Background     Fact    2,   184   acres   of approximately                                                            284   acres
        is presently      zoned  AF-20.      This    means    that   over  65 percent                                                          of the
        subject    parcel    is in the   AF-20      zoning    district.      Therefore,                                                          the
        request    meets   the  requirement       of Criterion       2 above   in that                                                         the
         predominant                   zoning        district            is    at   least          55    percent           of     the     subject
        property                and     will       not     reduce         in    area        the     predominant                  zoning         district.

        In that   the   property   will   be rezoned     entirely   to AF-20,    the zone
        boundary   will    follow  existing     property   lines  and  will   result  ina
        single   zoning   district    for   the  entire   property,   the   subject  reguest                                                                     is
        consistent    with   Criterion    3 and   4 above.

CONCLUSIONS:

        to     entirely                AF-20.
