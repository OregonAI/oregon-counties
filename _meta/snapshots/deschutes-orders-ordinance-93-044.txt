WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners adopted ordinance
93- 041 to comply with revisions to the One and Two Family Dwelling
Structural Code ( Code) in areas designated as wildfire hazard
areas; and
WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners ( Board) took such
action with the understanding that a map of wildfire areas
submitted by the Oregon Department of Forestry ( Department)
constituted the criteria developed by the Department to implement
the Code' s wildfire hazard provision; and
WHEREAS, the Department has now informed the County that said
map did not constitute the Department' s wildfire hazard criteria
and that said criteria have not now been finalized;
NOW THEREFORE, THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS ORDAINS as
follows:
Section 1. REPEAL. Ordinance No. 93- 041, amending Chapter
15. 04. 037, Wildfire Hazard Designation, Building and Construction
Codes and Regulations, is hereby repealed in its entirety.
Section 2. EMERGENCY. This Ordinance being necessary for the
immediate preservation of the public peace, health and safety, an
emergency is declared to exist, and this Ordinance takes effect on
its passage.
DATED this 21st day of July 1993.
