THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY,
OREGON, ORDAINS as follows:
Section 1. That Title 18 of the Deschutes County Code be
amended by the deletion of Section 18. 16. 020. G, Livestock feed
lot or sales yard, as a permitted use.
Section 2. That Title 18 of the Deschutes County Code be
amended by the deletion of Section 18. 20. 020. G, Livestock feed
lot or sales yard, as a permitted use.
Section 3. That Title 18 of the Deschutes County Code be
amended by the deletion of Section 18. 24. 020. G, Livestock feed
lot or sales yard, as a permitted use.
Section 4. That Title 18 of the Deschutes County Code be
amended by the deletion of Section 18. 28. 020. G, Livestock feed
lot or sales lot, as a permitted use.
Section 5. This ordinance being necessary for the immediate
preservation of the public peace, health and safety, an emergency
is declared to exist, and this Ordinance takes effect on its
passage.
