§ 71.01  REGULATIONS GOVERNING
PARKING IN COUNTY PARKING LOTS.
(A) Certain parking spaces have been assigned
to state and county officials, county departments,
police vehicles and holders of disabled permits and
these spaces are so designated by appropriate signs.
Only those persons and members of the departments
so designated may park in the spaces assigned for
their use between the hours of 7:00 a.m. and 6:00
p.m., Monday through Friday.
(B) No person shall park a motor vehicle in a
“no parking” zone or in any other place than a
marked, designated parking space.
(C) Only service vehicles which are loading and
unloading or providing requested service within the
courthouse may park in a parking space marked
“service vehicles.”
(D) All parking spaces not otherwise reserved
are available for use by the general public and county
employees on a first-come, first-served basis.
(E) This section shall be enforced by the
Umatilla County Sheriff's Department.
(Ord. 79-17, passed 6-20-79; Ord. 96-10, passed  12-
18-96)
§ 71.99  PENALTY.
(A) Pursuant to ORS 161.545 through 161.635,
violation of this section shall be an unclassified
misdemeanor.
(B) For offenses other than improperly parking
in a “disabled permit” space, violators of this section
may be issued a citation with a fine of $15 and
directing then to appear in District Court. If the fine is
paid in full within five working days, the court
appearance is not required. A repeat violation
occurring within one year of a previous offense will be
issued a citation directing them to appear in District
Court with a fine of $30.
(C) Any person parking in a space designated as
parking for disabled persons, whose vehicle does not
conspicuously display a disabled person parking
permit described under ORS 811.602 or 811.606,
commits the offense of unlawful parking in a space
reserved for disabled persons. Pursuant to ORS
811.615, this violation is a Class B traffic infraction,
punishable by a fine of not less than $190.
(D) In addition to enforcement by citation, the
county has the right to have any improperly parked
vehicle towed away at the owner's expense in order to
rectify interference with a governmental function or to
abate a safety hazard posed by the illegal parking. A
vehicle that is removed under § 71.01 may be removed
and stored at the owner's expense. If the vehicle is not
claimed and the fees for removal and storage not paid
within 30 days of removal, the vehicle shall be
considered abandoned for the purposes of ORS
87.172, 87.192, and 87.196 and shall also be
considered abandoned for the purposes of this chapter.
(Ord. 79-17, passed 6-20-79; Ord. 96-10, passed  12-
