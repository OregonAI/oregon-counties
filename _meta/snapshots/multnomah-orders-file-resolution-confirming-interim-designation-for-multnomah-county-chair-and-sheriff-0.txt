BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2023-001
Confirming Interim Designation for Multnomah County Chair and Sheriff.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Multnomah County Charter Section 4.50(3) and Multnomah County Code 5.003 require
that elected officials designate an interim occupant to serve until a vacancy is filled.
b. Charter Section 4.10 states the qualifications for County officers and Code Section
5.003(B)(4) requires that the designee meet the Charter qualifications for appointees of such
offices.
c. Jessica Vega Pederson, Multnomah County Chair, designates Serena Cruz as interim
occupant of that office in the event of a vacancy.
d. Nicole Morrisey O’Donnell, Multnomah County Sheriff, designates Captain Carey Kaer
as interim occupant of that office in the event of a vacancy.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
The Board confirms Serena Cruz to serve as interim occupant for the Multnomah County Chair,
and Captain Carey Kaer to serve as interim occupant for Sheriff in the event of a vacancy in
that office.
ADOPTED this 5th day of January, 2023.
