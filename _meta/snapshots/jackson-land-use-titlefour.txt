TITLE FOUR - Subdivision Regulations

(EDITOR
'S NOTE:  The County Subdivision Regulations are a part of the County's Land
Development Ordinance (Chapters 1 through 60 of Ordinance 1982-32, passed
November 10, 1982). A revised edition of this Ordinance, as amended through January 1,
1985, was published in early 1985. Because of this separate publication, this Ordinance
was not included as a part of these Codified Ordinances, but a copy of the same may be
physically inserted into this volume, following Appendix I, for the convenience of the
user of these Codified Ordinances. This revised edition contains its own index and is
therefore not indexed in the General Index for these Codified Ordinances. This Title Four
of this Part Twelve of these Codified Ordinances is reserved for possible future
codification of these Regulations.)
