1026.01 PURPOSE; AUTHORITY.

The purpose of this chapter is to make minor additions to the Local Improvement District
process defined in state law, ORS 371.605 to 371.660.  These additions are added to
better inform the public and to allow additional public input at a time when public input
is most valuable to the Commissioners decision making process.  The additions made by
this chapter are additions to the requirements of ORS 371.605 to 371.660; all
requirements of ORS 371.605 to 371.660 shall continue to apply.  This chapter is
authorized by ORS 371.610(3).

1026.02 DEFINITIONS

For purposes of this ordinance, the definitions contained in ORS 371.605 apply.

1026.03 NOTICE OF PETITION OR RESOLUTION TO DISTRICT
PROPERTY OWNERS.

Within 30 days of referral of the resolution or petition by the county court to the
engineer, per ORS 371.625, the engineer shall cause notice to be mailed to the owner of
each parcel of land to be assessed for the proposed improvement.  Said notice shall be
sent via first class mail to the address on record with the County Assessor.  Said notice
shall include, at a minimum, notice of the proposed improvement and the potential
assessment therefore, and a summary of the Local Improvement District process.

1026.04 PUBLIC HEARING BEFORE MAKING ORDER FOR
IMPROVEMENT

If the number of objections mentioned in ORS 371.630 is not received, the county court
by order may set the time and place for the hearing of objections or support for the
proposed improvements or the proposed assessments.  Said hearing shall occur not less
than 10 days following the date of the order setting the hearing.  At least five days prior
to the hearing, the engineer shall cause notice of the hearing to be mailed to the owner of
each parcel of land to be assessed for the proposed improvement.  Said notice shall be
sent via first class mail to the address on record with the County Assessor.  After close of
the hearing, the county court shall deliberate and may make an order directing the
improvement be made, per ORS 371.635(1).
