BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-043
Establishing Fees for Building Permits and Other Related Permits administered by City of
Portland pursuant to Chapter 29 of the Multnomah County Code and Repealing Resolution
No. 2025-049; Establishing Land Use Services Fees administered by City of Portland
pursuant to Chapter 39 of the Multnomah County Code and Partially Repealing Resolution
No. 2025-043.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Chapter 29, Building Regulations, and Chapter 39, Zoning Code, of the Multnomah
County Code (MCC) provide that the Board shall establish certain fees by
resolution.
B. On July 17, 2025, the Board adopted Resolution No. 2025-049 establishing fees for
building permits, electrical permits, plumbing permits, mechanical permits, and other
related permits applicable within the City of Portland Urban Services Boundary
pursuant to MCC Chapter 29.
C. On June 12, 2025, the Board adopted Resolution 2025-043 establishing County
fees, including land use services fees applicable within the City of Portland Urban
Services Boundary pursuant to MCC Chapter 39.
D. Multnomah County has entered into intergovernmental agreements (IGAs) with the
cities of Gresham, Troutdale, and Portland to provide building and land use
planning services within certain areas of unincorporated Multnomah County.
E. Pursuant to the Gresham IGAs, fees for building and land use services provided by
the City of Gresham are as set by the City of Gresham and need not be adopted by
the County.
F. Pursuant to the Troutdale IGAs, fees for building and land use services provided by
the City of Troutdale are as set by the City of Troutdale and need not be adopted by
the County.
G. Pursuant to the Portland IGAs, Portland proposes fees for building and land use
services provided by the City of Portland within the Portland Urban Services
Boundary, which are then set by Multnomah County.

H. New fees applicable within the Portland Urban Services Boundary are shown in
Exhibit A (Fees for Building Permits and Other Related Permits) and Exhibit B (Land
Use Services Fees). The City of Portland has approved the increased fees effective
July 10, 2026, in order to cover the cost of providing services on behalf of
Multnomah County. Fees for building services were approved under the Oregon
Structural Specialty Code in accordance with OAR 918-020-0220 and ORS
455.210.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. Pursuant to MCC 29.010 and 39.1245, fees for the areas of unincorporated
Multnomah County within the Portland Urban Services Boundary are set out in the
attached Exhibit A (Fees for Building Permits and Other Related Permits) and
Exhibit B (Land Use Services Fees).
2. On July 10, 2026, Resolution No. 2025-049 is repealed and this Resolution takes
effect takes effect on that same date.
3. On July 10, 2026, land use services fees applicable within the City of Portland
Urban Services Boundary established by Resolution No. 2025-043 are repealed
and this Resolution takes effect on that same date. All other County fees
established by Resolution 2025-043 remain in effect.
ADOPTED this 18th day of June, 2026.
