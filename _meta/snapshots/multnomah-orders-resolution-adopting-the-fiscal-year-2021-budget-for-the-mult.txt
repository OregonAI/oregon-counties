BEFORE THE MULTNOMAH COUNTY LIBRARY DISTRICT BOARD
RESOLUTION NO.  2020-050
Adopting the Fiscal Year 2021 Budget for the Multnomah  County Library District and Making
Appropriations Thereunder, Pursuant to ORS 294.456.
The Multnomah County Library District (MCLD) Board Finds:
a. The MCLD budget, as prepared by the duly appointed MCLD Director/Budget Officer,
has been considered and approved by the MCLD Board  acting as the Budget
Committee.
b. A public hearing on this budget was held before the Multnomah County Tax
Supervising and Conservation Commission on the 9th day of June, 2020.
c. The budget is now on file in the Multnomah County Library’s Administrative Office.
d. The MCLD Board has made one amendment to the above described budget  and that
amendment is attached to this resolution as Attachment A.
e. The appropriation authorized is as follows:
GENERAL FUND (1520)
Library Services $ 92,187,142
Cash Transfers $   4,938,522
Operating Contingency $      500,000
Total Appropriation   $ 97,625,664
CAPITAL FUND (2514)
Library Services     $       270,000
Capital Outlay  $    1,600,000
Operating Contingency    $  32,852,206
Total Appropriation      $  34,722,206
f. The Tax Supervising and Conservation Commission has certified the budget and
there are no recommendations and no objections.
The Multnomah County Library District Board Resolves:
1. The budget, including Attachment A, is adopted as the budget of the MCLD.
2. The appropriation shown above is authorized for the fiscal year July 1, 2020
to June 30, 2021.
ADOPTED this 23rd day of June, 2020.
MULTNOMAH COUNTY LIBRARY
DISTRICT BOARD
