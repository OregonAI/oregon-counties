BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
ORDER NO. 2020-105
Legalization of a Portion of NW Rocky Point Road, as County Road No. 5031.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. NW Rocky Point Road was established as County Road No. 445 in 1888 and for
portions of said road, between Engineer’s Stations 75+91.54 and 84+40.70, the
location of the road as traveled and used for at least the past 10 years at this
location does not conform to the existing records for this county road.
b. NW Rocky Point Road has been traveled and used by the public and maintained by
the County for more than 25 years.
c. On August 25, 2020, consistent with ORS 368.201 to 368.221, the Board of
Commissioners adopted Resolution No. 2020-055 initiating proceedings for
legalizing a portion of NW Rocky Point Road and directed the County Surveyor to
conduct a survey of the road in its traveled location.
d. On October 1, 2020, the County Surveyor filed his survey with the Board Clerk and
the County Engineer filed a report supporting legalization of NW Rocky Point Road.
Subsequently, on October 1, 2020, the Board adopted Resolution No. 2020-087
Scheduling a Public Hearing and Directing Notice Thereof for the Legalization of a
Portion of NW Rocky Point Road, County Road No. 445.
e. County Transportation has provided notice of this December 10, 2020 proceeding
in compliance with ORS 368.206(1)(c).
f. The Board has determined that the legalization of the Road is in the public interest.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Orders:
1. As authorized under ORS 368.201 to 368.221, that the portion of NW Rocky Point
Road, County Road No. 445 , between Engineer’s Stations 75+91.54 and
84+40.70, as more particularly described in the attached Exhibit A (“the Road”), is
hereby legalized as a county road, specifically to be known as County Road No.
5031.
2. Further, a s provided by ORS 368.216(2), t he County Surveyor and County
Transportation staff shall comply with all of the requirements of ORS 368.106(1)-(4),
to perfect the legalization of the Road.
3. Upon the completion of the legalization procedures required under ORS 368.201 to
368.221, any records showing the location of the Road that conflict with the location
of the Road as described in this Order are void, and the Road exists as shown on
this Order.
ADOPTED this 10th day of December, 2020.
