BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY , OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2023-085
Resolution Consenting to Chair Vega Pederson's Appointment of Rachael Banks as the
Director of the Health Department.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3) provides that the Chair’s appointment of
department heads (directors) is consent of a majority of the board of commissioners.
b. The Chair appointed Rachael Banks as the Director of the Health Department with an
expected start date beginning November 15, 2023.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
In accordance with Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3), the Board consents to the
appointment of Rachael Banks as the Director of the Health Department.
ADOPTED this 5th day of October, 2023.
