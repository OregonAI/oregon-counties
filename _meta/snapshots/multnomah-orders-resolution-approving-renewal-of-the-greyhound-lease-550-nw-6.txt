BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2022-088
Approving renewal of the Greyhound Lease, 550 NW 6th Avenue Portland, Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The use of transient lodging is one of many approaches employed by the Joint Office of
Homeless Services (JOHS) to address homelessness and related issues.
b. Extending the lease located at 550 NW 6th Avenue (via the proposed “Third
Amendment”) will enable JOHS to continue operat ing an existing congregate shelter
through at least the winter months of FY23.
c. Approval of the Third Amendment in substantial conformance with the proposed terms
is in the best interests of Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. Execution of the Third Amendment on terms substantially similar to those proposed is
approved.
2. Renewals, extensions and amendments may be executed without further Board action.
ADOPTED this 29th day of September, 2022.
