BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2020-010
Proclamation Celebrating the 100th Anniversary of the League of Women Voters of the United States and Declaring
February 14, 2020 as League of Women Voters Day.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
1. The League of Women Voters was founded in 1920 as a "mighty political experiment" by the foremothers of the suffragist
movement at the National American Woman Suffrage Association.
2. Their goal was to help the 20 million women who were granted the right to vote by the 19th Amendment understand and
carry out their new responsibility as voters.
3. With the success of this effort and the tireless efforts over the last 100 years to strengthen and uphold its mission to
empower voters and defend democracy, the League has become a trusted nonpartisan, grassroots organization.
4. The League has sponsored legislation and fought in the courts to protect and strengthen voting rights and access, and
for free and fair elections, civil rights, children, community health, and education.
5. The League has consistently been noted for its nonpartisan election information, including sponsorship of candidate
forums and information on state and local ballot issues, as well as its commitment to register, educate, and mobilize
voters.
6. The League champions government systems that are open, transparent, inclusive, and equitable.
7. The League believes that active and engaged citizens, irrespective of gender, ethnicity, or political affiliation, are the
hallmark of democracy;
8. We honor and congratulate the League of Women Voters on its 100th Anniversary and commend the League for its
significant contributions to empowering voters and making democracy work.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
February 14, 2020 as League of Women Voters Day and celebrates the 100th Anniversary of the League of Women Voters
of the United States.
ADOPTED this 13th day of February, 2020.
