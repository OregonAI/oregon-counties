BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO.  2025-056
Approving a Room -Block Agreement (RBA) at the Motel Located at 24124 SE Stark St,
Gresham, OR.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. The use of family shelter is one of many approaches employed by the Homeless
Services Department (HSD) to address homelessness and related issues.
B. The Room-Block Agreement will add 53 transient lodging units to the HSD portfolio at
the motel located at 24124 SE Stark St, Gresham, OR 97030.
C. The term of the agreement shall commence in October 2025 and expire on June 30,
2035.
D. The County shall have the right to terminate the agreement annually, on June 30th of
each year during the term of the agreement with sixty days’ prior written notice provided
to the property owner.
E. The County shall have the right to purchase the Premises for a Purchase Price of six
million two hundred thousand dollars ($6,200,000).
F. Rent shall be set at $65 per room per night, and increase by 2% on each anniversary of
the agreement beginning July 1, 2026.
G. Approval of the Room -Block Agreement in substantial conformance with the proposed
terms is in the best interests of Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. Entering into a Room-Block Agreement on terms substantially similar to those proposed
is approved.
2. Renewals, extensions and amendments thereof may be executed without further Board
action.
ADOPTED this 25th day of September, 2025.
