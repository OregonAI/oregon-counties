BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO.
Authorizing the Annual Designation of a Portion of Compensation as a Housing
Allowance for Chaplains Serving Inmates and Employees of the Multnomah County
Sheriff’s Office.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The Multnomah County Sheriff’s Office employs Lewis Kyle as a chaplain serving
adults in custody and employees of the Multnomah County Sheriff’s Office.
b. The Sheriff’s Office does not provide housing to chaplains.
c. 26 USC § 107(2) allows clergy to exclude from the calculation of their gross income the
housing allowance paid as part of their compensation, to the extent used by them to rent
or provide a home.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
Lewis Kyle is allowed to designate $60,000 of his compensation as a housing
allowance for calendar year 2026, subject to the requirements and limitations set
forth in the Internal Revenue Service Code and all other applicable regulations.
