Use of One-Time-Only FundsUnrestricted one-time-only resources present organizations with temptations that are hard toresist. Policy StatementIt isthepolicyof theBoardthat theCountywill fundongoingprogramswithongoingrevenues,and to restrict the allocation of one-time revenues to nonrecurring expenditures.

● Restricted–for resourcesconstrainedonusebyexternallyimposedcreditors(suchas through debt covenants), grantors, contributors, or laws or regulations of othergovernments or constraints imposed by law through constitutional provisions orenabling legislation.● Committed – for resources constrained on use authorized by the Board of CountyCommissioners via resolution or ordinance.
