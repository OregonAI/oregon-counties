BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2020-100
Authorizing the Annual Designation of a Portion of Compensation as a Housing Allowance
for Chaplains Serving Inmates and Employees of the Multnomah County Sheriff’s Office.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The Multnomah County Sheriff’s Office employs Lewis Kyle and Mychal Lemon as
chaplains serving adults in custody and employees of Multnomah County Sheriff’s
Office.
b. The Sheriff’s Office does not provide housing to Chaplain Kyle or Chaplain Lemon.
c. 26 USC § 107(2) allows clergy to exclude from the calculation of their gross income the
housing allowance paid as part of their compensation, to the extent used by them to rent
or provide a home.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
The following chaplain s are allowed to designate the following amounts of their
compensation as housing allowance for calendar year 20 21, subject to the
requirements and limitations set forth in the Internal Revenue Code and all other
applicable regulations:
Housing Allowance
Chaplain Amount Per Annum
Lewis Kyle $40,000
Mychal Lemon $22,000
ADOPTED this 3rd day of December, 2020.
