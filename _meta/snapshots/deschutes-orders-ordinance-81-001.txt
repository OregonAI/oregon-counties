Section 1. That Section 4. 040, Exclusive Farm Use Zone,
EFU-20, Su section ( 2), Uses Permitted Out- right, is amended to read

2) Uses Permitted Outright.
A) Farm use as defined in ORS 215. 203(2).
B) Propagation or harvesting of a forest product.
