EXHIBIT A

7.20.01 Variance; Written Approval of Director R equired. The weight limit for any vehicle  specifically
approved in writing by the Director of Public Works as eligible for a weight variance under  this ordinance is
increased as provided in Section 7.20.02 on that portion of Niederberger Road identified on the attached Exhibit
“A.” Exhibit “A” is incorporated into this ordinance by reference.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 727 eff 07/18/03]

7.20.02 Maximum Loaded Weight, Length, Width and Types and Classes of Vehicles. The maximum loaded
weight, length, width and types and classes of vehicles or combinations of vehicles that may be operated under
the variance granted by this ordinance is described as follows:

Type of Vehicle: Two axle, articulated dump truck.
Maximum Length: 23 feet, 5 inches
Maximum Width: 8 feet, 2 inches
Maximum Weight: 55,000 lbs. Gross Vehicle Weight

[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 727 eff 07/18/03]

7.20.03 Height Limits Not Modified. This ordinance does not change any height limit imposed by ORS 818.080
and 818.090. This ordinance is subject to the maximum size, weight and width limits established under ORS
810.050.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 727 eff 07/18/03]

7.20.04 Penalties. A person or entity who violates the weight limit allowed by this ordinance is subject to the
penalties provided under ORS 818.060.
