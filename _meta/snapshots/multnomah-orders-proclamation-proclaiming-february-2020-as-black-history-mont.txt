BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2020-013
Proclaiming February 2020 as Black History Month and Black Future Month in Multnomah County, Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Black History and Black Future Month is observed throughout the United States to celebrate and
recognize the vital role and contributions of African Americans in our history and culture. Black History
Month was first celebrated in 1976.
b. Multnomah County recognizes that racial and ethnic diversity enriches our community.
c. Multnomah County recognizes the systemic challenges that Black Americans face in achieving equity,
opportunity, and social mobility.
d. Multnomah County further recognizes the immense economic impacts of our Black community
members.
e. Multnomah County honors the rich heritage, extraordinary contributions, and ongoing accomplishments
of African Americans, including successful entrepreneurial endeavors and thriving local businesses.
f. Multnomah County honors and celebrates the roles that our Black employees play in working towards
eliminating disparities in their communities, and creating a better future for all community members.
g. Multnomah County honors the workforce equity contributions of Employees of Color (EOC) and Black
employees that ensure safety, trust and belonging for all in our community.
h. Multnomah County celebrates Black excellence and African American advocacy of social justice, cultural
contributions, racial justice and hope not only this month - but 365 days a year.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
February 2020 as Black History Month and Black Future Month in Multnomah County, Oregon, in
recognition and celebration of the contributions of African Americans to our community.
ADOPTED this 20 th day of February, 2020.
