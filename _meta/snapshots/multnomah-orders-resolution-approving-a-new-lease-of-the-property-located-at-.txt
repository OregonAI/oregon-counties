 BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-003
Approving a New Lease of the Property Located at 1808 SE Belmont Street, Portland, Oregon
97214 for the EASA Behavioral Health Division.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. A lease of premises within the property located at 1808 SE Belmont Street, Portland, Oregon
97214 (“Lease”) will provide the EASA (Early Assessment and Support Alliance) program in
the Behavioral Health Division with appropriate space necessary for their office and client
space needs.
b. This Lease would create a permanent and centralized space for youth, young adults and
families who are receiving services from the Multnomah County’s Behavioral Health
Division’s Early Assessment and Support Alliance (EASA) program. This program provides
assessment, early intervention, related mental health services, and employment assistance
in the beginning years of onset of psychosis. The EASA program is client-facing with mental
health services including Individual and Family Therapy, Family Training, Consultation,
Occupational Therapy, Psychiatric Medication Management, Nursing, Peer Support
Services, Supported Employment Services, Case Management, and Psychiatric Medication
Administration. EASA is a specialized program that serves individuals who have their first
onset of psychosis between the ages of 12-25 years of age.
c. Approval of the Lease in substantial conformance with the proposed terms is in the best
interests of Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The County Chair is authorized to execute a lease in substantial conformance with the terms
proposed.
2. The County Chair is authorized to execute renewals, extensions and amendments of the
Lease and any ancillary instruments without further Board action.
ADOPTED this 11th day of January, 2024.
