 CHAPTER 51 2017

 PARKING ON COUNTY ROADS

 51.105  VEHICLES ON COUNTY ROADS.

 (1)   ORS 811.550 to 811.585 applies to motor vehi cles stopped, parked, or left standing
on a County road, except by the owner or occupant of property abutting such County road, or by a
guest or invitee of such owner or occupant, while s uch guest or invitee is making use of the
premises.

 (2) This section does not apply to portions of a C ounty road:

  (a) In a platted subdivision within an Urban Grow th Boundary, as defined in the
Polk County Comprehensive Plan, if such County road has curbed roadside
parking; or

  (b) Specifically designated for parking by an off icial traffic control device.

 (3)   For purposes of this section, "County road" includes the traveled surface, shoulders,
ditches, cut slope, and fill slope of a County road.  [Ord. 88-24, Sec. 1]

 51.110  SAFETY RESTRICTIONS.

 (1)   Pursuant to the authority granted to the Pub lic Works Director in Chapter 32, the
Board of Commissioners declares that there shall be no parking in a County Road where the Public
Works Director has determined so, and subsequently caused appropriate signage to be erected.
Appeals to this determination by the Public Works Director can be made in writing to the Board of
Commissioners.  In the event an imposed parking res triction has been appealed, the Board of
Commissioners shall hold a public hearing within 20  days to hear the appeal.  At the end of the
hearing, the Board of Commissioners will make a det ermination that the parking restriction shall
remain in affect or direct the Public Works Department to remove the signs.

 (2)   For purpose of this section, "County road, i ncludes the traveled surface, shoulders,
ditches, cut slope, and fill slope of a County road.  [Ord. 91-12, Sec. 1]

 51.990  PENALTIES.

 Violation of Section 51.105 or 51.110 is punishabl e as provided in ORS 203.065.  [Ord. 88-
24, Sec. 2; amended by Ord. 91-12, Sec. 2]
