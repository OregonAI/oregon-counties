CHAPTER 247
Pension Plans

EDITOR
'S NOTE:  Pension plans for County officers and employees are provided for
and amended from time to time by the Board of County Commissioners. Copies of the
latest relevant legislation and of such plans may be obtained, at cost, from the office of
the County Counsel.

There are no sections in Chapter 247. This chapter has been established to provide a place
for cross references and any future legislation.
