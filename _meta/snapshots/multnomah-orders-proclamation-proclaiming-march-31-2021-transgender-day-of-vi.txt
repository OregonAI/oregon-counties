BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2021-15
Proclaiming March 31, 2021, Transgender Day of Visibility in Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. March 31st marks International Transgender Day of Visiblity, a day to celebrate
and lift up the voices and contributions of our gender expansive community of;
transgender, Two Spirit, Non -Binary, and Intersex people, among others, here in
Multnomah County.
B. This day is dedicated to celebrating the accomplishments and victories of our
gender expansive community, while raising awareness of the work that still needs
to be done to address anti-transgender violence and achieve lived equality.
C. Members of the gender expansive community of Multnomah County have actively
engaged in every aspect of life in our community, yet continue to face
discrimination, harassment, and violence at rates much highter than cisgender
community members. We recognize that these level s of hate and violence
disproportionately affect our Black-Latinx Trans Femmes.
D. Multnomah County and the Office of Diversity and Equity are committed to
honoring the contributions of our gender expansive employees as we strive for a
workplace that fosters Safety, Trust, and Belonging. As we “Inclusively Lead with
Race”, we recognize that our BIPOC gender expansive employees face unique
duel barriers due to institutional racism and transhopbia.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
1. March 31, 2021, Transgender Day of Visibility in Multnomah County, Oregon,
and with this proclamation affirms commitment to support our gender expansive
community.
ADOPTED this 1st day of APRIL, 2021.
