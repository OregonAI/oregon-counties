BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO.
To Adopt and Extend the Declaration of Emergency
 in Executive Rule 432 Until April 11,
2026, Due to the Impacts of Federal Immigration Enforcement Actions.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
1. On December 12, 2025, Executive Rule 432 Declaration of Emergency Due to
the Impacts of Federal Immigration Enforcement Actions, was issued by the
Multnomah County Chair.
2. Multnomah County Code 25.450(B) states that the initial declaration of
emergency may not last more than 30 days. A declaration of emergency may be
extended or terminated by the Board.
3. As stated in the Declaration of Emergency, Multnomah County is home to a
diverse and vibrant community of people representing many races, ethnicities,
and nationalities, including immigrants and refugees from all over the world.
Multnomah County, and jurisdictions across Oregon have recognized the
deleterious effects of the federal government’s callous and heavy-handed
approach to immigration enforcement.
4. On December 18, 2025, the Board of County Commissioners adopted Resolution
2025-067 Reaffirming and Strengthening the County’s Sanctuary Status, Actions,
and Advocacy.
5. The conditions supporting the underlying Declaration of Emergency and
Resolution 2025-067 continue, and in the wake of unconstitutional and unlawful
federal immigration policies and practices that have generated fear and anxiety
among members of the County’s many communities, it is necessary to extend the
emergency.
2026-003
NOW, THEREFORE, the Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves to:
To extend the state of emergency declared in Executive Rule 432 to 11:59pm on April
11, 2026 unless otherwise modified.
