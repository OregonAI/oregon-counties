5.40.01. Moratorium. The location and operation of a Dispensary is  prohibited in all areas subject to the
jurisdiction of Yamhill County until May 1, 2015. As used herein, Dispensary includes any facility that dispenses
marijuana pursuant to ORS 475.314 or any other provision of Oregon law.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 888 eff 02/20/14; AMENDED 889 eff 05/01/14]

5.40.02 Violations; Remedies.
a) The violation of this ordinance is hereby declared to be a nuisance under ORS 203.065 and the prohibition of
Dispensaries under this ordinance is hereby added to the list of laws enforceable by YCC 1.10, Yamhill County
Code Enforcement Code.
b) The remedies available under Senate Bill 1531 (2014) for a violation of the moratorium imposed by this
ordinance are not exclusive of any other remedies available under any applicable federal, state or local law
and that it is within the discretion of Yamhill County to seek cumulative remedies for a violation of th e
moratorium imposed herein.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 888 eff 02/20/14; AMENDED 889 eff 05/01/14]
