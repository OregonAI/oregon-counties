BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2022-033
Proclaiming the Month of April 2022 as Child Abuse Prevention Mont h in Multnomah
County, Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Child abuse and neglect is a serious public health and safety problem that
impacts all of our community, and finding solutions requires input and action from
everyone.
b. According to the most recently published Oregon Department of Human Services
Child Welfare Data Book, there were 78,632 reports of child abuse and neglect in
Oregon in 2020.  In Multnomah County, there were 12,902 reports of abuse and
neglect. The leading family stress factor when child abuse was present  was
substance abuse  by a parent/caregiver . The next most common stress factor
was domestic violence.
c. Child abuse and neglect has long -term, physical, psychological, emotional and
social effects that have lasting consequences for survivors of abuse  into
adulthood.
d. Due to the  ongoing global pandemic over the past two  years, the challenges to
protecting children continue to be more acutely felt.
e. All of us in Multnomah County must work together to increase awareness about
child abuse and contribute to promote the social and emotional well ‐being of
children and families in a safe, stable, nurturing environment.
f. We must all recognize the signs of c hild maltreatment and take appropriate steps
to safeguard children by reporting concerns and connecting families with the help
they may need.
g. Effective child abuse prevention  and intervention  efforts succeed because of
coordinated partnerships between government, child welfare, health, schools,
law enforcement and community organizations.
h. That resource and adoptive parents, child protective workers, faith leaders,
community mentors,  medicine, mental health, teachers, and law enforcement
officials, all have a role in protecting and supporting children who have been
tragically abused or neglected.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
1. The Month of April 2022 as Child Abuse Prevention Month in Multnomah County.
2. The Board of County Commissioners call upon our community to support the
effort to prevent child abuse, protect children and strengthen families.
ADOPTED this 28th day of April, 2022.
