PREFACE TO THE REPUBLICATION
This Code constitutes a republication of the general and permanent ordinances of the
County of Benton, Oregon.
Source materials used in the preparation of the republication were the County Code,
as updated through Ordinance 2024-232, on February 6, 2024, and subsequent ordinances.
The source of each new section is included in the history note appearing in parentheses
at the end thereof. The absence of such a note indicates that the section has not
been amended. By use of the comparative tables appearing in the back of this republication,
the reader can locate any section of the Code, as updated.
