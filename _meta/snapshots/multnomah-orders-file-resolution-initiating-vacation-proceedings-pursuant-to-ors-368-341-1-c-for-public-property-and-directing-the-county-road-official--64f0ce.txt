BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO.  2020-068
Initiating Vacation Proceedings Pursuant to ORS 368.341(1)(c) for Public Property, and
Directing the County Road Official to Prepare a Report as Required by ORS 368.346(1)
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. ORS 368.341 authorizes a county governing body to initiate proceedings to vacate
public property under ORS 368.326 to 368.366 if the county governing body adopts
a resolution declaring its intent to vacate property.
B. It is the County’s intention to vacate County right of way known as the NE 238th
Connector, County Road No. 5008, and NE 242nd Connector, County Road No.
5007, in east Multnomah County. The portion of the public property proposed for
vacation is more particularly described in the attached Exhibit 1 (the “Public
Property”).
C. County’s Transportation Division no longer needs or intends to design and construct
the Public Property to be used as county roads within the County road system.
Consequently, it is in the best interest of the public to vacate the Public Property.
D. The Public Property runs through real property currently owned by the Reynolds
School District.
E. These portions of Public Property were established as County Road Nos. 5007 and
5008 in 1995, per Board Order No. 95-111.
F. Vacation of the Public Property will unencumber the underlying real property owned
by the Reynolds School District, giving Reynolds School District greater flexibility in
its use of its real property at this location.
G. This resolution complies with the requirements in ORS 368.341.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. Pursuant to ORS 368.341(1), this Board initiates a vacation proceeding regarding
the Public Property being more particularly described in the attached Exhibit 1.
2. The Transportation Director is directed to prepare and file a report with the Board in
accordance with ORS 368.346(1).

3. This Board declares its intention to vacate the Public Property if, after reviewing the
