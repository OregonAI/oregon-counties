BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2025-004
Acknowledging Receipt of the Multnomah County Annual Comprehensive Financial Report and
Schedule of Expenditures of Federal Awards for Year Ended June 30, 2024.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The County’s audit f or the year ending June 30, 2024  has been completed and filed
with the appropriate authorities within prescribed deadlines.
b. The County received an unmodified opinion on its financial statements from its external
auditors. Financial statements are presented fairly in accordance with US GAAP.
c. The audit identified one compliance finding, the County had one over expenditure in
the Facilities Management Fund.
d. The County had one internal control finding (significant deficiency) with the audit of
federal expenditures “Single Audit”.
e. There were no significant deficiencies with the three districts  (Dunthorpe-Riverdale
Service District, Mid County Service District, and Library District)  that have their own
financial report (CUFR).
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The Annual Comprehensive Financial Report for the year ended June 30, 20 24 and the
related audit reports and recommendations are accepted.
2. The Board directs the Department of County Management to implement  the
recommendations made by the County’s external auditors and resolve compliance  and
internal control findings.
ADOPTED this 23rd day of January, 2025.
