BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2020-018
Scheduling a Public Hearing and Directing Notice Thereof for the Consideration of the
Legalization of a Portion of SE Foster Road, County Road No. 81.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. SE Foster Road was establish as County Road No. 81 in 1874 and for portions of
said road, from the easterly boundary of the City of Portland to the South boundary
of Multnomah County, the location of the road cannot be accurately determined
due to numerous alternations of the road or a defective survey of the road, and the
road as traveled and used for at least the past ten years does not conform to the
existing records for this county road.
b. On February 7, 2019, consistent with ORS 368.201 to 368.221, the Board of
Commissioners initiated proceedings for the legalization of this portion of SE
Foster Road and directed the County Surveyor to conduct a survey of the road.
c. As required under ORS 368.206(1), the C ounty Surveyor has completed the
survey of the road, attached here as Exhibit A, and prepared a report to the Board
with the proposed new County Road Number “5030.”
d. The above referenced statutes require the County to hold a public hearing to
consider legalization of SE Foster Road and provide notice thereof by personal
service to the abutting property owners and by posting.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The Board of County Commissioner will hold a hearing on Thursday, May 14, 2020
at 9:30 am, in the Multnomah Building, First Floor Commissioners Boardroom 100,
501 SE Hawthorne Boulevard, Portland, Oregon.
2. The purpose of the hearing is to determine whether the legalization of a portion of
SE Foster Road, as County Road No. 5030, is in the public’s interest.  The hearing
will concern SE Foster Road from the easterly boundary of the City of Portland to
the South boundary of Multnomah County.
3. The County Surveyor is directed to provide notice of the hearing to abutting
property owners by service and by posting in a manner consistent with ORS
368.401-369.426.
4. The notice shall advise that all persons interested in or concerned with the road
are invited to attend the hearing.
5. Further the notice shall provide the following information:
• That any objections to the proposal or other information relating thereto must be
filed in the Multnomah County Surveyor’s Office, 1600 SE 190th Avenue, Portland,
Oregon 97233, on or before May 12, 2020;
• A statement as follows: “For more information, call Jim Clayton, County Surveyor
at 503-988-5573”.
ADOPTED this 19th day of March, 2020.
