BEFORE  THE  BOARD  OF  COUNTY  COMMISSIONERS  FOR  MULTNOMAH  COUNTY,  OREGON   RESOLUTION  NO.  2026-027   Resolution  Supporting  a  Gap  Analysis  of  the  County’s  Preschool  for  All  Program.    The  Multnomah  County  Board  of  Commissioners  Finds:   A.   Voters  in  Multnomah  County  passed  Ballot  Measure  26-214  in  November  2020
which

established

the

Preschool

for

All

Program,

providing

up

to

ten

hours

per

day

of

tuition-free,

full-day,

year-round,

school-year

schedule

options

for

all

three

and

four

year

olds

with

a

parent

or

legal

guardian

residing

in

Multnomah

County

with

equitable

access

provided

to

people

of

color

and

historically

marginalized

communities.

The

Ballot

Measure

also

established

a

new

tax

on

Multnomah

County

residents

and

taxable

income

derived

with

the

County

to

fund

the

Preschool

for

All

Program.
  B.   Multnomah  County’s  Preschool  for  All  (PFA)  Program  is  administered  by  the
Department

of

County

Human

Services’

Preschool

and

Early

Learning

Division.

PFA

is

in

the

final

stages

of

growth

where

intentionality

is

key

and

the

Division

is

engaging

in

a

multi-phased

gap

analysis

approach

to

meet

family

needs

to

reach

universality.
  C.   The  Division  has  been  focused  on  rapid  expansions  with  two  key  priorities:
coverage

in

areas

where

priority

families

are

located,

and

not

turning

away

any

qualified

providers.

  D.   A  True  Cost  of  Care  Fiscal  Modeling  Tool  is  being  developed  for  the  Division  by
Prenatal

to

Five

Fiscal

Strategies,

as

approved

by

the

Multnomah

County

Board

of

Commissioners

on

February

5,

2026.
  E.   A  Technical  Advisory  Group  (TAG)  convened  in  July  2025  and  reviewed  the  PFA
Program’s

ongoing

revenue

needs,

financial

sustainability,

fiscal

trends,

and

supported

equitable

early

learning

access

across

the

County

and

made

recommendations

to

the

Board

on

April

7,

2026,

which

included

commissioning

a

preschool

cost

study

to

understand

the

true

costs

of

delivering

preschool

services,

commissioning

demographic

forecasting,

and

staying

focused

on

universality.
   F.   Given  population  changes,  updated  demographic  data  shows  that  potentially  the
actual

number

of

preschools

will

be

significantly

lower

than

earlier

estimates

and

that

saturation

rates

may

be

higher.

The

new

demographic

forecasting

study

will

provide

new

projections.

G.   Because  of  the  potentially  lower  numbers  at  expected  preschools  and  the  faster
enrollment

of

participating

preschool

providers,

Multnomah

County

may

be

closer

to

universal

preschool

than

ever

before.
  H.   The  “gap”  is  the  difference  between  current  enrollment  and  “universal  preschool.”    I.   Geographic  and  demographic  disparities,  variances  in  types  of  available  care,
ranges

in

saturation

and

parent

and

community

needs

survey

results

are

key

elements

to

include

as

the

PFA

Program

closes

the

gaps

to

reach

universality.
  J.   The  Division  has  completed  an  initial  analysis  in  Phase  One  of  a  multi-phased
Gap

Analysis,

that

includes

data

and

analyses

of:
  a.  Geographic  distribution  of  seats  in  the  program  year  2025-2026;    b.  Anticipated  PFA  seats  in  2026-2027;    c.  Geographic  distribution  of  families  who  applied  for  PFA  in  2025-2026;     d.  Results  from  analysis  using  the  Child  Opportunity  Index  conducted  by  the
Division’s

external

evaluation

partners;

  e.  Demographic  information  pulled  from  Census/ACS  data;  and   f.  Family  feedback  to  the  Division  through  qualitative  evaluation  activities  in
2025-2026.
  K.   Developing  a  plan  to  close  the  gaps  to  achieve  universal  preschool  will  best  meet
the

goals

of

intentionally

and

effectively

reaching

universality.

  The  Multnomah  County  Board  of  Commissioners  Resolves:   1.   To  support  the  work  of  the  Preschool  and  Early  Learning  Division  in  the
Department

of

County

Human

Services

in

conducting

a

multi-phase

Gap

Analysis,

as

well

as

establishing

a

timeline,

implementation

plan

and

deliverables

aimed

at

intentionally

and

effectively

reaching

universality

in

alignment

with

the

goals

of

the

program

as

passed

by

voters

and

that

considers

geographic

and

demographic

disparities,

variances

in

types

of

available

care,

and

the

data

from

the

parent

and

community

needs

assessment.
  2.   Phase  One  of  the  Gap  Analysis  to  achieving  universal  preschool  is  complete.
Phase

Two

runs

from

April

15,

2026

and

is

expected

to

end

on

June

30,

2026.

Phase

Two

analyzes

additional

quantitative

data

from:
  a.  Waitlist  data;
b. Information about other publicly funded programs; and
c. Setting type, including alternative hours and programming settings.
3. Phase Three will begin in July 2026 and focuses on qualitative analysis of why
families decline a PFA placement offer and what isn’t working for families -
including scheduling needs, language needs, desired curriculum, and other
unmet preferences - to better meet family needs as the program approaches
universality.
4. In Fall 2026, the Division will begin reviewing 2027-2028 provider applications
and seat allocations. Phase Four of the Gap Analysis will pull together the
analyses from the three prior phases to inform fiscal year 2028 allocations and
help inform to whom the Division conducts provider outreach during its final stage
of growth.
5. To receive a report and public presentation from the Division on the Gap Analysis
by December 31, 2026, when Phase Four is slated to be completed.
Presentation will include all findings, as well as information about how the
analysis has informed the allocation process.
6. To receive a plan from the Division to close the gaps to achieve universal
preschool, as identified in the Gap Analysis, by February 28, 2027.
ADOPTED this 21st day of May, 2026.
