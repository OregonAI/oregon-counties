299.01 JURISDICTION OF STATUTORY ESTABLISHMENT OF WAYS OF
NECESSITY.

(a) The Board of County Commissioners is hereby removed from jurisdiction of the
statutory establishment of ways of necessity. Pursuant to ORS 376.200, the Circuit  Court
of the County shall have jurisdiction of the statutory establishment of ways of necessity.

(b) Nothing in subsection (a) hereof affects any proceedings to establish a way of
necessity if that proceedings was filed on or before August 6, 1980.

(Ord. 80-16. Passed 8-20-80.)
