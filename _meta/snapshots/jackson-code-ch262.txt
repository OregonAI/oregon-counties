262.01 ESTABLISHMENT; COMPOSITION.

There is hereby established in and for the County a County Road Specifications and Road
Variance Advisory Committee, consisting of five members, as follows:  The County
Engineer or a representative designated by the Community Development Director, the
Planning Manager or a representative designated by the Community Development
Director, the County Surveyor and two members of the County Roads Committee.

(Order 277-81. Passed 9-30-81; Ord. 2003-4. Passed 1-22-03.)
