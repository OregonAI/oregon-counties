BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2025-058
Approving the Project Plan for Construction for the Rockwood Community Health Center
Upgrade Project per the FAC-1 Administrative Procedure.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Multnomah County acquired the Rockwood Community Health Center property on
January 13, 2023.
B. The Facilities and Property Management Division assessed the current condition
of the facility and proposed, in coordination with Integrated Clinical Services, a
collection of improvements and upgrades. These improvements were presented in
a series of Program Offers in the FY 2024 budget.
C. In the FY 2024 budget, Program Offer 78237-Rockwood Community Health Center
- Priority 1, Program Offer 78238 -Rockwood Community Health Center - Priority
2, and Program Offer 78239 - Rockwood Community Health Center - Priority 3
were adopted.
D. Multnomah County received a Notice of Award on September 23, 2024 for a
Federal Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) grant for
Rockwood Community Health Center improvements.
E. Leadership in the Department of County Assets, the Multnomah County Health
Department and Integrated Clinical Services recommends approval to proceed
with construction for the Rockwood Community Health Center Upgrade Project.
F. Approval of the Project Plan for Construction is in the best interests of Multnomah
County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
The Project Plan for  Construction is approved and the project team is authorized to
proceed with the next steps in project delivery including construction of the work.
ADOPTED this 2nd day of October, 2025.
