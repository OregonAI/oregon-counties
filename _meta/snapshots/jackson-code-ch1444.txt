CHAPTER 1444
Unified Board of Appeals

EDITOR'S NOTE:  Provisions relating to the Unified Board of Appeals are codified in
Section 1420.02 of this Building and Housing Code (Section 204 of the Oregon State
Structural Safety Code).

There are no sections in Chapter 1444. This chapter has been established to provide a
place for cross references and any future legislation.
