1068.01 ESTABLISHMENT.

The County Library System is hereby established under ORS Chapter 357, subject to all
requirements and privileges thereof. All policies, procedures and organizational matters
that are in conflict with the new law are hereby amended accordingly.

(Res. Unno. Passed 9-12-75.)
