 ZDO 808: Cemeteries And Crematories 
 

 
 

 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 808.01 Cemetery Standards Cemeteries shall comply with the following standards: Minimum Lot Size: The minimum lot size is 50 acres. Location of Graves: Graves shall be located a minimum of 30 feet from any public right-of-way. 808.02 Crematory Standards Crematories shall comply with the following standards: Minimum Lot Size: The minimum lot size is five acres. Separation from Dwellings: Crematories shall be located a minimum of 500 feet from any existing dwelling other than a dwelling on the subject property. [Amended by Ord. ZDO-268, 10/2/18] Return to the Zoning and Development Ordinance main page. Contact zoninginfo@clackamas.us for additional information."
