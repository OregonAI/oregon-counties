BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2026-021
Resolution Consenting to Chair Vega Pederson's Appointment of Nathaniel VerGow as
the Director of the Homeless Services Department.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3) provides that the Chair’s appointment
of department heads (directors) is consent of a majority of the Board of
Commissioners.
B. The Chair appointed Nathaniel VerGow as the Director for the Joint Office of
Homeless Services with an expected start date beginning May 4, 2026.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
In accordance with Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3), the Board consents to
the appointment of Nathaniel VerGow as the Director of the Homeless Services
Department.
ADOPTED this 9th day of April, 2026.
