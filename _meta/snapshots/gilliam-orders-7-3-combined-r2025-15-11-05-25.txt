IN THE COUNTY COURT FOR GILLIAM COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. R2025-15

A RESOLUTION DECLARING COUNTY VEHICLE AS SURPLUS EQUIPMENT

Whereas, the County Court for Gilliam County sitting as the County governing
body at a regularly scheduled meeting on this 5
th day of November 2025; and

Whereas, the Gilliam County Sheriff’s Office has requested the County Court
declare the vehicle listed below as surplus equipment; and

Whereas, the Gilliam County Sheriff’s Office reported the vehicle is six years old,
has approximately 133,357 miles, and has exceeded its useful life.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Gilliam County Court resolves as follows:

1.  The Gilliam County Court declares the following vehicle as surplus equipment:

2019 Dodge Charger VIN #2C3CDXKT2KH565869

2.  The Gilliam County Court directs the Gilliam County Sheriff’s Office to dispose
 of the vehicle via sale through an online public auction site.

 ADOPTED this 5th day of November 2025, by the Gilliam County Court.

Cris Patnode, County Judge
