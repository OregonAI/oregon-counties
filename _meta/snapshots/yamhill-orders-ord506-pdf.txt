      THE  BOARD   OF   COMMISSIONERS                         OF     YAMHILL           COUNTY,          OREGON   (the
Board),   sat  for    the  transaction                         of     county           business          in  special
session           on May  9,  1990, Commissioners  David                               E.     Bishop,       Dennis         L.
Goecks,           and Ted  Lopuszynski  being present.

      WHEREAS,            Fred and Bland Herring have                            made application  to                  the
Department of             Planning and Development for                           a change in the zone                  map
designation    on          a 33        acre  portion  of Tax Lot  3314-1000 from EF-40,
Exclusive   Farm           Use,        to AF-10,   Agriculture-Forestry/Small   Holding;
and

         WHEREAS,          the    Planning           Commission            determined                that   only       the
westernmost  20 acres  of the subject  area (approximately)  is plan
designated for Rural Residential uses and would accommodate an AF-
10 zone designation,  which is considered a rural residential zone;
and

      WHEREAS,  the Planning Commission voted unanimously to approve
the zone change request    from EF-40   to AF-10    for only that portion
of  the   subject   area  contained   within    the    Chehalem  Richland-
Sunnycrest Exception Area and plan designated for rural residential
uses;        and

         WHEREAS,         no     appeal        has   been     taken         from        the     decision        of     the

FINDINGS:

A.    Background           Facts

      Le   Lot Size:    The subject property  is composed of two tax
lots under the same ownership that total 133.36 acres.     The request
pertains  to only   the western  33 acres  of Tax Lot  3314-1000   (see
attached maps).

      2.   Access:    The  133.36 acres    is located   northwest  of  the
intersection   of  Herring   Lane and   Red   Hills Road.     The portion
subject  to the zone change request     fronts only Herring Lane.

     s.    On-site Land Use:                          Mixed agriculture and two dwellings.
The subject 33 acres contains                         one dwelling and is otherwise devoted
to livestock grazing.

         4.         Surrounding         Land    Use:     Agriculture     and       rural     residential.

       §,    Surrounding  Zoning:     North,   AF-20                         and    AF-10;        east,       AF-
20;   south,  EF-40  and AF-10;   west,  AF-10.

      6.   Comprehensive Plan Map Designation:    A majority of the
property is designated Agriculture/Forestry Large Holding     (AFLH).
The westernmost 20 acres  (approximately)  of the property is within
the Chehalem Richland/Sunnycrest   exception area and is designated
Agriculture/Forestry                   Small        Holding   (AFSH).

      7.   Soils:      According to the Soil Survey of Yamhill         County,
the  soils   on    the   property    are   primarily   a mix    of   Woodburn,
Willakenzie,    and Chehalem.     The subject 33 acres are predominantly
Willakenzie,    which    are  agricultural    capability  Class    III  or VI,
depending on slope steepness.         There are smaller areas of Jory and
Nekia     soils,         which    are       Class     III.    (See   soils     map,    attached)

B.     Ordinance              Provisions          and     Analysis

      it         Section   1208.02  of the Yamhill   County Zoning Ordinance
provides        criteria   for quasi-judicial   review of requests  for zone
boundary        amendments.    These criteria are:

        (a)   The              proposed            change   is consistent                  with  the   goals,
        policies,              and other          applicable provisions of                 the Comprehensive
        Plan;

        (b)   There   is an existing, demonstrable   need   for the  par-
        ticular   uses allowed by the requested  zone,   considering  the
        importance of such uses to the citizenry or the economy of the
        area, the existing market demand which such uses will satisfy,
        and the availability and location of other lands so zoned and
        their suitability for the uses allowed by the zone;

        (c)   The   proposed    change   is   appropriate  considering  the
        surrounding land uses,      the density and pattern of development
        in  the   area,  any   changes   which   may  have occurred  in the
        vicinity to support the proposed amendment and the availabili-
        ty of utilities     and services    likely to be needed by the an-
        ticipated uses    in the proposed district;

        (d)   The             other lands in the county already designated for                                   the
        proposed              uses are either unavailable or not as well suited                                  for
        the     anticipated               uses     due    to   location,      size,        or    other    factors.

        (e)         The        amendment           is     consistent        with     the        current       Oregon
       Administrative                     Rules     for     exceptions,       if    applicable.

        The     "current              Oregon      Administrative            Rules    for        exceptions"      are
contained           in       OAR    660-04-018.           Those    rules     state,        in    relevant      part:

      2:    Regarding   criterion   (a) above,   the proposal     to rezone
the entire   33 acres   referenced   in the application      to AF-10 would
not  be  consistent    with   the  county's    comprehensive     plan.     As
demonstrated by the attached exception area map,          the area has two
separate   plan  designations.     The  easternmost    13 acres     are plan
designated "Agriculture/Forestry Large Holding"         (AFLH).    The AF-10
zone   designation            is       a   "Rural   Residential"           district,   and     is   incon-
sistent  with  the  AFLH  plan    designation.     Because  of this,  the
county  cannot  approve  the   zone   change  application  as submitted,
without amending the plan and adopting a new exception.

      3:   With   the  applicant's  tacit   approval,   the    county   has
proceeded to consider the applicant's request for a zone change toa
include  only   that  area   within the   boundaries  of    the   Chehalem
Richland/Sunnycrest rural residential exception area.         Plan policy
I.B.i.a.   of  the        Revised   Goals    and                  Policies,    Yamhill    County  Com-
prehensive    Plan         (Ord.  206,    1979)                   states    that  areas    designated
Agriculture/Forestry Small Holding (AFSH),                                 are "the appropriate and
desired   location    for    rural   residential                            development".      A  zone
designation    change     to   AF-10   for  that                           portion   of   applicant's
property within    the AFSH     plan  designated                           area would promote     that
policy.

      4.     Regarding  criterion   (b)   above,   the applicant   has indi-
cated    there   is  a demand   for   small    acreage   tracts  that  allow
dwellings and small-scale farm use.          It is implied that the demand
for such land exceeds the current supply,          and that zoning accounts
for the lack of supply.

      The  westernmost     20  acres   of  the   property     is   within   the
Chehalem Richland/Sunnycrest       exception   area.     The Newberg-Dundee
exception area     is adjacent,   and both areas     include    AF-10  zoning.
According   to the Exceptions     Study Areas Dwelling       Intensification
Analysis   (9~86),   the two exception areas contain 1531 acres of AF-
10 zoning,    which would allow     153 dwellings.      At the    time of the

      S:.    Regarding criterion   (c) above,   the land surrounding the

smaller than ten acres     (see attached maps).     The zone change would

by small-acreage agricultural use.       The proposed zoning designation

      Public    facilities necessary  for residential development are
available    to the site.   The Willakenzie soils on the property are
considered generally suitable for on-site sewage disposal systems,
according              to       the        County        Sanitarian       (oral      communication,             2-90).

      6.               The           findings            in   Section  B.4          of   this  report  apply    to
criterion               (d)          as well.              Only  the AFSH          areas   around  Newberg   were
considered,                     so     the        location       aspect       of     criterion          (d)      has        been
addressed.   Areas designated Very Low Density Residential    on the
Comprehensive Plan map were not considered because they are planned
as purely rural residential with agriculture as an incidental use,
so they are of a different    character than that  planned for AFSH
areas.

      qs   The portion   of the area   subject to the requested  zone
change within   the existing  exception area does not require   a new
exception because the uses allowed by the requested zone have been
incorporated  into the exception justification.

        8.             Regarding                 criterion
                                                         (e)         above,        the    Chehalem     Richland/Sun-
nycrest exception area was established under a "committed"   excep-
tion   to statewide planning goal   3 (agricultural  lands).     The
exception              allows              rural     residential          (and     agricultural)              uses     of    the
type contemplated by the AF-10 zone designation.     Existing types
of development in the exception area include rural residential and
agricultural uses.   The uses allowed under the AF-10 zone designa-
tion are therefore the same as existing types of development in the
exception area.

        9.             Also           regarding            criterion       (e),      and     in    addition            to    (8)
above,  the                 uses        contemplated  by the  AF-10 zone  designation are
rural uses.                  The       intensity of development allowed in the AF-10 zone

above,         the    uses          allowed           in    the   AF-10       district               are     identified            and
authorized             in  AFSH  plan-designated                              areas  by  specific                          related
policies   in          the county's acknowledged                              comprehensive plan.

CONCLUSIONS:

      a    The request to change the zone designation to AF-10 for
that portion of applicant's   parcel that is plan designated "Agri-
culture/Forestry Large Holding" is not consistent with the county's
comprehensive  plan.

     2.    The request   to change the zone designation to AF-10 for
that portion of applicant's     parcel that is plan designated     "Agri-
culture/Forestry   Small   Holding"   and  within    the Chehalem   Rich-
land/Sunnycrest rural residential exception      area is consistent with
the county's comprehensive plan.

         3.          There          is    a    lack    of   developable             land        in    AF-10        zoned     areas,
so   there       is        a   need       for        additional        land    with            that        designation.

     4.    The proposed change  is appropriate for the area because
uses and densities allowed in the AF-10 zone are in character with
the surrounding area,  and necessary public services and utilities
are available.

         5.       There is a                    lack of other              land          in     the     county        available
with     the     AF-10 zoning                   designation.

         6.          The       approval              granted      is   consistent               with        OAR    660-04-018.
