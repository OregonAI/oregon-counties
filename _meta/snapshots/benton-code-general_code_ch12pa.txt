 ### CHAPTER 12 - PARKING

 ### 12.005 - Parking Violations.

(1)
In all prosecutions for violation of motor vehicle parking laws, it shall be sufficient
for a police officer, sheriff, or deputy sheriff to charge the defending by an unsworn
written notice to appear in court at a time and place certain if that notice clearly
states:
(a)
The date, place and nature of the charge;
(b)
The time and place for the defendant's appearance in court;
(c)
The name of the issuing officer;
(d)
The license number of the vehicle.
(2)
The notice shall either be delivered to the defendant personally or placed in a conspicuous
place upon the vehicle involved in the violation. The notice shall serve as the complaint
in the case. In all other respects the procedure now provided by law in such cases
shall be followed.
(3)
Benton County Circuit Court shall have jurisdiction over parking offenses committed
within the County.
