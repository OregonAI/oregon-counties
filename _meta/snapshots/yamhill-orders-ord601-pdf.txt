incorporated herein provide a justification for the Board’s tentative decision of October 4, 1995.

E.      The attached Exhibit "A" demonstrates that the proposed amendment to the Yamhill
County Comprehensive Plan Map and the Official Zoning Map of Yamhill County are in the best

          Ordinance Provisions and Analysis
i

          The request is to replat a portion of the Earlwood Plat and rezone it and an adjacent
          property to AF-40 so that it will remain in forest use and not be developed as 10-acre
          residential lots.

          The zone change portion of the request must comply with the standards and criteria in
          the Yamhill County Zoning Ordinance (YCZO),         Section 1208.03,     which states:

          A quasi-judicial zone change to (1) amend the designation of land from Exclusive
          Farm Use, Agriculture/Forestry, or Forest to another of these zones, or (2) change the
          minimum lot size of land designated Exclusive Farm Use or Agriculture/Forestry, may
          be authorized, pursuant to Subsection 1208.01, provided that the request satisfies all
          applicable requirements of this ordinance, and also provided that the applicant
          demonstrates compliance with the following criteria:

          A.       The proposed amendment shall comply with the goals, policies, and other
                   applicable provisions of the comprehensive plan.

                                                                            EXHIBIT "A" - Ordinance 601
                                                                                                   Page 2
B.     The proposed designation shall be appropriate for the existing or intended use
       of the property.

C.     The proposed amendment shall result in an area of at least 160 contiguous
       acres with the requested designation, including adjacent land.

D.     For proposed changes within or to an Exclusive Farm Use designation, the new
       minimum lot size shall be appropriate to maintain the existing commercial
       agricultural enterprise in the area.

E.     For proposed changes within or to an Agriculture/Forestry designation, the
       new minimum lot size shall be shown to assure:

       1.     The opportunity for economically efficient forest and agriculture
              practices typically occurring in the area; and

       2       The opportunity for the continuous growing and harvesting of forest tree
               species; and

       =       The conservation of other forest values found on forest lands.

These criteria are addressed, respectively, in Findings B.2 through B.6, below.

Comprehensive Plan Policy (c) of Goal II.A.1 states:

Yamhill County recognizes that certain areas of the county are characterized by such a
mixture of farm and forest use that the agricultural lands and forest lands goals of this
Plan are both applicable. Farm and forest resources within these areas shall be
protected through mixed-use zoning that recognizes both types of use....

The subject property contains soils which are suitable for both agricultural and forest
use. The location and hillside topography of the property are similar to other areas of
the county zoned AF-20, but are dissimilar to the valley floors which are zoned EF-
40 and the coast range hills that are zoned F-80.   Consequently, the mixed farm and
forest designation is appropriate and the request complies with criterion (a).

Regarding criterion (b), the applicant states that the property has been logged in the
past and will be reforested rather than developed as individual 10 acre lots that could
each be used for a residence.   The AF-40 designation is appropriate for the intended
use,

There are no adjacent lands zoned AF-40, so the 88 acre subject property will be
smaller that the 160 acre block required by criterion (c). However, an area of
approximately 115 acres adjacent to the subject property is zoned EF-40, so there will
be more than 160 total contiguous acres zoned for resource use.

The proposed zoned change does not involve an EF designation, so criterion (d) does
not apply.

       exception area, and the request would reverse that and make the land subject to

       Replat Provisions and Analysis
IP

       Since the county recognizes individual subdivision lots, regardless of the minimum lot
       size required by the zoning designation, a replat of lots 9 - 12 and 14 of the Earlwood
       Plat is part of this request.   Without the replat, each of the subdivision lots could still
       be sold and potentially developed separately even if the zoning is changed to AF-40.

       There are no specific requirements in the Yamhill County Land Division Ordinance to
       govern the replat of a previously recorded subdivision plat. However, a new plat
       map will need to be recorded showing the vacated lots. The two platted roads must
       be retained in order to provide access to lots 7, 8 and 13 of the Earlwood Plat. These
       factors should be included as a condition of approval.

CONCLUSIONS:

1.     The request is for an amendment to the Yamhill County Comprehensive Plan map
       from Agriculture/Forestry Small Holding to Agriculture Forestry Large Holding, a
       zone change from AF-10 to AF-40, and a replat of a portion of the Earlwood Plat.

       The proposal complies with the criteria in Section 1208.03 of the Yamhill County
       Zoning Ordinance for a zone change because the AF-40 designation is appropriate for
       the intended use of the property for forestry and will result in a contiguous area of
       resource land that is larger than 160 acres.

       With conditions, the replat can comply with the provisions of the Yamhill County
       Land Division Ordinance.

DECISION:

In accordance with the implementing ordinance, the Board hereby approves the requested
zone change from AF-10 to AF-40 and a comprehensive plan amendment from Agriculture
Forestry Small Holding to Agriculture Forestry Large Holding on Tax Lots 3236-3500, 3600
and part of 2800, and a replat of lots 9, 10, 11, 12 and 14 of the Earlwood Plat. Approval
of the replat is made subject to the following conditions:

1,             A plat map approved by the Planning Director which shows the vacated lots
               shall be recorded with the County Clerk not later than October 11, 1996. All
               platted roads which have not been vacated prior to the time of recording shall
               be retained and shown on the face of the plat.
