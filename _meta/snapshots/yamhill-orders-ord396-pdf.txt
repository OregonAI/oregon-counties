IV.   ZONE CHANGE           REVIEW CRITERIA

Section   1207.02 of the Yamhill                County Zoning Ordinance,                    adopted         by the Board of
Commissioners in December of 1982, states that an amendment                                    to the Official Zoning
Map may be authorized, provided that the applicant demonstrates the following:

          ©   That        the     proposed       amendment               is   consistent    with      the        goals     and
                 policies of the Comprehensive Plan;

          0      That there is an existing, demonstrable need for the particular uses
                 proposed for the property once the zone is changed, considering the
                 importance of such uses to the citizenry or the economy of the area,
                 the existing market demand for any goods or services which such uses
                 will provide and the presence or absence and location of other such
                 uses or similar uses in the area;

          o      That the proposed amendment is timely, considering the pattern of
                 development        in the area, surrounding land uses, any changes                                      which
                 may have occurred in the neighborhood or community to warrant the
                 proposed amendment, and the availability of utilities and services to
                 serve the proposed uses or other potential uses in the proposed zoning                                               |
                 district; and
                                                                                                                                      P{
          o      That     there    are   no    other   lands           in the County       already zoned            for the
                 proposed uses, or, if there are such lands, that they are either                                                     |
                 unavailable or unsuitable due to location, size or other factors.

The applicant proposes that the zoning of the subject property be changed: from Light
Industrial (LI) to Neighborhood Commercial (NC).      The NC District is intended to
provide limited convenience commercial services for a specific residential market area
outside urban growth boundaries.

An analysis of why the proposed zone change to NC is consistent with the four criteria
listed in Section 1207.02 of the Zoning Ordinance is presented below.

A.    Compliance with Comprehensive Plan Policies

                        explanation      of     why    the   proposed           amendment      from         an    industrial     to

                        Comprehensive Plan.
