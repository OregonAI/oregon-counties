 ### PREFACE TO THE REPUBLICATION

This Code constitutes a republication of the general and permanent ordinances pertaining
to the Zoning, Partition and Subdivision regulations of Union County, Oregon.
Source materials used in the preparation of the republication were the County Zoning,
Partition and Subdivision regulations, as updated through July 2024, and subsequent
ordinances. The source of each new section is included in the history note appearing
in parentheses at the end thereof. By use of the comparative tables appearing in the
back of this republication, the reader can locate any section of the Code, as updated.
Acknowledgments
The publisher is most grateful to Inga Williams, Planning Director, for her cooperation
and assistance during the progress of the work on this republication. It is hoped
that their efforts and those of the publisher have resulted in a County Zoning, Partition
and Subdivision Code which will make the applicable laws of the County readily accessible
to all citizens and which will be a valuable tool in the day-to-day administration
of the County's affairs.
