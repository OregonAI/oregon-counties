1227.11 TERMINATION OF URBAN RENEWAL AGENCY OF JACKSON
COUNTY.

(a) The Urban Renewal Agency of Jackson County is terminated.

(b) All facilities, files and personnel of the Urban Renewal Agency of Jackson County
are transferred to Jackson County, Oregon.

(c) The termination of the Urban Renewal Agency of Jackson County shall not affect
any outstanding legal actions, contracts or obligations of the Urban Renewal Agency of
Jackson County.  Jackson County shall be substituted for the Urban Renewal Agency of
Jackson County on any outstanding legal actions, contracts or obligations of the Urban
Renewal Agency of Jackson County, and, for the purpose of those legal actions, contracts
or obligations, Jackson County shall be considered a continuation of the Urban Renewal
Agency of Jackson County and no a new entity.

(Ord. 2014-5. Passed 6-04-14)
