CHAPTER 882
Tax for Fire Patrol on Private Forest Lands

EDITOR
'S NOTE:  Upon certification by the Office of the State Forester to the Board of
County Commissioners of the budgeted cost for providing forest patrol services for fire
protection on private forest lands in the County, the Board levies a tax and directs the
County Assessor and the County Tax Collector to assess and collect the same and directs
the County Treasurer to pay it to the Office of the State Forester, pursuant to ORS
477.270.

Copies of the latest relevant legislation may be obtained, at cost, from the Office of the
County Counsel.

There are no sections in Chapter 882. This chapter has been established to provide a place
for cross references and any future legislation.
