BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON

RESOLUTION NO. 2021-067

Consenting to Chair Kafoury’s Appointment of Tracey Massey  as the Director of the
Department of County Assets, Mohammad Bader as the Director of the Department of
County Human Services and Ebony Clarke as the Director of the Health Department.

The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:

a. Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3) provides that the Chair’s appointment
of department heads (directors) is subject to consent of a majority of the Board.

b. The Chair appointed Tracey Massey as the Director of the Department of County
Assets beginning August 1, 2021.

c. The Chair appointed Mohammad Bader  as the Direct or of the Department  of
County Human Services beginning August 1, 2021.

d. The Chair appointed Ebony Clarke as the Director of the Health Department
beginning August 1, 2021.

The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:

1.  In accordance with Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3), the Board
consents to the appointment of Tracey Massey as the Director of the Department
of County Assets.

2.  In accordance with Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3), the Board
consents to the appointment of Mohammad Bader  as the Director of the
Department of County Human Services.
