BEFORE THE MULTNOMAH COUNTY LIBRARY DISTRICT BOARD
RESOLUTION NO.
Adopting the Fiscal Year 2023 Budget for the Multnomah County Library District and Making
Appropriations Thereunder, Pursuant to ORS 294.456.
The Multnomah County Library District (MCLD) Board Finds:
a. The MCLD budget, as prepared by the duly appointed MCLD Director/Budget Officer,
has been considered and approved by the MCLD Board acting as the Budget
Committee.
b. A public hearing on this budget was held before the Multnomah County Tax
Supervising and Conservation Commission on the 1st day of June, 2022.
c. The budget is now on file in the Multnomah County Library’s Administrative Office.
d. The MCLD Board has made two amendments to the above described budget and
those amendments are attached to this resolution as Attachments A and B.
e. The appropriation authorized is as follows:
GENERAL FUND (1520)
Library Services $       100,306,769
Cash Transfers $         18,456,969
Operating Contingency $ 500,000
Total Appropriation $       119,263,738
Unappropriated Ending Fund Balance $           9,886,108
Total Requirements $       129,149,846
CAPITAL FUND (2514)
Library Services $           6,386,534
Operating Contingency $         47,251,603
Total Appropriation $         53,638,137
Total Requirements $         53,638,137
f. The Tax Supervising and Conservation Commission has certified the budget and
there are no recommendations and no objections.
2022-058
The Multnomah County Library District Board Resolves:
1. The budget, including Attachments A and B, is adopted as the budget of the MCLD.
2. The appropriation shown above is authorized for the fiscal year July 1, 2022
to June 30, 2023.
ADOPTED this 16th day of June, 2022.
