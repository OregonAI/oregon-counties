         NOW   ON THIS     DAY,   the above-entitled matter having come              on regularly for
consideration, said day being one duly set in term for the transaction of public business and
a majority of the Court being present; and

         IT APPEARING TO THE COURT:             That on June 26, 1983, Wasco County adopted
by ordinance the City of The Dalles Zoning Ordinance for land lying within the urban
growth   area, and has previously entered into an (amended)                  urban growth      area joint
management     agreement   with   the   City, which   requires   that plans        and    implementing
ordinances be coordinated within the urban growth area (outside city limits and inside the
urban growth boundary); and

         IT FURTHER APPEARING TO THE COURT:                  That on Thursday, August 5, 1993,
the City of The Dalles Planning Commission held a public hearing on the matter and made
a motion to adopt the proposed change to the City of The Dalles Urban Growth Boundary
and Zoning Map; and

         IT FURTHER APPEARING TO THE COURT:                      That on Tuesday, September 7,
1993, the City of The Dalles City Council held a public hearing on the matter and made a
decision to adopt the proposed change to the City of The Dalles Urban Growth Boundary
and Zoning Map; and

         IT FURTHER APPEARING TO THE COURT:                That on Monday, September 13,
1993, the Wasco County Planning Commission held a public hearing on the matter, asked
for testimony, deliberated and offered to the Wasco County Court a recommendation for
approval of the proposed amendment to the City of The Dalles Urban Growth Boundary
and Zoning Map; and

         IT FURTHER      APPEARING      TO THE COURT:        That on October 6, 1993, the
Court undertook a review of the above recommendation for a proposed amendment to the
City of The Dalles Urban Growth Boundary and Zoning Map; and

         IT FURTHER APPEARING TO THE COURT:                That the Court took notice of the
record, heard the staff report and asked for testimony and evidence and then closed the
hearing for further input.   The Court then announced it would deliberate; and

         NOW   THEREFORE:         Based upon the adopted findings of fact and substantial
evidence in the record, and the Court being fully advised in the premises;

         IT IS HEREBY    ORDERED:       That the request by Kim Schafer to amend the City
of The    Dalles Urban   Growth    Boundary   and Zoning   Map,   for property   described   as
Township 2 North, Range 13 East W.M., Section 32, Tax Lots 2603 and 2604, from County
"R-2" to City (Urban Growth Boundary) "R-1", be approved.

         EMERGENCY       CLAUSE:     Inasmuch as this ordinance amendment is necessary for
the immediate preservation of health, peace and safety, an emergency is hereby declared
to exist and this ordinance amendment shall be in full force and effect immediately upon
the filing of this order with the Wasco County Clerk.

         SIGNED this 20th day of October, 1993.
