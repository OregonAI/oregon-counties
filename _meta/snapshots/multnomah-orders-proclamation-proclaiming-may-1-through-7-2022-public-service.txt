BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2022-035
Proclaiming May 1 through 7, 2022 Public Service Recognition Week in Multnomah
County, Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Since Multnomah County was established 167 years ago, its employees have dedicated
themselves to bettering the community and the lives of those who live here.
b. Today, approximately 6,000 Multnomah County employees provide community members
a wide range of health and human services, public safety services, and other
government services.
c. Multnomah County, and all counties, take seriously their responsibility to protect and
enhance the health, welfare and safety of its residents in sensible and cost effective
ways.
d. Each and every day, Multnomah County employees provide the diverse services
demanded by the American people of their government with efficiency and integrity.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
The week of May 1 through May 7, 2022 as “Public Service Recognition Week” in Multnomah
County, Oregon and calls upon the people of our community to recognize the crucial role public
employees in Multnomah County have in improving our community and lives.
ADOPTED this 28th day of April, 2022.
SUBMITTED BY: Deborah Kafoury, Multnomah County Chair
