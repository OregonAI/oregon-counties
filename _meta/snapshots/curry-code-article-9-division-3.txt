ARTICLE NINE

DIVISION THREE             TIE DOWN CHARGES            AT    THE   BROOJQNGS
                           STATE AIRPORT
SECTION 9.03.010           TIE DOWN FEES
A transient aircraft tie down   fee at the Brookings      State Airport is hereby
established:
(1) A one day fee shall be charged for each day or portion of a day an aircraft
is tied down at the shai ad   State Airport.   However, no fee shall be assessed
on a plane which incurs a freight handler's charge on a flight to or from the
Brookings State Airport.
(2) The amount of the fee shall be set by county order.

(3) The fee is payable by use of an envelope/collection box system prior to the
aircraft leaving the tie down area.
SECTION 9.03.020           EAILURE TO PAY FEE
Any owner, agent of the owner or operator of any transient aircraft who fails to
timely pay a tie down fee as pro by this ordinance commits an infraction
enforceable under Article Ten of this ordinance.
