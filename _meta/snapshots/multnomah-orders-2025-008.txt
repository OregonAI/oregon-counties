BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2025-008
Declaring Space Within the New NW Library Building, Located at 2030 NW Pettygrove Street,
Portland, Surplus.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. This matter comes to the Board pursuant to Administrative Procedure FAC-3, which
establishes the County's policy for lease of surplus County real property to a non-County
tenant.
B. Except for short term permits, the Board of County Commissioners is required to declare
property surplus before it can be offered for lease. When Facilities and Property
Management determines that a property is surplus, it will prepare a Resolution for
presentation to the Board of County Commissioners for its consideration.
C. The new NW Library building located at 2030 NW Pettygrove Street, Portland (the
“Property”) is owned by Multnomah County. Planned library programming will use
approximately 11,300 square feet out of a total 17,600 square feet, as shared with the
Board during the Resolution No. 2024-068 presentation. The remaining 6,300 square
feet of space (the “Space”) is not needed and is appropriate for declaring surplus. The
revenues from leasing the non-library space will be used to offset the costs of
building and property management of the site
D. The Multnomah County Director of Facilities & Property Management Division
(“Director”) has determined that there are no other current or anticipated library, or
County uses for the Space and it is appropriate for lease to a non-County tenant. The
Director of Multnomah County Libraries supports the Director’s recommendation.
E. It is in the best interests of the County to act on the Director's determinations and
recommendations.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
A. The Space is declared surplus.
B. The Director will take appropriate steps towards preparing the Space for lease and
return to the Board at a future public meeting with a recommendation on leasing the
Space.
ADOPTED this 30th day of January, 2025.
