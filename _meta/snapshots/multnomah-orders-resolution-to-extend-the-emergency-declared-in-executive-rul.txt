BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY
COMMISSIONERS FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY,
OREGON
RESOLUTION NO.
To Extend the Emergency Declared in Executive Rule 392 to October 15, 2021, or until
Rescinded.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Unusually hot and dry conditions have and continue to create an imminent risk of
wildfires.  On June 23, 2021, the Multnomah Fire Defense Board issued a burn ban
throughout all areas of Multnomah County, west of the Sandy River.  On June 29, 2021,
Governor Kate Brown, issued Executive Order No. 21-17, declaring a statewide State of
Emergency Due to Imminent Threat of Wildfires.  Fireworks present an unreasonable risk
of wildfires under current conditions.  Local jurisdictions in Oregon and Washington have
banned the use of fireworks, including a ban on the sale and use of fireworks in Portland,
Gresham, unincorporated Clark County, Washington and Vancouver, Washington.
B. On July 1, 2021, Executive Rule 392 was adopted Declaring an Emergency to ban
the sal e and use of consumer fireworks as defined by ORS 480.111(2) and display
fireworks as defined by ORS 480.111(4) throughout unincorporated Multnomah County.
The Declaration of Emergency is set to expire on July 31, 2021.
C. Extreme wildfire damage including the possibility of loss of life and extensive loss
of property, both private and public can be expected from the use of fireworks.
D. Multnomah County Code 25.450(B) states that the initial declaration of emergency
may not last for more than 30 days.  A declaration of emergency may be extended or
terminated by the Board.
E. This Resolution extends the emergency actions declared by Executive Rule 392.
