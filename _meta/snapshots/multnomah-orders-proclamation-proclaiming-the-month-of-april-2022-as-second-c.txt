BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2022-032
Proclaiming the Month of April 2022 as "Second Chance Month" in Multnomah County,
Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Every person is endowed with human dignity and value.
b. Everyone deserves an opportunity to rebuild their lives, reach their fullest
potential and become a productive member of society.
c. Individuals with a criminal history often struggle to find housing, secure
employment, pursue higher education, regain voting rights, volunteer in their
community, and pay for significant debt that arises as a result of their conviction and
incarceration.
d. The COVID-19 pandemic has exacerbated many of the struggles faced by
individuals with a criminal history.
e. The Department of Community Justice provides services and information for
those individuals who are returning to their community through supervision,
programmatic activities and outreach.
f. 831 individuals were released from state custody in 2021, 754 required housing
assistance, 460 were identified as persons who could use mental health supports, 196
have medical mobility issues, and 29 were identified with a cognitive or intellectual
disability.
g. The designation of April as Second Chance Month would contribute to increased
public awareness about the need for support and closure for those who have paid their
debt, and opportunities for individuals, employers, and communities to extend second
chances.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
The month of April 2022, is declared Second Chance Month in Multnomah County,
Oregon to honor reentry efforts in our community that support individuals making the
challenging transition back into society from incarceration.
ADOPTED this 21th day of April, 2022.
