Establishing Permit Fees for the Mid -Multnomah County Street Lighting Service District
No. 14.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The Mid-Multnomah County Street Lighting Service District No. 14 (“District”) is a
special district formed  under Oregon Revised Statute (“ORS”) Chapter 451.  As
authorized under ORS 451.550(1), the District supervises, manages, controls,
operates, and maintains street lighting facilities in unincorporated Multnomah
County and the cities of Troutdale, Fairview, and Maywood Park.
b. The Multnomah County Board of County Commissioners serves as the governing
body for the District (“District Board”).
c. As part of its authority under ORS 451.550 and ORS 451.600, the District may
charge fees to cover the costs of providing street lighting facility services, including
the costs of implementing permitting requirements to ensure construction,
installation, or alteration of District facilities, or attachments made to District
facilities, meet District standards.
d. The District Board adopted an ordinance establishing permit requirements and that
the District may charge fees, to be set by District Board Resolution, for District
services provided as part of permitting processes.

The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The Construction Permit fee is set at $575.00 and shall remain in effect until
amended by the District Board.
2. Public Bodies, as defined in the District’s Administrative Rules, are exempt from
the Construction Permit fee.
