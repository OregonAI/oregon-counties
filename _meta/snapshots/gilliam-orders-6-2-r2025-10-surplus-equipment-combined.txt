IN THE COUNTY COURT FOR GILLIAM COUNTY, OREGON

RESOLUTION NO. R2025-10

A RESOLUTION DECLARING COUNTY VEHICLE AS SURPLUS

WHEREAS, the Gilliam County Facilities Director and Maintenance Fleet Manager has
requested the County Court declare a vehicle as surplus equipment; and

WHEREAS, the vehicle listed below has been used by Gilliam County for 14 years and
has served its useful life; and

WHEREAS, the vehicle has fuel pump, oil pump, and tire sensor issues;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Gilliam County Court hereby resolves as follows:

1. The Gilliam County Court declares the following vehicle to be surplus equipment:

 2011 Chevrolet Equinox Vin# 2CNFLEEC5B6224792

2. The Gilliam County Court directs the Gilliam County Sheriff’s office to dispose of
the vehicle via sale through an online public auction site.

Adopted by the Gilliam County Court this 4th day of June, 2025.
