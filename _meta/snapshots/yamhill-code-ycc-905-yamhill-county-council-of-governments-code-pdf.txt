EXHIBIT A: AGREEMENT OF MID-WILLAMETTE COUNCIL OF GOVERNMENTS

9.05.01 Intent. That it is the intent of the Board of Commissioners of Yamhill County to create, along with the
other parties to the agreement, an intergovernmental entity known as the Mid- Willamette Valley Council of
Governments.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 537 eff 06/24/92]

9.05.01 Effective Date; Public Purpose; Powers, Duties & Functions. That the effective date, specification of
public purposes for which MWVCOG is created, and the powers, duties, and functions of the MWVCOG are as
stated in the agreement attached hereto as Exhibit “A” and by this reference incorporated herein.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 537 eff 06/24/92]

9.05.01 Agreement Ratified . That the agreement is hereby ratified and the chairman and clerk are hereby
authorized and directed to sign the same on behalf of Yamhill County.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 537 eff 06/24/92]
