BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY , OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2024-033
Proclaiming May 5 through May 11, 2024 as Public Service Recognition Week in
Multnomah County, Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Since Multnomah County was established 170 years ago, its employees have dedicated
themselves to bettering the community and the lives of those who live here.
B. T oday, approximately 6,300 Multnomah County employees provide community members
a wide range of health and human services, public safety services, and other
government services.
C. Multnomah County, and all counties, take seriously their responsibility to protect and
enhance the health, welfare and safety of its community in sensible and cost effective
ways.
D. Each and every day, Multnomah County employees provide the diverse services our
community deserves from their government, with efficiency and integrity.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
The week of May 5 through May 11, 2024 as Public Service Recognition Week in Multnomah
County, Oregon and calls upon the people of our community to recognize the crucial role public
employees in Multnomah County have in improving our community and lives.
ADOPTED this 2nd day of May, 2024.
