 Chapter 10.04
 
 
PROHIBITING PARKING ON CERTAIN CLATSOP COUNTY ROADS AND RIGHTS-OF-WAY 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 § 10.04.010
 
 
Definitions. 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 

 “County road” means a public road under the jurisdiction of the County that has been designated as a County road under ORS 368.016 . “County road right-of-way” means a strip of land of established width as stated in resolution and orders accepting the road as a County road under ORS 368.016 . “Motor vehicle” means any device in, upon or by which any person or property is or may be transported or drawn upon a public roadway which is self-propelled or designed for self-propulsion and further includes a “trailer” standing alone or in conjunction with another motor vehicle—“trailer” meaning every vehicle without motive power designed to be drawn by another vehicle. “Trailer” includes, but is not limited to, the following types of trailers: 1.  Balance trailers; 2.  Bus trailers; 3.  Commercial bus trailers; 4.  Farm trailers; 5.  Mobile homes; 6.  Pole trailers; 7.  Self-supporting trailers; 8.  Semi-trailers; 9.  Special use trailers; 10.  Travel trailers; and 11.  Truck trailers. “Owner” means the registered owner of the vehicle as displaced in the records of the Motor Vehicles Department. “Parked motor vehicle” means a stationary, unoccupied motor vehicle, regardless of whether the engine is running or not. ( Ord. 91-11 § 3 ) 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 § 10.04.020
 
 
Prohibitions. 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 

 A.  The Clatsop County Roadmaster is authorized to designate which County roads and County rights-of-way shall be subject to the provisions of this chapter when, in the opinion of the Roadmaster, the safe and adequate flow of traffic necessitates the implementation of the provisions of this chapter. Signs shall be posted to designate the road or right-of-way on which parking is restricted. The action by the Roadmaster so authorized may be taken without any action by the Clatsop County Board of Commissioners and/or amendments of this chapter. B.  Except for temporary purposes when necessary to avoid conflict with other traffic or when in compliance with directives of a peace officer or traffic control device, it shall be unlawful for anyone to: 1.  Park a motor vehicle within 10 feet of the traveled surfaces of a County road designated pursuant to subsection A of this section. 2.  Park on the right-of-way of a County road designed pursuant to subsection A of this section. ( Ord. 91-11 § 4 ; Ord. 95-23 § 3 ) 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 § 10.04.030
 
 
Citation and penalties. 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 

 Violation of this ordinance is a Class B violation under Chapter 1.11 . The Clatsop County Sheriff or any Oregon peace officer is authorized to cite violators by placing a citation directed to the vehicle’s owner upon the windshield of any vehicle found in violation of this chapter using the Uniform Traffic Citation and according to the terms and conditions cited therein. ( Ord. 91-11 ; Ord. 96-15 )
