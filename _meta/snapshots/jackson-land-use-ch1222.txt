1222.01 AUTHORITY TO ESTABLISH; INTENT; REVIEW AND
ADMINISTRATIVE AUTHORITY.

ORS 215.020 authorizes the establishment of planning commissions for counties. This
chapter exercises that authority and is intended to clarify the role of the Planning
Commission. In addition to the function prescribed by Section 1222.04, the Planning
Commission shall also have the powers and duties of a hearings officer, as prescribed by
ORS 215.010 to 215.100 and 215.402 to 215.422.

(Ord. 15-79/80. Passed 11-13-79; Ord. 87-1. Passed 3-18-87.)

1222.02 ESTABLISHMENT.

There is hereby established in and for the County a Planning Commission which shall
exercise such authority as is conferred by statute or ordinance.

(Ord. 195-77. Passed 6-22-77.)

1222.03 MEMBERSHIP.

(a) The Planning Commission shall consist of five members appointed by the Board of
Commissioners for four-year terms, provided that the appointments may be staggered for
one, two, three or four-year terms.

(b) Any vacancy on the Planning Commission shall be filled by the Board of
Commissioners for the unexpired term.

(c) Members of the Planning Commission shall be residents of various geographic areas
of the County, provided that the majority shall be from unincorporated areas. No more
than two voting members shall be engaged principally in the buying, selling or
developing of real estate for profit, either as an individual, as a member of any
partnership, or as an officer or employee of any corporation that is engaged principally i n
the buying or developing of real estate for profit. No more than two voting members shall
be engaged in the same kind of occupation, business, trade or profession.

(d) There shall be a six- month probationary period for all new members. Prior to
completion of this six-month period, a Commission member may be removed by majority
vote of the Board without cause. A Planning Commission member may be removed by
the governing body for misconduct or nonperformance of duty.

(e) Members of the Planning Commission shall s erve without compensation, other than
reimbursement for duly authorized expenses.

(f) The Board of Commissioners may designate one or more officers of the County to
be nonvoting members of the Commission.

(g) As to quorum and voting requirements, the Planning Co mmission shall be governed
by Section 250.02 of the Codified Ordinances.

1222.04 FUNCTIONS.

Functions of the Planning Commission are to:

(a) Assist the Board of County Commissioners in formulating policies and
implementing established policies;

(b) Assist the Board by relieving it of much of the time consuming effort of
investigating planning issues, identifying optional solutions and obtaining substantial,
effective public support and input for proposed plans;

(c) Balance planning goals with public wishes and reconcile the resulting
recommendations with the requirements of law prior to forwarding them to the Board for
consideration and adoption; and

(d) Provide insight on planning issues from the position of an informed citizen who, by
profession, experience or interest, can perceive issues and solutions in a manner which
may not be readily recognized by professional planners or elected officials.

(Ord. 15-79/80. Passed 11-13-79.)

1222.05 CONFLICTS OF INTEREST.

No member of the Planning Commission shall participate in any commission proceeding
or action in which any of the following has a direct or substantial financial interest: The
member or his or her spouse, brother, sister, child, parent, father-in-law, mother -in-law or
partner, or a business in which he or she is then serving or has served within the previous
two years, or any busi ness with which he or she is negotiating for or has an arrangement
or understanding with concerning prospective partnership or employment. Any actual or
potential conflict of interest shall be disclosed at the meeting of the Commission where
the action is being taken.

(Ord. 15-79/80. Passed 11-13-79.)

1222.06 AUTHORITY AND RESPONSIBILITIES.

(a) The Planning Commission is hereby delegated the authority to review and
recommend concerning the following matters in an advisory capacity to the Board of
Commissioners:

(1) Legislative or quasi-judicial amendments to the County Comprehensive Plan,
the Land Development Ordinance or other ordinances related directly to the
development and use of land;
1222.06 Planning Commission 6

(2) Projects and plans of special districts to determine consistency with the County
Comprehensive Plan;

(3) Comprehensive plans of incorporated communities to determine consistency
with the County Comprehensive Plan;

(4) Citizen involvement activities;

(5) An annual work program for the Planning Commission; and

(6) Any other tasks which the Board of Commissioners may assign to the Planning
Commission, or other tasks the Planning Commission elects to initiate with
Board concurrence.

(b) In the exercise of the above authority, the Planning Commission shall have the
following responsibilities:

(1) To adopt bylaws governing the election and duties of officers, establishment of
subcommittees, setting of meetings, order concerning the agenda, rules on
attendance and other matters which the Commission may find necessary to
effectively perform its delegated authority and responsibility.

(2) To become informed and have an understanding of:

A. Its authority by law;

B. Basic land use planning practices;

C. The documents upon which land use decisions are based, such as the
Comprehensive Plan, Land Development Ordinance and other
informational and advisory materials provided by the Development
Services Department; and

D. The structure, activity and function of the citizen advisory committees.

(3) To make recommendations to the Board of Commissioners based upon the
following:

A. Direction and limitation provided by local and State law;

B. Consideration of sound planning practice;

C. Consideration of public opinion;

D. Thorough evaluation of the issues and alternative solutions.

(4) To advise the Board periodically concerning the status of tasks under
consideration, their projected dates for completion and recommendations for
change in program direction.

make similar use determinations and approve major partitions and subdivisions under the
Land Development Ordinance; to make recommendations to the Board of Commissioners

(1) Joint meetings between the Board of Commissioners and Planning Commission

1222.08 STUDY AND RECOMMENDATION PROCESS.

(a) The standard process for studying an issue and developing recommendations of the
Planning Commission shall be as follows:

(1) A work plan set to deal with the issue;

(2) Staff investigation of the issue and preparation of a report and
recommendations;

(3) Planning Commission review of the staff report, with preliminary
modifications as required;

(4) Solicitation of the Citizen Advisory Committee's advice;

(5) Planning Commission review and response to Citizen Advisory Committee's
comments, with proposal modifications as necessary;

(6) Public hearing set;

(7) Deliberations conducted following hearing testimony; and

(8) Development of findings and recommendations for the Board of
Commissioners.

(b) Variations to the process identified in subsection (a) hereof may occur when:

(1) Substantial agreement cannot be obtained concerning an issue;

(2) A study is terminated by the Planning Commission without reaching a
conclusion and recommendations;

(3) More extensive public comment is required; and

(4) A special procedure is developed by the Board of Commissioners for specific
issues.

1222.11 PURPOSE AND DEFINITIONS.

(a) Purpose.  The purpose of the County in adopting Sections 1222.11 to 1222.14 is to
shape its local land -use planning organization to more expediently meet those urban
planning obligations which it recognizes in White City.

(b) Definitions.  In Sections 1222.11 to 1222.14, the terms below listed shall be given
the following meanings:

(1) White City  - means all that area lying within the White City Urban
Containment Boundary, together with any area contiguous to it and hereafter
included in an Urban Unincorporated Community planned and acknowledged
as such pursuant to OAR Chapter 660, Division 22.

1222.12 ESTABLISHMENT AND MEMBERSHIP OF WHITE CITY
PLANNING COMMISSION.

(a) Establishment. In addition to the planning commission established on June 22, 1977,
there is established a second County planning commission, known as the White City
Planning Commission, to perform, exclusively within White City, those functions
provided in Section 1222.13.

(b) Membership. The White City Planning Commission shall consist of five members
appointed by the Board of Commissioners for four- year terms, provided that the initial
appointments may be staggered for one, two, three or four- year terms. All members of
the commission must be residents of Jackson County. Three members must be residents
of various geographic areas within White City. In all other respects, the provisions of
Section 1222.03 shall apply.

(Ord. 96-35. Passed 8-7-96; Ord. 2001-33. Passed 12-19-01.)

so that White City may be acknowledged as an Urban  Unincorporated Community,
within the meaning of OAR 660, Division 22, by January 1, 1998, the time limit
expressed in OAR 660-22-070.
