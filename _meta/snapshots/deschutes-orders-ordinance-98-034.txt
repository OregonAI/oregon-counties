EXHIBIT " A"
11. 08. 135. Alternative initiation of delayed
exchange.
The board may initiate a delayed exchange
prior to enteringinto anexchangeagreementby
adopting a resolution declaring its intention to
make an exchange for cash and declaring the
project to be acquired with the cash. The cash
received for a delayed exchange shall be not less
than the value of the land. The procedures set
forth in this chapter shall be applicable to a
delayed exchange initiated pursuant to this
section. ( Ord. 98- 034 § 1, 1998)
