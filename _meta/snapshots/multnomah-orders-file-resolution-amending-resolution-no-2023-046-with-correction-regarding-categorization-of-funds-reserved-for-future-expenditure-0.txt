BEFORE THE MULTNOMAH COUNTY LIBRARY DISTRICT BOARD
RESOLUTION NO.  2023-071
Amending Resolution No. 2023-046, with correction regarding categorization of funds reserved
for future expenditure.
The Multnomah County Library District (MCLD) Board Finds:
a. The Multnomah County Library District's FY 2023 -24 budget in the amount of
$199,345,489, as approved by the Budget Committee on April 27 and certified by the
Multnomah County Tax Supervising and Conservation Commission (TSCC) on May 31,
2023, was adopted by the MCLD Board on June 8, 2023 in Resolution No. 2023-046.
b. An administrative error was made in the categorization of funds ‘Reserved for Future
Expenditure’ in the findings of Resolution No. 2023-046. These funds should have been
categorized as unappropriated, rather than part of the total appropriation for MCLD in
FY 2024.
c. The TSCC has requested the MCLD Board amend Resolution No. 2023-046 to correct
the MCLD appropriation total for FY 2024.
d. The table below reflects the corrected categorization of these funds, with $4.6 million
Reserved for Future Expenditures reflected as part of the unappropriated fund budget.
GENERAL FUND (1520)
Library Services    $ 110,514,127
Cash Transfers $     2,311,202
Operating Contingency $     1,000,000
Total Appropriation $ 113,825,329
Reserved for Future Expenditure $     4,635,082
Unappropriated Balance  $   18,677,748
Total Fund Budget $ 137,138,159
CAPITAL FUND (2514)
Library Services  $   1,020,000
Operating Contingency $  61,187,330
Total Appropriation  $   62,207,330
Total Fund Budget            $   62,207,330
e. This categorization correction does not materially change any aspect of the FY 2024
budget for the MCLD.

The Multnomah County Library District Board Resolves:
1. Finding (e) in Resolution No. 2023-046 is amended to reflect corrected total
appropriation amounts for the MCLD General Fund, as shown in the table above  in
Finding (d).
2. All other aspects of Resolution No. 2023-046 and the MCLD FY 2024 Adopted Budget
remain in effect as adopted on June 8, 2023.
