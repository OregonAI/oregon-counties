      THE        BOARD        OF    COMMISSIONERS           OF    YAMHILL           COUNTY,        OREGON            (the    Board),
on the 4th day of January,    1984,  sat for the transaction of                                                             County
business  in regular session,    Commissioners  Dave Bishop, Ted                                                            Lopus-
zynski and Robin J. Hamblet being present.

      WHEREAS,              Tax     Lot    4520-300,        a    40    acre        parcel        located        on     Peavine
Road,  approximately   6 1/2 miles                               southwest of McMinnville,   Oregon
currently has the boundary line                                  for "“EF-40" and "AF-20"  zones run-
ning through it;   and

   WHEREAS,  the entire 40 acre subject property                                                  is     Plan-designated
Agriculture/Forestry Large Holding;   and

      WHEREAS,              Karen     Swenson       has     applied           to    the    Board         for    readjustment
of the boundary  which,   pursuant                               to Section  3.01(I)  of the  Zoning
Ordinance,  No. 310,  1982,  would                               result  in the parcel  being  zoned
"AF-20"  in its entirety;   and

      WHEREAS,              the     subject       parcel        has    been        in    the     same     ownership           since
1972;        and
   WHEREAS,  notice of the requested zone change has been                                                            sent to
surrounding property owners and published  in a newspaper                                                            of general
circulation;  and

    WHEREAS, the proposed readjustment would not reduce in area the
predominent  zoning currently existing on the subject property,    in
that the "AF-20"   district now covers 55% of the subject property
(22     out       of     40    acres)       and    the     proposed          readjustment               will      result       in
the     remainder              of    the    40    acre     parcel       also        being        zoned     "AF-20";           and

    WHEREAS, the proposed boundary readjustment will follow the exis-
ting property line of the parcel;     NOW THEREFORE based upon and for
the reasons  set forth in the attached findings of fact and conclu-
sionary findings  for approval,  labeled Exhibit   "A" and incorporated
herein           by    this        reference,

      IT IS HEREBY  ORDAINED  BY THE BOARD   that   the    Official -Zoning Map
of    the Yamhill  County  Zoning Ordinance,    No.   310,   1982,  be and here-
by is amended  to designate  the zoning on Tax Lot 4520-300 as "AF-20"
in its entirety,  as set forth on the attached sketch map labeled
Exhibit "B" and incorporated herein by this reference.

      THIS        ORDINANCE           being       necessary           for     the       health,         safety        and    welfare

Findings      of     Fact

1.    The applicant has requested to change  the zoning on the sub-
      ject 40 acre parcel from EF-40/AF-20  mixture to entirely
      AF-20.

2.    The entire  40 acre subject property is                                     Plan        designated
      “Agriculture/Forestry Large Holding".

3.    Approximately            22     acresof        the    parcel         are     zoned        AF-20        and    the
      remaining        18     acres     are     zoned       EF-40.
4.    The proposed  zone realignment will                                 follow       the existing pro-
      perty lines and will result in the                                  parcel       being zoned AF-20
      in its entirety.

5.    The    parcel     has     been     in   the      same        ownership           since         1972.

6.    The    following         public     agency           comments         have       been      received:

      a)    County     Sanitation         ~   Comments             will     be     given        at    time     of
            partitioning.

7.    Notice of the requested                   zone change has been sent to surround-
      ing property owners and                   published  in a newspaper of general
      circulation.

Conclusionary          Findings
Approval

The   following        conclusionary             findings           can     be     made        for    approval        of
the   request:

1.    In    that the Comprehensive  Plan                         Map designation   for the parcel
      is    “Agriculture/Forestry  Large                         Holding"  and because this plan

      designation           applies      to     both       the     EF-40         and     AF-20       Districts
      the    proposed         zoning     boundary            readjustment                to    make     the    en-
      tire    parcel        zone      AF-20     is    consistent            with         the     Comprehen-
      sive    Plan     Map     designation            on     the    parcel.

2.    The proposed zoning boundary readjustment will not reduce
      in area the predominant    zoning currently existing on the
      subject property in that the AF-20 District now covers
      55% of the subject property      (22 out of 40 acres)    and the
      proposed readjustment   will   result   in the remainder    of the
      40 acre parcel also being     zoned AF-20.

 3.   In that the parcel will be rezoned    from its present EF-40/
      AF-20 mix to completely  AF-20,  the  boundary   readjustment
      will follow existing property   lines  and  will  result ina
      single  zoning district for the entire parcel.
