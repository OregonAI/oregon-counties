ORDINANCE NO. 89- 021
WHEREAS, there has been demonstrated needs to provide grant
funds for pre - project planning costs for development projects as
defined in Section 2. 16. 010, Deschutes County Code; and
WHEREAS, Chapter 2. 16, Economic Development, Deschutes
County Code, does not provide for grants to entities proposing
projects; now, therefore,
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY,
OREGON, ORDAINS as follows:
Section 1. Chapter 2. 16, Deschutes County code, is amended
by the addition of the following:
2. 16. 065 Development Projects - Grants.
A. The Board may approve a grant of funds upon such
conditions the Board deems appropriate for a develop-
ment project if the grant funds are to be used for pre -
project planning costs, the grant does not exceed one-
third of the pre - project planning costs and the Board
finds that grant participation in the development
project is in the public interest."
