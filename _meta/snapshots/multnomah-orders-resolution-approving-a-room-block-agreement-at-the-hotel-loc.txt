BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2022-097
Approving a Room-Block Agreement at the Hotel Located at 121 NE 181st Avenue, Gresham, Oregon
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The use of emergency shelter is one of many approaches employed by the Joint Office of
Homeless Services (JOHS) to address homelessness and related issues.
b. Entering an agreement to rent a block of rooms at the hotel located at 121 NE 181st Avenue
(“Room-Block Agreement”) will add 44 new emergency shelter units to the JOHS portfolio and
will support safety on the streets for individuals or families who have been referred to a
permanent supportive housing (PSH) program, but have not yet moved into a permanent
apartment. It prioritizes households based on factors such as behavioral health needs,
disabilities, and length of homelessness and provides a safe, low-barrier, housing first,
housing-focused, and supportive twenty-four (24) hour motel shelter room. This strategy has
proven highly effective in other communities to ensure that people who have been identified for
upcoming PSH apartments have the stability they need to be able to work effectively with their
housing case managers to prepare to enter a PSH unit. This program supports the rapid and
effective expansion of PSH through the utilization of Bridge Shelter that closes a critical gap in
the Coordinated Access and housing placement process and helps JOHS meet both its
housing placement and PSH goals as outlined in the Metro SHS Measure Local
Implementation Plan (LIP).
c. Approval of the Room-Block Agreement in substantial conformance with the proposed terms is
in the best interests of Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. Execution of the Room Block Agreement on terms substantially similar to those proposed is
approved.
2. Renewals, extensions and amendments may be executed without further Board action.
ADOPTED this 10th day of November, 2022.
