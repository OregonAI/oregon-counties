9.10.01 Public Purpose; Membership.
a) An intergovernmental entity to be known as the “Enterprise for Employment and Education” (hereinafter “the
Entity”) is hereby authorized to be created by intergovernmental agreement between Polk, Marion and
Yamhill Counties. The Chair of the Board is authorized to sign the intergovernmental agreement and other
documents necessary to create the Entity and accomplish the purposes of this ordinance.
b) Upon adoption of an authorizing order or resolution and execution of appropriate intergovernmental
agreements, the following units of local government from Oregon Work Force Region 3 in addition to
Yamhill, Marion and Polk counties may become members of the Entity: Chemeketa Community College,
Salem-Keizer School District 24-J, Willamette Regional Education Service District, and Yamhill Education
Service District.
c) The Entity is created to coordinate and oversee the. Oregon Work Force Region 3 public investment in work-
force related employment, education, and training services, and to enhance employment opportunities for the
citizens of Marion, Polk, and Yamhill Counties.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 621 eff 02/05/97]

9.10.02 Powers, Duties, and Functions.
a) Upon adoption of an implementing intergovernmental agreement between Yamhill, Polk and Marion counties,
the Entity shall assume all assets, functions, and authority previously vested in, owned by or assigned to the
Mid-Willamette Private Industry Council and the Mid-Willamette Work Force Quality Committee.
b) The entity shall carry out all responsibilities pursuant to the Job Training Partnership Act (29 U.S.C. 1501, et
seq.) and the Work Force Quality Act (Chapter 667 Oregon Laws 1991).
c) The entity shall have authority to enter agreements and contracts with other public and private entities, adopt
revenue and expenditure budgets, make, adopt, and amend by- laws, appoint members and committees, and
exercise other powers essential to fulfil ling the functions and duties of the entity as provided in ORS
190.030(1).
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 621 eff 02/05/97]
