BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2025-032
Requesting approval of a resolution to amend Transportation on-call contract task
order WSP 104 for the Broadway Deck Replacement project by an additional
$1,334,651.93.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. The Board, acting as the Multnomah County Public Contract Review Board
(PCRB) and pursuant to PCRB Rule 49 -0910(3), must approve contract
price increases beyond 33 percent of the original contract price for contracts
greater than $150,000.
B. The Broadway Deck Replacement Project (“Project”) has completed the
design phase and is now entering the construction phase, resulting in the
need to add construction engineering support services to the original
architectural and engineering (A&E) design task order; causing a necessary
task order increase greater than 33 percent of the original task order price.
C. This amendment (“Amendment”) will compensate the A&E consultant for
construction engineering support services required to support construction
of the Project.
D. The added work is within the general scope of the original contract, and this
Amendment is in accordance with the requirements of Public Contract
Review Board Rule 49-0910.
To amend and increase the total amount of task order WSP 104 associated with
contract number DCS -SVCSGEN-784-2018-conv with WSP USA, Inc. by an
additional $1,334,651.93 for a total task order value of $4,061,192.98 for the
Project.
