      THE   BOARD               OF COMMISSIONERS  OF YAMHILL                                      COUNTY,       OREGON  (the
Board),   on the                29th day of June,   1983, sat                                     for  the      transaction
of County business,  in special session,                                                   Commissioners                 Robin         J.
Hamblet and Dave Bishop being present.

      WHEREAS,  the Board on March 30,  1983, enacted Ordinance
333 entitled   "In the Matter of an Ordinance  to Amend the Yamhill
County Comprehensive Plan of 1974 for Property Known as the Stell-
flug Property";    and

            WHEREAS,           in    said       Ordinance               333,         the    subject           property           was        iden-
tified         as       Yamhill       County           Tax       Lots          3529-400           and     3532-400,             when        in
actuality the subject property                                      is         properly           identified             as     Tax     Lots
2529-400  and 2532-400; and

            WHEREAS,           the    above-described                          error       was     inadvertant                 and     con-
stituted a typographical  error                                     which            may    be     summarily             corrected               by
an amendment to Ordinance  333;                                     and

     WHEREAS,                  no rights  are prejudiced by the correction    of these
typographical                  errors without a formal public hearing;   NOW,   THERE-
FORE,

      IT           IS    HEREBY ORDAINED                    that        Ordinance                333    be    and    hereby            is
amended            as    follows:

            Section 1.  Lines  8                       and 9 of paragraph                          3 of Ordinance   333
            shall be amended to                        read "particularly                          described  as Yamhill
            County        Tax       Lot    2529-400               and    2532-400,                and”.

            Section  2.    Lines   3-6 of paragraph 6 of Ordinance    333 shall
            be amended to read "the 5 acre portion of Tax Lot 2532-400
            as shown on the sketch plan attached hereto as Exhibit           'B'
            and by this reference made a part hereof,        as  ‘Recreation
            Commercial’    and the 52 acre. portion of Tax Lots     2532-400     and
            2529-400    as 'Parks,    Recreation and Open Space'

            This Ordinance being necessary   for the health,  safety and wel-
fare        of the citizens of Yamhill County,   and an emergency having
been        declared,  shall be effective upon passage hereof.

            DONE        this    29th       day        of    June,        1983         at    McMinnville,                 Oregon.
