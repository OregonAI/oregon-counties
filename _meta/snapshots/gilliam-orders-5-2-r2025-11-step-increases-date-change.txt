IN THE COUNTY COURT FOR GILLIAM COUNTY, OREGON

RESOLUTION NO. R2025-11

A RESOLUTION ADOPTING EMPLOYEE PAY STEP INCREASE EFFECTIVE DATE

WHEREAS, Gilliam County currently provides eligible employees with a step increase
on July 1st of the fiscal year following the anniversary date that coincides with the
current step increase schedule; and

WHEREAS, the County Court would like to amend the effective date for step increases;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Gilliam County Court hereby resolves as follows:

1. Employees shall receive a step increase on the date the employee becomes
eligible. This date typically coincides with the employee’s original hire date with
Gilliam County.
2. Step increases will occur on the employment anniversary date of each eligible
year, as more thoroughly detailed in the employee’s relevant Salary Schedule
(Exhibit A). Employees are eligible for step increases until they have reached the
top step of their relevant Salary Schedule, unless work performance is evaluated
as unsatisfactory.
3. The AFSCME Local 3326 Union has approved this change by adopting a Letter
of Agreement effective July 1, 2025.
4. The effective date of this Resolution shall be July 1, 2025.

Adopted by the Gilliam County Court this _16th_ day of July 2025
