Rancho Rajneesh. On July 20, 1984, LCDC again reviewed the Comprehensive Plan for the

Seven Mile Hill area, and found the Plan and Map changes to be in compliance with

Statewide Land Use Planning Goals; and

       IT FURTHER APPEARING TO THE COURT:                   That on Monday, August 16, 1993,
