Administrative Policy 525

Animals are restricted in Marion County owned, operated or leased facilities.  The presence of
service animals or working animals in county premises whose use or uses are on-going or
frequent, require prior written approval from the Business Services Director as established in
Administrative Policy # 525.

Service animals are defined as dogs or miniature horses that are individually trained to do
work or perform tasks for people with disabilities.
