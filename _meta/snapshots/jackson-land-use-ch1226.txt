Comprehensive Plan

EDITOR'S NOTE:  The latest revision of the Jackson County Comprehensive Plan was
adopted by the Board of County Commissioners by Ordinance 82 -33, passed December
15, 1982.  The Plan is the official long -range general land u se policy document for the
County. It sets forth general land use planning policies and allocations of land uses and
serves as the basis for the coordinated development of physical resources and the
development or redevelopment of the County based on physi cal, social, economic and
environmental factors. Copies of this document are available for public inspection at the
office of the Department of Planning and Development.
