BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO.  2024-055
Approving an Increased Contract Price for the Gateway Residential and Service
Buildings Electrical Systems.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Multnomah County owns the Gateway Children Services and Residential buildings
located at 10305 E Burnside St., Portland, Oregon (“Building”).
b. In fiscal year 2022, a Capital project was submitted as part of the 5-year capital
improvement plan for electrical system replacement for both Gateway Children's
Services and Residential buildings. After the project had already been awarded to
a contractor, further investigation identified additional requirements to ensure
adequate and safe repair and renovation to the buildings.
c. Through ITB-69-2023 the contract for the Project was awarded to “PORTLAND
ELECTRICAL CONSTRUCTION, INC.,” (DCA-CNSTR-15135-2023) (“Contract”).
d. Additional costs in the amount of $196,590.51  were identified to complete the
project effectively due to updated clearance, access and serviceability code
requirements. These code requirements result in the need to repair or renovate
several building elements such as conduits underneath sidewalks and workspace
clearances to ensure we are providing the appropriate accessibility and
serviceability safety requirements.
e. The cost for the additional work requires Board approval pursuant to Public
Contract Review Board (PCRB) 49 -0910(3), due to the increased costs of
renovation or remodeling (such as the Project) exceeding 33%.
f. No additional funding from the Fiscal Budget 2024 is required.
g. Approval of the increased Contract price from $436,720 to $633,310.51
(“Increased Contract Price”) is in the best interests of Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
Pursuant to PCRB 49-0910(3), the Increased Contract Price is approved.
ADOPTED this 20th day of June, 2024.
