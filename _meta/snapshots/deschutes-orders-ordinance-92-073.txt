WHEREAS, Ordinance No. 92- 054 amended Deschutes County Code
Chapter 2. 12 defining usual business days and hours of operation of
Deschutes County; and
WHEREAS, it is necessary to establish hours of operation for
the Deschutes County Clerk, Treasurer, and Community Development
Department on December 24 and December 31, 1992; now, therefore,
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY, OREGON
ORDAINS as follows:
Section 1. That the hours of operation for Deschutes County
Clerk, Treasurer, and Community Development Department shall be
from 7: 30 a. m. to 3: 00 p. m. on December 24, 1992, and from 7: 30
a. m. to 5: 30 p. m. on December 31, 1992.
Section 2. The hours set forth in Section 1 of this ordinance
shall be modified as follows: ( A) Community Development Department
shall be closed from 12: 00 noon to 1: 00 p. m. on each day; ( B)
Treasurer shall not be open on December 24, 1992.
