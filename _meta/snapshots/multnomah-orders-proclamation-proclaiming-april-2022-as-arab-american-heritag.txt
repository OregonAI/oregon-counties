BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2022-024
Proclaiming April 2022 as Arab American Heritage Month in Multnomah County, Oregon.
T
he Multnomah County Board of Commissioners finds:
A. Multnomah County, Oregon is deeply enriched by the diversity of its residents and is
proud to be home to a thriving Arab American community.
B. F or over a century, Arab Americans have been making valuable contributions to virtually
every aspect of American society, including in science, medicine, law, business,
education, technology, government, and culture.
C. Victor George Atiyeh, who served as the 32nd Governor of Oregon from 1979 to 1987,
was the first Arab American elected as Governor of a US state.
D. Arab Americans, like all Americans, share the desire to be a part of a peaceful and
diverse society, in which every individual is treated equally and feels safe.
E. It is imperative to increase awareness about key issues and priorities within the Arab
American community and combat harmful stereotypes, prejudice and discrimination.
F. T he incredible contributions and heritage of Arab Americans have helped us build a city
and a better nation.
G. In July 2021 Governor Brown signed into law HB 2914, which declares April of each year
as Arab American Heritage Month. Oregon is the second state along with Illinois which
has permanently recognized Arab American Heritage Month. This year is a historic event
for Arab Americans in Oregon and nationwide.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners proclaims:
April 2022 as National Arab American Heritage Month in Multnomah County, and encourages all
residents to observe this month.
ADOPTED this 7th day of April, 2022
