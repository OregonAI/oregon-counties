2.10.01 Office of Accountant. The Board of County Commissioners of Yamhill County hereby creates the Office
of County Accountant. The provisions for the oath of the Accountant, for bonding and sureties of the Accountant
and subordinance thereof, and for the dutie s and powers of the Office of Accountant shall be as prescribed in
ORS 210.120 to 210.230, provided however, that the bond to be posted as provided in ORS 210.120 shall be in
the amount of $20,000.00.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 382 eff 07/01/84]

2.10.02 Appointment, Qualifications. The position of County Accountant shall be filled by the Board of
Commissioners of Yamhill County. The person appointed shall possess such qualifications as the Board in its
discretion shall deem appropriate.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 382 eff 07/01/84]

2.10.03 Term. The County Accountant shall not be appointed to a fixed term but shall serve at the pleasure of
the Board of County Commissioners.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 382 eff 07/01/84]
