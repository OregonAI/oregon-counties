BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2023-061
Declaring a Portion of the Juvenile Justice Complex Temporary Surplus; and Approving
a Lease Renewal.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Approximately 1,724 square feet of space (the “Premises”) within County owned
Juvenile Justice Complex ("JJC") located at 1401 NE 68th Avenue, Portland,
Oregon is not needed for any County use and is presently being used by CASA
for Children, Inc. an Orego n non -profit public benefit corporation ("CASA").
Pursuant to FAC-3, Sec. IV, it is appropriate to continue to declare the Premises
temporary surplus in order to continue to lease the space.
b. CASA’s original lease is set to expire on June 30, 2023, and a ll renewal options
have been exercised. There is mutual desire between the County’s Department of
Community Justice and CASA to continue to lease the Premises at JJC to CASA
for its youth program for an additional two year term, with a right to renew for up to
five additional two-year terms which could extend the lease until 2035.
c. The continued lease of the Premises will allow CASA to remain in close proximity
with State Court Offices and Multnomah County programs provided at the JJC.
The County’s Department of Community Justice believes that this is a productive
and appropriate co-tenancy and has program benefits.
d. It is in the best interests of the County to declare the Premises temporary surplus
and to execute a lease renewal substantially in confor mance with the terms set
forth in Exhibit 1 (the Lease Renewal) to this Resolution.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The Premises is declared temporary surplus for the term of a lease (as extended
by renewal or amendment) executed in substantial conformance with the terms set
forth in Exhibit 1 (Lease Renewal) to this Resolution.
2. The Chair is authorized to execute such lease and any ancillary instruments and
to execute renewals or amendments of the same without further Board action.
ADOPTED this 22nd day of June, 2023.
