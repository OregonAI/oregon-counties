BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-069
Approving a change to ambulance staffing in accordance with the Settlement
Agreement between Multnomah County and American Medical Response Northwest,
Inc. (AMR).
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Ambulance staffing is a state-required, material component of county ambulance
standards. Multnomah County’s standards are included in Ordinance 1238,
County Code 21.400 et seq., and in contracts for ambulance services.
B. Per Ordinance 1238, Multnomah County Code 21.417, and the contract for
ambulance services, the Multnomah County Emergency Medical Services
Medical Director (EMSMD) serves as the physician supervisor for all Emergency
Medical Services (EMS) providers in the employ of licensed ambulance services
within the county and working within the county.
C. The EMSMD is responsible for approving the standards for training and
continuing education for EMS providers. This includes issuing, limiting, and
setting standing orders and authorization of 911 ambulance lead Paramedics,
and 911 ambulance non-lead Paramedics.
D. The current minimum emergency ambulance staffing standard is two Oregon
State Licensed Paramedics, one an EMSMD certified and approved lead
Paramedic, and one EMSMD County certified and approved non-lead
Paramedic.
E. The EMSMD may not implement protocols or take actions that would change the
patient care standards specifically identified in the ASP or in County Code
Chapter 21.400 et seq without approval of the Board of Commissioners (§ 21.417
(F)).
F. The EMSMD is responsible for recommending any alteration of ambulance
staffing standards when such a change is desirable or necessary. The criteria
and process for an EMSMD recommended staffing change is referenced in
Multnomah County’s Ambulance Service Plan (ASP).
G. Through a mediated dispute resolution process with AMR, the EMSMD has
consulted an expert panel of operational and clinical experts to evaluate a
staffing change proposal. This group and the EMSMD now recommend a
temporary change to ambulance staffing standards as detailed in the Settlement
Agreement between AMR and Multnomah County.
H. The EMSMD continues to require one State Licensed and County EMSMD
approved lead Paramedic on all ALS and Hybrid ALS ambulances.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
To approve the ambulance staffing changes detailed in the Settlement Agreement, for
the duration of the Settlement Agreement, and suspend those portions of the
Ambulance Service Plan, Ordinance 1238, and all applicable provisions of the
Multnomah County Code, including MCC § 21.406, that conflict with the Settlement
Agreement.
ADOPTED this 15th day of August, 2024.
