BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2021-034
Consenting to Chair Kafoury’s Appointment of Serena Cruz as the Director of the Department of
County Management for Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3) provides that the Chair’s appointment of
department heads (Directors) is subject to consent of a majority of the Board.
b. The Chair appoints Serena Cruz as the Director of the Department of County
Management beginning July 1, 2021.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
In accordance with Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3), the Board consents to the
appointment of Serena Cruz as the Director of the Department of County Management.
ADOPTED this 20th day of May, 2021.
