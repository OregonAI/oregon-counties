8.05.01  Authorization of Social Games

LEGISLATIVE HISTORY

8.05.01 Social Games. Social games as defined in ORS 167.117 are hereby permitted in Yamhill County i n
private businesses. private clubs, or places of public accommodation where no house player, house bank or house
odds exist, and there is no house income from the operation of the social game.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 60 eff 06/26/74]
