BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2020-096
Recognizing the Observance of Veterans Day on November 11, 2020, in Multnomah
County, Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. In 1954, Congress formally created Veterans Day to pay tribute to all American
Veterans and current service members, renaming Armistice Day which had been
celebrated as a legal federal holiday since 1938.
B. Many people throughout our country’s history have s erved as members of the
Armed Forces, Active, Reserve and National Guard , including women, people of
color, immigrants, people who identify with all sexual orientations and gender
identities, people with disabilities, and other brave individuals. The diver sity of
veterans is a tremendous strength that makes our Armed Forces stronger and
more vibrant.
C. Veterans Day is a time for Americans to honor every service member who has
ever worn one of our Nation’s uniforms. Throughout our history, Americans have
answered the call to duty and served our country with honor and dignity.
D. In Multnomah County we continue to work toward greater inclusivity and equity in
how we support people who have served our country. We are seeking to better
understand the needs of thes e individuals and communities so that we can
address the full intersectionality of their lives and meet their needs as they
transition back to civilian life.
E. Multnomah County is home to an estimated 42,635 veterans and current service
members. Multnomah C ounty is committed to providing the respect and support
that all veterans have earned, including improved access to employment, benefits,
housing, and health care.
F. At Multnomah County, the Multnomah County Veterans Service Office works
directly with veterans and their families in an effort to ensure that they receive all
State and Federal benefits available to them. The Multnomah County Veteran
Employee Resource Group provides support and identifies resources for County
employees who are veterans or are married or related to a current or former service
member. Multiple other County departments serve veterans through their
programs.
G. The Multnomah County Veterans Task Force was convened in 2010 by
Commissioner Diane McKeel to assess and identify the services provided to
military service members and  veterans in Multnomah County. Today, the Task
Force provides a collaborative forum for education and advocacy, with the goal of
including service members, veterans and their families in decisions about policy
and resource allocation.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
In observance of Veterans Day on November 11, 2020, o n this day all veterans and
current members of the Armed Forces, Active, Reserve and National Guard will be
remembered and honored.
ADOPTED this 5th day of November, 2020.
