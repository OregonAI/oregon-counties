WHEREAS, the general fund budget levy measure for the 1983- 84
fiscal year was defeated; and
WHEREAS, it is necessary to cut back operations in programs
funded by general fund resources in the remainder of the 1982- 83
fiscal year and the 1983- 84 fiscal year because of the anticipated
revenue shortfall; and
WHEREAS, in order to balance the 1983- 84 fiscal budget, hours
of operation must immediately be reduced; and
WHEREAS, ORS 203. 035 allows the County to exercise authority
within the County over matters of County concern, to the fullest
extent allowed by Constitutions and laws of the United States and
of this State; now, therefore,
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY,
OREGON, ORDAINS as follows:
Section 1. That the regular business days for Deschutes
County shall be Monday through Thursday of each week, except legal
holidays and as provided in Section 2 of this Ordinance.
Section 2. That the Board of County Commissioners may
establish days of operation for any Deschutes County Department
different from the regular business days described in Section 1 of
this Ordinance.
Section 3. That signs shall be posted at or near all door-
ways to Deschutes County buildings, setting forth the regular
business days of Deschutes County.
Section 4. This Ordinance being necessary for the immediate
preservation of public peace, health and safety, an emergency is
declared to exist, and this Ordinance takes effect on June 13,
