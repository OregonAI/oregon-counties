EXHIBIT A (to Ordinance 2022-012)

2.04.052        Mode of Selection: Commissioner.

As approved by the electors at the November 2022 General Election, the mode of selection for
Commissioner positions shall be non-partisan. Political parties will not nominate candidates for
Commissioner.   Party affiliations of candidates for Commissioner will not be listed on the ballot.   No
notation of party membership or non-membership will be included in county records.

MEETING    DATE:         December   14, 2022

SUBJECT:   Consideration of Ordinance No. 2022-012, Enacting Section 2.04.052 of the
           Deschutes County Code (DCC) to change the mode of selection of Commissioner
           positions from partisan to non-partisan

RECOMMENDED MOTION:
Move approval of Ordinance No. 2022-012, Enacting Section 2.04.052 of the Deschutes
County Code.

BACKGROUND         AND   POLICY IMPLICATIONS:
In November 2022, Ballot Measure No. 9-148 was passed by the electors of Deschutes
County, mandating that the mode of selection for Commissioner positions become non-
partisan. DCC section 2.04.052 will codify that mode of selection.

Pursuant to State law, this ordinance will take effect immediately upon passage.
