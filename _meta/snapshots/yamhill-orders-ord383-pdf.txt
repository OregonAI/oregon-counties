 Findings      of    Fact       >
 1.   The     applicant             has    requested                to       change        the        zoning       on    the
      subject        property             from      an        EF-40/AF-20                 mixture           to    entirely
      AF-20.

j2.   The subject property is 148 acres and includes  3 tax lots
      under’ 2 ownerships. Approximately  84 acres of the subject
      property are zoned EF-40 and the remaining 64 acres are
      zoned     AF-20.

 3.   The EF-40/AF-20   District boundary on the property generally
      follows vegetation lines,   with the EF-40 zoned area being
      relatively  cleared and the AF-20 zoned area wooded.   The
      zoning pattern on the surrounding area is consistent with
      the EF-40/AF-20  zoning pattern which exists throughout the
       County.    In general AF-20                             zoned          lands        are wooded, or other-
      wise   uncleared and steep;                              EF-40          zoned        lands are predominantly
       cleared and cultivated.

 4.   There     is    currently              a dwelling,                     a barn        and        shop       located        on     the
      subject property.    The applicant has indicated that approx-
      imately 23 acres of the property have been farmed in the past;
      however,  because of steep slopes,   rocky soil and wet areas,
      crop production   has not been profitable or continuous.    ~

 5.   During        1979       and    1980       approximately                       20,000           to     25,000      fir     trees
      were planted              on the        subject property, thereby significantly
      reducing  the             number        of acres of the property that are now
      cleared.            Additionally,                  through              natural            seeding,          fir    trees
      have     begun       to       grow     over        some        of       the     remaining               cleared          area.

 6.   The     soils       on    the        subject            property              are    predominantly                 Peavine
      silty clay loam with slopes of between 2 to 30 percent.                                                                        These
      soils are generally agricultural Class IV and VI and all                                                                       soils
      on the property  are woodland site class 2 for Douglasfir                                                                      pro-
      auction.                                                                                                                             ‘

 7.    Surrounding parcels range                               in size              from 2 acres to over 100
       acres.   Parcels generally                              conform              to the minimum parcel size
      standards  of the AF-20 and EF-40 Districts.   Land uses                                                                  on
      nearby properties  include timber production,  livestock                                                                  grazing,
      pasture and hay production,  and grain production.

 8.   A 62     acre parcel                 abutting the subject property on the south-
      east     was rezoned                 from EF-40/AF-20  to entirely AF-20 in 1983
       (Z-246-83).

     The   following       conclusionary           findings             can        be. made      for    approval         of
     the   request:

     Le    The   proposed  zoning boundary realignment is consistent with
           the   Comprehensive Plan in that the Comprehensive Plan Map
           designation          of    "AFLH"     applies          to    both        the     EF-40      and     AF-20
           Districts.           Further,        both    the       EF-40        and      AF-20     Districts            are
           considered          agriculturally           protective                 and,     therefore,          the
           proposed       realignment           would       not    be     inconsistent                with     County
           agricultural             policies.
           There    is    an    existing,        demonstrable              need           for   the    proposed
           zone realignment considering that the EF-40 District is                                                     in-
           tended to preserve and maintain the production of crops
           while    the    AF-20       District        is     intended             to     preserve      and     main-
           tain the production of crops and forest products;  and further
           considering that due to the planting of 20,000 to 25,000
           Douglas  fir trees and the natural seeding of the property to
           fir trees,  the subject property more nearly meets the intent
           and purpose of the AF-20 District rather than the EF-40 Dis-
           trict.

           The   proposed           zone   realignment            is    timely            considering          the     fol-
           lowing changes that have occurred on the subject property and
           the surrounding area since the Official Zoning Map was adopted
           in 1976:                 :

           a)    20,090 to 25,000 Douglas fir trees have been planted on.
                 the subject property, thereby significantly reducing the
                 number of acres of the property that are now cleared or
                 are suitable for preservation for crop production.  Addi-
                 tionally,through natural    seeding   fir trees are encroach-
                 ing into part of the remaining cleared area and, there-
                 fore,   the amount of land that is suitable    for crop pro-
                 duction    is being further reduced and replaced with lands»
                 under timber production.

     Recommendation             -

     Based on findings of                  fact and         conclusionary   findings                     for    approval,
     the staff recommends                  approval         of the request.
