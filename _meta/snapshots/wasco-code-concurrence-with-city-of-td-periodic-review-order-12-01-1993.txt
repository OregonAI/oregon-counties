       NOW    ON   THIS DAY,     the above-entitled matter having come on regularly for

consideration, said day being one duly set in term for the transaction of public business and

a majority of the Court being present; and

       IT APPEARING TO THE COURT:             That on June 26, 1983, Wasco County adopted

by ordinance the City of The Dalles Comprehensive Plan and implementing ordinances for

land lying within the urban growth area, and has previously entered into an (amended)

urban growth area Joint Management Agreement with the City, which requires that plans

and implementing ordinances be coordinated within the urban growth area (outside city

limits and inside the urban growth boundary); and

       IT FURTHER APPEARING TO THE COURT:                    That on April 22, 1993, the City

of The Dalles Planning Commission reviewed and recommended the City Council approve

the Final Periodic Review     Order.   The   City Council of the City of The     Dalles then

considered the "Final Periodic Review Order" amendments and approved the final Order

on May 3, 1993, and thereafter forwarded that decision to Wasco County; and

       IT FURTHER          APPEARING     TO THE COURT:       That on December 1, 1993, at

11:15 a.m., in the County Courtroom of the Wasco County Courthouse, The Dalles, Oregon,

Court were determined to be qualified to hear the matter;

          IT FURTHER APPEARING TO THE COURT:               That the Court took notice of the

record, heard the Staff report and then requested testimony and evidence from interested

parties, only the proponent spoke.      The Court then announced it would deliberate, and

based upon the evidence and findings of fact, the County Court, being fully apprised in the

premises;

          NOW THEREFORE THE WASCO COUNTY COURT DOES HEREBY ORDAIN

AS FOLLOWS:         By a 3-0 vote of the County Court of the County of Wasco, State of

Oregon,     the proposed   City of The Dalles Final Local Periodic Review     Order, herein

attached hereto and by this reference incorporated is adopted and found to be consistent

with the Joint Management Agreement previously entered into between the City of The

Dalles and Wasco County.

       EMERGENCY           CLAUSE:     Insomuch as these amendments are necessary for the

immediate preservation of health, safety and welfare, an emergency is hereby declared to

exist and the amendments shall be in full force and effect immediately upon the adoption

by the County Court.
