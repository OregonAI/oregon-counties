B.   Ordinance         Provisions               and       Analysis

1.    The    decision            is     based        upon     consideration                of            the    zone       change
      criteria         set       forth         in    Section        1208.02          of    the            Yamhill          County
      Zoning        Ordinance                (YCZO).         The    specific             review            criteria             are
      as    follows:

      (a)      That        the    proposed             change       is    consistent                     with     the      goals,
               policies           and        any     other     applicable                provisions                  of    the
               Comprehensive                   Plan.

      (b)      That        there        is     an    existing,           demonstrable                     need       for    the
               particular uses allowed by the requested zone,
               considering the importance of such uses to the
               citizenry o r the economy of the area, the existing
               market demand which such uses will satisfy,  and the
               availability and location of other lands so zoned and

             their       suitability              of    the    uses         allowed    by       the    zone.

     (c)     That the proposed change is appropriate considering                                                     the

     The    criteria       are        addressed          in    Findings         2   through           5,    below.

2.   Regarding       criterion              (a)    above,       the         property       is    Plan
     designated Agricultural/Forestry Large Holding  (AFLH).  This
     designation was established to preserve farmland for farm
     use,    so   all     agricultural                 goals     and        policies      would        apply.
     Section      II,     Goal        1,    directs       the        county:

             To   conserve            Yamhill          County’s        farm     lands       for       the
             production of crops                       and livestock and to ensure
             that the conversion                       of farm land to urban use
             where       necessary           and       appropriate            occurs       in    an
             orderly       and        economical          manner.

     Following          this     Goal       is    Policy       As

             Yamhill       County           will       provide        for     the   preservation
             of farm lands through appropriate zoning,
             recognizing comparative economic returns to
             agriculture and alternative uses, changing
             ownership patterns and management practices,
             changing          market        conditions              for     agricultural
             produce,           and    various          public        financial           incentives.

     These goals    and policies encourage   farm uses in the farm
     area.   As indicated in Finding A.3,     the property presently
     is in orchard use.      The applicant has stated the use would
     continue,   and that the orchard would in fact be better
     managed      with         a resident          on    the        site.      This    may       very       well     be
      the case,  although that factor is more relevant                                            at the        time
      a dwelling   is requested. The AF-20 designation                                            would,
      however,  also protect the farm use of the land,                                            so the

     request        appears        to    comply        with        the     Comprehensive           Plan.

     The    purpose         statement           of    the    Agricultural/Forestry                   zoning
     district        (YCZO        Section        403.01)           describes        the     land    in     this
     zone as being   "...primarily foothill and ridgetop holdings
     above the flat terrace and valley floor commercial
     agricultural  areas,   and below the contiguous timberlands  of
     the Coast Range."    As indicated in the Background Facts,   the
     property slopes   in areas over 30 percent and the elevation
     is    between         200    and     450    feet       above        mean     sea    level.      Both       the
     elevation and               slope are indicative of the foothills,                                    which
     is consistent               with the intent of the AF-20 zone.

     Regarding        criterion            (b)       and    (d)     above,        the    language        of    the
     requirements   indicates  they primarily apply to zone change
     requests  that would result in a substantially different type
     of permitted use,    such as from residential  to commercial.
     In this case,   the request   is from one farm zone to another,
     so    the     types     of    uses       allowed        are     not        significantly        differ-
     ent.   The only difference would be whether the parcel would
     be eligible   for a dwelling in conjunction with the existing
     farm use,   so the need issue is not really relevant.

     The high per-acre value of the existing type of crop and
     intensity of use,   coupled with the unique characteristics   of
     the parcel,  make it suitable   for the AF-20  zoning
     designation.    That is,  a 20 acre parcel with this crop can
     be considered a commercial agricultural operation.

     Regarding criterion  (c) above, as noted in                                          Finding A.4,
     surrounding lands are in a mixture of farm                                           and forest uses,
     with a few scattered rural residential    lots.   The subject
     parcel has an existing orchard use, which is in character
     with other farm uses in the area.     The applicant has stated
     that   she would   likely apply to construct one dwelling on
     the parcel.    One additional dwelling would not create a
     significant burden on the existing services     in the area.

     Regarding criterion                   (e) above the                  request does not need an
     exception, and none                   is proposed.                   Therefore criterion (e)
     does     not    apply        to    this     request.

     the     Comprehensive              Plan.
