WHEREAS, 27th Street is now the official name for what was
formerly Denser Road within the Bend Urban Area.
WHEREAS, Section 19. 84. 010 of the Deschutes County Code
concerning setbacks from certain arterials within the Bend Urban Area
refers to certain arterials by street names that have subsequently
been officially changed;
WHEREAS, it is desirable that the references to specific
arterials in Section 19. 84. 010 reflect their current officially
designated street names;
WHEREAS, the Bend Urban Area Planning Commission, after notice
given and hearing conducted in accordance with applicable law, has
recommended approval of proposed text amendments to Section 19. 84. 010,
as set forth herein, that would make the desired changes; now,
therefore,
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY, OREGON,
ORDAINS AS FOLLOWS:

County Code is amended as follows:
