BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2022-028
Proclaiming April 17 through April 23, 2022 as Volunteer Week in Multnomah County, Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. This year, National Volunteer Week takes place from April 17th to April 23rd and provides an
opportunity to recognize the invaluable contributions of volunteers to supporting safe, healthy and
equitable communities.
b. For Multnomah County’s 2022 Volunteer Awa rds, departments and offices have nominated 43
volunteers to be recognized for their extraordinary service to Multnomah County and our community .
These volunteers have made significant contributions to Multnomah County’s work in the Department of
Community Services’ Animal Services Division, the Department of Community Justice, the Departments
of County Assets & Management, Multnomah County Library, the Office of Community Involvement, and
the Multnomah County Charter Review Committee.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
The week of April 17 through April 23, 2022 as Volunteer Week in Multnomah County, Oregon. We take
great pleasure in honoring all volunteers with our sincere gratitude and appreciation for their selfless
efforts.
ADOPTED this 21st day of April, 2022.
