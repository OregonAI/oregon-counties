The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Acting as the Governing Body for Mid -
Multnomah County Street Lighting Service District No. 14 Finds:
a. The Mid-Multnomah County Street Lighting Service District No. 14 (“District”) was formed
as a special district under Oregon Revised Statute (“ORS”) Chapter 451 through a special
election in 1967.  The District was originally named the Tulip  Acres Street Lighting Service
District, and the Board of County Commissioners (“Board”), which serves as the governing body
for the District, renamed the District to its current name by Order in 1970.
b. The District was formed for the purpose of providing street lighting facilities for the District,
which covers unincorporated Multnomah County and the cities of Troutdale, Fairview, and
Maywood Park.
c. As a condition of development approval or as otherwis e required by the code of a local
jurisdiction, developers often are required to construct, install, or alter street lighting facilities
within the District, which are or become District assets.
d. To ensure best practices, it is in the best interest of the District to adopt standards for new
street lighting facilities installed within the District, as well as standards for alterations made to
existing street lighting facilities within the District.  Those standards should be implemented
through a permitting process administered by a District manager or representative.
e. Utilities and other entities often want to attach equipment to existing or new District street
light poles or other District facilities.  To ensure best practices, it is in the best interest of  the
District to adopt standards for attachments to District street light poles and other facilities.  Those
standards should be implemented through a permitting process admini stered by a District
manager or representative.
f. Given the evolving nature of te chnology and electrical standards, the District requires
flexibility to amend its processes and standards to stay current with generally accepted best
practices.
g. Delegating authority to a District manager or representative to adopt administrative rules
to implement standards for District facilities and attachments to those facilities, as well as to
acquire street light facilities, will allow the District to more efficiently carry out its duties.

h. Under ORS 451.550(1), the District may supervise, manage, control, operate and
maintain the District’s street lighting facilities.  Under ORS 451.600, no person or entity may
alter, repair or make a connection with any service facility maintained by the District, unless that
person or entity obtains permi ssion from the District and pays all deposits, charges or fees
required by the District for granting such permission before undertaking the work.
i. Under ORS 451.570, the District may adopt and enforce reasonable and necessary
regulations for the protection  of public health, safety, and general welfare and for all purposes
consistent with the District’s authorization to construct, operate and maintain street lighting
facilities.
j. Under ORS 451.550(3), the District may acquire such real and personal property  and
rights of way, either within or without the county, as in the judgment of the governing body of the
District are necessary or proper in the exercise of the powers of the District, and to pay for and
hold the same.   Under ORS 451.550(4), the District m ay make and accept contracts, deeds,
releases and documents , which, in the judgment of the governing body of the District, are
necessary or proper in the exercise of the powers of the District.
k. Under ORS 198.530, because the Multnomah County Home Rule Cha rter provides a
process for enacting, amending, and repealing ordinances, the District has followed that process
to adopt this Ordinance and set the manner of adopting, amending, and repealing associated
regulations.
The Mid-Multnomah County Street Lighting Service District No. 14 Ordains as Follows:
Section 1. A permit is required for the following new development or redevelopment in the
public right-of-way within the Mid-Multnomah County Street Lighting Service District No. 14
service area:
A. Any new construction or installation of poles, luminaires, circuits, junction boxes,
service cabinets, foundations, or other related facilities that are or will become a
facility or property owned by the Mid-Multnomah County Street Lighting Service
District No. 14;
B. New development or redevelopment that interferes with or otherwise impacts
existing facilities owned by the Mid-Multnomah County Street Lighting Service
District No. 14 by removing or altering the location, function, or operation of those
facilities;
C. Installation of attachments on poles owned by the Mid-Multnomah County Street
Lighting Service District No. 14; and
D. Installation of small wireless facilities on facilities owned by the Mid-Multnomah
County Street Lighting Service District No. 14.

Section 2.  A manager or representative of the Mid-Multnomah County Street Lighting Service
District No. 14 may adopt, amend, and repeal administrative rules to establish standards and a
permitting and enforcement process for development and redevelopment of s treet lighting
facilities within the Mid-Multnomah County Street Lighting Service District No. 14 service area.
Section 3. A manager or representative of the Mid-Multnomah County Street Lighting Service
District No. 14 may acquire and accept ownership of street lighting facilities by any means
allowed under ORS 451.550(3), as in the judgment of the manager or representative are
necessary or proper in the exercise of the powers of the Mid-Multnomah County Street Lighting
Service District No. 14, and may pay for and hold the same.
Section 4. The District may charge fees for District services provided as part of the permitting
process.  The Board will set the fee amounts by resolution.
