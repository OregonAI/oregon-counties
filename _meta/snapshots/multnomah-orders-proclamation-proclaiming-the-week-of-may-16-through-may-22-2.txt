BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2021-033
Declaring the Week of May 16 through May 22, 2021 as “National Public Works Week” and
recognizing the contributions of all Multnomah County Public Works Employees.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Public works infrastructure, facilities, and services are of vital importance to the health,
safety and well-being of the citizens of Multnomah County;
b. The public works infrastructure, facilities, and services could not be provided without
the dedicated efforts of public works professionals, including engineers, surveyors,
technicians, planners, and operations and maintenance staff and administrators;
c. County public works professionals design, build, operate, and maintain the
transportation system, stormwater infrastructure, sewage, fleet, public buildings and
facilities that are vital to the people and communities of Multnomah County; and
d. Understanding the role that public infrastructure plays in protecting the environment,
improving public health and safety, contributing to the economic vitality, and enhancing
the quality of life of the community is in the interest of the citizens of Multnomah
County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
The week of May 16 through 22, 2021 as "NATIONAL PUBLIC WORKS WEEK," and calls
upon the citizens of this community to recognize the contributions that all public works
employees make every day to our health, safety, comfort, and economic prosperity.
     Adopted this 20th day of May, 2021.
