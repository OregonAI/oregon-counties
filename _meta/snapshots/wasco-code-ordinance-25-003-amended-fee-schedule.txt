IN THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF THE STATE OF OREGON
IN AND FOR THE COUNTY OF WASCO
IN THE MATTER OF AMENDING WASCO COUNTY’S SERVICES FEE SCHEDULE FOR THE BUILDING CODES
DEPARTMENT
ORDINANCE 25-003

THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF WASCO COUNTY OREGON DOES ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:

Section 1. PURPOSE

Wasco County provides core services to all residents which are paid for through the annual tax base. The
County strives to proactively deliver these services in alignment with its Vision and Mission statements.

In some instances, special services are required by various state statutes, or to meet the needs of residents beyond
the scope of core services. The purpose of this Ordinance is to establish the fees to be collected by Wasco County
Building Codes Department for performing services, and to establish a fee schedule.

Section 2. AUTHORITY

This Ordinance is enacted pursuant to the authority granted to general law Counties by ORS 203.035-
ORS 203.065 and by ORS 192.440.

Section 3. FEE SCHEDULE

Fees shall be charged and collected by the indicated Department before the filing, recording or copying of
subject documents or the provision of requested services. The current schedule of County fees is provided in
Appendix D. Additional fees may apply as authorized under the Oregon Revised Statutes or other applicable laws.

Section 4. ENACTMENT PROVISIONS (1)

(1) CONFORMANCE WITH LAW

Except as expressly provided herein, this Ordinance shall in no way be a substitute for or eliminate the
necessity of conforming with any and all State and Federal laws, rules and regulations including but not limited to
the payment of all other fees required by law and other Ordinances now in effect or subsequently adopted.

(2) SEVERABILTIY

If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, phrase or portion of this Ordinance is for any reason held
invalid or unconstitutional by a Court of competent jurisdiction, such portion shall be deemed as a separate,
distinct and independent provision and such holdings shall not affect the validity of the rem aining portion of this
Ordinance.
     ORDINANCE 25-003

(3) EFFECTIVE DATE

This Ordinance shall take effect on September 22, 2025 upon its adoption, and all previous resolutions,
orders, or ordinances establishing conflicting fees are hereby repealed and shall be of no further force or effect.

Regularly passed and adopted by the Board of Commissioners of the County of Wasco, State of Oregon,
by a 3 to 0 vote on this 18th day of June, 2025.
