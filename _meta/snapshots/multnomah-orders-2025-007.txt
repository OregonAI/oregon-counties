BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO.  2025-007
Approving an Increased Contract Price for the Duniway-Lovejoy Elections Fire Sprinkler
System Installation Project.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Multnomah County owns the Duniway-Lovejoy Election building located at 1040
SE Morrison St., Portland, Oregon (“Building”).
B. In fiscal year 2022, a Capital project was approved as part of the 5 -year capital
improvement plan for fire sprinkler system installation for the Building to meet code
requirements (the “Project”). After the Project had already been awarded to a
contractor, further investigation identified additional requirements to ensure
adequate and safe repair and renovation to the Building.
C. Through a Request for Proposal process (RFP -4-2023) a contract for the Project
was awarded to “RJS Construction” (“Contract”).
D. Additional costs in the amount of $272,000 were identified as needed to complete
the Project effectively due to updated code requirements that were not originally
found in the design process. These code requirements result in the need to add
additional fire sprinkler branches and heads to provide adequate coverage
throughout areas that were initially hidden to ensure the County is meeting the
appropriate NFPA 13 safety code requirements.
E. The cost for the additional work requires Board approval pursuant to Public
Contract Review Board (PCRB) 49 -0910(3), due to the increased costs of the
Contract for renovation and remodeling exceeding 33% of the original Contract
price.
F. No additional funding from the Fiscal Budget 2025 is required. Planned additional
funding from the Fiscal Budget 2026 with the Asset Preservation fund will be
allocated to the Project.
G. Approval of the increased Contract price from $615,000 to $887,000 (“Increased
Contract Price”) is in the best interests of Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
Pursuant to PCRB 49-0910(3), the Increased Contract Price is approved.
ADOPTED this 30th day of January, 2025.
