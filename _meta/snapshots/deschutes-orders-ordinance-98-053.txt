ORDINANCE NO. 98- 053
WHEREAS, the City of Bend and Deschutes County have through an intergovernmental
agreementtransferred planning authority within the Bend Urban Area to the City of Bend; and
WHEREAS, City and County agree that pursuant to that agreement the Bend City Council will
should have the power to appoint all members of the Bend Urban Area Planning Commission and that the
County' s Bend Urban Area Planning Commission should otherwise be amendedto be consistent with the
City' s land use ordinances insofar as is practicable; now, therefore,
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY, OREGON,
ORDAINS as follows:
Section 1. AMENDMENT. Deschutes County Code Chapter 2.20 is amended to read as
described in Exhibit " A," attached hereto and by this reference incorporated herein, with new language
underlined and language to be deleted in stfikethfough.
Section 2. EFFECT ON CURRENT COUNTY APPOINTEES. Notwithstanding Section
2.20.040 of the Deschutes County Code, passageof this ordinance shall have the effect of terminating the
tenure of the current County appointees to the Bend Urban Area Planning Commission, once those
appointeeshave been so notified in writing by the Board of County Commissioners.
Section 3. EMERGENCY. This ordinance being necessaryfor the immediate preservation
of the public peace, health and safety, an emergency is declared to exist, and this ordinance takes effect
on its passage.
DATED this 26`" day of August, 1998.

2. 20. 003. Definition - Urban Growth
Boundary.
As used in this chapter, the Urban Growth
Boundary is the urban growth boundary line
acknowledged by the Land Conservation and
Development Commission, which identifies and
encompasses urban and urbanizable land. In the
acknowledged 1981 Bend General Plan, the
urban growth boundary was identified as the
Initial Urban Growth Boundary. ( Ord. 98- 053 §
1, 1998)
2. 20. 005. Definition - Urban Reserve
Boundary.
As used in this chapter, the Urban Reserve
Boundary is the boundary line that identifies
and encompasses the city of Bend, the Urban
Growth Boundary and adiacent lands designated
as Urban Reserve. ( Ord. 98- 053 § 1, 1998)
2. 20. 007. Definition - Urban Reserve.
As used in this chapter, the Urban Reserve
refers to lands lying outside of the urban growth
boundary and identified in the Bend Area
General Plan as Urban Reserve and
encompassed by the Urban Reserve Boundary.
Ord. 98- 053 § 1, 1998)
