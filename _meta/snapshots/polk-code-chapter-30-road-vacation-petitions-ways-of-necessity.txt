CHAPTER 30     1994

 ROAD VACATION PETITIONS - WAYS OF NECESSITY

 30.110  FILING FEE.

 Every petition for vacation of a road or decr ease of road width be accompanied by a filing
fee in an amount prescribed by the Board of Commissioners upon submission of the petition to the
Board of Commissioners for consideration. [Ord. 159, sec. 1; amended by Ord. 89-5, Sec. 4]

 30.120  TRUST FUND.

 Such fee, upon order of the Board of Commissione rs, shall be transferred to a trust fund in
the County Treasurer's office, for the use of the County Surveyor or County Roadmaster. [Ord. 159,
sec. 2]

 30.130  FEE NONREFUNDABLE; EXCEPTION.

 The fee referred to in Section 30.110 is nonrefundable unless petitioner withdraws the
petition and requests refund of the fee before th e Board of Commissioners orders action on the
petition to be initiated. [Ord. 159, sec. 3]

 30.150  WAYS OF NECESSITY.

 Jurisdiction over the establishment of ways of necessity under ORS 376.150 to 376.200 is
vested in the governing body for Polk County. [Ord. 87-10; amended by Ord. 94-18, Sec. 2.]
