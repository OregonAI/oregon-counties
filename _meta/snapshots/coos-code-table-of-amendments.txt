The following ordinances have amended Ordinance 91-01-002L
(91-1-0794) which originally adopted the Coos County Code:

Ordinance 91-06-007L (91-2-0335) - Revised various Code
provisions, 02.02.020, 02.05.060, 04.01.010, 04.01.020,
05.01.090, 07.01.170(1), 07.01.240(1), 09.03.020, 09.04.030, to
reflect changes in the fees charged for County services.

Ordinance 91-07-014L (91-3-0060) - Revised Article Five, Division
One, 05.01.070, 05.01.080 and 05.01.090, to revise the expiration
date of dog licenses.

Ordinance 91-09-017L (91-3-0852) - Revised Article Four, Division
Eight, 04.08.010, 04.08.020, 04.08.030 and 04.08.040, to extend
the applicability of the Road Naming Ordinance to private roads.

Ordinance 92-01-003L (92-1-0393) - Revised Article Nine, Division
Three, 09.03.020, to revise the fees charged by the Surveyor for
reviewing land divisions.

Ordinance 92-03-005L (92-1-1279) - Revised Article Six, Division
One, which establishes park regulations.

Ordinance 92-04-006L (92-2-0237) - Revised Article Five, Division
One, 05.01.080, to address the requirements for Rabies
Vaccination Certificates in commercial and non-commercial kennel
license applications.

Ordinance 93-02-002L (93-1-1195) - Revised Article Seven,
Division One, 07.01.220, to extend the term of franchises from
five (5) to ten (10) years with automatic renewal.

Ordinance 93-03-007L (93-1-1646) - Added Division Two to Article
Seven to add a Protected Resource Activity provision intended to
address protecting, preserving and providing for the continuance
of resource activities in the County.

Ordinance 93-03-008L (93-1-1652) - Added Division Two to Article
Eleven to provide an accelerated redemption period for foreclosed
property in the event that the property is subject to waste or,
in the case of unoccupied property, a substantial depreciation in
value.

Ordinance 93-08-015L (93-3-0531) - Revised Article Four, Division
Eight to provide a system to assign road names and rural
addresses.
