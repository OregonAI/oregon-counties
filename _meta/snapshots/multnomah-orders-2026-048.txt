BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2026-048
Adopting the Safer Sandy Boulevard Concept Design.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. The Safer Sandy Boulevard Concept Design Plan (the “Safer Sandy Plan”) aims to
improve conditions for all users—including people walking, biking, driving, and
accessing transit—by addressing key safety concerns and identifying infrastructure on
NE Sandy Boulevard (Sandy Blvd) between 201st Ave and 230th Ave.
B. The Sandy Plan culminates a year and a half effort of planning and outreach along the
Sandy Corridor in the City of Fairview and Gresham. This included extensive
community engagement including focus groups, workshops, and surveys to
understand road users’ experience on Sandy;
C. The Sandy Plan is a ten (10) percent conceptual design plan that includes preliminary
analysis on its impacts to adjacent properties, street lighting, and utility needs,
intersection operations and design, and bus stop placement. This plan includes
preliminary cost analysis for construction.
D. In 2022, Multnomah County was awarded Regional Flexible Funds for 2025-2027
cycle to construct improvements identified in the Safer Sandy Plan.
E. Upon adoption of the plan, Multnomah County Transportation will begin work on the
final design and construction plan for improvements on a segment of Sandy Blvd
(201st to 223rd). Improvements include midblock crossing, sidewalk installation and
bike lanes.
F. The Safer Sandy Plan is included as Exhibit A and Public Engagement Summary is
included as Exhibit B to this Resolution.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. To accept the Safer Plan to guide future transportation planning decisions through
the Plan’s vision, implementation, and final recommendations; and
2. To adopt the final report as identified as the “Safer Sandy Boulevard Concept
Design Report” prepared by Alta Planning + Design, Inc in substantially the same
form as the attached Exhibit “A”.
ADOPTED this 2nd day of July, 2026.
