BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO.
Resolution Establishing Multnomah County’s Commitment to Investigate and Provide a Plan
for How the County Can Require Financial Assurances from CEI Hub Owner/Operators.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. The CEI Hub contains many pieces of infrastructure that would be critically damaged
in the event of a major earthquake.
B. The CEI Hub’s potential to spill or release hazardous materials poses a health risk to
Multnomah County residents.
C. As the local public health authority, Multnomah County has an interest in protecting the
public’s health from CEI Hub-induced risks.
D. Multnomah County does not have the financial resources to cover costs associated
with Multnomah County’s response to a spill or release at the CEI Hub.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
A. To establish policies to assure that the County has sufficient financial resources to
cover costs associated with Multnomah County's response to a spill or release at the
CEI Hub located along the West shore of the Willamette River.
B. It is the Board’s policy that the Health Department, Emergency Management, Risk
Management, Office of Sustainability, and Purchasing investigate and determine
feasibility of the County requiring financial assurances from CEI Hub owners/operators
similar to those proposed in the draft ordinance.

C. Next steps may include, but are not limited to:
a. Internal convening of a project team, led by the Department of County
Management, to evaluate the policy options;
b. Assessment of County costs in the worst-case scenario of a CEI hub spill or
release.
c. Request For Information or other solicitation to identify the products are
available to cover County costs; and
d. Recommendation on implementation, management, and enforcement of an
ordinance similar to this draft ordinance.
