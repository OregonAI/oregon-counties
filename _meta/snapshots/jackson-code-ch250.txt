250.01  ADVISORY COMMITTEES.

(a)  Definitions. As used in this Chapter, “Advisory Committee” means any committee,
council, board, or other body established pursuant to this Chapter to  serve in an advisory
or informational capacity to the Board of County Commissioners.

(b)  Advisory  Committees.  The following Advisory Committees are hereby established:

(1)  Active Transportation Advisory Committee.

(2)  Airport Advisory Committee.

(3)  Animal Services Advisory Committee.

(4)  Cannabis Advisory Committee.

(5)  County Roads Advisory Committee.

250.02  QUORUM AND VOTING REQUIREMENTS.

(a)  Scope. This Section shall apply to all Advisory Committees, unless expressly
otherwise provided by law.

(b)  Purpose.  The purpose of this  Section is to establish a uniform  procedure  for
Advisory Committees  to exercise their authority.

(c)  Quorum.  A majority of duly appointed members of an Advisor y Committee  shall
constitute a quorum. Without a quorum, a meeting shall not be called to order.

(d)  Voting.

250.03  ESTABLISHMENT OF BYLAWS.

(a)  Adoption of Bylaws. The Board of Commissioners shall adopt bylaws in
accordance with this Section for each Advisory Committee.  Advisory Committee
members shall not adopt Committee bylaws, nor shall Advisory Committees designate
subcommittees unless expressly directed to do so by the Board of Commissioners.

(b)  Amendments to Bylaws. The Board of Commissioners may adopt amendments to
bylaws of Advisory Committees.  Advisory Committee members shall not amend bylaws.

(c)  Bylaw Requirements. Bylaws to an  Advisory Committee shall include, without
limitation:
(1)  The purpose of the Advisory Committee;

(2)  The number of Advisory Committee members;

(3)  The number of Advisory Committee members required to constitute  a quorum
of the Advisory Committee;

(4)  The qualifications to serve as an Advisory Committee member;

(5)  The term of office for an Advisory Committee member;

(6)  The obligations of an Advisory Committee member including, but not limited
to, disclosure of actual or potential conflicts of interest, records  retention
requirements, attendance at meetings  of  the  Advisory Committee,  and any
other obligations for membership as determined by Order of the Board; and

(7)  The process for selection of officers of the Advisory Committee.

(d)  In general, bylaws for an Advisory Committee shall conform to the following
template, unless otherwise adopted or ordered by the Board of Commissioners.

Article 3: Officers

The Advisory Committee shall  annually elect  members of the  Advisory Committee
to serve as Chair and Vice-Chair.  Elections shall be held the first meeting of the
calendar year. The Chair shall conduct meeting business, and the Vice-Chair shall
conduct business in the absence of the Chair.

Article 4: Meetings

A.  Meetings shall be held [monthly/bi-monthly/quarterly/annually].
Additional meetings may be called at the request of the Board of
Commissioners in accordance with all applicable special meeting
laws.

B.  A quorum of members must be present in order for Advisory
Committee meetings to be called to order.

C.  All meetings are subject to Oregon Public Meetings Law and must
be properly noticed, and shall be posted on the Meeting Calendar
on the County’s public website.

D.  Emergency meetings may be held in accordance with Oregon
Public Meetings Law.
Article 5: Compensation

Members of the Advisory Committee shall serve without compensation.  However,
all expenses necessarily incurred by members to conduct the business of the
Advisory Committee shall, if incurred pursuant the express authority of the [insert
department name] Department Director, and subject to budgetary  limitations and
County policy, and the limitations prescribed by law, be paid from the [insert
department name] Department budget.

Article 6: Conflicts of Interest

Advisory Committee members must disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest
in accordance with all applicable ethics laws, statutes, rules, and regulations.  The
existence of an actual conflict of interest shall be cause for immediate termination of
Advisory Committee membership.

Article 7: Amendment to Bylaws

Any Amendments to these Bylaws must be approved by Order of the Board of
Commissioners.
