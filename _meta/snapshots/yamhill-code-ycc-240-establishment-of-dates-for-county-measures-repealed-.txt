2.40.01 REPEAL OF ORDINANCE 261

LEGISLATIVE HISTORY

2.40.01 Repeal of Ordinance 261 . Effective February 6, 1991, Ordinance 261 is repealed in its entirety. The
county clerk shall schedule an election in accordance with ORS 250.221, 1989 replacement part, for any county
measure referred to the voters by initiative or referendum petition for which the county clerk has, on or after
February 6, 1991, determined that the required number of verified signatures allow inclusion on a ballot.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 518 eff 02/06/91]
