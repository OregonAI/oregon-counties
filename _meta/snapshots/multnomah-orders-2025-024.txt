BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO.  2025-024
Approving the Sobering and Crisis Stabilization Center FAC-1 Project Design Plan.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The Sobering and Crisis Stabilization Center (“Project”) will be located in a Multnomah
County owned property located at 1901 - 1907 SE Grand Avenue, Portland, Oregon
97214.
b. The state Legislature in 2024 through House bills 5204 and 5701 allocated to
Multnomah County $25,000,000 to support the construction of a behavioral health
drop off and permanent sobering center.  Approximately $3.6 million was used for
planning, design and construction of the current Coordinated Care and Pathway
Center and $6.2 million was used for the acquisition of the property at 1901 - 1907 SE
Grand Avenue and the associated due diligence work.
c. The County’s Facilities & Property Management (FPM) division within the Department
of County Assets began a months-long search for a property that would meet the
criteria for the Project in June 2024.  This search resulted in locating and purchasing
the property located at 1901 - 1907 SE Grand Avenue, which closed on April 3,
2025.
d. Scott Edwards Architecture (SEA) is leading the Project design team and Fortis
Construction was hired as the Construction Manager/General Contractor
(CMGC).  The Project Design Plan includes conceptual design for a Project Scope, an
Initial Draft Project Schedule that shows a Project completion date of summer 2027,
and an Initial Cost Estimate of $29,800,000  At the completion of design, FPM will
seek approval from the Board of County Commissioners to proceed with the
construction phase of the Project.
e. The current planning for the Sobering & Crisis Stabilization Center is to be a trauma
informed facility resource for first responders and community providers for deflection,
medication assisted treatment, sobering withdrawal management and sheltering.
f. Consistent with this Resolution and the FAC-1 Administrative Procedure, full Project
design may proceed in preparation for construction.
g. Approval of the Project Design Plan is in the best interests of Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
The Project is approved to continue with full Project design.
ADOPTED this 7th day of April, 2025.
