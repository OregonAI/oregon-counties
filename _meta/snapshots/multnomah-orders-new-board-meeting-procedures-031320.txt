NOTICE: IMPLEMENTING NEW BOARD MEETING PROCEDURES

EFFECTIVE 3/13/2020

The health and safety of our community and staff members are at the forefront of

our minds as we navigate county business in light of the
​
COVID-19 pandemic. It

is critical that we take prompt and decisive action based on the best information

we have today that aligns with our goal of slowing the spread of the virus and

protecting our staff and community members to our best ability.

In accordance with the Chair’s
​
Declaration of Emergency
​
, the rules associated

with Board of County Commissioner meetings will be temporarily altered as

necessary measures to align with local and state social distancing guidance.

Effective immediately, the following social distancing protocols will apply to board

meetings and briefings:

●
No more than 50 people will be permitted in the boardroom. Presenters

and essential personnel will be prioritized;

●
Keep a physical distance of 3 feet from other people around you at all

times during the meetings. Additional chairs will be provided to the extent

practicable;

●
View board meetings via
​
livestream
​
 rather than in person as feasible;

●
Oral testimony will be permitted, however, we strongly encourage that

public testimony be submitted in writing or other electronic means.

Testimony can be submitted to
​
boardclerk@multco.us
​
.

These rules will change as needed given the evolving needs surrounding this

pandemic. We greatly appreciate your understanding as we work to keep our

community safe.
