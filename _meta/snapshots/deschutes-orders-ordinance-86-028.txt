THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY,
OREGON, ORDAINS as follows:
Section 1. The first paragraph of Section 1. 440, Ordinance
No. 81- 009, Deschutes County Sign Ordinance, is amended to read
as follows:
1. 440 VARIANCE APPLICATION. An applicant
for a sign permit may see a variance to the
provisions of this ordinance. Variance
requests for sign location or for sign height
or area which are within twenty ( 20) percent
of the applicable permitted height or area
requirements specified within this ordinance
may be determined by the Planning Director.
The decision of the Planning Director may be
appealed to the Hearings Officer. Requests
for variances which exceed twenty ( 20) per-
cent of the applicable permitted height or
area requirements specified within this
ordinance and other deviations from this
ordinance shall be determined by the Hearings
Officer as provided in Ordinance, No. 82-
011. A variance may be granted upon a find-
ing by the appropriate review body that all
of the following criteria can be satisfied:"
Section 2. This Ordinance being necessary for the immediate
preservation of the public peace, health and safety, an emergency
is declared to exist, and this Ordinance takes effect on its
passage.
