ORDINANCE NO. 81- 034
WHEREAS, the Deschutes County Clerk is responsible for the
recording of all legal documents in Deschutes County in accordance
with the Oregon Revised Statutes; and
WHEREAS, orderly receipt of documents for recordation is
necessary to allow the proper recording of documents with the County
Clerk; and
WHEREAS, in order to provide sufficient services with
respect to recording documents, a deadline for receiving documents on
each judicial day is needed; now, therefore,
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY,
OREGON, ORDAINS as follows:
Section 1. That the hours of receiving and recording
documents in the Deschutes County' Clerk's office shall be from
8: 30 a. m. until 4: 00 p. m. on each judicial day.
Section 2. This Ordinance being necessary for the
immediate preservation of public peace, health and safety, an
emergency is declared to exist, and this Ordinance takes effect on
its passage.
