                                                FINDINGS

      These findings are adopted by the Yamhill County Board of
Commissioners     (the Board)  in support of its action of December
31, 1986,    to amend the Yamhill County Comprehensive Plan,    1974
to allow an expansion of the urban growth boundary of the City
of Dundee.     There are two areas of amendment of the existing
urban growth boundary of the City of Dundee affected by Ordi-
nance   438.   First,  the amendment allows expansion of the urban
growth boundary to take in the easterly portion of County Road
79  (Edwards Drive)    to coincide with a surrender of jurisdiction
and subsequent     acceptance of jurisdiction over that portion of
Edwards Drive within the City of Dundee.        The amendment relating
to Edwards Drive will extend the urban growth boundary from the
centerline of Edwards Drive to the easterly right-of-way.        The
second area of amendment takes      in the fully developed City of
Dundee cemetery and the tax lot on which the City of Dundee
water storage tank is located.       Both areas of amendment are
contiguous.    to the existing city limits.

        The Board           makes      the    following     findings            in    support      of   Ordi-
nance     4383

      1.  The Board finds that due to the insubstantial   effect of
the addition of one half of Edwards Drive to the urban growth
boundary and the full development of the Dundee Cemetery,     and the
tax lot supporting the water storage tank,   that the seven Goal 14
amendment criteria are not applicable  to this amendment.     The
Board has considered each factor and finds no conflict with any
Goal 14 amendment factor by the urban growth boundary expansion.

         2.        The    Board     finds     that   no   impact     on    either           resources       pro-
tected        by    the    goal   or    the    requirement         for    the        city    to   provide
services will be made by the expansion                              of    the        urban    growth     boun-
dary accomplished by Ordinance 438.

      3.  The Board finds that there are no areas within the UGB
which could accommodate   a water storage tower because of the need
for high elevation not found elsewhere within the UGB.    The Board
further  finds that there are no areas within the UGB which could
reasonably accommodate   a cemetery.

      4,  The Board finds that there are no long-term environmental,
economic,  social, or energy consequences  resulting from the expan-
sion of the urban growth boundary to encompass the easterly portion
of Edwards Drive,  the Dundee City Cemetery and the Dundee water
storage tank.

      5.   The Board finds that the proposed uses as roadway,  ceme-
tery,   and water storage tank are compatible with other existing
adjacent uses.

      6.  The Board finds that the expansion of the urban growth
boundary to encompass   the easterly portion of Edwards Drive, the
Dundee City Cemetery and the Dundee water storage tank is in the
best interests of the citizens of Dundee and Yamhill County.
