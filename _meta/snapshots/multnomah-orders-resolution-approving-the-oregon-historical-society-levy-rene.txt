RESOLUTION NO.  2021-008
Approving the Oregon Historical Society Levy Renewal – Explanatory Statement and Ballot
Title.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The Oregon Historical Society houses and preserves Oregon's collection of historical
documents, photos, books, maps and artifacts. The Museum and Library provide public
access to research, study and interact with these materials to learn Oregon history.
b. In 2016  Multnomah County residents passed a five -year levy of $.05 per $1,000
assessed valuation to support the Oregon Historical Society, including the Oregon
History Library and Museum.
c. A renewal of the five -year levy of $.05 per $1,000 assessed valuation will  keep the
Oregon History Library and Museum open to the public. An independent citizen
oversight committee created to review all expenditures and ensure dollars are spent as
promised will continue to provide accountability of the funds raised by the levy.
d. The ballot title and explanatory statement for renewal of a five-year levy of $0.05 per
$1,000 of assessed property value have been prepared and are attached to this
Resolution as Exhibit A.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The attached ballot title and explanatory statement are approved and certified to the
Director of the Multnomah County Division of Elections.
2. The board clerk shall submit this resolution, ballot title and explanatory statement to the
Multnomah County Elections Director for further action as required by law.
ADOPTED this 18th day of February, 2021.
