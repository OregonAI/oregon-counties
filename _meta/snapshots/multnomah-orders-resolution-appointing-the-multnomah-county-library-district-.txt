BEFORE THE MULTNOMAH COUNTY LIBRARY DISTRICT BOARD
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-039
Appointing the Multnomah County Library District Budget Officer:
The Multnomah County Library District Board Finds:
A. State law requires the Multnomah County Library District Board to designate one person
to serve as District Budget Officer (Oregon Revised Statute 294.331).
B. The current District Budget Officer, Vailey Oehlke , is retiring on May 31, 2024.
C. Annie Lewis, Deputy Director of Multnomah County Library, is qualified to serve as  the
Multnomah County Library District Budget Officer.
The Multnomah County Library District Board Resolves:
To appoint Annie Lewis as the Multnomah County Library District Budget Officer, effective June
1, 2024.
ADOPTED this 30th day of May, 2024.
