TITLE: Public Contracting Rules PROCEDURE #: 901-A

OBJECTIVE: To establish a procedur e for Public Contracting Rules.

REFERENCE: Policy # 901

POLICY STATEMENT: This policy establishes a sound and responsive public contracting system
based on public contracting code for the purchase of all goods and/or
services required to conduct the county’s business.

APPLICABILITY: All county departments.

PROCEDURES: Procedures for Policy #901 are part of the Public Contracting Rules,
adopted by Ordinance 1211 effective March 1, 2005 and further revised by
adopted Ordinance #1299 on January 27, 2010.  See Intranet site for
