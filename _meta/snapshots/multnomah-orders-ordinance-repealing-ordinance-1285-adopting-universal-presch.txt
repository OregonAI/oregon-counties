BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR
MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
ORDINANCE NO. 1286
Repealing Ordinance 1285 - Adopting Universal Preschool Now! Initiative Petition and Declaring
an Emergency.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Ordinance 1285 - Universal Preschool Now! was adopted on September 3, 2020.
B. It is considered necessary to repeal this Ordinance because a similar measure was
referred to the November 2020 ballot.
Multnomah County Ordains as Follows:
Ordinance 1285 is repealed.
FIRST READING AND ADOPTION: September 3, 2020

FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
