       THE           BOARD   OF      COMMISSIONERS                 OF       YAMHILL            COUNTY,       OREGON           (the         Board)
on the 4th day of May,    1983, sat for the transaction                                                              of County
business,  in regular session,   Commissioners Robin J.                                                              Hamblet,
Ted Lopuszynski   and Dave Bishop being present.

    WHEREAS,    the Board of Commissioners     on January 5, 1983,     adopted
Ordinance    325 entitled     "In the Matter of An Ordinance     to Amend the
Yamhill County Comprehensive Plan of 1974          for Property Owned by
Dale Compton"      and Ordinance    327 "In the Matter of an Ordinance      to
Amend the Yamhill County Comprehensive         Plan of 1974   for Property
Owned by S.B.Nicoll";       and

   WHEREAS,  the property description   and Plan Map designation   change
set forth in Ordinance    325 for Dale Compton is actually the property
description  and Plan Map designation   change for S.B.Nicoll  and the
property description   and Plan Map designation   change in Ordinance
327 for S.B.Nicoll   is actually  the property description  and Plan Map
designation  change for Dale Compton;   and

    WHEREAS    the juxtaposition of the above-described  descriptions    and
designations was inadvertant     and constituted  a typographical  error
which may be summarily corrected by an amendment of Ordinance       325
and 327;    and

   WHEREAS,   no rights are prejudiced by                                                    the correction  of                     these
typographical   errors without condu¢ting                                                    formal hearings;.
