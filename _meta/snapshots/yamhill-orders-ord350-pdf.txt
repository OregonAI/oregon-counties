    IT       APPEARING TO THE BOARD that a procedure  is set forth in the
Solid        Waste Ordinance  for the establishment of Disposal Franchise
rates        and         for        the      establishment                  of    Collection              Franchise            rates,           and

    IT FURTHER APPEARING                                  TO THE BOARD                 that  the  current  procedures
are   independent and, as                                 such, do not                 provide   for  simultaneous  con-
sideration  of Collection Franchise                                               rates        when       increases            are        requested
by Disposal Franchises,   and

    IT FURTHER APPEARING TO THE BOARD that Collection                                                                    Franchise ex-
penses  are necessarily  affected by increases allowed                                                                   Disposal Fran-
chises,                and

       IT FURTHER APPEARING  TO                                 THE  BOARD             that  it        is in the best   interests
of     the citizens of Yamhill                                  County   to            provide         a procedure  whereby   rate
increases for Disposal                                   Franchises              and    Collection            Franchises                  are    con-
sidered jointly, and

       IT    FURTHER               APPEARING              TO    THE     BOARD          that     the       Solid        Waste         Advisory
Committee                has        recommended                that     the       following            Ordinance              be     enacted
by the Board;  NOW,   THEREFORE,   IT IS HEREBY                                                     ORDAINED  BY THE BOARD
that  the Solid Waste   Ordinance,   as amended                                                     by Ordinances   154 and
192,        be         and    hereby           is    amended           as       follows:                                  ;

   SECTION                   1.        Section 10.01 of the Solid Waste Ordinance                                                    shall       be
amended by                   the       addition of the following subsection:

       i.        (1)         When       an     application              for       a    rate     increase            is    submitted
                             to     the      Board        by    a    disposal           franchise,             the       applicant
                             shall mail a copy of the application   to all collec-
                             tion franchises which use the disposal   site operated
                             by the applicant  as their primary disposal   site.  Any
                             collection              franchise              whose       rates        are    established                   by
                             County may, within 10 days of receipt by the County
                             of the disposal rate increase   application, submit to
                             the Board an application   for a collection rate increase.
                             Any such application  submitted within the 10 day period
                             shall be considered  jointly with the disposal   rate in-
                             crease          request           pursuant           to    the     process         authorized                 by
                             this       Ordinance              for     establishment                 of    rates.

                             tion operations,   the Committee  shall give due considera-
