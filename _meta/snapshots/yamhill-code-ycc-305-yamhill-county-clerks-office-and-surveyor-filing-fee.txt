3.05.02 Fee for Recording of Plats.
a) In addition to any other fee charged by the Yamhill County Clerk for recording or filing instruments, the fee
for performing the services set forth in ORS 92.090 and 92.100 shall be as follows:
(i) Plats of 100 lots or less: $45
(ii) Plats of over 100 lots: $40 plus $1.00 for each lot over 100.
b) Said fee shall be paid to the Yamhill County Clerk prior to acceptance of the above-mentioned instrument for
recording.

3.05.10 Land Corner Preservation Fee.
a) In addition to any other fee charged by the Yamhill County Clerk  for recording or filing instruments, the
Yamhill County Clerk shall charge a fee of $15.00 for recording the following instruments pursuant to ORS
205.130(2):
(i) Deeds and mortgages of real property, powers of attorney, and contracts affecting the title to real
property, authorized by law to be recorded;
(ii) Assignments of any document identified under subsection (i), and any other interests affecting the
title to real property, when properly acknowledged or proved;
(iii) Certificates of sale of real property under execution or order of court, or assignments of previously
recorded certificates or of any interest in real property, when properly acknowledged or proved;
(iv) Instruments presented for recording by the United States or the State of Oregon, or a political
subdivision of either, that affect title to or an interest in real property or that lawfully concern real
property; and
(v) Instruments recognized under state law or rule or federal law or regulation as affecting title to or
an interest in real property if the instrument is properly acknowledged or proved.
b) In addition to any other fee charged by the Yamhill County Clerk for recording or filing instruments, t he
Yamhill County Clerk shall charge a fee of $0.00 for recording certified copies of death records of any person
appearing in the County records as owning or having a claim or interest in land in Yamhill County.
c) Fees collected pursuant to this section shall be paid to the County treasurer and shall be deposited to the credit
of the Public Land Corner Preservation Account. Said account shall be used only to pay expenses incurred or
authorized by the Yamhill County Surveyor in the establishment, reestablishment and maintenance of corners
of government surveys under ORS 209.070(5) and (6).

3.05.12 Fees Established by Board Order . The Yamhill County Board of Commissioners (Board)  may from
time to time establish by Board Order such fees as the Board deems appropriate for making copies of public
records, search of public records and such other miscellaneous matters involving public requests for information
and records. Such fees shall be reasonably related to cost of providi ng such service or copies and may vary
between County departments.
