1.0 Purpose

To establish policies and procedures to donate personal property to other
government agencies and non profit organizations.

2.0 General Policy

General Services Department may donate personal property to other government
agencies and non profit organizations based upon surplus property being
available.

3.0 Policy Guideline/Procedures

a. Personal property may be donated to other government agencies and non
profit organization when surplus property is available and in such a manner
as determined by General Services.  Compensation may or may not be
required dependent upon the value of the property as determined by General
Services.

b. County vehicles will not be donated with the exception of exchanges for
goods or services by the Sheriff Department with other law enforcement
agencies.
