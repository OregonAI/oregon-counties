BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2025-012
Consenting to Chair Vega Pederson’s Appointment of Christopher Neal as the Director
of the Department of County Management for Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3) provides that the Chair’s appointment of
department heads (Directors) is subject to consent of a majority of the Board.
b. The Chair appoints Christopher Neal as the Director of the Department of County
Management beginning April 7, 2025.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
In accordance with Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3), the Board consents to
the appointment of Christopher Neal as the Director of the Department of County
Management.
ADOPTED this 13th day of March, 2025.
