Section 301.00 - Classification of Districts

301.00  Classification of Districts
For the purposes of this Ordinance, the unincorporated area of Yamhill County, Oregon,
is hereby divided into the following districts:
A.  Natural Resource Districts

Forestry District  ............................................................... F-80
Exclusive Farm Use District
/square4 80 acre minimum .......................... EF-80
/square4 40 acre minimum .......................... EF-40
/square4 20 acre minimum .......................... EF-20
Agriculture/Forestry Large Holding District
/square4 80 acre minimum .......................... AF-80
/square4 40 acre minimum .......................... AF-40
/square4 20 acre minimum .......................... AF-20
Mineral Resource District  .................................................... MR
Parks, Recreation, and Open Space District  ............................ PRO
B.  Rural Residential Districts

Agriculture/Forestry Small Holding District  .............................. AF-10
Very Low Density Residential
/square4 5 acre minimum ............................ VLDR-5

/square4 2 1/2 acre minimum........................ VLDR-2 1/2

/square4 1 acre minimum ............................ VLDR-1

Low Density Residential
/square4 12,000 District ............................. LDR-12,000

/square4  9,000 District .............................. LDR-9,000

/square4  6,750 District .............................. LDR-6,750

C.  Commercial Districts

Recreation Commercial District  .............................................. RC

Highway/Tourist Commercial District  ....................................... HC
D.  Industrial Districts

Resource Industrial District  ................................................... RI

Heavy Industrial District  ....................................................... HI
E.  Public Facility Districts

Public Assembly/Institutional District  ...................................... PAI
Public Works/Safety District  ................................................. PWS
Public Airports/Landing Fields District  ..................................... PALF
F.  Overlay Districts

Floodplain Overlay District  .................................................... FP
