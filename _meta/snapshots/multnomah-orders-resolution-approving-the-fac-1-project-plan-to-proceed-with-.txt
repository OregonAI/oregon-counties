BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2023-069
Approving the FAC-1 Project Plan to Proceed with the Construction Phase of the
Multnomah County Justice Center Electrical Bus Duct System Replacement Project.
The
 Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. T
he Justice Center is a commercial condominium with Multnomah County as
owner of Units 1 and 5, the City of Portland as owner of Unit 2, and a third-party
as owner of Unit 4. Unit 3 no longer exists. The Justice Center is located at 1120
SW 3rd Avenue, Portland Oregon.
B. T
he Justice Center’s incoming building electrical service, busway risers, and
electrical distribution equipment are mostly of original construction (1981) and
need to be replaced. The bus duct system will be replaced with a conduit and
wire system (the “Project”), which is beneficial in that it is easier to access,
maintain and repair.
C. LR
S Architects is leading the project design team, which includes: Multnomah
County Facilities & Property Management; the City of Portland's Office of
Management and Finance; and the Multnomah County Sheriff’s Office.
D. C
onstruction is scheduled to begin in January 2024 and completed in July 2027.
E. P
ursuant to FAC-1, the project team has prepared, and recommends approval of,
theFAC-1 Project Plan, setting forth the plan for the Construction Phase, which
includes the total project cost estimate of between $20.5 and $25.5 million.
F. A
pproval of the FAC-1 Project Plan, setting forth the plan for the Construction
Phase, is in the best interests of Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. T
he Project Plan is approved and the Project team is authorized to proceed with
the Construction Phase of the Project, including the solicitation of bids or
proposals for construction of the Project
ADOPTED this 20th day of July, 2023.
