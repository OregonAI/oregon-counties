BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY , OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-073
Authorizing the Appointment of a Member to the Homelessness Response System Steering
and Oversight Committee.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Under the Intergovernmental Agreement dated July 1, 2024, between Multnomah
County and the City of Portland, the Homelessness Response System (HRS) will be
governed by the Steering and Oversight Committee (SOC) and its two subcommittees.
B. The SOC is responsible for the following: setting strategy and Key Performance
Indicators; monitoring progress and performance toward goals; ensuring alignment of
jurisdictional investments toward strategies and performance; adjusting annual goals
at the completion of each year; assessing strategies based on performance; and
reviewing audits of the various components of the HRS.
C. The SOC is composed of five voting members – the County Chair, the City Mayor, one
County Commissioner, one member of City Council, and an east Multnomah County
city elected Mayor. The SOC will also include five non-voting members: one business
sector representative (nominated by any business chamber of commerce located in
Multnomah County), one behavioral health expert that is not a current provider with the
Parties (nominated by the County), a Supportive Housing Services (SHS) taxpayer
(who is a non-Portland resident), the CEO of Health Share Oregon, and the CEO of
Home Forward.
D. The County Chair will serve as the chairperson of the SOC.
E. The City Mayor will select the member from City Council and the County Chair will
appoint the member from the County Commission. The Parties will jointly approve the
business sector representative, behavioral health expert, and SHS taxpayer.
F. Peter C. Andrews has been nominated by the Portland Metro Chamber of Commerce
to serve as the SOC’s business sector representative. He completed an interest
application and an interview with staff.
G. Mr. Andrews is the Chief Operating Officer of Melvin Mark Companies, and has served
as a Volunteer at Blanchet House, as the Chair of Downtown Clean and Safe with
Central City Concern, and as the Chair of the Here Together Committee. Peter also
participated in the development of the Homelessness Response Action Plan.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. Peter C. Andrews is appointed to the SOC seat for a business representative.
2. This appointment will last for one year.
