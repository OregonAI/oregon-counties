1080.01 ESTABLISHMENT.

There is hereby established a charge to be levied to all development within the urban
growth boundary (UGB) of Medford, outside the City limits, to be known as the
Surcharge to the Street System Development Charge (SurSDC), which shall be collected
at the time of the building permits.

(Ord. 2000-34. Passed 10-25-00.)

1080.02 SurSDC RATE; DEPOSIT TO SDC FUND.

(a) The SurSDC shall be $50.00 per trip or the Trip Share Factor x $50 (see attached
Jackson County SDC fee worksheet).

(b) All funds collected pursuant to this ordinance shall be deposited in the County's
SDC Fund. A separate revenue account shall be set up within this fund and shall be
identified as South Medford SDC Surcharge.

(Ord. 2000-34. Passed 10-25-00.)

1080.03 APPEAL.

An applicant who disagrees with the required SurSDC shall have appeal rights as
described in Sections 1025.06 and 1025.13.

(Ord. 2000-34. Passed 10-25-00.)

1080.04 CREDIT FOR CONSTRUCTION OF PUBLIC IMPROVEMENT.

An applicant may receive a credit against the SurSDC for the construction of a qualified
public improvement as provided for and defined by ORS 223.304(3).

(Ord. 2000-34. Passed 10-25-00.)

1080.05 EFFECTIVE DATE; TERMINATION OF SurSDC.

(a) The effective date of this Chapter shall be the 1st day of January, 2001.

(b) The SurSDC shall be terminated by action of the Board of Commissioners at such
time that sufficient revenues have been generated to satisfy the local match requirement
for South Medford Interchange project.

(Ord. 2000-34. Passed 10-25-00.)
