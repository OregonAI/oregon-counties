PROCLAMATION NO. 2020-085

Proclaiming October 2020 as National Disability Employment Awareness Month in Multnomah
County, Oregon.

The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:

a. Approximately 42 million people in the United States have some sort of disability. This
represents our nation’s largest, most inclusive and most diverse minority group: all ages,
genders, religions, ethnicities, sexual orientations, and socioeconomic levels are
represented. Disability is a natural part of human experience. People with disabilities are
people first, who happen to have a disability.

b. National Disability Employment Awareness Month (NDEAM) is an annual campaign that
takes place each October to celebrate the contributions of workers with disabilities and
educates about the value of a workforce inclusive of their ski lls and talents. The 20 20
NDEAM theme is “Increasing Access and Opportunity.”

c. According to the U.S. Department of Labor , Office of Disability Employment Policy, the
July 2020 unemployment rate for people with a disability was 14.8%, compared to 10.4%
for those without a disability.

d. People with a disability bring with them to their workplace a wealth of lived experience
that has required flexibility and tenacity of spirit because they encounter daily barriers in
a society designed for people with ability privilege.

e. At Multnomah County, we commit to providing a workplace that embraces a philosophy
of accepting the whole person of each of our staff, and that fosters a sense of safety,
trust, and belonging for employees with disabilities.

f. Multnomah County recognizes that accessibility touches all aspects of employees work
lives, not just physical barriers. This includes our norms and attitudes, communications,
and sense of belonging.

g. Multnomah County commits to actively engage in outreach and recruitment efforts with
our community members with disabilities and show that we are a desirable place of
employment for individuals with disabilities.

h. Multnomah County affirms its commitment to fostering an inclusive work environment and
recognizes the many contributions of individuals with disabilities.

The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
The month of October 2020 as National Disability Employment Awareness Month in Multnomah
County, Oregon. We celebrate and recognize the significant contributions of people with
disabilities to the workforce and to the community and invite other organizations to do the same.
ADOPTED this 1st day of October, 2020.
