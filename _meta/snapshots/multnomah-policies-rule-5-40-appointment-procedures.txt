§  5-40-010  Responsibility  for  Employment  Lists  A.  Eligible  lists  of  candidates  for  employment  will  be  maintained  by  the  departments  that
established

the

recruitments

and

lists.

B.  Recall  and  transfer  lists  will  be  maintained  by  Central  Human  Resources  for  all
classified

job

profiles.

§  5-40-020  Types  and  Order  of  Candidate  Lists  A.  Recall  Lists:   Unless  a  collective  bargaining  agreement  specifies  otherwise,  these  are
established

by

classification

and

contain

regular

employees

who,

An

a

or

will

not

placed

on

recall

lists.

1.  When  filling  a  vacancy,  names  from  the  recall  list  will  be  considered  ahead  of
candidates

on

all

other

lists.

A

to

to

who

specific

or

knowledge

if

or

on

the

job

within

the

normal

period.

If

the

manager,

Department

Human

Resources

and

mutually

determine

does

not

have

the

required

or

knowledge

for

a

the

Department

Human

Resources

staff

must

state

in

writing

what

qualifications

required

for

the

position

the

employee

lacks

and

send

to

Relations

The

for

to

2.  When  an  employee  declines  an  offer  of  recall,  the  employee’s  name  will  be
removed

from

list.

B.  Transfers,  Demotions,  Reinstatement  following  Demotion  or  Separation:

to

and

a

General

Transfer

a.  Class  Transfer  lists  will  contain  the  names  of  classified  employees  seeking
transfers

from

one

to

b.  General  Transfer  Lists  will  contain  the  names  of  qualified  employees
requesting:

i.  A  transfer  to  a  position  in  another  job  profile  having  a  pay  range
with

same

top

or

ii.  Reinstatement  following  voluntary  demotion  to  the  job  profile  from
which

demoted,

or

an

profile,

or

to

a

previously

job

or

iii.  Regular  employees  who  separated  from  County  service  in  good
standing

may

reinstatement

within

one

(1)

year

of

from

regular

County

to

the

same

or

a

previously

held

job

profile,

or

abolished,

an

equivalent

job

profile.

Employee

eligibility

is

one

(1)

year

of

5.  Unless  covered  by  the  provisions  of  a  collective  bargaining  agreement,  names  of

§  5-40-030  Duration  of  Eligible  Lists  or  Eligibility  Lists  may  be  of  fixed  duration  or  designated  as  open  until  filled.
A.  Recall  Lists.  The  term  of  eligibility  on  the  recall  list  is  eighteen  (18)  months  from  the  date
a

name

is

placed

on

the

recall

list

or

for

the

in

applicable

collective

bargaining

agreement.

B.  Class  Transfer  List.  Employees  will  submit  requests  to  be  on  the  transfer  list  in  the
manner

specified

by

Central

HR.

Names

will

remain

on

the

list

for

one

(1)

year.

Eligibility

may

be

renewed

annually.

C.  General  Transfer  List  (except  reinstatement  following  separation:  see  (D)  below).
Employees

will

submit

requests

to

be

on

the

transfer

list

in

the

manner

specified

by

Central

HR.

Names

will

remain

on

the

list

for

one

(1)

year.

Eligibility

may

be

renewed

annually.

D.  Reinstatement  Lists.  The  term  of  eligibility  to  remain  on  a  reinstatement  list  is  one  (1)
year

from

the

date

of

layoff

or

reassignment

to

Regular

the

Regular

from

County

service

good

standing

are

reinstatement

for

one

(1)

year

the

date

of

separation

and

may

not

E.  Internal,  External,  and  Open  until  Filled  Lists.  These  lists  are  established  by  job  profile.
The

department

will

The

The

(2)

§  5-40-040  Abolition  of  Lists  Lists  may  be  abolished  prior  to  their  normal  expiration  date  for  the  following  reasons:
A.  All  candidates  have  been  interviewed  and  found  not  acceptable  for  documented  job
related

reasons;

or

B.  The  list  was  illegally  constituted;  or
C.  The  list  is  comprised  of  fewer  than  five  (5)  candidates  for  each  vacancy;  or
D.  The  job  profile  for  which  the  list  was  established  has  been  abolished,  or  the  minimum
qualifications

for

the

job

profile

have

changed;

or

E.  Any  other  business-related  reason  approved  by  the  Multnomah  County  Chief  Human
Resources

Officer.

When  a  list  is  abolished,  all  remaining  candidates  will  be  notified  and  may  reapply  for  the  new
recruitment.

§  5-40-050  Certifications  And  Referral  of  Eligible  Names  A.  Upon  notice  that  a  position  in  the  classified  service  will  be  filled,  the  names  of  the
highest-ranking

eligible

candidates

from

for

job

will

the

Names

equal

to

or

position,

provided

demonstrated

for

the

position.

Candidates

from

active

will

considered

to

those

qualified

by

assessment.

B.  The  hiring  manager  may  require  that  only  candidates  with  specific  position  related
experience,

education,

or

ability

and

availability

for

the

location

The

from

than

three

(3),

who

and

and

for

of

the

will

certified

for

each

All

the

At

(5)

2.  When  candidates  are  self  certified  or  are  otherwise  without  score,  all  candidates
who

the

and

for

will

Departments

3.  When  the  number  of  eligible  candidates  on  a  non-scored  or  self-certified  list

(25),

a

(3)

4.  If  a  list  contains  fewer  than  five  (5)  certified  candidates,  the  entire  list  may  be

5.  Under  Oregon  Veterans  Preference  law  (ORS  408.225  to  237,  ORS  408.497),  all  veteran  applicants  who  meet  the  minimum  qualifications  for  a  position  must  be

C.  Subject  to  provisions  of  the  applicable  collective  bargaining  agreements,  names  of

for

§  5-40-060  Consideration  of  Candidates  Candidates  shall  be  considered  after  either  an  interview  or  review  of  all  application  material  as
required.

A.  Hiring  managers  are  responsible  to  partner  with  Department  Recruiters  to  implement
equity

based

interview

practices,

such

as

sending

the

interview

questions

ahead

of

time,

ensuring

a

diverse

panel,

etc.

B.  The  hiring  manager  is  responsible  for  contacting  candidates  for  interviews  and  may
further

assess

candidates

for

the

position.

When

a

certificate

of

eligibles

is

issued

in

order  from  the  highest  to  lowest  score,  candidates  for  interviews  will  be  selected  in  that
same

order,

unless

otherwise

required

by

law.

C.  When  an  issued  certificate  of  eligibles  contains  tied  scores,  all  candidates  with  that
score

will

be

considered

if

one

(1)

candidate

with

that

score

is

considered,

or

if

not

scored

or

self-certified,

all

candidates

will

be

interviewed

unless

there

are

over

twenty-five

(25)

candidates.

D.  If  names  are  certified  on  a  scored  list,  all  candidates  with  higher  scores  than  the  person
hired

must

be

considered.

If

names

are

certified

without

scores,

an

attempt

must

be made  to  contact  all  candidates  on  the  list  provided  to  the  hiring  manager.
E.  In  accordance  with  the  Gladys  McCoy  Standard  (Multnomah  County  Resolution  No.  2018-114),  when  a  competitive  recruitment  is  utilized  for  an  unclassified  Department  Director,  Deputy  Director,  or  other  senior  leadership  position  as  determined  by  the
Chair,

reasonable

efforts

will

be

made

to

interview

the

highest

qualified

candidates

of

color

and

underrepresented

communities

within

the

workforce.

F.  When  the  initial  attempt  to  contact  a  candidate  is  unsuccessful,  a  candidate  will  not  be
determined

to

be

unavailable

until:

1.  Written  notice  has  been  emailed  to  the  candidate  requesting  to  schedule  an
interview

appointment

within

five

(5)

calendar

days

of

the

email;

or

2.  Two  (2)  attempts  on  separate  days  have  been  made  to  contact  the  candidate
based

on

the

contact

information

listed

on

their

application,

including

but

not

limited

to

voice

mail

messages,

emails,

text

messages,

and/or

U.S.

mail,

requesting

to

schedule

an

appointment

within

three

(3)

business

days.

G.  When  the  hiring  manager  has  selected  the  final  candidates,  the  recruiter  will  draft  the
conditional

offer

of

employment

letter

in

the

system,

contingent

the

following:

1.  Check  references.  Compensation  decisions  for  initial  appointment  to  County
employment

will

not

or

current

or

Compensation

includes

wages,

salary,

benefits,

and

2.  Conduct  criminal  history  checks  as  may  be  required  for  the  position.
a.  Except  as  provided  in  §  5-40-060  (E)(2)(b)  below,  criminal  history  checks
can

only

be

conducted

after

an

initial

interview

or

after

a

conditional

offer

of

employment

is

made

if

is

no

interview

conducted.

b.  The  requirement  that  criminal  history  checks  come  after  an  initial  interview
or

conditional

offer

of

employment

does

not

apply

following

i.  If  federal,  state,  or  local  law,  including  corresponding  rules  and

criminal

history;

ii.  When  the  position  with  the  County  is  in  a  law  enforcement  agency
or

the

or

iii.  When  the  County  is  seeking  a  non-employee  volunteer.
3.  Conduct  other  background  investigations  as  may  be  required  for  the  position.
H.  It  is  generally  the  hiring  manager’s  responsibility  and  discretion  to  make  the  final  hiring
decision.

A

variety

factors

may

affect

the

hiring

decision

i.e.

panel

feedback,

Veterans

Preference,

reference

and

background

checks,

balance

of

skills

the

team,

rules

The

I.  After  the  hiring  manager  has  selected  the  finalist,  the  manager  will  order  drug  testing  if

for

§  5-40-070  Closing  the  Recruitment  Process  A.  Results  of  the  recruitment  must  be  entered  into  the  applicant  tracking  system  by  the
hiring

department.

B.  Candidates  interviewed  will  be  notified  of  the  results  of  the  interview  process  by  the
hiring

department.

C.  The  department  must  retain  recruitment  records  for  appointments  to  classified  and
unclassified

positions

for

three

(3)

Each

will

include

individual

applications,

tests

utilized,

scores

given,

a

certificate

of

eligibles

list,

interview

notes,

and

other

relevant

information.

§  5-40-080  Notification  of  Appointment  Status  A.  All  newly  appointed  employees  will  be  notified  in  writing  of  their  appointment  status  and
the

end

date

of

B.  Classified  employees  appointed  or  reclassified  to  an  unclassified  County  position  must

acknowledging
 notice  that  they  are  waiving  their  classified  status  as  required  in  MCC  §  9.120.
