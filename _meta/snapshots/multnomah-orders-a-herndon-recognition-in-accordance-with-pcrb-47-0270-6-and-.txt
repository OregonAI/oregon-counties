RESOLUTION NO.
Authorizing a Contract  Amendment to  Increase the Amount for Contract DCM -SVCSGEN-

The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The Board is acting as the Multnomah County Public Contract Review Board (PCRB)
to approve, pursuant to PCRB Rule 47 -0270 (6), a request from the Department of County
Management, Central Human Resources Division, to increase the amount  and extend the
period of service  for an existing contract with The Robbins Company, d/b/a Herndon
Recognition (DCM-SVCSGEN-1120-2016-conv).
b. The County originally awarded a contract with The Robbins Company, d/b/a Herndon
Recognition after a competitive solicitation process (RIP 4000004297)  for the Employee
Recognition Years of Service Awards . The period of the contract was from 04/01/2016
through 03/31/2021. The original contract amount was $150,000 in requirements funding.
c. In September 2020,  in accordance with PUR -9, sourcing of cooperative agreements
began. Several confo rming cooperatives were identified, and many attempts wer e made to
contract with cooperative suppliers. Only one responded and negotiations failed.
d. In March 2021, DCM formally requested Purchasing Manager approval to delay a new
procurement for the Employee Serv ice Awards due to difficulties locating  Cooperative
suppliers willing to contract wi th Multnomah County . DCM applied  under the provisions of
Special Procurement P100 – Contract Extension per Policy, which permits the extension of
existing contracts about to lapse when a procurement process is planned or co ntinuing but
not yet completed. Special Procurement P-100 contract extension #3462933  was approved
and extended the contract through September 30, 2021. No additional funding was allowed
under the existing procurement authority.
e. Subsequent to the initial request for contract extension, DCM determined the need for
additional funding requirements of $20,000,  increasing the contract value to $170,000,  and
another six months extension of the contract, from September 30, 2021 to March 31, 2022, to
allow time to complete the sourcing of Employee Recognition Years of Service Awards. This
request for additional funding and six additional months is beyond the Purchasing Manager’s
authority and is referred to the Board for consideration.
f. This request is in accordance with the requirements of Public Contract Review Board
Rule 47 -0270 (6 ) Intermediate Procurements – Amendments, which provides for Board
approval of cumulative amendments that increase the total Contract Price to a sum greater
than $150,000.

The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The request to increase the contract amount for Contract Number DCM -SVCSGEN-
1120-2016-conv with The Robbins Company d/b/a Herndon Recognition by an additional
$20,000, for a total contract value of $170,000  and extend the contract term for an additional
six months to March 31, 2022 is approved.
