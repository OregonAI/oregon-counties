        THE       BOARD            OF     COMMISSIONERS                   OF    YAMHILL        COUNTY,       OREGON       (the     Board),
on    the        17th       day          of    August,            1983,        sat    for     the     transaction         of   County
business,             in          special            session,        Commissioners                  Robin    J.    Hamblet,        Dave
Bishop,           and       Ted          Lopuszynski               being        present.

        WHEREAS,                  Section            6(4)     of    Ordinance           No.     62,       1974,    provides        that
an application  for an amendment to the Yamhill County Comprehensive
Plan Map shall  first be referred to the Yamhill County Planning Com-
mission and that the Planning Commission   shall recommend approval or
denial of such applications   to the Board of Commissioners, and

        WHEREAS,                  Ray,        Thomas         and    John        Garland        and     Roger      Smith    have        filed
an application with the Yamhill County Planning Department          to change
the Comprehensive    Plan Map relating to two parcels      comprising  a 27
acre tract;  to wit,    a change   in the Comprehensive   Plan Map on a 2.3
acre parcel  from "Very Low Density Residential"        to "Heavy Industrial",
and on a 24.7 acre parcel      from "Very Low Density Residential"     to "Light
Industrial";   and

     WHEREAS, on August 4th and August 11, 1983,  the Yamhill County
Planning Commission held a public hearing  relating to said request,
and voted to allow an amendment to the Yamhill County Comprehensive
Plan,         1974,         on      the        2.3       acre      parcel        to    allow        the    Map    designation           to    be
changed  from "Very Low Density Residential"  to "Heavy Industrial"   and
at the same hearing voted to deny any change    in designation on a 24.7
acre parcel,  based upon the Resolution  and Findings  attached hereto;
and

     WHEREAS,   on August    17,    1983,  the Yamhill County Board of Commis-
sioners held a public hearing           to consider the action of the Planning
Commission and voted to approve           a change in the Comprehensive Plan
Map designation      relating to the 2.3 acre parcel         from "Very Low Den-
sity Residential"      to “Heavy Industrial"        and further voted to continue
the matter of the change         in designation     relating to the 24.7 acre par-
cel for 120 days to allow the County Planning              Department  to complete
an inventory of available          industrial   lands within the Newberg area and
to reconsider     the change in the Plan Map designation           for the 24.7 acre
parcel,   in 120 days,     or when the inventory had been completed;

    NOW,           THEREFORE,  based upon and for the reasons  set forth                                                          in    the
attached           Resolution,  and the Findings attached thereto,

ORDINANCE               No.         352             Page      1
       IT     IS    HEREBY       ORDAINED        THAT   the    Comprehensive     Plan     Map    of
Yamhill County be and hereby is amended to change the Plan Map
designation  on the subject   2.3 acre parcel   from "Very Low Density
Residential"   to "Heavy Industrial",   consistent with and as set
forth in the attached Resolution     and Findings   of the Yamhill County
Planning Commission.

     This Ordinance being necessary    for the health,  safety and welfare
of the citizens   of Yamhill County,   and an emergency having been de-
clared to exist,    shall be effective  immediately upon passage  hereof.

       DONE        this    17th    day     of    August,      1983,   at   McMinnville,        Oregon.
