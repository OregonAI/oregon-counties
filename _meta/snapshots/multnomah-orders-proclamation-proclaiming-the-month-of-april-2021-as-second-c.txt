BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON  PROCLAMATION NO.
Proclaiming the Month of April, 2021 as "Second Chance Month" in Multnomah County,
Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Every person is endowed with human dignity and value.
b. Everyone deserves an opportunity to rebuild their lives, reach their fullest
potential and become a productive members of society.
c. Individuals with a criminal history often struggle to find housing, secure
employment, pursue higher education, regain voting rights, volunteer in their
community, and pay for significant debt that arises as a result of their conviction and
incarceration.
d. The Department of Community Justice provides services and information for
those individuals who are returning to their community through supervision,
programmatic activities and outreach.
e. 1,244 individuals were released from state custody in 2020, 800 required housing
assistance, 440 were identified as persons who could use mental health supports, 719
lived with a substance abuse disorder, and 164 have medical mobility issues.
f. The designation of April as Second Chance Month would contribute to increased
public awareness about the need for support and closure for those who have paid their
debt, and opportunities for individuals, employers, and communities to extend second
chances.
PROCLAMATION NO. 2021-020
Page 2 - Proclaiming the Month of April, 2021 as "Second Chance Month" in Multnomah
County, Oregon
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
The month of April 2021, is declared Second Chance Month in Multnomah County,
Oregon to honor re~entry efforts in our community that supports individuals making the
challenging transition back into society from incarceration.
Dated this  15th day of April, 2021.
