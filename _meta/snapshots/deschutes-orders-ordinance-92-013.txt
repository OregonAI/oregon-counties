Section 1. That Section 6. 04. 050( A), Dogs Kept Primarily in
Kennels, be amended to read as follows:
6. 04. 050 Dogs Kept Primarily in Kennels.
A. Owners of dogs kept primarily in a kennel and not
allowed to run at large are entitled, upon making the showing
required in this section, to be licensed at the kennel fee
rate. A person requesting licensing at the kennel rate shall
establish by affidavit or signed statement ( 1) if the kennel
is a commercial kennel, that the kennel complies with
applicable land use laws and ordinances; ( 2) that the person
houses his or her dogs primarily in a kennel, as defined
herein; ( 3) that the person has [ 5] 4 dogs or more; ( 4) that
the person has not been convicted of animal abuse under
county or state law for failure to maintain minimum care
standards [ in his or her kennel]; and ( 5 ) that the person has
not been convicted under [ state or] county or state law for
allowing his or her dogs to be at large during any period for
which he or she has had a kennel license.
Section 2. That section 6. 04. 055( A) be amended to read as
follows:
6. 04. 055 Kennel Licenses.
A) Kennel licenses shall be valid for a one year
period from the date of purchase.
Section 4. That Section 6. 04. 010( D), Dog Licenses, be added as
follows:
6. 04. 010 Definitions.
D. Commercial Kennels. " Commercial kennel" means any
premises where 4 or more dogs, at least 4 months of age, are
kept commercially for board, breeding, training or sale
