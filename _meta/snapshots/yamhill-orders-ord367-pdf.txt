Findings       of    Fact

 Ls   The  applicant  has                     requested                a plan  amendment/zone         change   on  a
      10,000  square foot                     tract  and              a 15,000  square    foot    tract   to amend
      the  Comprehensive                      Plan  Map               designation    from    "“AFLH"   to  “Public
      Facilities”           and        to     change            the        zoning         from     "EF-40"                to        “PWS”     (see
      maps   attached).    A conditional                                   use   permit          has     also  been   requested
      to   allow   extension   of a water                                    line   from          the     well   sites   to  the
      City.

      The   requested       plan   amendment/zone        change    is  necessary     to
      accommodate   two   proposed     new  well  sites  for  the City   of Lafayette.
      The  new wells    are   part  of   a HUD-funded   improvement   program  for  the
      City's        municipal          water            system.

      Lafayette          has  had   a history   of water    problems     associated                                                           with
      bacteria          and   seasonal    inadequacy    of   its   water    supply.                                                            The
      existing          water    supply   is  fed  from    springs    and   suffers                                                           from
      bacteriological                  contamination                  in        excess     of    EPA     standards.                       Earlier
      this   year,          a     grant            was        awarded             to     the     City          to         correct            these
      problems.

      Well   log   data    for  Lafayette      and   the  surrounding     area    indicate     that
      the aquifer       south   of  the    city    is the   best   source   of pure     water     in
      the  quantities        needed     for   the    City's   water    system.       Test   wells
      have    been    drilled       on    the    subject      sites    and    indicate        that
      sufficient      water     is  available         to meet    the  needs    of   the   City    of
      Lafayette's     water    system    without      creating    problems    of   drawdowns      on
      nearby   wells.

      The  two  test   wells     will     be  enlarged     from   their     existing     six-inch
      diameter    to    standards        necessary     to    meet     the     water     needs    of
      Lafayette.     Pumps    will    be   installed    in   the   wells.       The   pumps   will
      be  enclosed     in   a  fiberglass        structure,       and     the    well    will    be
      enclosed    with    cyclone        fencing.    The     fenced      area     will    measure
      approximately      50'x50'.

      The  proposed     water   main  will                            be        installed          along         County   right-of-
      way,  in    areas   zoned   "EF-40"                            and        "AF-20".          There          are  no  plans    to
      allow   any   hookups   outside   of                           the        Lafayette         Urban         Growth   Boundary.

      The     proposed   well   sites  are  currently                                     vacant         and          have           been     used
      for     pasture  and  wheat   production  in the                                    past.

      Soils     on the    subject              sites           are    Agricultural                Class             II,        Woodburn        and
      Amity     silt   loam.

      Surrounding      lands      are   generally       in agricultural          production.         The
      applicant     has     indicated      that    because      ground     water    supplies     in  the
      area    appear     to   be excellent,          and    because      the   use   of   the  subject
      sites    will    be    limited      to   the   wells      and    pumps    necessary      for   the
      water     system,       the     water     supply      for     surrounding         agricultural
      operations     and     residences       should      remain     adequate,      and    the  impacts
      of  the   proposal       on   surrounding       land   uses     should    be negligible.

Decision-Making                  Criteria

The  Planning    Commission's     decision    must    be  based   upon   the  goals   and
policies  of  the   Comprehensive     Plan,  upon    the  amendment    review   criteria
as  set  forth    in    Section   1207    of  the    Zoning    Ordinance,      upon   the
conditional   use    permit  review   criteria    as  set  forth   in  Section   1202   of
the    Zoning            Ordinance,               and            upon         the   criteria                 for         the     taking          of     an
exception           as     set    forth      in     Section                   19a   of     HB     2295.

Approval

The  following                  conclusionary                        findings       can     be     made            for       approval        of        the
application:

 1.    The      request            is     consistent                     with       the         goals            and         policies        of        the
       Comprehensive    Plan   as                                      noted   on  Pages                     2     through              4   of         the
       applicant's justification                                      information.

 2.    There  is a need   for   the  proposed     municipal   wells    in  that  the  City
       of  Lafayette    currently     has   both    quality   and   quantity    problems
       associated    with   its    existing    water    supply,    and    since   the  two
       proposed   wells    are    expected     to   alleviate     these    problems    and
       provide           Lafayette          with            an        adequate           supply        of        clean         water.

 3.    The proposed    amendment     to   accommodate    a new municipal        water    source
       for  Lafayette     is   timely    considering      that  the   City    of   Lafayette
       currently   has   problems     associated    with   the quantity     and    quality    of
       its  existing   water     supply    and  considering    that    the   City     recently
       received    grant     funding    to   allow   upgrading     of   its   water     supply
       system.

 4.    In  that     the    only     existing        "PWS"     zoned     lands      in  the  County    are
       devoted      to   existing        land    uses,      there     are    no    developabl  e    “PWS”
       zones   available         to   accommodate         the    proposed       use.    Further,    since
       the   proposed       wells      are    dependent         upon    an   adequate      groundwater
       supply     and    because      the   subject       sites    are     located    in an area     that
       has   been     found    to    have     a good      groundwater           supply,    the   subject
       sites   are     well   suited      for   the    proposed      plan     amendment/zone      change.

  5.   The  proposed     municipal                                    water    main   extension                            is  listed             as         a
       conditional   use   in: both                                  the  “EF-40"   and  "“AF-20"                        Districts.
