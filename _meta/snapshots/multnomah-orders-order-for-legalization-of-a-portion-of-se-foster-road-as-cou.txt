BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
ORDER NO. 2020-030
Legalization of a Portion of SE Foster Road, as County Road No. 5030.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. SE Foster Road was established as County Road No. 81 in 1874 and the location of the
road cannot be accurately determined due to numerous alterations of the road, a defective
survey of the road and adjacent property, and the road as traveled and used for at least the
past 10 years at this location, between the easterly boundary of the City of Portland and the
South boundary of Multnomah County, does not conform to the existing records for this
county road.
b. SE Foster Road has been traveled and used by the public and maintained by the County
for more than 25 years.
c. On February 7, 2019, consistent with ORS 368.201 to 368.221, the Board of
Commissioners adopted Resolution No. 2019-014 initiating proceedings for legalizing a
portion of SE Foster Road and directed the County Surveyor to conduct a survey of the
road in its traveled location.
d. On March 19, 2020, the County Surveyor filed his survey with the Board Clerk and the
County Engineer filed a report supporting legalization of SE Foster Road. Subsequently,
on March 19, 2020, the Board adopted Resolution No. 2020-018 Scheduling a Public
Hearing and Directing Notice Thereof for the Legalization of a Portion of SE Foster
Road, County Road No. 81.
e. County Transportation has provided notice of this May 14, 2020 proceeding in compliance
with ORS 368.206(1)(c).
f. The Board has determined that the legalization of the Road is in the public interest.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Orders:
1. As authorized under ORS 368.201 to 368.221, that the portion of SE Foster Road, County
Road No. 81, located in unincorporated Multnomah County, Oregon as more particularly
described in the attached Exhibit A  (“the Road”), is hereby legalized as a county road ,
specifically to be known as County Road No. 5030.
2. Further, as provided by ORS 368.216(2), the County Surveyor and County Transportation
staff shall comply with  all of the requirements of  ORS 368.106(1)-(4), to perfect the
legalization of the Road.
3. Upon the completion of the legalization procedures required under ORS 368.201 to 368.221,
any records showing the location of the Road that conflict with the location of the Road as
described in this Order are void, and the Road exists as shown on this Order.
ADOPTED this 14th day of May, 2020.
