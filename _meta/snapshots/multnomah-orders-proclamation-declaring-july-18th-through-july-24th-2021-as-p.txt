BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO.
Declaring July 18th through July 24th, 2021 as Pretrial, Probation and Parole Supervision
Week in Multnomah County, Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The Department of Community Justice (DCJ) staff possess a wide variety of skills and
experience that helps to meet the vision of the department, "Community Safety through
Positive Change;
b. DCJ staff treats justice involved adult and youth and their families with dignity, while
recognizing the right of the public to be safe-guarded from criminal activity;
c. DCJ staff supervises over 10,000 adult probationers and parolees annually,
processes over 24,000 cases in our Recognizance unit, receives over 2,200 youth
referrals, and serves approximately 875 youth and their families which includes
diversion, informal and formal supervision;
d. DCJ staff uses best practices when holding justice involved individuals accountable
as they deliver supervision, sanctions, and treatment resources to improve public
safety and address the factors that drive crime;
e. DCJ staff works in partnership with law enforcement and community agencies towards a
shared vision of a safer community;
f. DCJ staff respects victims’ rights and are dedicated to providing services and
protection for survivors;
g. DCJ staff advocates for the restoration of communities harmed by crime and
delinquent behavior; and
h. DCJ staff integrates trauma informed practices and brain science into our work and
applies an equity lens to inform our decisions; and
i. DCJ staff have continued to provide services to our justice involved individuals and
their families in the midst of the COVID-19 pandemic. This includes staff who have
kept 24/7 operations open (Detention, Assessment and Evaluation residential
program, and Recognizance unit). Other staff quickly pivoted to working remotely
and came up with creative ways to provide supervision and services to JIIs and
survivors.
2021-064
j. DCJ staff recognizes that their impact is long term, helping justice involved
individuals to change their behavior, restore their families and build stronger
communities.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
July 18th through July 24th, 2021 is declared PRETRIAL, PROBATION and PAROLE
SUPERVISION WEEK in Multnomah County, Oregon, in honor, recognition and respect for
the dedication and contributions of the County’s Community Justice staff.
ADOPTED this 22nd day of July, 2021.
