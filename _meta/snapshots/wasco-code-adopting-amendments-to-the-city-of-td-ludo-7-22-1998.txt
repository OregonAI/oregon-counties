         NOW ON THIS DAY, the above-entitled matter having come on regularly for

_ consideration, said day being one duly set in term for the transaction of public business and a

 majority of the Court being present; and

         IT APPEARING TO THE COURT:             That on October 23, 1997, an amended Joint

 Management Agreement between Wasco County and the City of The Dalles was filed with the

 Wasco County Clerk requiring both the City and County to adopt an amendment to the text or

 map of any of the City of The Dalles implementing ordinances which are applicable to the Urban

 Growth Area or Boundary and have been adopted by the County (Section 4.A.1.b.); and

         IT FURTHER APPEARING TO THE COURT:                 That on May 11, 1998, after conducting

 legally notified legislative public hearings (April 13" and May 1 1", 1998), the City Council of

 the City of The Dalles, Oregon, unanimously adopted (4-0) amendments to the City of The

 Dalles General Ordinance No. 98-1222, City of The Dalles Land Use and Development

 Ordinance; and

         IT FURTHER APPEARING TO THE COURT:                 That on May 20, 1998, the City of The

 Dalles submitted proposed City of The Dalles Land Use & Development Ordinance amendments
