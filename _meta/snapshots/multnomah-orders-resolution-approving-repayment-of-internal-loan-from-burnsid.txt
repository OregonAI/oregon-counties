BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO.  2020-056
Resolution approving repayment of internal loan from Burnside Bridge Fund to Risk
Management Fund in the amount of $3,000,000 for the Burnside Bridge Feasibility Study.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. As authorized by ORS 294.468 the Board of County Commissioners approved an
internal loan in the amount of $3 million from Risk Management Fund to Willamette Bridge
Fund for the Burnside Bridge Feasibility Study under resolution 2015-116.
b. In fiscal year 2 019 a new capital fund d edicated to Burnside Bridge project
expenditures and resources was established. All resources dedicated to Burnside Bridge
project were transferred from Willamette Bridge Fund to Burnside Bridge Fund.
c. The outstanding internal loan balance of $3 million will be fully paid in fiscal year 2020
from Burnside Bridge Fund to Risk Management Fund rather than Willamette Bridge Fund.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
The Board of County Commissioners approves internal loan repayment in fiscal year 2020
from Burnside Bridge Fund to Risk Management Fund. This resolution add s to Resolution
No. 2015-116.
ADOPTED this 25th day of June, 2020.
