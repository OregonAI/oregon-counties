        SECTION 4:        POWERS,        DUTIES AND FUNCTIONS OF CEOJJC

        CEOJJC    shall be empowered with all powers, rights, duties, including expenditure of appropriate

funds and retaining of personnel necessary for those functions in the coordinated delivery of the public

services set forth in Section 3 above and in the furtherance of the administration of CEOJJC and the

intergovernmental agreement. CEOJJC shall expressly have the power to:

        I.       Make, perform, and carry out contracts of every kind and description made for any

                 lawful purpose, with any person, firm, association or corporation, either public or

                 private, or with any government, or any agency thereof. All contracts are subject to the

                 Public Contract laws of the State of Oregon as set forth in ORS, Chapter 279.

        II.      Maintain financial records and books of CEOJJC in accordance with acceptable

                 accounting practices.

        Ill.     Manage and invest monies of CEOJJC in accordance with ORS 294.035.

        IV.      Hire employees, independent contractors and other staff to carry out the administration

                 and business activities of CEOJJC.

                 Assume annual indebtedness and liabilities not to exceed annual revenues.

        VI.      Dispose, divide and distribute any assets acquired by CEOJJC in accordance with by-

                 laws adopted by its Board of Directors or as authorized by the Board of Directors.

        Vil.     Enter into contracts to provide personal services.

        VIL.     Implement public services, plans, goals and objectives to the extent funds are appropriate

                 for such purposes.

        IX.      Approve new counties into CEOJJC.

        X.       Select   a CEOJJC Director.   Review and evaluate director performance.

        XI.      Adopt an annual budget to include funding allocation between counties, programs and
