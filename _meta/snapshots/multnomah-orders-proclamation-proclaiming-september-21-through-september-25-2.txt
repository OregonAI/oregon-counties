BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2020-072
Proclaiming September 21 through September 25, 2020 as Minority Enterprise Development
(MED) Week in Multnomah County, Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Multnomah County’s growth and prosperity depends on the full participation of all
residents at every level of our economy.
b. Each year, minority entrepreneurs return valuable resources to our community in the
form of taxes, wages and employment for thousands of workers.
c. Minority entrepreneurs are an indispensable force in our economy. More minorities own
businesses than ever before providing innovations, products and services across Multnomah
County.
d. Small businesses account for nearly 95% of all businesses in Multnomah County, many
of whom are minority-owned.
e. Minority entrepreneurs serve as leaders, mentors, teachers and models for our youth
who are business and civic leaders for tomorrow.
f. Recognizing the contributions that minority enterprises make to the social and economic
fabric of our County, we will continue to remove any barriers that prevent talented men and
women of every racial and ethnic background from participating fully in Multnomah County’s
economy.
g. Multnomah County celebrates the achievements and accomplishments of minority
business owners; we are delighted to pay tribute to them for their contributions on behalf of
Multnomah County’s economic growth.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
September 21 through September 25, 2020 as MINORITY ENTERPRISE DEVELOPMENT
WEEK IN MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON, to thank all minority business owners for their
contributions to the County and to show our continuing commitment to the promotion of
minority business opportunities.
ADOPTED this 10th day of September, 2020.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
