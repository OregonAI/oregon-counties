An Ordinance Amending Title 2. 08.020 of the
Deschutes County Code Authorizing County Clerk to * ORDINANCE NO. 2013- 011
Not Refund Overpayment for Recording Fee of
10. 00 or Less and Declaring an Emergency.
WHEREAS, the Deschutes County Finance Department estimates the direct cost to the County for
issing a refund check is between $ 2. 00 and $ 4. 00 and total cost, including overhead allocation, is approximately
between $ 10 and $20; and
WHEREAS, the County Clerk's Office sometimes receives payments for recording fees which exceed.
the amount of the recording fee; and
WHEREAS, the cost to the County of refunding overpayments sometimes exceeds the amount of the
overpayment; and
WHEREAS, current Deschutes County Code (" DCC") says that no refund for overpayment shall be
issued for any overpayment received by check of $10. 00 or less received by the County Clerk's office for
payment of recording fees; and
WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners finds that it is in the public interest to change
Deschutes County Code such that no overpayment of $10. 00 or less for recordings fees will be refunded
regardlessof the form in which the payment was made; now, therefore,
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY, OREGON, ORDAINS
as follows:
Section 1. AMENDMENT. DCC 2. 08.020 is amended to read as described in Exhibit " A," attached.
hereto and by this reference incorporated herein, with new languageunderlined and language to be deleted in
