BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY , OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2023-022
Resolution Consenting to Chair Vega Pederson's Appointment of Margi Bradway as the
Director of the Department of Community Services and Dan Field as the Director of the Joint
Office of Homeless Services.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3) provides that the Chair’s appointment of
department heads (directors) is consent of a majority of the board of commissioners.
b. The Chair appointed Margi Bradway as the Director for the Department of Community
Services with an expected start date beginning May 22, 2023.
c. The Chair appointed Dan Field as the Director for the Joint Office of Homeless
Services with an expected start date beginning April 28, 2023.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. In accordance with Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3), the Board consents to
the appointment of Margi Bradway as the Director of the Department of Community
Services.
2. In accordance with Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3), the Board consents to
the appointment of Dan Field as the Director of the Joint Office of Homeless Services.
ADOPTED this 20th day of April, 2023.
