BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2023-008
Acknowledging Receipt of the Multnomah County Annual Comprehensive  Financial Report and
Schedule of Expenditures of Federal Awards for Year Ended June 30, 2022.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The County’s audit for the year ending June 30, 2022  has been completed and fil ed
with the appropriate authorities within prescribed deadlines.
b. The County received an unqualified opinion on its financial statements from its external
audit
ors.
c. The audit identified one instance where a “Significant Deficiency in Internal Controls”
exists and management has resolved the issue. The one instance is in connection with
the audit of the County’s expenditures of Federal awards (Single Audit).
d. Ther
e were no significant deficiencies wit h the three districts  (Dunthorpe-Riverdale
Service District, Mid County Service District, and Library District)  that have their own
financial report (CUFR).
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The Annual Comprehensive Financial Report for the year ended June 30, 20 22 and the
related audit reports and recommendations are accepted.
2. The Board directs the Department of County Management to implement  the
re
commendations made by the County’s external auditors.
ADOPTED this 2nd day of February, 2023.
