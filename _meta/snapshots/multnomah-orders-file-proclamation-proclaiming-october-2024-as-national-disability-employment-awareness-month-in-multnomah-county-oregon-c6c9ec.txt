BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY , OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO.
Proclaiming November 4, 2024, as Election Hero Day in Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Election Hero Day is an opportunity to honor and show our appreciation for the
essential contributions our Election Workers make to our democracy and
electoral process here in Multnomah County.
B. This day also gives us an opportunity to bring awareness to the intimidation,
harassment, and attacks that have occurred towards Election Workers around
the country for the last few years. We acknowledge these attacks are harmful
and create undue stress and trauma for all Election Workers.
C. We acknowledge that our ability to provide fair, accessible, accurate, and
transparent elections depends on the service of these dedicated Election
Workers. They truly are upholding our democracy with their service to Multnomah
County voters.
D. T oday, we honor and affirm our commitment to providing a respectful and safe
workplace for our Election Workers, while also working alongside our partners
and other jurisdictions to ensure our workers will be protected from intimidation
and harassment while performing their essential Election administration duties.
2024-093
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
Monday, November 4, 2024, as Election Hero Day in Multnomah County and
encourages everyone to thank an Election Worker!
ADOPTED this 24th day of October, 2024.
