WHEREAS, on May 15, 1991, the Board of County Commissioners
adopted Ordinance No. 91- 016, amending the Deschutes County Code by
adding a new Section 15. 04. 055, prohibiting the installation of
certain solid fuel heating devices; and
WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners finds that the
provisions of the new Section 15. 04. 055 of the Deschutes County
Code are unduly restrictive and that additional study is necessary
in order to develop appropriate regulations; now, therefore,
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY ORDAINS
as follows:
Section 1. Section 15. 04. 055 of the Deschutes County Code is
hereby repealed.
Section 2. This Ordinance being necessary for the immediate
preservation of the public peace, health and safety, an emergency
is declared to exist, and this Ordinance takes effect on its
passage.
