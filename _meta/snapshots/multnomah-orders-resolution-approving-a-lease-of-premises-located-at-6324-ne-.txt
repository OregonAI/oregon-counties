BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO.  2021-059
Approving a Lease of Premises Located at 6324 NE Halsey Street, Portland, Oregon, for EOC
Office and Storage Space.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. A lease of premises within the property l ocated at 6324 NE Halsey Street , Portland,
Oregon (“Lease”), will provide necessary office and storage space for the Multnomah
County Emergency Operations Center ( EOC) as part of its COVID -19 r esponse.
Specifically, such lease will promote efficiency through consolidation of office and
storage operations throughout the County at one site and by moving all such operations
indoors.
b. Approval of the Lease in substantial conformance with the proposed terms is in the best
interests of Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The County Chair is authorized to execute a lease in substantial conformance with the
proposed Lease.
2. The County Chair is authorized to execute renewals of the Lease and execute
amendments to the lease without further Board action.
ADOPTED this 24th day of June, 2021.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Deborah Kafoury, Chair
SUBMITTED BY: Tracey Massey, Interim Director, Department of County Assets
