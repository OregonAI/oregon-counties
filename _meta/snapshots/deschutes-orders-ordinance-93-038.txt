WHEREAS, the City of Bend has previously adopted an amendment
to the City Zoning Ordinance to allow a 5 - acre minimum lot size for
planned unit developments, which was reviewed and approved by the
Bend Urban Area Planning Commission.
WHEREAS, Title 19 of the Deschutes County Code should be
consistent with the Bend Zoning Ordinance.
WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners has considered the
recommendation of the Planning Commission; now therefore,
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY, OREGON
ORDAINS AS FOLLOWS:
Section 1. Section 19. 104. 040 of Title 19 is amended as
follows:
Minimum Size for Planned Unit Developments. No application shall
be accepted for an area of less than [ 10] 5 acres in any R Zone, or
for an area of less than 4 acres in any other zone.
Section 2. EMERGENCY. This Ordinance being necessary for
the immediate preservation of the public peace, health and safety,
an emergency is declared to exist, and this Ordinance takes effect
on its enactment.
