        THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF YAMHILL COUNTY, OREGON (the Board)
sat for the transaction of county business in special session on July 3, 1996   Commissioners Robert
Johnstone, Thomas E. E. Bunn, and Dennis L. Goecks being present.

         WHEREAS,        Yamhill County has properties currently on its inventory of significant
historic landmarks, also known as the Yamhill County Cultural Resources Inventory, which were
so designated under former Ordinance 466, as amended.

         WHEREAS, 1995 Oregon Laws Chapter 693, and Yamhill County Ordinance 598, (both
effective September 9, 1995) require the county to allow a property owner to remove a historic
property designation that was imposed on their property by the local government;

        WHEREAS,        on March 15, 1996 the County received an application from Delford M.
Smith ("Applicant"), on behalf of the Estate of Michael K. Smith, requesting that the property
known as Tax Lot No. 4301-00100 (Landmark Identification No. 111, known as Gordon House
and Barn at 2280 Fulquartz Ln, Dundee, OR 97115) as more particularly described on Exhibit
"A" attached hereto and incorporated herein by this reference, be removed from the Yamhill
County Cultural Resources Inventory on the Yamhill County Comprehensive Plan and Plan Map;

        WHEREAS,        on June 5, 1996 the Yamhill County Landmarks Commission held a public
hearing to consider the removal of the property at which testimony and evidence was received, and
that following the close of the hearing and the record, the Landmarks Commission deliberated and
voted 3-1 to recommend to the Board of Commissioners that the Board adopt an ordinance to
remove the historic property designation from the property based upon the findings contained in
the staff report;

        WHEREAS, on June 19, 1996, the Board held a public hearing on the proposed removal
of the historic property designations for the listed properties, at which testimony and evidence was
received, and that following the close of the hearing and the record, the Board deliberated and
voted 2-0 (Commissioner Bunn excused) to tentatively approve the application for removal of the

A.     BACKGROUND FACTS

       The request involves a property owner of a designated historic landmark who has
requested the property to be removed from the Yamhill County Cultural Resources Inventory.

B.     ORDINANCE CRITERIA AND ANALYSIS

1.      The Yamhill County Historic and Archeological Preservation Ordinance No. 598,
Sections 100.02 and 100.05, lists the following criteria for removal of a designated landmark.
The Commission shall consider the following criteria in determining whether to approve a
removal request:

       Section 100.02

        "Provisions of this Chapter shall apply to significant historic landmarks and
       archaeologically, sensitive areas designated in the adopted inventory.

2.      The Board finds that the subject site is a significant historic landmark.The Board finds that
the applicant has indicated his desire to remove the property from historical landmark designation
based on the Owners Consent provision Section 100.05(D)(3) of the ordinance. The applicant
states that he did not consent to be included on the Yamhill County Cultural Resources Inventory.

CONCLUSION:

1.      The property owner requested the subject property to be removed from the Yamhill County
Cultural Resources Inventory.

2.       The property owner did not give his consent for the property to be included on the Yamhill
County Cultural Resources Inventory.

3.       1995 Oregon Laws Chapter 693 provided for this process and the application satisfies the
criteria set forth in the statute and the ordinance.

DECISION::

        The Board approves removal of the subject site from the Yamhill County Cultural
Resources Inventory based on the owner consent provision of Section 100.05(D)(3) of the Yamhill
County Historic and Archeological Preservation Ordinance No. 598.
