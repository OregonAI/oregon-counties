BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
ORDINANCE NO. 1333
Amending MCC Chapter 22 to Change Name of the Joint Office of Homeless Services
and MCC Chapter 3.
(Language *stricken is deleted; underlined language is new.)
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The Joint Office of Homeless Services was created as a Non-Departmental unit
on June 23, 2016, when the Board of County Commissioners amended
Multnomah County Code Chapter 25.
b. County Ordinance 1295, read and adopted on May 27, 2021, established the
Joint Office of Homeless Services as a department of Multnomah County via an
amendment of County Code Chapter 25 to create Chapter 22 for the inclusion of
the Joint Office of Homeless Services.
c. A “Homelessness Response System Intergovernmental Agreement” between
Multnomah County, a political subdivision of the state of Oregon, and the City of
Portland, a municipal corporation of the state of Oregon, was approved by the
Portland City Council on July 3, 2024 and by the Multnomah County Board of
Commissioners on July 11, 2024. This inter-governmental agreement states that,
by December 31, 2024, the County must put forward for approval by the
Multnomah County Board of Commissioners an ordinance to change the JOHS
name in a manner that removes the word “Joint” from the name.
Multnomah County Ordains as Follows:
Section 1. MCC Chapter 22 is amended as follows:
CHAPTER 22: JOINT OFFICE OF HOMELESS SERVICES HOMELESS SERVICES
DEPARTMENT
Joint Office Of Homeless Services Homeless Services Department
§ 22.100 - OFFICE DEPARTMENT ESTABLISHED.
A Joint Office of Homeless Services Homeless Services Department is established.
The Department Office shall be responsible to procure and administer contracts for
direct services to single adults, families, youth and victims of domestic violence in need
of housing support.  The Department Office shall lead community-based planning and
oversight and serve as the Lead Agency for the local Continuum of Care.
§ 22.110 - DUTIES.
(A) Administer all aspects of the Joint Office of Homeless Services Homeless
Services Department;
(B) Advise the Board of County Commissioners and the Portland City Council
on matters of concern to the Homeless Services Department Joint Office;
and
(C) Manage the Homeless Services Department Joint Office staff, budget and
compliance.
Section 2. The County Attorney is authorized to revise any code sections and all
written contracts, ordinances, resolutions, Board orders, Chair executive rules, bonds
and other legal documents to change the name of the department from the Joint Office
of Homeless Services to the Homeless Services Department.
