BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2020-033
Proclaiming May 2020 as Asian and Pacific Islander Heritage Month in Multnomah
County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Asians and Pacific Islanders are among the fastest growing communities in
Oregon and Multnomah County.
B. Asians and Pacific Islanders have lived and worked in Oregon for more than 200
years, contributing to the state’s rich history, economy and culture. This migration has
been both voluntary and forced due to war and environmental degradation in their
native countries.
C. Oregon’s Asian and Pacific Islander history is both heroic and harrowing as the
community has worked to thrive despite harassment and discrimination, both of which
are on the rise today. Today this discrimination is at a high with a national and local
spike in hate crimes against the community, especially targeting its Muslim, South
Asian, and Southeast Asian and Chinese members.
D. Despite the significant contributions of Asians and Pacific Islanders, communities
have been subject to historic and contemporary trauma.  Multnomah County is
committed to learning from and working together with community partners in order to
eliminate disparities by removing impediments to success through programs to increase
social inclusion, reduce poverty, ensure language access, healthcare, and support
meaningful civic engagement.
E. Multnomah County welcomes and supports Asian and Pacific Islander
Oregonians as vital members of its community. The county actively works with the API
community to increase understanding and honoring how cultural values can be
integrated in multiple systems to better serve the API community.
F. The month of May 2020 is nationally recognized as a time to celebrate the
contributions of Asian and Pacific Islanders to our society and collective history.
G. The goal of this proclamation is to continue joint efforts to eliminate barriers to
the success and safety of the thriving Asian and Pacific Islander Community in
Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
The month of May 2020 as Asian and Pacific Islander Heritage Month celebrating all
residents of Multnomah County who identify as Asian and/or Pacific Islanders.
ADOPTED this 28th day of May, 2020.
