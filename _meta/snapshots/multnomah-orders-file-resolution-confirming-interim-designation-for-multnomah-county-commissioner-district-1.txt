BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2022-027
Confirming Interim Designation for Multnomah County Commissioner, District 1.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Multnomah County Charter Section 4.50(3) and Multnomah County Code 5.003
require that elected officials designate an interim occupant to serve until a vacancy is filled.
b. Charter Section 4.10 states the qualifications for County officers and Code Section
5.003(B)(4) requires that the designee meet the Charter qualifications for appointees of such
offices.
c. Sharon Meieran, Multnomah County Commissioner, District 1, designates Cynthia
Castro as interim occupant of that office in the event of a vacancy.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
The Board confirms Cynthia Castro to serve as interim occupant for the Multnomah County
Commissioner District 1 in the event of a vacancy in that office.
ADOPTED this 14th day of April, 2022.
SUBMITTED BY: Sharon Meieran, County Commissioner, District 1
