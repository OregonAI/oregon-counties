ORDINANCE NO. 86- 025
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES
OREGON, ORDAINS as follows:
COUNTY,
Section 1. Section 3 of Ordinance No. 203. 12, " Right to
Review Hearing", is amended as follows:
03. Right to Review Hearing. Nothing in this ordi-
nance prevents a review of denial of a building permit
by a due process hearing pursuant to Ordinance No. 82-
011. The burden of proof shall be that the water
supply system will satisfy state standards for purity
and/ or adequacy ( depending on the ground for denial),
even if the building permit is issued."
Section 2. Paragraph 3 of Section 4 of Ordinance No.
203. 12, " Mandatory Investigation", is amended as follows:
The hearing may be conducted in accordance with the
hearing procedure set forth in Ordinance No. 82- 011, or
such other procedure as the Board may announce before-
hand."
Section 3. This Ordinance being necessary for the immediate
preservation of the public peace, health and safety, an emergency
is declared to exist, and this Ordinance takes effect on its
passage.
