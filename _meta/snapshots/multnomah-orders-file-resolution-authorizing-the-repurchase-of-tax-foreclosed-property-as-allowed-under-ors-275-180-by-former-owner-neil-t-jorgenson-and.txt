BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-013
Authorizing the Repurchase of Tax Foreclosed Property as Allowed under ORS 275.180 by Former Owner Neil
T Jorgenson and Execution of Deed and Closing Documents
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Neil T Jorgenson is the former owner of certain real property (R188360) more particularly described in
the proposed deed to (“Former Owner”), attached and identified as Exhibit A (“Property”).
b. On or about October 11, 2021, judgment was entered in Multnomah County Circuit Court foreclosing the
delinquent taxes levied against the Property.
c. On October 17, 2023, the County Tax Collector deeded all right, title and interest in the Propert y to
Multnomah County as authorized under ORS 312.200.
d. Former Owner made a request to the County, in compliance with MCC Section 7.402, to repurchase
the Property for $80,744.21 (“Repurchase Price”), an amount consistent with ORS 275.180 and MCC
Subsection 7.402(B).  The County has received payment in the amount of the Repurchase Price.
e. It is in the best public interest that the Propert y be sold to the Former Owner as described above i n
Paragraph d).
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The County Chair is authorized to execute  the deed and related escrow closing documents conveying
the Property to Neil T Jorgenson.
