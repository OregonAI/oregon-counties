CODIFIED ORDINANCES OF JACKSON COUNTY

PART SIXTEEN – FIRE PREVENTION CODE

CHAPTER 1610
Fire Districts

EDITOR
'S NOTE:  The Board of County Commissioners approves the boundaries of the
various fire protection districts in  the County for taxation purposes. The districts adopt
fire prevention codes and enforce the same within their respective jurisdictions. The
adoption of such codes is approved by the Board pursuant to State law. Copies of all
relevant legislation, including descriptions of the districts and the fire prevention codes
adopted by the districts, may be obtained, at cost, at the office of the County Counsel.

There are no sections in Chapter 1610. This chapter has been established to provide a
place for cross references and any future legislation.
