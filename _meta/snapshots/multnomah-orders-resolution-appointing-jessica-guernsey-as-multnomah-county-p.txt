BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2020-090
Appointing Jessica Guernsey as Multnomah County Public Health Administrator.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. ORS 431.418 provides that the local public health authority s hall appoint a qualified local
public health administrator.
b. The Board of County Commissioners is the Governing Body of the Local Public  Health
Authority with authority to appoint the Public Health Administrator. ORS 431.003(7).
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. In accordance with ORS 431.418, the Board of County Commissioners appoints Jessica
Guernsey as the Multnomah County Public Health Administrator, effective October 19, 2020.
2. In accordance with ORS 431.418, the Board has considered standards for the selection of
the Multnomah County Public Health Administrator as prescribed by the Oregon Health Au thority.
These standard s include: (a) a  bachelor’s degree; and  (b) p ublic health work experience and
education that demonstrates competencies in the foundational programs as defined by ORS
431.141 and foundational capabilities as defined by ORS 431.131.
3. This action is following procedures as requir ed by OAR 333 -014-0520(2). Upon
appointment of Jessica Guernsey  as the Public Health Administrator, the Board of County
Commissioners, through the Health Department Director, will provide notice of the appointment to
the Oregon Health Authority along with a copy of the administrator’s resume or curriculum vitae.
ADOPTED this 15th day of October, 2020.
