HOMELESS SERVICES DEPARTMENT

§ 22.100 DEPARTMENT
ESTABLISHED.

A Homeless Services Department is established.
The Department shall be responsible to procure
and administer contracts for direct services to
single adults, families, youth and victims of
domestic violence in need of housing support.
The Department shall lead community-based
planning and oversight and serve as the Lead
Agency for the local Continuum of Care.  The
Director of the Office is appointed by the Chair
and reports directly to the Chair.

§ 22.110  DUTIES.
The Director will:
(A) Administer all aspects of the Homeless
Services Department;

(B) Advise the Board of County
Commissioners on matters of concern to
the Homeless Services Department;

(C) Manage the Homeless Services
Department staff, budget and
compliance; and

(D) Convene the Policy & Budget
Alignment Committee (PBAC)
established by Intergovernmental
Agreement with the City of Portland.
