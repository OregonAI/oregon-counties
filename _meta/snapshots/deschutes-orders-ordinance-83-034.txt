ORDINANCE NO. 83- 034
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY,
OREGON, ORDAINS as follows:
Section 1. No person shall operate a restaurant, temporary
restaurant or limited service restaurant without a current license
to do so from the Health Division of the Department of Human
Resources of the State of Oregon.
Section 2. No person shall operate or maintain a public
swimming pool, bath, spa, wading pool or bathhouse without a cur-
rent license to do so from the Health Division of the Department
of Human Resources of the State of Oregon.
Section 3. No person shall establish, operate, maintain, or
manage a travelers' accomodation or recreation park without secur-
ing a current certificate of sanitation.
Section 4. Violation of any provision of this Ordinance is a
Class B infraction.
Section 5. This Ordinance being necessary for the immediate
preservation of public peace, health and safety, an emergency is
declared to exist, and this Ordinance takes effect on its passage.
