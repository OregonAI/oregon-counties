 14.205  SUBDIVISIONS.

 The County Surveyor shall collect a fee pr escribed by the Board of Commissioners for
services provided under ORS 92.100. [Ord. 253, Sec. 7; amended by Ord. 89-5, Sec. 1]

 14.210  SURVEY RECORDING FEE.

 (1) A fee shall be charged and collected in  advance by the Polk County Surveyor for the
filing and indexing of a map of a survey. The amount of the fee shall be prescribed by the Board of
Commissioners. All monies collected under this s ection shall be deposited with the Polk County
Treasurer at least once a month to be credited to the General Fund.

 (2) The purpose of this fee is to recover the costs incurred by Polk  County for the filing
and indexing of maps of a survey. [Ord. 86-2A, Secs. 2 and 3; amended by Ord. 89-5, Sec. 2]

 14.215  PUBLIC LAND CORNER PRESERVATION FUND.

 A Public Land Corner Preservation Fund is creat ed. Monies in the fund shall be used only to
pay expenses incurred and authorized by the County Surveyor in the establishment, re-establishment
and maintenance of corners of government surveys under ORS 209.070(5) and (6). [Ord. 87-17, Sec.
1]

 14.220  RECORDING FEE.

 A fee in an amount prescribed by the Board of Commissioners is established for recording
all instruments under ORS 205.130(2) in addition to any other fee charged by the County Clerk. All
monies collected under this section shall be deposited with the County Treasurer at least once a
month to be credited to the Public Land Corner Preservation Fund. [Ord. 87-17, Sec. 1; amended by
Ord. 89-5, Sec. 3]
