 CHAPTER 10 1993

 COUNTY OFFICERS
 (Generally)

 10.050  COMPENSATION AND EXPENSES.

 (1)   Except as otherwise governed by Stat e law, the members of all County boards,
commissions, and committees shall serve without compensation or reimbursement of their expenses.

 (2)   This section does not apply to a County officer or employee who serves as a
member of a board, committee, or commission, with respect to the compensation or reimbursement
that officer or employee receives other than by virtue of such membership.

 (3)   This section does not prevent reim bursement of a member of a board, commission,
or committee for travel outside Polk County on offici al business, if such travel is authorized in
advance by the board, committee, or commission and by the Board of Commissioners. [Ord. 88-7,
Sec. 1]

 (Board of Commissioners)

 10.105  SALARY ADJUSTMENTS.

 (1)   County Commissioners during their current  term of office shall be limited to only
those across-the-board salary adjustments extended to all other non-represented employees.

 (2)  The salary limitation set forth in th is section specifically includes recommended
adjustments resulting from salary surveys or any other actions.

 (3)   This section cannot be repealed w ithout a Public Hearing, notice of which having
been published in a newspaper of general circulation within the County, not less than ten (10) days
in advance of said hearing. [Ord. 86-8]

 (County Surveyor)

 10.205  SELECTION.

 The mode of selection of the Polk County Su rveyor shall be by appointment of the Polk
County Board of Commissioners. [Ord. 85-9, Sec. 1]

Note:  Chapter 756, Oregon Laws 1985, allowed counties to change the mode of selecting the
County Surveyor upon approval of the elector ate. Ordinance No. 85-9, abolishing the
elective office and adopting Section 10.205, was approved by Polk County voters at the May
1986 Primary Election, effective January 6, 1987.

 (Board of Equalization)

 10.305  COMPENSATION AND EXPENSES.

 (1)   Pursuant to ORS 309.022(2), the Polk  County Board of Equalization shall be
entitled to a per diem allowance as follows:

  (a) For one-half day service or less, $15.

  (b) For one-half day service or more, up to and including one full day, $30.

 (2)  Polk County Board of Equalization members shall be entitled to reimbursement for
expenses as provided in the Polk County Travel Regulations. [Ord. 85-10, Secs. 1 and 2]

 (Emergency Services)

 10.910  DEFINITIONS. As used in Sections 10.910 to 10.930:

 (1)   "Board" means the Polk County Board of Commissioners.

 (2)   "Emergency," "emergency program management," "emergency program manager,"
and "emergency services" have the meanings defined by ORS 401.025. [Ord. 91-3, Sec. 2]

 10.920  EMERGENCY PROGRAM MANAGER.

 (1)   The Board of Commissioners shall appoint an Emergency Program Manager who,
subject to supervision by the Board under ORS 401.305 and the authority of the Governor under
ORS 401.115, shall exercise the functions of emer gency program management in coordinating
emergency services in Polk County during an emergency.

 (2)   The Emergency Program Manager shall serve at the pleasure of the Board and
without additional compensation.

 (3) In appointing the Emergency Program Ma nager, the Board in accordance with ORS
401.035 shall designate another person or persons to  serve as such if the Emergency Program
Manager is unavailable during an emergency. [Ord. 91-3, Sec. 3]

 10.930  POWERS OF BOARD DURING EMERGENCY.

 (1)   During the existence of an emergenc y, the Board may exercise all powers conferred
on it by ORS 401.305 to 401.335. If a quorum of the Bo ard is unavailable, any one member may
exercise such powers on behalf of the Board during the existence of the emergency, until a quorum
becomes available.  If no member of the Board is available, the Emergency Program Manager may

exercise such powers on behalf of the Board during the existence of the emergency, until at least one
member of the Board becomes available.

 (2)  If an elective official or head of a county agency is unavailable during an emergency,
the Board, or sole member available, may designate another person to carry out the duties of that
official or agency head during the existence of the emergency, until the official or agency head
becomes available or a successor is chosen in the regular manner. If no member of the Board is
available, the Emergency Program Manager may make such designation.

 (3)   To the extent permissible under th e Oregon Constitution and statutes, all acts
performed under this section shall be deemed valid as if performed in the regular manner.  [Ord. 91-
3, Sec. 4]
