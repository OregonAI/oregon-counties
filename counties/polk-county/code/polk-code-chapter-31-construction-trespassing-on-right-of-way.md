 CHAPTER 31     2011

 CONSTRUCTION TRESPASSING ON RIGHT-OF-WAY

 31.110  INSPECTION FEE /  PLAN REVIEW.

 Pursuant to ORS 374.310, every application to the County Public Works  Department for a
permit  to construct or maintain any facility within a publ ic right-of-way in Polk County shall be
accompanied by a fee in an amount prescribed by the Board of Commissioners. [Ord. 162, Sec. 1;
amended by Ord. 258, Sec. 1; Ord. 89-5, Sec. 5; Ord 99-4]

 31.120  NON-PERMITTED ACTIONS IN RIGHT-OF-WAY.

 Any person who places material, facilities or obst ructions in the public right-of-way without
first obtaining a permit from the County Public Wor ks Department is subject to the penalties
provided for in this Chapter.

31.130  PENALTIES.

 Violation of Sections 31.110 through 31.120 shall be punishable upon conviction by a fine
of not more than $500 for a non-continuing violatio n and a fine of not more than $1,000 for a
continuing offense.  These penalties shall be in addition to any other remedies provided by law to
Polk County.
[Ord. 11-07]
