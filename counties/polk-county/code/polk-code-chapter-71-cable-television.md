CHAPTER 71  1994

 CABLE TELEVISION

 71.105 FINDINGS.

 The Polk County Board of Commissioners he reby adopts the attached Exhibit A, which
constitutes and shall be known as the Polk County Cable Communications Franchise Ordinance. All
earlier versions of Chapter 71 are repealed. [Ord. 94-14, Sec. 1.]

 71.110 DOCUMENTATION.

 The Polk County Cable Communications Franchis e Ordinance, referred to as Exhibit A, has
not been set out in full because of its great length. The original copy referred to has been recorded in
the County Clerk's Office. [Ord. 87-9; Ord. 94-03, repealed by Ord. 94-14, Sec. 1.]
