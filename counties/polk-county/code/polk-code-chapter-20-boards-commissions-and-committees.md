 CHAPTER 20     1993

 GENERAL PROVISIONS FOR BOARDS, COMMISSIONS, AND COMMITTEES

 20.010  APPLICABILITY.

 This section shall apply to all advisory boards, commissions, and committees that are
appointed solely by the Polk County Board of  Commissioners or for which Polk County is
legally responsible, unless to do so would cause Count y to be in violation of state or federal law.
In the event that the County has adopted, by ordi nance, a rule of conduct for a specific County
advisory board, commission or committee that conf licts with a provision of Polk County Code
Sections 20.010 - 20.050, the rule relating to speci fic committee shall prevail over the general
provisions of Polk County Code Sections 20.010 - 20.050.

 20.015  APPOINTMENTS.

 (1)  The Board of Commissioners shall select and appoint members of County
advisory boards, commissions, and committees. The Board may establish procedures for member
selection and appointment to supplement the provisions of this code section.

 (2)  Applications for appointment to County boards, commissions, and committees
shall be filed with the Board of Commissione rs. The Board of Commissioners may limit the
times when it will accept applications. The Board shall review applications submitted and may
refer applications to advisory boards, commissions, and committees for screening and
recommendations.

 (3)  All persons appointed to serve on County advisory boards, commissions and
committees shall serve on such advisory boards,  commissions, and committees at the pleasure of
the Board of Commissioners. Appointments shall be  made for such terms as designated by the
Board of Commissioners, subject to removal by the Board of Commissioners, without cause, at
any time. Whenever practicable, the Board of Commissioners shall provide the member being
removed from an advisory board, commission or committee position with an opportunity to
address the Board of Commissioners concerning their removal prior to removal from the
advisory board, commission or committee.

 20.020  CHAIRPERSONS.

 (1)  Except as otherwise provided by federal or state law, the Board of Commissioners
shall designate as chairperson one member of each County advisory board, commission and
committee, the members of which are appoint ed by the Board of Commissioners. Advisory
boards, commissions and committees may make recommendations to the Board of
Commissioners concerning the selection of a ch airperson and said recommendation shall be
considered by the Board of Commissioners prior to selection of a chairperson.

 (2)  Chairpersons are eligible for redesigna tion at the expiration of their terms. If a
chair becomes vacant, the Board of Commissione rs shall appoint another person to fill the
unexpired term.

 (3)  Nothing in this section prevents an advisory board, commission, or committee
from selecting other officers as necessary to expedite its proceedings.

 20.025  SCHEDULE FOR DESIGNATION OF CHAIRPERSONS.

 The Board of Commissioners shall designate  a chair for each County advisory board,
commission or committee to serve for a term beginning when the Board of Commissioners
makes its designation at a regular meeting a nd ending when the successor is appointed and
qualified.

 20.030  BYLAWS.

 (1) Bylaws may be drafted, reviewed, and recommended to the Board of
Commissioners by any advisory board, commission, or committee. Bylaws may also be prepared
by the Board of Commissioners.

 (2)  The Board of Commissioners shall have  the sole authority to adopt, amend, or
repeal bylaws for County advisory boards, commissions, and committees. Bylaws shall be
adopted by an order of the Board of Commissioners.

 (3)  Bylaws shall govern the conduct of  the County advisory board, commission, or
committee but shall not, in any way, bind the Board of County Commissioners. Bylaws may
contain more stringent requirements or procedur es than those specified in Polk County Code
Section 20.010 - 20.050.

 20.035  FUNCTIONS.

 The functions and activities of a County a dvisory board, commission, or committee shall
be strictly limited to those functions delegate d to the advisory board, commission, or committee
by the Board of County Commissioners. An a dvisory board, commission, or committee that
disregards this limitation may be dissolved or dissolved and reorganized by the Board of
Commissioners. A person appointed to serve on a County advisory board, commission or
committee who fails to obey this restriction may be removed from membership on the County
advisory board, commission or committee by the Board of Commissioners.

 20.040  PUBLIC MEETINGS AND RECORDS LAW.

 All County advisory boards, commissions, committees and subcommittees shall conduct
their business in public, in full compliance with  the Oregon Public Meetings Law, currently
codified as ORS 192.610 through 192.710. All minutes and records of said advisory boards,
commissions and committees are public records whic h must be made available to the public as
required by the Oregon Public Records Law,  currently codified as ORS 192.410 through
192.595. All members shall also comply with all St ate laws relating to conflicts of interest,
financial interest, and ethics laws.

 20.045  QUORUMS.

 A quorum is required before a County advisory board, commission, or committee or any
of its members may discuss or decide any matte r relating to the advisory board, commission, or
committee's business. A quorum shall be a majority  of the persons then appointed by the County
to serve on the advisory board, commission, or committee, excluding vacant positions. All
actions or recommendations of an advisory board, commission or committee must be approved
by a majority of the members of the entire a dvisory board, commission or committee appointed

 20.050  ABSENCES.

 (1)  Except as otherwise provided by federa l or state law, any member of a County
advisory board, commission or committee appointed by the Board of Commissioners who fails to
attend two consecutive meetings or three meetings during any period of one year or less, whether
regular, adjourned or special, shall forfeit memb ership unless the member is prevented from
attending by the serious illness of the member or the family of the member, or for any other
cause that, in the judgment of the Board of Commissioners, constitutes a valid reason for failing
to attend.

 (2)  The chairperson of each County a dvisory board, commission, or committee shall
promptly report to the Board of Commissioners the name of any member failing to attend two
consecutive meetings or three meetings during any period of one year or less. This information
may also be reported to the Board of Commissioners by County staff members or committee
members. Nothing contained in this subsection prevents the County from acting to remove a
member if no report of absences has been made to the Board.

 (3) Before the Board of Commissioners decides that a position has been vacated by a
member for failing to attend meetings, the Board of Commissioners shall give reasonable
opportunity to the member to justify the absences.

 (4) Upon declaring a position vacant as provided in this section, the Board of
Commissioners shall appoint a successor to serve the unexpired portion of the term for that
position as soon as practicable.
