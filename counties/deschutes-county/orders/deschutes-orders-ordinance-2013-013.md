WHEREAS, Deschutes County Code (" DCC") 12. 08.010 specifies the name and form for a permit to
authorize work in a public right- of-way; and
WHEREAS, in order to provide timely and efficient service to the Deschutes County Road
Department's customers and to allow for flexibility to change the form in response to any change in regulations
without having to amend the DCC each time, the above DCC provision should be changed to a general
description of the permit form; and
WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners (" Board") find it in the public interest to allow such
flexibility; and
WHEREAS, Board desires to have a general effective date for Deschutes County ordinances similar to
the 30-day effective date provided for city ordinances; now, therefore,
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUN'T'Y, OREGON, ORDAINS
as follows:

hereto and by this reference incorporated herein, with new language underlined and language to be deleted in
st~ gh.
