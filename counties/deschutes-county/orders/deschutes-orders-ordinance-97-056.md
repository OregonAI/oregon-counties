1. 01. 095. Existing Liabilities.
The repeal, express or implied, of any
ordinance, ordinance provision, code section, or
any map or line on a map by any ordinance shall
not release or extinguish any duty, condition,
penalty, forfeiture, or liability incurred under
such ordinance, unless a provision of the
ordinance shall so expressly provide, and such
ordinance repealed shall be treated as still
remaining in force for the purpose of sustaining
any proper action or prosecution for the
enforcement of such duty, condition, penalty,
forfeiture, or liability, for the purpose of
authorizing the prosecution, conviction and
punishment of the person or persons who violated
the repealed ordinance. ( Ord. 97- 056 § 2, 1997)
