BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY, OREGON
An Ordinance Amending Deschutes County Code
Chapter 1. 16, Code Violations and Enforcement,
to Allow Peace Officers to Issue Citations for
Violations of Deschutes County Code Chapter
6. 08, Animal Control Where There are
Reasonable Grounds to Issue a Citation.
ORDINANCE NO. 2009- 005
WHEREAS, Deschutes County Code Chapter 1. 16 adopted the violation procedures as set forth in ORS
153. 030 to 153.145; and
WHEREAS, ORS 153. 042 states an enforcement officer may only issue a violation citation if the
conduct alleged to constitute a violation takes place in the presence of the enforcement officer and the
enforcement officer has reasonable grounds to believe that the conduct constitutes a violation unless as
otherwise specifically provided by law; and
WHEREAS, there have been numerous incidents where a person has declined to sign a citation for an
animal control violation as provided for under Deschutes County Code 1. 16. 070 or been unable to sign due to
incapacity and no citation has been issued even though sufficient reasonable grounds existed to issue a citation;
and
WHEREAS, there are public safety circumstances where an animal control citation should be issued
even though a person such as a neighbor or minor cannot or will not sign a citation, now, therefore,
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY, OREGON, HEREBY
ORDAINS as follows:
