ORDINANCE NO. 87- 005
WHEREAS, ORS 161. 015 defines " peace officer" to mean a
sheriff, constable, marshall, municipal policemen, member of the
Oregon State Police or investigator of the Criminal Justice
Division of the Department of Justice and such other persons as
may be designated by law; and
WHEREAS, ORS 153. 110 authorizes all peace officers and
persons specifically authorized under law of any political sub-
division of the State to enforce infractions pursuant to ORS
153. 110 to 153. 310 and 153. 990; and
WHEREAS, it is necessary that the Board of County Commis-
sioners authorize persons to enforce infractions of County Ordi-
nances; now, therefore,
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY,
OREGON, ORDAINS as follows:
Section 1. That the Board of County Commissioners, by Order
shall designate individuals, in addition to peace officer, who
are employes or agents of the County, to enforce infractions of
County Ordinances.
Section 2. That the appointment of a County Officer to
enforce infractions of County Ordinances shall be continuous
until such appointment is revoked by the Board of County Commis-
sioners.
Section 3. This Ordinance being necessary for the immediate
preservation of the public peace, health and safety, an emergency
is declared to exist, and this Ordinance takes effect on its
passage.
