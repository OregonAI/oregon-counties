his application denied under this Section on the

shall prohibit the County from taking enforcement
action against any dealer found to have failed to
comply with this or any other applicable County
ordinances."
Section 3. This Ordinance being necessary for the immediate
preservation of public peace, health and safety, an emergency is
declared to exist, and this Ordinance takes effect on its
passage.
