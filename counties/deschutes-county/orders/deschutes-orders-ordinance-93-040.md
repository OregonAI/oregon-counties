ORDINANCE NO. 93- 040
WHEREAS, Ordinance 93- 18- 1 amended Title 19 to require a
redevelopment plan for partitions in the SR 2. 5 zone;
WHEREAS, Planning Department staff advised the Board of County
Commissioners that the redevelopment plan was not included in the
amendments to the SR 2. 5 zone adopted by Ordinance 93- 018- 1 and the
requirement for a redevelopment plan was included in Title 19. 020. 55
by clerical error;
WHEREAS, the Urban Area Planning Commission recommended that a
redevelopment plan for the SR 2. 5 was not necessary for the
maintenance of future development options;
WHEREAS, after notice was given and hearing conducted in
accordance with applicable law, the Board of County Commissioners;
now, therefore,
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY, OREGON
ORDAINS as follows:
Section 1. Subsection ( B)( 1)( c), of Section 19. 20. 055, " Land
Divisions," of Title 19 is deleted.
Section 2. This Ordinance being necessary for the immediate
preservation of the public peace, health and safety, an emergency is
declared to exist, and this Ordinance takes effect on its enactment.
