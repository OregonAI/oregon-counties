8 Section I. PURPOSE
9 The purpose of this ordinance is to authorize the
10 Board of County Commissioners to perform the duties of a local
11 contract review board in lieu of permitting the Public Contract
12 Review Board to perform the functions of reviewing public contracts
13 as required by Oregon Laws 1975, Chapter 771.
14 Section II. CREATION OF LOCAL CONTRACT REVIEW BOARD
15 The Deschutes County Board of Commissioners is hereby
16 designated a local contract review board to perform the functions
17 of the Public Contract Review Board.
