as follows:
Section 1. Section 19. 16. 030 Deschutes County Code is amended by the
addition of subsection ( G) which reads:
G. Low intensity recreational uses in SM zones within the Inner
Urban Growth Boundary, ( IUGB), such as BMX bicycle tracks,
ballfields, and parks and open space, subject to the criteria
set forth in Section 19. 100. 030, of this Title and the
following:
1. The surface mining shall have been completed on the area
to be utilized for recreational purposes and the site
reclaimed prior to or in conjunction with the proposed
recreational use. These pre - conditions shall not apply
where no resource exists on the area to be used.
2. A showing that with respect to the proposed recreational
use, the adjacent mining operation, as currently
operated and as may forseeably be operated in the
future, will be able to operate without violating DEQ
