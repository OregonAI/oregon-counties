THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY,
OREGON, ORDAINS as follows:
Section 1. Section 6. 040, Exceptions to Yard Requirements,
Deschutes County Zoning Ordinance of 1979, is amended by the
addition of subsection 5, which reads as follows:
5) Structures on parcels created prior to November 1,
1979, may be granted an exception to the 100 foot
setback from the high water line or mark along a stream
or lake subject to Ordinance 82- 011 and the following
criteria:
A) An application shall be filed which includes:
1) A detailed explanation of the planned
development.
2) An explanation of why an exception is
necessary.
3) A site plan, drawn to scale, and accom-
panied by such drawings, sketches and
descriptions necessary to describe and
illustrate the proposed development.
The site plan shall, at a minimum,
include:
a. An inventory of existing vegeta-
tion, including trees, on the lot
located within 200 feet of the high
water line or mark along the stream
or lake. The inventory shall be in
sufficient detail to allow the re-
view and evaluation of the impacts
of the proposed development.
