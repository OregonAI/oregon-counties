EXHIBIT " C"
FINDINGS OF FACT IN SUPPORT OF LEGISLATIVE AMENDMENTS TO
ORDINANCE NO. PL- 17, SISTERS URBAN GROWTH BOUNDARY ZONING ORDINANCE
The amendments to the Sisters Urban Growth Boundary Zoning Ordinance,
Ordinance No. PL- 17, bring the Zoning Ordinance into compliance
with State Land Use Goals 2 and 5, as set out below.
1. The Zoning Ordinance is in compliance with Goal 2, Land Use
Planning, because all areas within the Urban Growth Boundary
are now zoned, and amendments to the Zoning Map distinguish
between UAR-22 and UAR-10 Zones.
2. The Zoning Ordinance is in compliance with Goal 10, Housing,
because the amendments assure that needed mobile homes and
multiple- family dwellings are subject only to clear and objective
standards in the conditional use and site design review process.
