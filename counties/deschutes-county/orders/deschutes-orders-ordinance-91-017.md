THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY, OREGON,
ORDAINS as follows:
Section 1. Section 5. 095( 1)( A)( f), Class A Manufactured
Homes, is amended to read as follows:
f. Have roofing materials of a type customarily used on
site - constructed residences, including wood shakes or
shingles, asphalt or fiberglass shingles, corrugated mat
finish colored metal and tile materials, but not including
high - gloss corrugated aluminum or fiberglass panels. [ Have
shake, shingle, composition or tile roofing materials.] The
roof pitch shall be a minimum of 2 1/ 2."
Section 2. Section 5. 095( 1)( B)( f), Class B Manufactured Home,
is amended to read as follows:
f. Have roofing materials of a type customarily used
on site - constructed residences, including wood shakes or
shingles, asphalt or fiberglass shingles, corrugated mat
finish colored metal and tile materials, but not
including high - gloss corrugated aluminum or fiberglass
panels. [ Have shake, shingle, composition or tile
roofing materials.] The roof pitch shall be a minimum
of 2 1/ 2."
Section 3. Section 5. 095( 1)( C)( a), Class C Manufactured Home,
is amended to read as follows:
a. Have at least five hundred and seventy- six ( 576) square
feet of occupied space, excluding tipouts and hitches; [ Have
more than three hundred and twenty ( 320) square feet of
