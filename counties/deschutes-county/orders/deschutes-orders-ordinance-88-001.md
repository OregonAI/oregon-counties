ORDINANCE NO. 88- 001
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY,
OREGON, ORDAINS as follows:
Section 1. Deschutes County Code Section 4. 12. 060 is
amended to read as follows:
Emergency fees may be adopted administratively by the
director of administrative services or the board of
county commissioners for a period not to exceed one
year. When a service is requested and no fee has been
adopted by the board of county commissioners, the
director of administrative services, on behalf of the
board of county commissioners, shall declare a fee
which shall be based upon the work to be performed and
cost of providing the service. Any fee charged by the
county which is set by or pursuant to any state legis-
lative act may be amended administratively by the
director of administrative services upon amendment of
such fee by the state legislature."
Section 2. This Ordinance being necessary for the immediate
preservation of the public peace, health and safety, an emergency
is declared to exist, and this Ordinance takes effect on its
passage.
