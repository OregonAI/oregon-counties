ORDINANCE NO. 82- 044
WHEREAS, the Bend Urban Area Planning Commission has recom-
mended an amendment to Section 26( 4) of Ordinance No. PL- 11,
Zoning Ordinance of the Bend Urban Area, Deschutes County, Oregon;
and
WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners of Deschutes
County, Oregon, held a hearing on October 20, 1982, for the pur-
pose of hearing testimony on whether or not the standards and
criteria recommended by the Bend Urban Area Planning Commission
should be adopted; and
WHEREAS, it appearing that the amendment will provide for the
location of churches, hospitals, and other religious institutions
on collector and local streets in addition to arterials; now,
therefore,
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY,
OREGON, ORDERS as follows:
Section 1. That Section 26( 4) of Ordinance No. PL- 11, Zoning
Ordinance of the Bend Urban Growth Boundary of Deschutes County,
Oregon, be amended as follows:
Churches, Hospitals, or other Religious Institutions.
In any Residential Zone, churches, hospitals, or other
religious institutions may be located on an arterial
or collector street. Such uses may also be located on
a local street, provided that there is sufficient
access to arterial or collector streets, and that such
uses do not unduly impact residential areas. Access
and required offstreet parking shall be designed so as
to minimize impact on existing traffic patterns and
adjoining properties. All buildings shall be set back
