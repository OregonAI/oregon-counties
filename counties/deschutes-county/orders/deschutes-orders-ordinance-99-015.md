authorized.
The Finance Director is authorized to allow
negative budget fund balances under the
following circumstances without a board
resolution authorizing an inter -fund loan:

fund balance are anticipated, but not yet
received;
B. The expenditures creating the negative
fund balance do not exceed the
appropriations in the approved budget;
C. The negative fund balance is expected to
be cleared at or prior to the end of fiscal
year.
