FINDINGS
I. APPLICABLE CRITERIA:
Title 22, Deschutes County Development Procedures Ordinance
II. BASIC FINDINGS:
PROPOSAL:  Text amendmentto DeschutesCountyCode (DCC) section 18.110.020 adding
Black Butte Ranch to the title.  Currently, the title indicatesthis sectiononly identifiesSeventh
Mountain/ Widgi Creek as a Resort District.    While accurate it is incomplete by not also
identifying Black Butte Ranch.
