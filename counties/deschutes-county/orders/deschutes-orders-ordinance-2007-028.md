EXHIBIT A
1. 16.070. Code Enforcement Officials - Designation by Board.
A The Board, by order, shall designate individuals, in addition to peace officers, who are
employees or agents of the county, to enforce violations of county ordinances. Private
individuals who commence actions under subsection B of this section are not agents or
employees of the county.
B. A private person may commence an action for a noise offense under DCC 8. 08, or an
animal control violation under DCC 6. 08 by eeffi6,iffg- signing to- the complaint before a
magistrate, clerk, deputy clerk of the court or sheriff's deputy. This action will be entered
in the court record.
C. If an ORS 153. 045 uniform violation citation form is not used then tea complaint under
DCC 1. 16.070( B) shall contain a statement that the complainant shall certify, under the
penalties provided in DCC 1. 16.090, that the complainant has reasonable grounds to
believe, and does believe, that the person cited committed the offense contrary to law. A
certificate conforming to DCC 1. 16.090 shall be deemed equivalent to a sworn
complaint.
D. When the complaint is certified by a private person, the court shall cause the summons to
be delivered to the defendant. The court may require the County Sheriff's Office to serve
the summons as provided in ORS 153. 535(1).
