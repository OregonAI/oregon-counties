Rule 660- 004 ( excerpt below).
660-004-0000(2) An exceptionis a decisionto excludecertain land from the requirementsof
oneor moreapplicablestatewidegoalsin accordancewiththeprocessspecifiedin Goal2,
Part II, Exceptions. The documentationfor an exceptionmust be set forth in a local
government's comprehensiveplan. Suchdocumentationmustsupporta conclusionthat the
standardsfor an exceptionhave beenmet.
