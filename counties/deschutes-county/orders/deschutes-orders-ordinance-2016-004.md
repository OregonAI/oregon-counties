BACKGROUND AND POLICY IMPLICATIONS:
This is a housekeepingamendment.  Specifically, the Environmental Soils Division of CDD has
ascertainedthat reference languageand fee setting languagein DCC 13.08 are not current with Oregon
DEQ rules and allowances. This amendment corrects that omission.
FISCAL IMPLICATIONS:
New fee language will allow County to recover actual costs.

Move adoption and signature of Ordinance 2016- 004, an Ordinance Amending Deschutes County
