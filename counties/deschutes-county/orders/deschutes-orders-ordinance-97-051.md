4. 01. 010. Finance department.
Effective July 1, 1997, a permanent Finance
Department is established for Deschutes County.
Ord. 97- 0514 1, 1997)
4. 01. 020. Purpose.
The purpose of the finance department is to
provide budget direction and coordination, audit
coordination, accounts payable and payroll
processing, administration of generally accepted
accounting principles and practices, fixed asset
direction and maintenance, internal controls
monitoring, solid waste financial assurance
monitoring, bond and certificates of participation
coordination, financial reporting, tax collection
and administration, transient room tax
administration, dog licensing administration and
requested financial analysis and research for
Deschutes County pursuant to ORS 203. 035 and
ORS Chapter 294. ( Ord. 97- 051 & 1, 1997)
4. 01. 030. Director of finance department.
The director of the finance department is
subject to the same employment rules and
conditions as other appointed department heads.
Ord. 97- 051 & 1, 1997)
4. 01. 040. Powers and duties.
The finance department director will serve as
the Tax Collector if so appointed under ORS
311. 055 by the Board of Commissioners.
The director who serves as both the finance
department director and tax collector will be
responsible for maintaining an effective and
