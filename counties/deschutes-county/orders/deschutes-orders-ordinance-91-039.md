WHEREAS, when the Deschutes County Code provisions creating
the Historical Landmarks Commission and relating to the
designation, alteration and demolition of historical landmarks were
adopted, no specific provisions were included relating to
violations and penalties; and
WHEREAS, where no specific penalty is provided for violations
of county code provisions, violations are classified as " Class B"
infractions, which are punishable by a maximum fine of one hundred
dollars ($ 100); and
WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners finds that
historical preservation furthers important community interests and
values, that alternation or demolition of historical landmarks in
violation of the provisions of the Deschutes County Code represent
significant damage to the public interest, and that such violations
should be punishable as " Class A" infractions, which are punishable
by a maximum fine of five hundred dollars ($ 500); now, therefore,
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF
DESCHUTES COUNTY, OREGON, as follows:
Section 1. That Chapter 2. 28 of the Deschutes County Code,
Historical Preservation and Historical Landmarks Commission, is
amended to add a new Section 2. 28. 120, as follows:
2. 28. 120 Violation - Infraction.
