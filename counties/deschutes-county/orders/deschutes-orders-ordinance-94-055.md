WHEREAS, prior to July 1, 1994, Deschutes County had
insufficient general fund revenue in the 1993- 94 fiscal year to
operate general fund supported operations on a full- time basis and
operated under shortened working hours; and
WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners accordingly enacted
Ordinance 93- 055 for the operation of general fund departments on
7. 5 hours, five - days - a - week basis; and
WHEREAS, sufficient funds are now available for the operation
of general fund departments on an 8 - hour day, five - days - a - week
basis; now therefore
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF DESCHUTES COUNTY, OREGON
ORDAINS as follows:
Section 1. That Deschutes County Code Section 2. 12. 010 is
amended to read as set forth in Exhibit " A," attached hereto and by
this reference incorporated herein, with additions in bold and
deletions bracketed.
Section 2. That this Ordinance being necessary for the
immediate preservation of public peace, health and safety, an
emergency is declared to exist, and this Ordinance takes effect on
passage.

2. 12. 010 Regular business days.
The regular business days for the county
shall be Monday through Friday of each week
from 8: 00 a. m. to [ 4: 30] 5:00 p.m. each

county department different from the regular
business days and hours described in section
