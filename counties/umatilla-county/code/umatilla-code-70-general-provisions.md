ROAD SIGNS
§ 70.01  EVALUATING NEED FOR SIGNS;
PLACEMENT.
(A) The County Public Works Department will
evaluate the need for warning signs or signs imposing
restrictions on the use of county roads and make a
recommendation to the County Board of
Commissioners.
(B) Permanent signs governing the use and
restrictions on use of county roads will be placed in
the county road right-of-way by order of the County
Board of Commissioners.
(C) Upon the request of the family of a victim of
an impaired driving crash and when certain
requirements are met, a sign can be installed on a
County Road at the site of a fatality caused by an
impaired driver.
(Ord. 94-12, passed 7-27- 94; Ord. 2009-11, passed
12-16-2009)
§ 70.02  CONFORMANCE TO MANUAL ON
UNIFORM TRAFFIC CONTROL DEVICES.
Placement of traffic control signs and other signs
restricting the use of county roads will conform to the
Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices, published
by the Federal Highway Administration, as amended
and supplemented by the Oregon State Department of
Transportation.  Only when these authorities permit
variance or deviation by local government, will
additional signs or other control measures be
considered by the Department of Public Works.
(Ord. 94-12, passed 7-27-94)
§ 70.03  REQUEST FOR INSTALLATION OF
SIGNS; INSTALLATION.
A member of the public may request that the
Director of Public Works install additional signs along
county roads in areas where the requester believes
additional signs are necessary.  The request will
contain a written justification of why the requester
believes the sign is necessary and a diagram of the
area where it is proposed the sign will be placed.  If
the requested sign is approved by the Board of
Commissioners, the requester will be required to
install and maintain the sign according to county
specifications, or pay the county to install and
maintain the sign.
(Ord. 94-12, passed 7-27-94)
§ 70.04  ENFORCEMENT BY SHERIFF'S
DEPARTMENT.
The provisions of this chapter and the use and
restrictions on use of county roads required by signs
erected by the County Public Works Department will
be enforced by the County Sheriff's Department under
the provisions of ORS 203.065.
(Ord. 94-12, passed 7-27-94)
