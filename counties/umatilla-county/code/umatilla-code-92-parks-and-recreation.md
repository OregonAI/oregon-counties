GENERAL PROVISIONS
§ 92.01  DEFINITIONS.
For the purpose of this chapter, the following
definitions shall apply unless the context clearly
indicates or requires a different meaning.
PARK AREA.   Includes any county park,
wayside, monument of scenic, historic or recreational
area under the jurisdiction of the Board of
Commissioners.
PARK MANAGER.  An individual in charge of
a county park area.
PARK SUPERVISOR.   County Public Works
Director in charge of the county parks and recreation
branch of the county.
(Ord. 78-4, passed 6-7-78)
§ 92.02  UNLAWFUL TO ENTER PARK WHEN
CLOSED.
No person, other than law enforcement officers or
authorized county personnel, shall enter or remain in
any park area between the daily closing time and the
daily opening time as established by the County Park
Supervisor and posted at the entrance to each park
area.
(Ord. 78-4, passed 6-7-78)
§ 92.03  PUBLIC DISTURBANCES
PROHIBITED; USE OF METAL DETECTORS.
The following activities are prohibited in any park
area:
(A) Setting up or using a public address system
in a park area or other designated facilities stated in
this chapter, without permission of the Park Manager.
(B) Operating or using any noise-producing
machine, vehicle, device or instrument in a manner
that is disturbing to other park area visitors.
(C) Participating in public demonstrations,
disturbances, or riotous behavior which interferes with
the reasonable use of the park or recreation area by the
general public.
(D) Using a metal detector within a park area
without the written permission of the Park Manager.
(Ord. 78-4, passed 6-7-78)
§ 92.04  LOST ARTICLES.
All articles found in park areas and other
designated facilities stated in this chapter, shall be
turned over to a Park Manager.  At the end of one
year, the article will be returned to the finder if it has
not been claimed.
9-36
(Ord. 78-4, passed 6-7-78)
§ 92.05  CAMPFIRES.
(A) Fires in park areas shall be confined to:
(1) Park camp stoves or fireplaces provided
for such purpose;
(2) Portable stoves in established park
picnic areas.
(B) No fire shall be left unattended and every
fire shall be extinguished before its user leaves the
park area.
(Ord. 78-4, passed 6-7-78)
§ 92.06  HUNTING AND FIREARMS.
No person shall:
(A) Hunt, pursue, trap, kill, injure or molest any
wildlife or disturb their habitats.
(B) Discharge any firearm, pellet gun, bow and
arrow, slingshot, or other weapon capable of injuring
any person or wildlife.
(C) Possess any loaded firearm in any park area.
(Ord. 78-4, passed 6-7-78)
§ 92.07  OPERATION OF MOTOR VEHICLES.
(A) Motor vehicles shall be operated only on
roads and in parking areas constructed or designated
for motor vehicle use.  No motor vehicle shall be
operated on any trail or in any park area not
constructed or designated for motor vehicle use, or on
any road or trail posted as closed to the public.
(B) Automobiles, trailers, or other vehicles shall
be parked only in designated parking areas. The
parking area shall be clearly designated in each park
and the Park Manager may post a map showing desig-
nated parking areas.
(C) No person shall operate any vehicle within
a park area at a speed in excess of 10 miles per hour
unless the roadway is posted otherwise.
(D) Two-wheel motorized vehicles are not
allowed in county parks.
(Ord. 78-4, passed 6-7-78)
§ 92.08  OVERNIGHT CAMPING.
Overnight camping is allowed only in designated
camping areas after payment of the applicable
camping fee.
(Ord. 78-4, passed 6-7-78)
§ 92.09  PETS IN PARK AREA.
(A) Any dog, cat or other animal of any kind
brought into or kept in a park area shall be confined in
a vehicle, or on a leash or not more than six feet long,
and shall be kept under control at all times.
(B) The owner is responsible for the animal's
behavior and containment at all times while in the
park.
(C) The authority of the County Park Manager
includes the authority to require any person keeping
the animal to undertake such measures (including the
removal of the animal from the park area) deemed by
the Park Manager to be necessary to prevent inter-
ference by the animal with the safety, comfort and
well-being of the park area.
(D) No animals, other than seeing-eye dogs or
other handicapped assistance dogs, shall be allowed in
any building.
(E) No person shall ride, drive, lead or keep a
saddle horse or any other animal in any park area,
except on such roads, trails or areas designated for that
purpose.
(F) No horse or other animal shall be hitched to
any tree or shrub in such a manner that may cause
damage to such tree or shrub.
(Ord. 78-4, passed 6-7-78)
§ 92.10  ERECTING SIGNS AND MARKERS.
9-37
No person shall, except in designated areas, erect
signs, markers, or inscriptions of any type within a
park area without permission from the Manager.
(Ord. 78-4, passed 6-7-78)
§ 92.11  SALE OF GOODS; ADVERTISING;
DISTRIBUTION OF MATERIALS.
The following activities are prohibited in a park
area unless specifically authorized by the County
Parks Manager:
(A) Operating a concession, either fixed or
mobile.
(B) Soliciting, selling, or offering for sale,
peddling, hawking or vending any goods, wares,
merchandise, food, liquids, or services.
(C) Advertising any goods or services by any
means whatsoever.
(Ord. 78-4, passed 6-7-78)
§ 92.12  VANDALISM AND LITTER.
(A) Flowers, shrubs, foliage, trees, plant life, or
natural products of any type shall not be picked, cut,
mutilated, or removed from any park area without
written permission from the Park Manager.
(B) No person shall mutilate, deface, damage, or
remove any table, bench, building, sign, marker,
monument, fence, barrier, fountain, faucet, traffic
recorder, or other structure or facility of any kind in a
park area.
(C) No person shall dig up, deface or remove
any dirt, stones, rock or other substance whatever,
make any excavation, quarry any stone, or lay or set
off any blast, or roll any stones or other objects, or
cause or assist in doing any of these things within a
park area.
(D) No bottles, cans, ashes, waste, paper,
garbage, sewage, or refuse shall be left in a park area,
except in the receptacles designated for that purpose.
(E) No person shall wash any clothing or other
materials, or clean any fish in a lake, stream or river in
a park area.
(Ord. 78-4, passed 6-7-78)
§ 92.13  GROUP PICNIC FEES.
(A) At designated camping locations and in the
two group picnic area, a user fee will be charged in
order to offset additional park administration and
maintenance costs. These fees are specified in the
Umatilla County Comprehensive Fee Schedule in
Chapter 36 of this code. Full payment for a reserved
group picnic area must accompany the group picnic
application. Campers must pay for the full term of
their stay within 30 minutes after checking into the
campground.
(B) Not less than five working days (Monday
through Friday) prior to the scheduled picnic, the
group leader must make a rrangements with the Park
Manager regarding parking, supervision, cleanup,
checkout time, and other arrangements.
(C) Upon arrival, the group leader will check in
with the Park Manager and the Manager will direct the
group to the reserved area.
(D) Notification of cancellation must be received
by the park office issuing the reservation at least three
working days (Monday through Friday) prior to the
permit date to receive a refund.  Three dollars shall be
deducted from any refund for county administra-tive
costs.
(E) Applicants must be 18 years of age or older.
The group must have adult supervision at all times.
(Ord. 78-4, passed 6-7-78)
ADMINISTRATION AND ENFORCEMENT
§ 92.25  PARK MANAGER
AUTHORIZED TO ADOPT RULES AND
REGULATIONS.
The County Park Manager is delegated the
authority to adopt rules and regulations governing the
following activities in county park areas:
9-38
(A) Hang gliding activities;
(B) The use of alcoholic beverages;
(C) The regulation of animals in county parks.
(Ord. 78-4, passed 6-7-78)
§ 92.26  PARK MANAGER TO ENFORCE
RULES AND REGULATIONS; EXPULSION
FROM PARK.
The County Park Manager in any park area is
authorized to obtain compliance of the public with the
park rules and regulations and to order any person
violating these regulations to leave the park area.
(Ord. 78-4, passed 6-7-78)
§ 92.27  EMERGENCY RULES.
The County Park Manager may establish special
rules and regulations on an emergency basis for 60
days when it is necessary to protect park visitors, the
resources, environment of the park area, or park
facilities.
(Ord. 78-4, passed 6-7-78)
