ADMINISTRATIVE POLICY 604 - Nepotism
Nepotism Disclosure Form

7/1/19

An employee in a close personal or financial relationship with another employee as outlined in Administrative
Policy 604, must inform their department head or human resources by completing this disclosure form.

The disclosure form must be completed within five (5) business days of the beginning of a close personal
relationship or the change in the reporting relationship. Personal relationships that are in the same chain of
command must be included.
