OBJECTIVE:                               To establish a procedure for issuance and security of keys or keycards to
                                         access Marion County court facilities.

REFERENCE:                               Policy #515

POLICY STATEMENT:                        All persons requiring entrance to Marion County court facilities will
                                         undergo security screening by qualified personnel.

APPLICABILITY:                           All persons requiring entrance to Marion County court facilities.

PROCEDURES:

l.           Definitions:

             1.1 Department Access Coordinator (DAC): An employee within a county department assigned by
                 the department head or elected official to coordinate key and keycard access for that
                 department

             1.2 Keycard: An electronic key that is programmed to provide entry into specific doors and/or
                 buildings at specific times

             General Screening:

             2.1 Screening of persons are normally accomplished using metal detectors, x-ray, hand-held metal
                 detectors, and visual inspection.

             2.2 Screening personnel are responsible for following all safety and security screening procedures
                 identified by the courts, Judicial Security Unit.

             2.3 Persons who refuse to be screened will be denied passage beyond the screening point. Those
                 with medical issues, such as a pacemaker, that would prevent them from going through the
                 metal detector, have the option of a pat down and wand search conducted on their person.

             Responsibilities:

             2.1 Keycard holders are responsible for becoming familiar with the Marion County Court
                 Facilities Security policy and procedures.

     2.2 Marion County Business Services Department is responsible for maintenance and installation
         of all locks, card readers, and owned and leased by Marion County.

     2.3 Keys and keycards are the exclusive property of Marion County. Under no circumstances
         shall any key or keycard be duplicated by anyone other than Business Services personnel.
         Marion County reserves the right to run access card usage logs to ensure compliance with
         Marion County Court Facilities Security policy and procedures.

           Marion County reserves the right to deactivate keycards at any time without notice.                                    The
           holder of a deactivated card shall promptly return it to Marion County Business Services.

     2.4 Keycard holders shall immediately notify Business Services if a keycard is misplaced, lost, or
           stolen. Failure to immediately report a lost or stolen keycard may result in disciplinary action,
           including loss of access privileges.

     2.5 Individuals shall only use or hold keycards that are officially assigned to them. No person
         shall allow the use of a keycard to give access to a person who does not have authority to be in
           a controlled space. Keycards may not be loaned or reassigned except through Marion County
           Business Services.

     2.6 Business Services will maintain records of keycards issued to members of the bar.

     2.7 Business Services shall be notified immediately of any changes to employment status or
         business arrangement, including suspension, termination, or reassignments that affects
           building access.

3.    Keycard Access:

     3.1 Access: All requests must be submitted in writing, via the keycard access request form (515-
         B), from the department head, elected official, or DAC to Business Services. Ifa request is
           denied by Business Services, the decision may be appealed to the Court Security Committee.
           Appeals from the Court Security Committee shall be referred to the Sheriff, Presiding Judge,
           and the Chairperson of the Board of Commissioners.

           Prior to obtaining akeycard, all applicants must review Policy #515 and sign aresponsibility form.
           Signed responsibility forms will be retained by Business Services until keycard access for the
           applicant has been deleted from the system. All keycards shall be returned to Business Services
           on the last day ofemployment.

           Business Services will forward requests to the Marion County Sheriff's Office’s court security
           supervisor for review prior to granting access. Marion County employees who are denied
           access may appeal the decision, as outlined in this policy.

     3.2 Courthouse and Court Facility Employees: Individual department administrators are
           responsible for submitting to the Business Services Department the names of employees
           requiring keycard access for their department. Access doors, days of entry and access hours are

  4.   Marion County District Attorney’s and Legal Counsel’s Offices:

       4.1 The District Attorney’s and Legal Counsel’s management team members may have 24/7
           keycard access to the Courthouse’s Common Doors. Access to the Courthouse or other
             court facilities outside of typical business hours shall be for business purposes only.

       4.2 Marion County Legal Counsel Assistants and Deputy District Attorneys and authorized
           staff members may have keycard access to the north door on the first floor of the Courthouse,
           and other court facilities Monday through Friday from 6:30 a.m. to 6:30 p.m.

       4.3 All other authorized Marion County District Attorney and Legal Counsel staff members
           may have keycard access to the north door on the first floor of the Courthouse, and other
             court facilities Monday through Friday from 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m.

       4.4 District Attorney and Legal Counsel staff members with large carts, files, trial exhibits shall
           enter through the Courthouse front door and may contact Judicial Security Unit to ask for
           by-pass security during normal business hours.

       entrance into the Marion County Sheriff's Office. Possession ofa valid keycard does not preclude
       the holder from being subject to random screening at any entrance door of the courthouse. The

     6.   Special Access Cards: Marion County department heads and elected officials may request an

7.         Public Safety Personnel:

           7.1 Uniformed and non-uniformed on duty public safety personnel and armored car uniformed
               personnel shall be allowed unobstructed access to Marion County court facilities.

           7.2 Prior to accessing the court facilities, each person must log in with the security staff and report
               any weapons. Prior to accessing court facilities, an agency badge and identification must be
                present, they must sign in on the logbook and sign out when they leave.

                outside of their clothing whenever in court facilities.

8.        Deliveries

                entrance door.        Delivery staff shall always be accompanied by staff responsible for receiving

                deliveries small enough to pass through the x-ray machine will be processed by the security
                staff.

           8.2 Deliveries made to other court facilities shall enter through the facility’s main entrance. The
               entrance security staff will contact appropriate office staff members to escort the delivery.
               Office staff must always accompany the delivery person(s) up until the time when the
               deliverers exit the building.

           8.3 Deliveries to the Juvenile Services Building, 2960 Center Street, may only occur if pre-
                approved by the department head or their designee, on a case-by-case basis, and only from
                known vendors that provide routine deliveries.
