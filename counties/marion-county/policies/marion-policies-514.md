ADMINISTRATIVE POLICIES
 SECTION: Health, Safety & Security POLICY: 514
TITLE: Volunteer Injury Coverage PROCEDURE: 514-A
 ORDER: 14-4
DEPT: Business Services DIVISION: Risk Management
ADOPTED: 07/96 REVIEWED:  08/22 REVISED: 01/14

PURPOSE:  The purpose of this policy is to establish organiz ational guidelines for insur-
ance coverage of accidental injuries to Marion County volunteers.

AUTHORITY:  The Marion County Board of Commissioners may estab lish rules and
regulations in reference to managing the interest a nd business of the county
under ORS 203.010, 203.035, 203.111, and 203.230.

APPLICABILITY:  The Volunteer Injury Coverage policy applies to:
1. Volunteers registered with the Marion County vol unteer
services coordinator while working under the direction and
control of the supervising department
2. Sheriff’s office registered volunteers while wor king under
the direction and control of the sheriff’s office, excluding
those in the following paragraph.

Volunteers in the following categories are covered under Marion County’s
self-insured workers’ compensation program (see pol icy 505 – Injured
Worker):
1. Sheriff reserve deputies
2. Sheriff cadets
3. Sheriff search and rescue volunteers while under  the
direction and control of the sheriff’s office, incl uding crew
18, jeep patrol, posse, search and rescue communica tions
(SARCOM), special vehicle unit, northwest search do gs, and
chuckwagon

GENERAL POLICY:  Marion County may provide accidental injury covera ge, as described in this
policy, to volunteers.  The coverage applies to volunteers who are
appointed, in writing, by a department and engaged in duties as directed by
that department.

POLICY GUIDELINES:

1. VOLUNTEER INJURY COVERAGE
1.1. Volunteer injury coverage is not primary cover age.

1.2. The volunteer injury coverage applies after al l other available coverage of the loss has been
sought.  Other coverage of the accidental injury includes but is not limited to:

1.2.1.   The volunteer’s group or personal medical or disability coverage.
1.2.2. In the case of an automobile accident, cover age provided by the owner’s or driver’s
insurer or self-insurer of any private vehicles or provided by Marion County on
county vehicles.
1.2.3. Any other applicable and collectible insuran ce that purports to cover the losses that
result from the accidental injury.

1.3. Limits:
1.3.1. The limits for volunteer injury coverage are  $10,000 for accidental death and
dismemberment, $5,000 for medical expenses, and $500 for dental expenses.
1.3.2.   Coverage under this policy will include all reasonable medical expenses associated
            with an injury to a covered volunteer while acting within the scope and course of
            his or her duties.

1.4. Exclusions:
1.4.1. The volunteer injury coverage policy specifi cally excludes payments for lost wages
or indemnity, pain and suffering.

1.4.2.  The volunteer injury coverage policy does not cover any loss caused in whole or
            in part by, or resulting in whole or in part from:

1.4.2.1.   Intentional or self-inflicted injury; suicide, or attempted suicide
1.4.2.2.   Illness, disease or mental infirmity
1.4.2.3.    Injuries resulting from being under the influence of drugs or intoxicants
1.4.2.4.    Stroke or cerebrovascular accident or event, cardiovascular accident or
event, myocardial infarction or heart attack, coronary thrombosis, or
aneurysm
1.4.2.5.    Conditions covered under workers’ compensation
1.4.2.6.    Declared or undeclared war
1.4.2.7.    Participation in a sporting event or other athletic activity.

1.4.3. Violation of Marion County policies or proce dures voids all Marion County cover-
age for volunteer injuries.

1.4.4.  Insurers may not claim contribution from th is coverage.  No party may make any
claim for volunteer injury coverage payments except the covered volunteer or his or
her estate.

2.  FEES AND CHARGES
There is no fee charged to the volunteer or to the department for volunteer injury coverage. The
cost for volunteer injury coverage will be paid fro m the self-insured workers’ compensation fund.

Each department is responsible for reporting volunt eer hours and days worked to the Marion
County volunteer services coordinator.

3.  RESPONSIBILITIES
 Business Services is responsible for the implement ation and revision of the policy and related
procedures.  Supervisors of injured volunteers are responsible for coordinating investigation and
documentation with Business Services – Risk Managem ent to ensure applicable rules and policies
are followed.

4.  POLICY REVIEW
Business Services will review this policy and the c orresponding procedures at least every three
years to ensure compliance with applicable laws and rules.
