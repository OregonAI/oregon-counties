524-C.1

ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURES

TITLE: Countywide Building Access Matrix PROCEDURE #: 524-C
DEPT: Business Services PROGRAM: Risk Management
EFFECTIVE DATE: 1/09 REVIEWED: 11/24 REVISED: 05/25

OBJECTIVE: To establish procedures for requesting and issuing countywide keycards for
access to buildings owned and operated by Marion County.

REFERENCE: Policy #524

POLICY STATEMENT: To establish the requirements for gaining countywide keycard access to
Marion County facilities.

APPLICABILITY: All county departments, county employees, volunteers and contracted
providers who may need countywide keycard access.

PROCEDURES:

1. Countywide Building Access Matrix:
1.1. Any request for countywide access will be approved or denied according to the guidelines of the
Countywide Building Access Matrix.
1.2. Any request outside of the Countywide Building Access Matrix must be authorized by the Marion
County CAO prior to access being granted.
