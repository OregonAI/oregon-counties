BEFORE THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS

FOR MARION COUNTY, OREGON

In the Matter of Revising Administrative )
Policy 515 Court Facilities Security and )
Its Procedure 515-A Access to Court        )
Facilities, and 515-B Access Request Form)

ORDER  No. 25-

This matter came before the Marion County Board of Commissioners at its regularly
scheduled public meeting on Wednesday, June 18, 2025, to consider approval of changes to
Marion County Policy and Procedure;

WHEREAS, Marion County Policy 515 relates to security for court facilities; and

WHEREAS, Marion County Policy 515-A relates to the procedure for issuing key or key
cards to access Marion County court facilities; and

WHEREAS, Marion County Policy 515-B relates to the procedure for Access Request
Form that need to be signed.

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the revised Marion County Administrative Policy 515
and its procedure 515-A and 515-B are hereby approved.

DATED at Salem, Oregon the 18th day of June, 2025.
