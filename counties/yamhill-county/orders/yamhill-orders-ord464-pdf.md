  FINDINGS     OF    FACT

  a.    The applicant has requested a zone change                                                 from VLDR 2 1/2 to
        VLSR 1 on 15 acres that is presently plan                                                 designated VLDR.

  2.    The subject property is the acreage remaining     from a 50 acre
        ownership  that  was divided  into  28, 1 acre   lots   and   one   2
        1/2 acre lot through lot size averaging.     Under the lot size
        averaging   provisions,   the  15  acre property    could   not   be
        further divided as long as it is zoned VLDK 2 1/2.

  3.    The  property is adjacent  to the northeast  boundary  of  the
        Newberg Urban Growth Boundary and city limits.   The property
        is accessed by Crestview Drive and Bird Haven Loop.

  4.    The  property     is   surrounded    by  lands   that  are    zoned    VLDR    2
        1/2  and    plan    designated     VLDR.     Zoning   on    adjacent     lands
        within   the City of Newberg        is R-1.     Lot sizes     to the north
        and   east   are    generally     1 acre    in  size;    to   the   west    and
        southwest    lots    are at urban densities       and to the south        lots
        are 10 acres.

  5.    The  subject              property  and   surrounding   area  was  included                                       in
        Exceptions               Code  Area   1.4   (North  Newberg).     According                                       to
        Exceptions              Statement          II,        adopted               in     1980     as    supplementary
        findings   for   the  taking   of an exception    to statewide   goals,
        "the  entire    North   Newberg   study  area  is committed    to rural
        residential    development.       It  is generally    characterized    by
        fair   or   better     water    availability,     fair   to good    soil
        suitability    for septic systems and good roads."

  6.    Since       1981,        there     have     been        15        new        dwellings           established      in
        Code       Area     1.4.         Additionally,               65    acres           were     rezoned     from     AF-
        10 to VLDR              2 1/2 based  upon   findings  that   there  was a need
        for  small              acreage,  rural   residential   lots   in  the  Newberg
_______area,

   da   There are over 300 acres of VLDR 1 zoned land in the Newberg
        area;   much   of   it within     the    Newberg     Urban    Growth    Boundary.
        A quick    survey     indicated     that    there   are   less   than 30 acres
        of    undeveloped          VLDR    1    zoned     lands      in   the     Newberg
        area,outside      the   Urban   Growth      Boundary.       Within    the    County
        as a whole there are less than 100 acres of undeveloped VLDR
        1  zoned     land.        it  does      not    appear      that    any     of   the
        undeveloped       VLDR    1 land     in    the   County    has   been     recently
        subdivided and is now available              for sale in one acre lots.

   e.   Public   facilities    or  services     provided   to  the   property
        include   electric  power   through   PGE,  telephone   through   GTE,
        fire protection by the Newberg Rural Fire District,          and road
        access  by County and public     roads.    Water  is to be provided
        by  Oxberg   Water   Company,   and  sewer   service  by   individual
        septic systems.

  9.    Since   1982,     the   Planning     Commission     has   approved      all   zone
        change   requests        for    higher    density      zoning   in    VLDR    plan
        designated       areas.       The   primary     concern    with    each    of  the
        previously     approved      zone   changes   in VLDR areas has been the
        adequacy      of    facilities       available      to   support      increased
        development.

  10.   Section   502,01     of   the Zoning   Ordinance    provides    that  "the
        purpose  of  the VLDR Districts         is  to provide    for medium-to-
        high density rural residential         development    on selected lands
        identified       as     Very  Low    Density     Residential       in  the
        Comprehensive       Plan."     Because     the  VLDR   plan   designation
        applies            to      the     VLDR          5,    VLDR             2        1/2      and    VLDR   i    zoned
        districts,  the Department  of Land Conservation & Development
        has  agreed   that Yamhill    County  may rezone   such   lands  ta
        higher   densities  without    notifying  the  DLCD   and  without
        taking a new exception.

Approval  of the request      is appropriatein that it will allow
for  increased     development   of an area which was deemed       to be
committed     to   rural   residential     development     through    the
exceptions       process,     thereby    providing     for    efficient
utilization     of  rural  land   adjacent   to the City of Newberg.
Further,     the     property    is   bordered    by one    acre   rural
residential   lots          on the  north   and    east   and   lies adjacent    to
city  residential           development   to    the   west   and   southwest  and,
therefore,     the           zone  change    to     VLDR   1   would    allow  for
development     consistent with the type,  density                    and   pattern    of
development     already occurring  in the area.

The proposed      development   can also be considered    appropriate
for   the   area   based upon   existing  and anticipated    services.
Water    will   be provided   by Oxberg   Water  Company;  County   and
public roads provide access        to the site;  and major utilities
(electricity and telephone)        are present  in the area;   and the
soils    in   the        area   have   been   found    to    be      appropriate      for
installation        of    individual    septic    systems.

Although other VLDR 1 zoned lands exist                in the County,     there
are  less  than 30 areas          of undeveloped      VLDR   1 zoned   land    in
the  Newberg    area,     outside     of the Urban Growth Boundary.            In
reviewing    the    30 acres      of undeveloped      VLDR   1 zoned   land    in
the Newberg     area,     it appeared      that none of it was presently
subdivided     into    one   acre    lots   and  being   made   available     for
development.          Further,      the   subject   property     is very    well
suited     for     VLDR     1   zoning      considering        the   existing
development   pattern   and   density   or  surrounding  properties,
soil   suitability    for   septic    system   installation,   water
availability,   the  existence   of  good access roads  and  other
public  services,   and  the  compatible  nature of  the proposed
development with adjacent     rural and urban residential uses.
