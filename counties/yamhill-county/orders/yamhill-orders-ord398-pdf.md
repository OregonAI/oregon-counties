The   following     conclusionary       findings         can   be   made   for   approval   of
the   request:

1;    In   that   the   Comprehensive     Plan     Map     designation       for   the   entire
      parcel is "“Agriculture/Forestry Large Holding" and because
      this plan designation applies to both the EF-40 and AF-20
      Districts and the proposed boundary adjustment to make the
      entire parcel zoned AF-20 is consistent with the Comprehen-
      sive Plan Map designation on the parcel.

      The proposed zone boundary adjustment will not reduce in
      area the predominant zoning currently existing on the subject
      property in that the AF-20 District now covers 56% of the
      subject property   (41 out of 73 acres) and the proposed adjust-
      ment will result in the remainder of the 73 acre parcel also
      being zoned AF-20.

      In that the parcel will be rezoned from its present EF-40/AF-
      20 mix to completely AF-20, the boundary adjustment will fol-
      low existing property lines and will result in a single zoning
      district for the entire parcel.
