2.           Regarding the location of the tract as specified in criterion (A) above, the average elevation of
             the lots in question ranges from approximately 480' to 760! above sea level. The topography is
             hilly and includes some ridge tops. The request meets this criterion.

             Regarding criterion (B) above, the 1992 aerial photograph of the area shows that more than
             50% of each parcel within the subject tract is forested. The parcels also contain some cleared
             areas. The purpose section of the Agriculture/Forestry District states in part:

                          The purpose of the Agriculture/Forestry District is to identify and protect lands
                          designated as Agriculture/Forestry Large Holding on the Comprehensive Plan,
                          that are a mixture of agricultural and forest management operations, and other
                          uses which are compatible with such operations.

             Due to the mixed nature of the use of the properties, and the fact that the applicants are using
             the properties for a mixture of farm and forestry uses, the requested change is appropriate. The
             request meets this criterion.

             Regarding criterion (C), the Official 1980 County Zoning Map, which was valid until December
             29, 1993,          shows the subject tract to have more than 50% of land zoned AF-20
             Agriculture/Forestry use (see 1980-1993 zoning map). As mentioned in Finding (A.11), 80%
             of the tract was zoned as AF-20 Agriculture/Forestry and 20% f the tract was zoned EF-40
             Exclusive Farm Use. The request meets this criterion.

             Regarding criterion (D) the lots contain Class III and IV soils and are, therefore, considered
             agricultural land pursuant to Goal 3, however, they also contain more than 50% of forested
             area. The majority of the soils are capable of producing 145 to 160 cubic feet of wood fiber
             per acre per year. Therefore, none of the lots are exclusively suitable for Goal 3 use or Goal 4
             use.

             Regarding criterion (E), the request involves approximately 82.5 acres to be rezoned to AF-80.
             Adjacent land is zoned AF-20. However, the standard does not require the area to be over
             160 acres of a particular zone but rather 160 acres of a particular zoning designation. The
             amendment would therefore result in an area of more than 160 acres with the requested
             Agriculture/Forestry designation.

             Criterion (F) above does not apply since the approval of the request would not reduce the
             minimum lot size on the property. The applicant wishes to retain the 80 acre minimum lot size.
