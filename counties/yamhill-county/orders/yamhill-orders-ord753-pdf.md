        THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF YAMHILL COUNTY, OREGON (the Board) sat for
the transaction of county business on Thursday, January 6, 2005, Commissioners Mary P. Stern, Leslie Lewis
and Kathy George being present.

        IT APPEARING        TO THE BOARD         that Evergreen Aviation Educational Center submitted an
application to the City of McMinnville and Yamhill County requesting that the current McMinnville urban
growth boundary be expanded to include an additional 34.58 acres of land for commercial purposes. More
specifically, the applicant is requesting the boundary be expanded to the north, west and east of the existing
Evergreen Aviation Museum to allow construction of a proposed expansion of the Evergreen Aviation
Museum complex. The property is further described as a portion of Tax Lots 600 and 1300, Section 23, T4S,
R4W, W.M.

        The McMinnville Urban Area Management Commission (MUAMC), McMinnville City Council, and
Yamhill County Board of Commissioners held a joint public hearing on April 12, 2004, at 7:00 p.m. on the
proposed amendment after due notice had been given in the local newspaper on March 25, 2004, and written
notice had been mailed to owners of property located within 750 feet of the affected property; and

         At the conclusion of the April 12 meeting, the reviewing bodies closed the hearing to further public
testimony. At the request of one of those testifying, the public record was kept open until 5:00 p.m., April 19,
2004, for the submittal of written testimony regarding the subjectrequest. In addition, the reviewing bodies
granted an additional seven days time (to 5:00 p.m., April 26, 2004) for the applicant to provide written
response to any objections that may have been filed by April 19, 2004. Friends of Yamhill County, and Mr.
Mark Davis filed timely written comments. Timely response to these objections was filed by the applicant.

        A joint public meeting of the MUAMC, McMinnville City Council, and Yamhill County Board of
Commissioners was held on April 26, 2004, at 7:00 p.m. (continued from their April 12,2004 meeting), for
the purpose of considering the prior testimony and written responses regarding the subject request.

         Atsaid meeting, the MUAMC approved the urban growth boundary amendment and recommended
said change to the Yamhill County Board of Commissioners and City Council. The County adopted Ordinance
738 on April 26, 2004, and that ordinance was timely appealed to the Land Use Board of Appeals. On June
10, 2004, the parties stipulated to a stay of the appeal. In August, 2004, the parties entered into a Settlement
