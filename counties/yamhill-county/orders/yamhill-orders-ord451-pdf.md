     OF FACT

1.    The    applicant  has                     requested       a    zone change from AF-10 to

      1401-84).    There are   presently  2  dwellings   located    on the
      subject property.    The land is not currently   in farm use.

3.    The property is located approximately          1000 feet northeast     of
      the Newberg Urban    Growth  Boundary.     The   property   is accessed
      by both David Lane and Owl Lane,       public      roads which connect
      to    County   roads      (Springbrook      Rd      and   Kincaid    Rd,
      respectively).

4,   The subject property and             lands     abutting        the      site    on the
     north,  west    and south        are   plan    designated        VLDR.      This     area
     was included      in Exceptions          Code   Area      1.4    (North     Newberg).
     According    to      the    Exceptions        Statement         II,      adopted        as
     supplementary     findings     for the       taking      of      an    exception        to
     statewide    Goals       in   1980,      “the   entire     North      Newberg      study
     area is committed to rural residential                   development.              It is
     generally      characterized             by      fair      or       better         water
     availability,       fair    to    good      soil     suitability           for septic
     systems     and      good      roads.           The     study       area      consists
     predominantly of parcels           10  acres    or   less    in    size    and     has 4
     existing subdivisions          which     comprise     over     50   percent      of   the
     total parcels”.

5.   Services currently provided to the property include electric
     power   through    PGE,    telephone      service     through    GTE,      the
     Newberg Public School District          29J,    fire    protection    by   the
     Newberg Rural Fire District,        the Yamhill County Sheriff‘s
     Department,   and.    access    via    two public roads with 60 foot
     rights-of-way.     One of the      roads     is    paved,    the   other is
     gravel  surfaced.     Water will be provided         by   individual     welle
      and     sewer        service        by    individual        septic      systems.

6.   A 35.34 acre property abutting the           subject property          on the
     Southwest was     rezoned from      AF-10 to VLDR-2      1/2   in'  1983  (Z-
     237-83;  Ordinance    No. 351).        The   property     was subdivided
     into 13   lots  in   May of 1985      (Country Estates      Subdivision).
     As part of the subdivision         process,    all    13    lots received
     septic   approvals.         Additionally,     a test well was drilled
     near the center of       the   subdivision     to   provide        a general
     basis of   water information        to prospective      land purchasers.
      The     well     log       for     the    test   well   indicated         a   depth   of   195°   and
      a   yield       of    50    gallons        per   minute.

      residences.

  CONCLUSIONARY              FINDINGS

  Approval
  The   following    conclusionary                     findings        can        be     made    for     approval           of
  the   application:

  1.      The request to amend the zoning on the subject property from
          AF-10    to    VLDR-2     1/2     is consistent       with the Comprehensive
          Plan in that the property              is    plan    designated     VLDR,    which
          provides for       residential       development on parcels of from one
          to five     acres.        Further,        the    property     was    included    in
          Exceptions      Code      Area      1.4    and,    through     the    exceptions
          process,     the    VLDR     plan        designation      was     found    to    be
          appropriate     for    the subject         property    and surrounding area,
          and was acknowledged as such by LCDC.

          With regard to specific        rural   area    development    goals and
          policies,   in    that the   subject property       is plan designated
          VLDR and the County recognizes        such lands as the appropriate
          and desired      location for rural    residential    development,  the
          proposed    zone     change  is     consistent     will     rural  area
          development goals.

  2.      Approval           of   the     request      will     allow       for        increased        development
          of     an        area       which     was      deemed        to         be      committed           to        rural
          residential    development     through     the   exceptions   process,
          thereby providing    for efficient utilization        of land adjacent
          to   the  City   of   Newberg.       Since    the subject property   is
          bordered           on   three       sides    by    VLDR-2     1/2        zoned        land    and         on     the
          west   and   south     by    developed     subdivisions,        use    of   the
          property as a farm        unit’is     severely    limited      and  is    nota
          realistic   use   of the land.        Additionally,      most services are
          already available to the site and,           therefore,      development of
          the   property    at     VLDR   densities      will    be    in accord with
          existing    land    uses,     planned      residential       densities      and
          available public services.
