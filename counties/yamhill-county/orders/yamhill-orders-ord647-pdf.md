ORDINANCE 647
THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF YAMHILL COUNTY , OREGON ("the Board") sat
for the transaction of county business in formal session on May 28, 1998, commissioners Ted
Lopuszynski, Robert Johnstone and Thomas E. E. Bunn being present.
THE BOARD MAKES THE FOLLOWING FINDINGS:
A. On April 30, 1997, the Board adopted Ordinance 626, the Solid Waste Ordinance, 1997.
Among other things , the Solid Waste Ordinance made substantial revisions to the methodology
used in determining rates and rate increases for collection franchises. A component of the
methodology was the consumer price index. Section 8(1)(f)(1) of the ordinance referred to the
"Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers for West-C, All Items (1982-84=100) ."
However, the United States Bureau of Labor Statistics has abandoned the "West-C" CPI and
replaced it with the "West B/e" CPI.
B. It is appropriate to amend Ordinance 626, the Solid Waste Ordinance, 1997, in two
respects. First, Section 8(l)(f)(1) should be modified to reflect current statistical measurements
for the consumer price index. Second, Section 8(1)(f)(2) should be deleted as superfluous.
Former Section 8(l)(f)(3) should be renumbered as Section 8(l)(f)(2). NOW, THEREFORE:
THE YAMHILL COUNTY BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS HEREBY ORDAINS AS
FOLLOWS:
Section 1. Modifications to Section 8(1)(0 of Solid Waste Ordinance, 1997.
Section 8(l)(f) of Ordinance 626, the Solid Waste Ordinance, 1997, is hereby amended to
read in its entirety as follows:
