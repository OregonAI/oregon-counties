        THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF YAMHILL COUNTY, OREGON (the Board) sat for the
transaction of county business at 7:00 p.m. on April 26, 2004, Commissioners Kathy George, Mary P. Stern
and Leslie Lewis being present for a special formal joint session with the McMinnville City Council and the
McMinnville Urban Area Management Commission, at the McMinnville Community Center.

         IT APPEARING TO THE BOARD that Evergreen Aviation Educational Center has submitted an
application to the City of McMinnville and Yamhill County requesting that the current McMinnville urban
growth boundary be expanded to include an additional 34.58 acres of land for commercial purposes. More
specifically, the applicant is requesting the boundary be expanded to the north, west and east of the existing
Evergreen Aviation Museum to allow construction of a proposed expansion of the Evergreen Aviation
Museum complex. The property is further described as a portion of Tax Lots 600 and 1300, Section 23, T4S,
R4W, W.M.

        The McMinnville Urban Area Management Commission (MUAMC), McMinnville City Council,
and Yamhill County Board of Commissioners held a joint public hearing on April 12, 2004, at 7:00 p.m. on
the proposed amendment after due notice had been given in the local newspaper on March 25, 2004, and
written notice had been mailed to owners of property located within 750 feet of the affected property; and

         At the conclusion of the April 12 meeting, the reviewing bodies closed the hearing to further public
testimony. At the request of one of those testifying, the public record was kept open until 5:00 p.m., April
19, 2004, for the submittal of written testimony regarding the subject request. In addition, the reviewing
bodies granted an additional seven days time (to 5:00 p.m., April 26, 2004) for the applicant to provide
written response to any objections that may have been filed by April 19, 2004. Friends of Yamhill County,
and Mr. Mark Davis filed timely written comments. Timely response to these objections was filed by the
applicant.

         A joint public meeting of the MUAMC, McMinnville City Council, and Yamhill County Board of
Commissioners was held on April 26, 2004, at 7:00 p.m. (continued from their April 12, 2004 meeting), for
the purpose of considering the prior testimony and written responses regarding the subject request.

       At said meeting, the MUAMC approved the urban growth boundary amendment                            and   has
recommended said change to the Yamhill County Board of Commissioners and City Council;

         IT IS HEREBY ORDAINED BY THE BOARD:

Section 1.      That the Board adopts the findings as contained within the applicant’s submitted narrative,
and as referenced in Exhibit “A” of the City of McMinnville staff report for Ordinance 4806, hereby
incorporated into this ordinance by this reference.

Section 2.      That the Comprehensive Plan is hereby amended to include the approximate 34.58 acres of
land located to the west, north, and east of the existing Evergreen Aviation Museum property and as depicted
on the applicant’s “supplemental application” (the boundary shall not include the easternmost 250 feet of
property, as shown on the original submittal). The property is further described as a portion of Tax Lots 600
and 1300, Section 23, T. 4S., R. 4 W., W.M.

Section 3.     That the property described in Section 2 of this Ordinance is hereby designated as “Future
Urbanizable” on the county’s Comprehensive Plan Map.

  This ordinance, being necessary for the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Yamhill County, and
an emergency having been declared to exist, is effective upon passage.
