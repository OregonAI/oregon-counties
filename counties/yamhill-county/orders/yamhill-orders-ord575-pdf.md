         WHEREAS,     on July 7, 1994, the Yamhill County Planning Commission          held a public hearing to

and

menting the Planning Commission’s decision; and

        WHEREAS, the findings and conclusions attached hereto as Exhibit “A* and by this reference
incorporated herein provide a justification for the approval of the application; and

         WHEREAS,    the attached Exhibit "A" demonstrates that the proposed amendment                  to the Official

        Section 1. The Official Zoning Map of Yamhill County is hereby amended as specified in the
attached Exhibit "B" to reflect a zoning designation of "Heavy Industrial" (HD) on a 28 acre portion of Tax
Lot 5633-900 identified on Exhibit "B’.

adopted in support of this ordinanee.

Yamhill County, and an emergency having been declared to exist, is effective upon passage.

REQUEST:                Zone change from AF-10 Agriculture/Forestry Small Holding             to HI Heavy
                        Industrial for expansion of an existing wood treating facility.

APPLICANT:              Taylor Lumber and Treating, Inc.

TAX LOT:                A portion of Tax Lot 5633-900.

COMPREHENSIVE PLAN DESIGNATION:

                The westerly portion (approximately one-third of the area) is within the Sheridan Urban
                Growth Boundary and is designated Industrial on the city plan. The remainder of the
                property is designated Heavy Industrial on the county plan map.

LOCATION:       Adjacent to the western boundary of the City of Sheridan on the north side of Highway
                18 Business Loop, approximately one-quarter mile west of the intersection of Highway 18
                Business Loop and Rock Creek Road

FINDINGS:

Aa    Background Facts

1     Property size:   Total area under consideration to be rezoned is approximately 23 ac.

2.    Access:   Oregon State Highway 18 Business Loop and Rock Creek Road

3.    On-site Land Use: The subject parcel is characterized as an agricultural use on the western
      portion of the subject parcel. The eastern portion of the parcel is utilized for pole storage overflow
      from the existing heavy industrial operation currently located to the east of the subject parcel.
      The site was purchased by Taylor Lumber in January, 1973. The comprehensive plan designation
      for the site has been Heavy Industrial since 1976. The subject portion of the parcel is bounded
      on the north by railroad tracks and on the south side by State Highway 18 Business loop.

4.    Surrounding Land Use: The surrounding area to the north and south is characterized by large-
      scale agricultural production of grains and hay. Directly east is an established mill site with a
      portion of the operation being inside the city limits of Sheridan. The adjacent portion in the
      county is zoned HI Heavy Industrial. There is a 5-acre parcel with a dwelling nearly surrounded
      by the subject property. Approximately 370 feet west. of the subject property is another area that
      is in heavy industrial use and is zoned as such. These sites are owned and operated by Willamina
      Lumber Company.

5.    Surrounding Zoning:     Adjacent zoning to the east is HI Heavy Industrial and city zoning
      designations within the city limits of Sheridan. The surrounding area to the north and south is
      zoned EF-40 Exclusive Farm Use. The area to the west is zoned AF-10 Agriculture/ Forestry
      Small Holding and HI Heavy Industrial. The land bounded by the railroad and the highway
      carries a Heavy Industrial Comprehensive Plan designation.

6.    Soils:  Sheet 48 of the Yamhill County Soil Survey indicates the property is composed
      predominantly of Chehalem silty clay loam (CeC), which is Class I agricultural soil.

B.      Oregon Administrative Rule Provisions and Analysis

        Oregon Administrative Rule (OAR) Chapter 660, Division 4, explains the requirements for taking
exceptions to statewide goals. The subject property is designated as Heavy Industrial on the Compre-
hensive Plan map   and is currently zoned AF-10      (see Finding A.3).   An exception to Goal 3 has already
been taken.

Cc      Ordinance Provisions and Analysis

1.    The zone change portion of the subject request must comply with the standards and criteria in
YCZO Section 1208.02. These provisions are as follows:

        (a)      The proposed change is consistent with the goals, policies, and any other applicable
        provisions of the Comprehensive Plan.

        (b)      There is an existing demonstrable need for the particular uses allowed by the requested
        zone, considering the importance of such uses to the citizenry or the economy of the area, the
        existing market demand which such uses will satisfy, and the availability and location of other
        lands so zoned and their suitability for the uses allowed by the zone.

        ©       The proposed change is appropriate considering the surrounding land uses, the density
        and pattern of development in the area, any changes which may have occurred in the vicinity to
        support the proposed amendment and the availability of utilities and services likely to be needed
        by the anticipated uses in the proposed district.

        (ad      Other lands in the county already designated for the proposed use are either unavailable
        or not as well-suited for the anticipated uses due to location, size, or other factors.

        (e)      The amendment       is consistent   with   the current   Oregon   Administrative   Rules   for
        exceptions, if applicable.

        The findings regarding these criteria are provided in subsections 3 through 7 below.

2.       Regarding Comprehensive Plan goals and policies, the policies under Goal .H.1 of the Plan text
state that:

       a.       The industrial development goal provides "to concentrate industries of similar types,
        service needs, and performance characteristics within designated areas of each of the existing
       urban centers."

        Although the subject property is not in an urban center it is located in an industrially zoned and
developed corridor between the cities of Sheridan and Willamina, adjacent to an existing industrial site,
and, as an expansion area for an existing, abutting industry. An approval of the requested amendment
on the subject property would concentrate industries of similar types and performance characteristics
within a designated industrial area.

        b.      County policies regarding industrial development outside of urban growth boundaries
        encourage locations which can adequately be served by necessary facilities, well removed and

         The proposed use of the property is an expansion of the existing use on an adjacent parcel. All
services are available to the existing heavy industrial operation and can be provided to the expanded site
for operation of dry kiln, pole peeler and log & piling storage area. Further, since the use of the subject
property as proposed is essentially an expansion of a use that is currently on-going on abutting property,
and surrounding lands are planned and zoned for agricultural use, the proposed use would not be in-
compatible with land uses on surrounding properties. The applicant has indicated that most of the
dwellings in the area have been purchased by the applicant and subsequently demolished to avoid any
potential conflict with the heavy industrial use in the future.

        GQ      Industrial policies provide that heavy industrial sites be identified and protected from en-

        Since the subject property is owned by the industrial user on adjacent land and because the
proposed site is required for continued operation of the adjacent lumber mill, approval of the requested
amendment would help to ensure that a heavy industrial site is identified as such on the Comprehensive
Plan and Zoning Map and, thereby, retained for industrial use.

        d.      The County economic development goal provides for maintaining a rate and pattern of
        economic growth sufficient to prevent high levels of unemployment and underemployment and
        strengthen the local economic bases.

        Approval of the request would allow an existing industry to continue to provide employment and
economic benefits to the Willamina-Sheridan area.

        The request is for a zone change for a 23-acre portion of a 130-acre parcel from AF-10 Agricul-
ture/Forestry Small Holding to HI Heavy Industrial.

        This portion of the parcel has been excepted from Goal 3. Areas that do not require a new
exception cannot reasonably accommodate the use because of unavailability of suitable land zoned for a
HI Heavy Industrial use.

         The Board finds that the proposed change is consistent with the goals, policies, and any other
applicable provisions of the Comprehensive Plan.

3.       Regarding the need for the proposed change as stated in criterion (b), the Board finds that there
is an existing, demonstrable need for the proposed change considering that the proposed land use is a re-
quired expansion for an existing industry. The applicant has indicated that the abutting mill site to the
east is running out of log storage and production space and that without additional area, the mill will not
be able to operate. Approval of the request would provide the mill with the area needed for storage and
treatment facility.

4,        Regarding criterion (c), the Board finds that the proposed change is appropriate considering the
surrounding land uses, the density and pattern of development in the area, any changes which may have
occurred in the vicinity to support the proposed amendment and the availability of utilities and services
likely to be needed by the anticipated uses in the proposed district. The requested change is appropriate
in that an existing wood processing and treatment mill has nearly exhausted the area on the mill property
presently available for storage of logs and pilings and the requested amendment will allow the mill to
expand the storage and processing area to the subject property abutting the mill site. The applicant has
indicated that this change will increase the efficiency of their manufacturing process. There is no evident
change to the neighboring area due to the fact that the business has been in existence for a very long time.

existing mill is very desirable. Although there are other HI-zoned sites in the County, these sites are devo-
ted to existing uses and, therefore, are not available for further development.    Moreover, this finding is

Based on the above findings, analysis, and conclusions, the Board hereby approves the request by Taylor
