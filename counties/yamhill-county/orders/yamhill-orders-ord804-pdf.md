A.         REQUEST:       The applicant is requesting that three lots totalling approximately 30 acres
           within the Newberg Urban Reserve Area be included within the Urban Growth Boundary
           and receive Comprehensive Plan designations of Commercial (COM), High Density
           Residential (HDR), Medium Density Residential (MDR), and Low Density Residential
           (LDR). The commercial land would be located along Highway 99W, and the low density
           residential would be located along the northern edge of the property, abutting the existing
           residential neighborhood. The applicant expects that the completed project will include
           approximately 210 residential units and 280,000 square feet of commercial office and
           retail space. The proposed development would construct new internal streets on the
           property and take access from the proposed extension of Crestview Drive west of the site.

B.         PROCESS:          The applicant's request for approval of an urban growth boundary
           amendment is being processed through the provisions of the Newberg Urban Area
           Management Agreement as follows:

                                         site for comment.
           November 14, 2006             Notice was posted on the site.
           November 11,2006              Notice was published in the Newberg Graphic.

      designated urban reserve land undet ORS 195. 145, rule or metropolitan service district
           action plan.”

Land Need
Establishment and change of urban growth boundaries shall be based on the following:
         (1)     Demonstrated need to accommodate long range urban population, consistent with
         a 20-year population forecast coordinated with affected local governments; and
         (2)       Demonstrated need for housing, employment opportunities, livability or uses such
         as public facilities, streets and roads, schools, parks or open space, or any combination of
         the need categories in this subsection (2).
In determining need, local government may specify characteristics, such ag parcel size,
        topography or proximity, necessary for land to be suitable for an identified need.
Prior to expanding an urban growth boundary, local governments shall demonstrate that needs -
      cannot reasonably be accommodated on land already inside the urban growth boundary.
Boundary Location The location of the urban growth boundary and changes to the boundary
          shall be determined by evaluating alternative boundary locations consistent with ORS
          197.298 and with consideration of the following factors:
          (1) Efficient accommodation of identified land needs;
         (2) Orderly and economic provision of public facilities and services;
         (3) Comparative environmental, energy, economic and social consequences; and
         (4) Compatibility of the proposed urban uses with nearby agricultural and forest activities
         occurring on farm and forest land outside the UGB.

          interchange. ODOT has begun the process of creating interchange area management

          to this general policy, “for a limited expansion of the Newberg UGB into the westerly
          portion of Urban Reserve Area C to accommodate construction of the Northern Arterial
          in the general location shown on the City of Newberg acknowledged Transportation
          System Plan.” The County has initiated a process to expand the exception to include this
          site, which is the remainder of Urban Reserve Area C. Newberg Comprehensive Plan
          policies give a more specific exemption specifically allowing UGB amendments in the
         area.                                                              .

3.        Land need. The City’s land needs table include a need for additional residential and
          commercial land within the UGB, including a need for a future community shopping
          center, The ad hoc committee looked at various locations for commercial land. The
          committee recommended that the subject property be considered this community
          shopping center, with a buffer of residential uses adjacent to the rural residential
          subdivisions.

          The proposed narrow strips of MDR and HDR may be difficult to develop as shown. The
          LDR strip serves as a buffer for the low density residential to the north. Staff
          recommends changing the MDR to HDR, and changing the dimensions of the residential
          designations to more closely match the proposed Gueldner UGB amendment to the west.
          The recommended comprehensive plan designations will, from north to south, include a
          170 foot strip of LDR with a 30 foot building setback from the northern property line, a
          159 foot strip of HDR, and the COM area as proposed by the applicant. The applicant
          may propose specific locations for consideration prior to the hearing.
