the county was appealed by 1000 Friends of Oregon. The court case dealing with this issue was
decided in 1996 in favor of DLCD and the County and against 1000 Friends, and pursuant to the
Court case, on February 14, 1997, DLCD issued its final order approving the county's periodic
review. Based on the final order establishing that the county had completed periodic review,          the
county began enforcing the new zoning designation.

more economical and therefore more profitable than it would be under the existing E zoning
