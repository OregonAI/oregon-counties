         IT APPEARING TO THE BOARD that Ms. Debra Webb applied for a Zone Change from AF-40
Agriculture/Forestry to Very Low Density Residential VLDR 5 for a 28.5 acre Parcel, Located at 10600 NE
Red Hills Road, Dundee, Yamhill County, Oregon, Tax Lots 3323-2700 and 3323-3101, Taking an Exception

December 4, 2003. The Commission took testimony from the applicant (no opponents appearing),
deliberated, and voted unanimously to approve the application. No-one has appealed that approval to the
Board of Commissioners; NOW, THEREFORE,

        IT IS HEREBY ORDAINED BY THE BOARD that the application is approved as detailed in the
Findings for Approval, attached as Exhibit “A” and by this reference incorporated. This ordinance, being
necessary for the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Yamhill County, and an emergency having
been declared to exist, is effective upon passage.

         DONE this 19" day of February, 2004, at McMinnville, Oregon.
