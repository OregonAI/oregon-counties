         THE BOARD   OF COMMISSIONERS    OF YAMHILL   COUNTY,   OREGON  (the

      WHEREAS, Willie D. Owens,    owner of Tax Lot 3325-600,
Charles Carsley,   owner of Tax Lot 3325-800,   and Jeffrey Coffman,
owner of Tax Lot 3325-900 have applied to the Planning and
Development Departments    of the City of Dundee and Yamhill County
in order to amend the comprehensive plan map of the city and the
county to allow an expansion of the urban growth boundary of the
City of Dundee to encompass    said parcels  totalling approximately
19    acres        in    size;      and

         WHEREAS,          pursuant            to     the     Dundee        Urban    Area     Growth     Manage-
ment Agreement adopted June 17,   1981, under authority of Board
Order 81-287,   a joint session of the planning commissions    of
Yamhill County and the City of Dundee was held on February 19,
1987,  to consider said application at a public hearing;    and

      WHEREAS,   on February 19, 1987, the Yamhill County Planning
Commission voted unanimously to recommend approval of the appli-
cation and to recommend that the Board amend the Yamhill County
Comprehensive Plan,    1974, to amend the urban growth boundary as
requested;   and

      WHEREAS, on February 19, 1987,   the                                     City of Dundee Planning
Commission voted 4 to 1 to recommend    to                                     the Dundee City Council
that said application be denied;   and

         WHEREAS,          Section           C.2.      of     said     Dundee       Urban     Area     Growth
Management Agreement provides   that following a public hearing
at the planning commission  level,  the governing body shall make
a determination based upon the facts and record presented at
public hearing;  and

         WHEREAS,          on     March        4,     1987,     the     Board       considered         said
application upon the record of the joint public hearing con-
ducted by the City of Dundee and the Yamhill County Planning
Commissions  on February 19, 1987; and

         WHEREAS, after consideration of the record and transcript
of    the planning commission hearing,   the Board voted unanimously
to    grant the application and directed staff to prepare    findings
of    fact and conclusionary   findings in  support of such approval;
and

      WHEREAS,   staff has prepared     findings of fact and conclu-
sionary findings     for approval,   and based upon such findings
attached hereto as Exhibit       "A" and by this reference    incorporated
herein,   it appears    appropriate  to grant   the application  as set
forth below;   and

                 3325-800,    and 3325-900 as identified on the attached
                 Exhibit   "B" which is incorporated herein by this
                 reference;    and

       b.        To designate  the parcels referred to in this section
                 as “Future Urbanizable"  on the Yamhill County Com-—
                 prehensive Plan Map.

1 above shall become effective when a parallel ordinance   amend-
ing the City of Dundee comprehensive plan map to allow expansion
of the City of Dundee's urban growth boundary to encompass    the
parcels described  in Section 1 is enacted by the Dundee City
Council and filed in the office of the city recorder of the City
of Dundee.

      Section 3.     This ordinance being necessary  for the health,
safety,   and welfare of the citizens of Yamhill County,    and an
emergency having been declared to exist,    shall become effective
upon passage hereof.
