THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF YAMHILL COUNTY, OREGON (“the

Commissioners Kathy George and Mary P. Stern participating, Commissioner Leslie
Lewis excused.

THE BOARD MAKES THE FOLLOWING FINDINGS:

A. | Community Corrections routinely provides criminal history background checks
through the Law Enforcement Data System (LEDS) on behalf of the Yamhill Community
Action Partnership (YCAP) for persons seeking shelter from YCAP.

B. —_ Inorder for Community Corrections to continue to provide the service for YCAP,
LEDS rules require a an authorizing county ordinance. This ordinance is made to meet
the LEDS requirement    NOW,   THEREFORE,

THE YAMHILL COUNTY BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS ORDAINS AS
FOLLOWS:

Section i    AUTHORIZATION The Yamhill County Community Corrections
Department is authorized to provide criminal history background checks through the Law
Enforcement Data System (LEDS) on behalf of the Yamhill Community Action
Partnership (YCAP) for persons seeking shelter provided by YCAP.

Section2       EFFECTIVE DATE; EMERGENCY CLAUSE. The first and second
readings of this ordinance were made October 20, 2009. To carry out the intent of this

this ordinance shall take effect immediately.
