YAMHILL COUNTY CODE

TITLE 2: COUNTY ADMINISTRATION

Chapter 2.50: Nonpartisan Nature of Office of Commissioner

TABLE OF CONTENTS
Section Title
2.50.01 NONPARTISAN NATURE OF OFFICE OF COMMISSIONER
2.50.02 EFFECTIVE DATE

LEGISLATIVE HISTORY

2.50.01 Nonpartisan Nature of Office of Commissioner . Beginning with filings for election to the office of
Yamhill County Commissioner for the primary election of 2004, all offices of Yamhill County Commissioner
shall thereafter be “nonpartisan” within the meaning of ORS Chapter 249 and other state elections laws.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 689 eff 2004]

2.50.02 Effective Date . This ordinance shall become effective upon the first date allowed by the Oregon
Constitution.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 689 eff 2004]

LEGISLATIVE HISTORY

Adopted via Ordinance No. 689 on 12/14/2000, approved via voter referendum on 11/05/2002, effective
starting in 2004
