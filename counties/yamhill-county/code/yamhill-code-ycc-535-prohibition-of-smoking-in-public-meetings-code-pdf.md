TABLE OF CONTENTS
Section Title
5.35.01 PROHIBITION
5.35.02 DEFINITIONS
5.35.03 PENALTY

LEGISLATIVE HISTORY

5.35.01 Prohibition. No person shall smoke any cigar, cigarette, or tobacco in any form at any meeting of any
public body.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 67 eff 02/26/75]

5.35.02 Definitions. As used in this Ordinance:
a) “Meeting” means any regular or special public meeting, session, or hearing of a public body to exercise or
advise in the matter of any power of government in buildings or rooms rented, leased,  or owned by Yamhill
County, Oregon.
b) “Public body” means Yamhill County, Oregon, any department, agency, board, or commission of Yamhill
County, Oregon.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 67 eff 02/26/75]

5.35.01 Penalty. Violation of Section 5.35.01 is a violation punishable by a fine of $10.00.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 67 eff 02/26/75]
