YAMHILL COUNTY CODE

TITLE 9: PARTNERSHIPS, PROGRAMS, AND COMMITTEES

Chapter 9.35: Authorization of Criminal Background Checks for YCAP

TABLE OF CONTENTS
Section Title
9.35.01 AUTHORIZATION

LEGISLATIVE HISTORY

9.35.01 Authorization. The Yamhill County Community Corrections  Department is authorized to provide
criminal history background checks through the Law Enforcement Data System (LEDS) on behalf of the Yamhill
Community Action Partnership (YCAP) for persons seeking shelter provided by YCAP.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 844 eff 10/21/09]
