SECTION 1
7.05.01.01 Title. This Ordinance shall be known as the Yamhill County Rural Addressing Ordinance.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 381 eff 06/20/84]

7.05.01.02 Purpose. To establish a building address system for  Yamhill County to designate structural location
to minimize the response times of fire, life -saving and other emergency  vehicles in emergency situations; to
facilitate rural delivery while providing for greater public convenience.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 381 eff 06/20/84]

7.05.01.03 Scope. The addressing system of this Ordinance, as set forth in Sections 2 and 3 thereof, shall provide
for the  extension of existing city addressing systems beyond urban growth boundaries, where necessary or
appropriate. The County rural addressing system shall not be applied within existing urban growth boundaries,
except where necessitated by the absence of Comprehensive city addressing systems. In no case  shall persons
continue under the existing system of rural route and box number outside the corporate limits of any city.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 381 eff 06/20/84]

SECTION 2
7.05.02.01 Definitions. The following definitions shall apply to the provisions of this Ordinance:
a) “Baseline” shall mean either of those lines running north and south or east and west, dividing Yamhill County
into four quadrants, more particularly described as follows:
(i) The North-South baseline shall be described as follows: Beginning at the point of intersection of
the Southerly boundary line of Yamhill County with the centerline of U.S. Highway 99W; thence
along said centerline in a northerly and easterly direction to its intersection with the centerline with
State Secondary Highway 47; thence along the centerline of said Highway 47 in a northerly
direction to its intersection with the Northerly boundary of Yamhill County.
(ii) The East-West baseline shall be described as follows: Beginning at the point of intersection of the
Westerly boundary line of Yamhill County with the Township line between Townships 3 and 4;
thence easterly along said Township line to intersection with the centerline of the westerly
extension of County Road 485; thence in a generally easterly direction along said centerline and
the centerline of Baker Creek Road to its intersection with the Centerline of U.S. Highway 99W;
thence in a northerly and easterly direction along said Highway centerline to its intersection with
the centerline of Riverwood Road; thence easterly along the centerline of said Riverwood Road to
its eastern termini; thence easterly to the Willamette River.
b) “Building” shall mean a structure designed or used for a residence, place of business, or other purpose as may
be authorized by County zoning, but does not mean accessory buildings or agricultural structures such as pole
barns.
c) “County Road” shall mean any public road under the jurisdiction of Yamhill County that has been designated
and accepted by the Board of Commissioners as a County Road pursuant to ORS Chapter 368.
d) “Local Access Road ” shall mean any public road under the jurisdiction of Yamhill County which has not
been designated a County road.
e) “Official Road Name Map” shall mean that County Road Map published by the County Road Department,
dated March, 1982, and any subsequent revisions thereto.
f) “Private Road” shall mean any road not dedicated to the public over which private interests exercise control.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 381 eff 06/20/84]

7.05.02.02 Numbering System.  All buildings receiving addresses  shall be numbered in accordance with the
following system of numbering which is hereby designated the Yamhill County Addressing System:
a) All numbering shall be established in accordance with the quadrant and numbering system shown on the
attached map and labeled Exhibit “A” and by this reference incorporated herein. The roads upon which the
major axis lies within 45 degrees of North or South shall be assigned numbers which increase or decrease in

a northerly or southerly direction. In a likewise manner, roads upon which the major axis lies within 45 degrees
of east or west shall be assigned numbers which increase or decrease in an easterly or westerly direction. As
a given road is traversed in a direction which increases the numeric value of addressing (outward from a
baseline), even numbers shall be assigned on the right and odd numbers on the left sides of the roads
respectively.
b) The numbering system shall include 37.88 potential  numbers for every 100 feet of distance along a  road,
providing 2000 numbers per mile.
c) Individual address assignments shall be based upon addressing maps which will be prepared in accordance
with this Ordinance by the Planning and Development Department and updated as required.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 381 eff 06/20/84]

7.05.03.01 Establishing the System . The Planning Director is  hereby authorized to and given the duty of
establishing the numbering of building addresses within Yamhill County in accordance with this Ordinance. The
Director shall give notice to the owners and/or occupants of all buildings receiving new addresses.

SECTION 4
7.05.04.01 Commission to Name Roads . The Planning Commission of Yamhill County is hereby given the
authority to name all  County roads and local access roads. In establishing a  name for a road, the Commission
shall consider the following standards:
a) Except as established on the Road Name Map, or  may be established pursuant to this Ordinance, any local
access or County road outside of an adopted urban growth boundary shall be designated as a “ road”, except
as provided in this Ordinance.
b) An extension of a named road shall bear the same name as the road itself.
c) Designation of other than “ road” may be established  upon a finding that the designation “road” would be
inconsistent with one or more of the criteria contained in this Ordinance.
d) A road inside an adopted urban growth boundary need not be designated “road” unless this is necessary to be
consistent with an extension of the same road outside of said urban growth boundary.
e) The name of the road shall be selected in accordance with the criteria found in Section 7.05.04.02 below.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 381 eff 06/20/84]

7.05.04.02 Road Naming Criteria. The following criteria shall be considered when selecting a road name:
a) Factors of historical significance related to person, place, circumstances or events.
b) Factors of geographical significance.
c) Factors of road location, function or direction.
d) Common usage of a name for the road.
e) Prior use of the name for the road.
f) Name consistency for continuous route including roads in other adjoining jurisdictions.
g) Avoidance of duplication of the same or similar road name.
h) Compliance with the provisions of Section 7.05.04.01 above.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 381 eff 06/20/84]

7.05.03.03 Notice of Road Name. The Planning Commission shall  give notice of all road names selected by
publishing same  in an appropriate newspaper. Selection of the appropriate  newspaper shall be in the sole
discretion of the Commission, provided that the Commission in choosing the appropriate newspaper shall select
that newspaper which in the opinion of the Commission provides the best coverage of the area in which the road
or roads to be named are located.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 381 eff 06/20/84]

SECTION 5
7.05.05.01 Appeal Rights.
a) Any person owning real property to which a number has been assigned by the Planning Director pursuant to
this Ordinance may appeal that decision to the Planning Commission as provided in Section 7.05.05.02 of this
Ordinance.
b) Any person owning real property adjoining a road which has been named by the Commission pursuant to this
Ordinance may appeal that decision to the Board of County Commissioners as provided in Section 7.05.05.02
of this Ordinance.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 381 eff 06/20/84]

7.05.05.02 Manner of Appeal. Any person having a right of appeal as provided in Section 7.05.05.01 above may
exercise that right by mailing or hand-delivering a letter to the Planning Director. Said letter shall state the basis
for the appeal, relief sought, and the person’s standing to appeal under Section 7.05.05.01 of this Ordinance. Upon
receipt of the letter  provided for in this subsection, the Planning Director shall  schedule a hearing before the
Planning Commission or Board of County Commissioners, whichever is applicable, on the first available hearing
date and, by mail, notify the person appealing and all other affected property owners of the time and place of said
hearing.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 381 eff 06/20/84]

7.05.05.03 Review. The Planning Commission or Board of Commissioners, whichever is applicable, shall hold a
public hearing on any appeal filed pursuant to Section 7.05.05.02 of this Ordinance for the purpose of determining
whether the Planning Director or Planning Commission, whichever is applicable, appropriately applied the criteria
of this Ordinance. The decision of the Planning Commission on an appeal under Section 7.05.05.0l(a) is final.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 381 eff 06/20/84]

7.05.05.04 Review. Appeals must be received by the Planning Director no later than thirty days after  the notice
provided for in Section 7.05.03.01 of this Ordinance  has been sent, or the notice provided for in subsection
7.05.04.03 of this Ordinance has been published, whichever is applicable. If no appeal is received within the time
provided for herein, the decision of the Planning Director or Planning Commission, whichever is applicable, shall
be final. No fee shall be charged for any appeals filed pursuant to this section.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 381 eff 06/20/84]

SECTION 6
7.05.06.01 Violations.
a) It shall be a violation of this Ordinance for  any owner or possessor of real property to knowingly violate the
rules of the Planning Director adopted under Section 7.05.03.02 of this Ordinance.
b) It shall be a violation of this Ordinance for any person to knowingly display an address number other than the
one assigned under this Ordinance.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 381 eff 06/20/84]

SECTION 8
7.05.08.01 Road Renaming. Notwithstanding any other provision of this Ordinance, once a road has been named
pursuant to this ordinance, said name may only be changed as provided in this section.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 421 eff 11/20/85]

7.05.08.02 Application. An application to change the name of a road shall be made to the Planning Director in a
form to be determined by the Director. Said application shall include:
a) Signatures of at least 50% of the property owners abutting the road proposed to be renamed. This subsection
shall not apply to those applications filed on or before December 5, 1985.
b) The proposed name for the road.
c) A statement of justification addressing the criteria set forth in Section s 7.05.04.01 and 7.05.04.02 of this
Ordinance.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 421 eff 11/20/85]

7.05.08.03 Fees. Notwithstanding Section 7.05.0 5.04 of this  Ordinance, the fee to be charged for applications
pursuant to Section 8 of this ordinance shall be $100. The Board may from time to time increase or decrease said
fee by board order without necessity of amending this ordinance. This fee shall not be required for any application
filed on or before January 15, 1986.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 421 eff 11/20/85]

7.05.08.04 Notice. Upon receipt of a completed  application and fee, the Director shall  schedule the application
for a hearing before the Planning Commission and provide notice of the date, time and place of said hearing as
follows:
a) Publication in an appropriate newspaper at least 10 days prior to the date of said hearing;
b) By mail to the owners of record of all property abutting said road;
c) By mail to the rural fire protection district serving said road;
d) By mail to the appropriate postmaster serving said road;
e) By written notice to the Yamhill County Sheriff;
f) By written notice to the Yamhill County Department of Public Works;
g) By mail to the Yamhill County Historical Society;
h) By mail to the city nearest to said road.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 421 eff 11/20/85]

7.05.08.05 Hearing Conduct and Criteria. The Planning Commission shall conduct its hearing in accord with
the County Ordinance regulating the conduct of public hearings. The Planning Commission shall base its decision
upon the criteria set forth in Sections  7.05.04.01 and 7.05.04.02. Written notice of the decision of the Planning
Commission shall be sent to all persons who specifically request the same in writing.
[ADOPTED VIA ORDINANCE NO. 421 eff 11/20/85]
