## Full text

BEFORE THE MULTNOMAH COUNTY LIBRARY DISTRICT BOARD
RESOLUTION NO.
Levying Ad Valorem Property Taxes for  the Multnomah County Library District, Oregon, for
Fiscal Year 2022.
The Multnomah County Library District (MCLD) Board Finds:
a. The MCLD Board has adopted the budget for the Multnomah County Library District
for Fiscal Year 2022.
b. That budget provides for ad valorem property taxes to be levied on all property in
Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Library District Board Resolves:
1. The MCLD Board levies the taxes provided for in the adopted budget.
2. These taxes are at the authorized tax rate as follows:
General Government Category
Operating Taxes Tax Rate/$1,000
Permanent Tax Rate $ 1.2200
Total Operating Taxes
$ 1.2200
Excluded From Limitation
Bonded Indebtedness Tax Amount
General Obligation Debt Levy $ 0.00
Total Debt Levy $ 0.00
2021-046
3. These taxes are levied upon all taxable property in Multnomah County.
ADOPTED this 3rd day of June, 2021.
MULTNOMAH COUNTY LIBRARY
DISTRICT BOARD
Deborah Kafoury, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
By
Jon Strauhull, Assistant County Attorney
SUBMITTED BY:
Donald Allgeier, Library Director of Operations
