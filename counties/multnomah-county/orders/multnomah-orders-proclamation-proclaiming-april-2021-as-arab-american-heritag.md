# Proclamation Proclaiming April 2021 As Arab American Heritage Month In Multnomah County. (Proclamation Proclaiming April 2021 As Arab American Heritage Month In Multnomah County.)

## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2021-018
Proclaiming April 2021 as Arab American Heritage Month in Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Whereas, Multnomah County, Oregon is deeply enriched by the diversity of its
residents and is proud to be home to a thriving Arab American population; and
B. Whereas, for over a century, Arab Americans have been making valuable
contributions to virtually every aspect of American society, including in science,
medicine, law, business, education, technology, government, and culture; and
C. Whereas, Victor George Atiyeh, who served as the 32nd Governor of Oregon
from 1979 to 1987, was the first Arab American elected as Governor of a State;
and
D. Whereas, Arab Americans, like all Americans, share the desire to be a part of a
peaceful and diverse society, in which every individual is treated equally and
feels safe; and
E. Whereas, it is imperative to increase awareness about key issues and priorities
within the Arab American community and combat harmful stereotypes, prejudice
and discrimination; and
F. Whereas, the incredible contributions and heritage of Arab Americans have
helped us build a city and a better nation; and
G. Whereas, National Arab American Heritage Month celebrates and honors the rich
contributions of Arab Americans and celebrates Arab American heritage and
culture. It also reflects an opportunity to deepen our understanding and
connection with these communities, and stand in solidarity to respond to hate
crimes, bigotry and misunderstanding and work to dispel stereotypes and
empower all Arab Americans in our communities.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
April 2021 as Arab American Heritage Month in Multnomah County Oregon, and with
this proclamation encourage all residents to observe this month.
ADOPTED this _8th day of April, 2021.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
  Deborah Kafoury, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
By:
 Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
SUBMITTED BY: Benjamin Duncan
