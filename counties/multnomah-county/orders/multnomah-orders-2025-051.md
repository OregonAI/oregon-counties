BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2025-051
Authorizing Staff to Initiate Proceedings to Legalize a Portion of NW Kaiser Road, County
Road No. 5032.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. ORS 368.201 to ORS 368.221 establishes the process and procedures the County
must follow to “legalize” a county road when there are irregularities in the County’s
records with respect to the legal description or otherwise for the road.
B. ORS 368.201(1) -(3) provides authority for a county governing body to initiate
proceedings to legalize a county road where doubt exists as to the legal
establishment of the road due to omission or defect, where the location of the road
cannot be accurately determined due to numerous alternations of the road or a
defective survey of the road or adjacent property, or where the road as traveled
and used for 10 years or more does not conform to the road as described in the
county records.
C. For portions of NW Kaiser Road, County Road No. 5032, from its intersection with
NW Germantown Road, southeasterly to the Washington County line, in the SW
1/4 of Section 8, T. 1N., R. 1W., W.M. (hereafter referred to as “the portion of NW
Kaiser Road”), the road as traveled and used for at least the past ten years does
not conform to the existing records for this county road.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. Pursuant to ORS 368.201(3), authorization to initiate proceedings to legalize the
portion of NW Kaiser Road, County Road No. 5032, is hereby granted.
2. The County Surveyor is directed to survey the portion of NW Kaiser Road to
determine the location of the road and the width of the road in compliance with
ORS 368.206(1)(a).
3. The County Engineer is directed to file a written report with the governing body
including the above referenced survey and any other information required by the
governing body in compliance with ORS 368.206(1)(b).
4. The County Engineer shall set this matter for further proceedings with the Board
of Commissioners to determine if legalization is in the public interest in accordance
with ORS 368.216, and shall provide notice of such proceedings under ORS
368.401 to 368.426 by service to owners of abutting land and by postin g as
required by ORS 368.206(1)(c).
ADOPTED this 21st day of August, 2025.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
