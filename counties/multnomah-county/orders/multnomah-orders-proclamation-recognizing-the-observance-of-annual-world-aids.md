BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2020-099
Recognizing the Observance of Annual World AIDS Day on December 1, 2020 in Multnomah
County, Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. World AIDS Day is recognized each year on December 1st. The theme for this year's
World AIDS Day is “Global Solidarity and Shared Responsibility.” This year marks the
32nd anniversary of World AIDS Day, whose purpose is to show support for people
living with and affected by HIV, and to remember those who have lost their lives to AIDS.
B. Multnomah County supports this theme through our continued commitment to implement
the “END HIV in Oregon Strategy” that includes 3 key points: Testing is easy, Prevention
works, and Treatment saves lives. Multnomah County has much at stake – forty-six
percent (1,082/2,364) of new HIV diagnoses in Oregon from 2009–2018 were among
Multnomah County residents. Multnomah County residents had the highest probability of
10-year survival (89%) compared to mixed urban/rural counties (86%) and more rural
counties of Oregon (83%).
C. The past 7 months have been pivotal to our community of persons living with HIV in light
of the COVID-19 pandemic, which has clearly impacted their economic, health and
quality of life on so many levels. Between the social isolation, limited access to
resources, loss of income, loss of housing, and food insecurity, the profound implications
on this vulnerable population has yet to be realized. Multnomah County to date has
experienced 9,502 total cases (1,156 cases per 100,000) of COVID-19 (not HIV) with
2% death rate as of 10/30/2020.
D. COVID-19 has demonstrated that during a pandemic, a community approach is critical in
a public health crisis that impacts our most vulnerable populations (e.g. persons living
with HIV) coupled with the intersectionality of eliminating white supremacy, stigma and
discrimination, and focusing on human rights and gender responsive approaches.
E. Our collective approaches amongst our community partners has truly exemplified this
true essence of responding to the COVID-19 pandemic and being responsive to the
needs of our HIV community. Collectively we have stood up more communication
channels to better coordinate and align services. We enhanced access to medical
motels and hygiene kits for people that are unhoused and at highest risk of COVID-19
complications. We have increased access to food and home delivered meals for people
without transportation or are homebound.
F. Serving the community for 30 years, Multnomah County HIV Health Services Center
continues to provide comprehensive HIV/HCV medical and social services during this
pandemic, pivoting to use telehealth and telemedicine in addition to in person visits that
includes same day antiretroviral therapy for newly diagnosed persons living with HIV.
G. The Multnomah County STD Clinic offers confidential HIV testing both in house and out
in the community including homeless camps. All testing services are non-judgmental,
confidential, and no one is refused service because of an inability to pay. People at risk
for HIV can also get HIV PrEP, a medication that protects someone from becoming
infected with HIV, at the Multnomah County STD Clinic. Testing is also available through
most health care providers as part of regular medical and family planning care.
H. Community partners supporting this work include Cascade AIDS Project, OHSU
Partnership Project, Ecumenical Ministries of Oregon HIV Day Center, Quest Center for
Integrative Health, Oregon Health Authority, Our House of Portland, HIV Alliance,
Eastern Oregon Center for Independent Living, and more.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
In observance of Annual World AIDS Day on December 1, 2020 in Multnomah County, Oregon,
we demonstrate our support for people living with and affected by HIV, and remember those
who have lost their lives to AIDS.
ADOPTED this 3rd day of December, 2020.
SUBMITTED BY: Commissioner Sharon Meieran, District 1
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Deborah Kafoury, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
