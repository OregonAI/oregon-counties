BEFORE THE MULTNOMAH COUNTY LIBRARY DISTRICT BOARD  RESOLUTION NO.  2023-046  Adopting the Fiscal Year 2024 Budget for the Multnomah County Library District and Making Appropriations Thereunder, Pursuant to ORS 294.456.    The Multnomah County Library District (MCLD) Board Finds:  a. The MCLD budget, as prepared by the duly appointed MCLD Director/Budget Officer, has been considered and approved by the MCLD Board acting as the Budget Committee.    b. A public hearing on this budget was held before the Multnomah County Tax          Supervising and Conservation Commission on the 31st day of May, 2023.  c. The budget is now on file in the Multnomah County Library’s Administrative Office.  d. The MCLD Board has made one amendment to the above described budget and that amendment is attached to this resolution as Attachment A.  e.        The appropriation authorized is as follows:      GENERAL FUND (1520)    Library Services    $ 110,514,127 Cash Transfers    $     2,311,202 Reserved for Future Expenditure   $     4,635,082 Operating Contingency    $     1,000,000 Total Appropriation    $ 118,460,411  Unappropriated Balance   $   18,677,748 Total Requirements    $ 137,138,159   CAPITAL FUND (2514) Library Services     $     1,020,000   Operating Contingency   $   61,187,330 Total Appropriation              $   62,207,330                                                                                          Total Requirements              $   62,207,330  f.        The Tax Supervising and Conservation Commission has certified the budget and there are no recommendations and no objections.
The Multnomah County Library District Board Resolves:
1. The budget, including Attachment A, is adopted as the budget of the MCLD.
2. The appropriation shown above is authorized for the fiscal year July 1, 2023 to June 30,
2024.
ADOPTED this 8th day of June, 2023
MULTNOMAH COUNTY LIBRARY
DISTRICT BOARD
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
