## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2026-007
Acknowledging Receipt of the Multnomah County Annual Comprehensive Financial Report and
Schedule of Expenditures of Federal Awards for Year Ended June 30, 2025.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The County’s audit f or the year ending June 30, 202 5 has been completed and filed
with the appropriate authorities within prescribed deadlines.
b. The County received an unmodified opinion on its financial statements from its external
auditors. Financial statements are presented fairly in accordance with US GAAP.
c. There were no significant deficiencies with Financial Statement Audit or Single Audit
(audit of federal expenditures). No compliance, internal control, or reporting findings.
d. There were no significant deficiencies with the three districts  (Dunthorpe-Riverdale
Service District, Mid County Service District, and Library District)  that have their own
financial report (CUFR).
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The Annual Comprehensive Financial Report for the year ended June 30, 20 25 and the
related audit reports and recommendations are accepted.
2. The Board directs the Department of County Management to implement  the
recommendations made by the County’s external auditors  and resolve  any applicable
compliance and internal control findings.
SUBMITTED BY: Eric Arellano, Chief Financial Officer, Department of County Management
ADOPTED this ___ day of ______, 20__.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
