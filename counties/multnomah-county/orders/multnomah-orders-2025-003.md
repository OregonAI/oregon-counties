## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2025-003
Confirming the Interim Designations for Multnomah County Commissioner District 1,
Multnomah County Commissioner District 2 and Multnomah County Commissioner
District 4.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Multnomah County Charter Section 4.50(3) and Multnomah County Code 5.005
require that elected officials designate an interim occupant to serve until a
vacancy is filled by election or appointment.
B. Charter Section 4.10 states the qualifications for County officers and Code
Section 5.005(B)(4) requires that the designee meet the Charter qualifications for
appointees of such offices.
C. Meghan Moyer, Multnomah County Commissioner District 1, designates Jake
Cornett as interim occupant of that office in the event of a vacancy.
D. Shannon Singleton, Multnomah County Commissioner District 2, designates Dr.
Lisa Bates as interim occupant of that office in the event of a vacancy.
E. Vince Jones-Dixon, Multnomah County Commissioner District 4, designates
Jonathan Ross, as interim occupant of that office in the event of a vacancy.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The Board confirms Jake Cornett to serve as interim occupant for Multnomah
County Commissioner District 1 in the event of a vacancy in that office.
2. The Board confirms Dr. Lisa Bates to serve as interim occupant for Multnomah
County Commissioner District 2 in the event of a vacancy in that office.
3. The Board confirms Jonathan Ross to serve as interim occupant for Multnomah
County District 4 in the event of a vacancy in that office.
ADOPTED this 23rd day of January, 2025.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
By:
Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
SUBMITTED BY: Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
