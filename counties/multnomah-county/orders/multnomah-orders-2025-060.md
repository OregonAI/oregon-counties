# 2025-060 (2025-060)

## At a glance

2025-060 — orders of Multnomah County. 2 pages.

## Full text

BEFORE  THE  BOARD  OF  COUNTY  COMMISSIONERS  FOR  MULTNOMAH  COUNTY,  OREGON   RESOLUTION  NO.  2025-060   Ratification  of  Resolution  No.  2025-060.    The  Multnomah  County  Board  of  Commissioners  Finds:
A.  Supplemental  Nutrition  Assistance  Program  (SNAP)  benefits  are  not  currently
being

funded

due

to

the

ongoing

Federal

government

shutdown.

B.  There  are  approximately  143,000  recipients  of  food  assistance  in  Multnomah
County,

including

over

38,000

children

and

youth

and

18,000

seniors,

and

thousands

of

people

with

disabilities

and

veterans.

C.    The  loss  of  food  assistance  totals  about  $29  million  dollars  monthly.  Other
existing

food

assistance

resources

will

not

be

able

to

cover

the

magnitude

of

this

need.

D.   The  financial  impact  of  losing  this  essential  food  benefit  will  force  many
households

to

make

impossibly

hard

choices

between

paying

for

food,

rent,

utilities

or

other

basic

needs.

E.   The  impact  of  the  loss  of  SNAP  funding  impacts  our  community  partners  who
provide

the

actual

food

resources,

as

well

as

other

social

service

programs

stepping

into

the

gap.

F.  There  are  reports  of  increased  federal  presence  in  parts  of  Multnomah  County,
leading

to

increased

fear

and

trauma

among

Multnomah

County

residents

G.        On  October  28,  2025,  at  the  direction  of  the  Chair,  Multnomah  County  moved  to
Partial

Activation

of

the

Emergency

Operations

Center

(EOC).

The

initial

steps

of

this

response

will

ensure

the

coordination

of

operations

between

County

offices

and

departments,

promote

alignment

on

internal

and

external

communications,

and

further

the

role

that

Multnomah

County

can

play

to

leverage,

coordinate,

and

deploy

critical

resources.

The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The Board requests that the County EOC provide ongoing situational monitoring,
and coordinate community assistance efforts and provide resources to those
affected. The Board requests continued regular situational assessment reports to
Commission offices.
2. To support this policy, the Board directs the County to produce an initial report of
recommended actions to the Board no later than November 6, 2025 and weekly
updates thereafter that considers:
a. The impact from the loss of SNAP benefits from the U.S. Department of
Agriculture to those needing food assistance.
b. The impact of federal presence in Multnomah County that increases fear,
trauma and disruption to families and their access to services.
c. Outline of actions to coordinate services being provided by state and local
governments, school districts, and community organizations.
d. What resources (funding, logistical, etc) are needed to implement a
response and the best use of any county resources or financial
assistance.
3. To convene regional partners to better coordinate the food assistance
ecosystem.
ADOPTED this 31st day of October, 2025.
Effective on the 6th day of November, 2025.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
