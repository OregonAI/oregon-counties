# Order Appointing Eric Burgin, Emily Soiney Iverson, And Charlie Fuchs As Commissioners To The Ramsey-Walmar Special Road District. (Order Appointing Eric Burgin, Emily Soiney Iverson, And Charlie Fuchs As Commissioners To The Ramsey-Walmar Special Road District.)

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
ORDER NO.  2022-018
Appointing Eric Burgin, Emily Soiney Iverson, and Charlie Fuchs as Commissioners to
the Ramsey-Walmar Special Road District.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a) The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners formed the Ramsey -Walmar
Special Road District by Order on September 19, 1985, in accordance with ORS
371.305 to 371.360 for the purpose of providing regular maintenance of roads
within the District not maintained by Multnomah County because such roads  do
not meet County standards.
b) ORS 371.338 provides the powers of the special road district vested in a board of
three commissioners appointed by the Board of Commissioners.
c) The terms of the most recent appointments to the District Board, Eric Burgin, Jason
Plush, and Andrew Inman have expired.
d) Ramsey-Walmar Special Road District resident  Charlie Fuchs, and returning
members Eric Burgin and Emily Soiney Iverson,  have been nominated to fill the
three positions; they have indicated they are willing and able to serve.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Orders:
1. Pursuant to ORS 371.338(1), Eric Burgin, Emily Soiney Iverson, and Charlie Fuchs
are appointed as follows:
a) Eric Burgin  is re-appointed as a commissione r of the Ramsey -Walmar
Special Road District for a one-year term beginning January 1, 2022 and
ending on December 31, 2022.
b) Emily Soiney Iverson is re -appointed as a commissioner of the Ramsey
Walmar Special Road District, for a three -year term to begin January 1,
2021 and ending December 31, 2023.
c) Charlie Fuchs, is appointed as a commissioner of the Ramsey -Walmar
Special Road District, for a three year term to begin January 1, 2022 and
ending December 31, 2024.
2. Pursuant to ORS 371.338(3), before entering upon the duties of office, Eric Burgin,
Emily Soiner  Iverson,  and  Charlie  Fuchs  shall  take  and  subscribe  an  oath  to
support the Constitution and laws of the State of Oregon and of the United States,
and to well and faithfully perform the duties of office to the best of their knowledge
and ability.
ADOPTED this 17th day of March, 2022.
SUBMITTED BY: Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Deborah Kafoury, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
