## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2020-055
Authorizing Staff to Initiate Proceedings to Legalize a Portion of NW Rocky Point Road, County Road No.
445
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. ORS 368.201 to ORS 368.221 establishes the process and procedures the County must follow to
“legalize” a county road when there are irregularities in the County’s records with respect to the legal
description or otherwise for the road.
b. ORS 368.201(1)-(3) provides authority for a county governing body to initiate proceedings to legalize
a county road where doubt exists as to the legal establishment of the road due to omission or defect,
where the location of the road cannot be accurately determined due to numerous alternations of the
road or a defective survey of the road or adjacent property, or where the road as traveled and used
for 10 years or more does not conform to the road as described in the county records.
c. For portions of NW Rocky Point Road, County Road No. 445, between Engineer’s Stations 75+91.54
and 84+40.70 (hereafter referred to as “ the portion of NW Rocky Point Rd.”) , the road as traveled
and used for at least the past ten years does not conform to the existing records for this county road.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. Pursuant to ORS 368.201, authorization to initiate proceedings to legalize  the portion of NW Rocky
Point Rd., County Road No. 445, is hereby granted.
2. The County Surveyor is directed to survey the portion of NW Rocky Point Rd. to determine the
location of the road and the width of the road in compliance with ORS 368.206(1)(a).
3. The County Engineer is  directed to file a written report with the governing body including the above
referenced survey and any other information required by the governing body in c ompliance with ORS
368.206(1).
4. The County Engineer shall set this matter for further proceedings to determine if legalization is in the
public interest in accordance with ORS 368.216, and shall provide notice of such proceedings under
ORS 368.401 to 368.426 by service to owners of abutting land and by posting  as required by ORS
368.206(1)(c).
ADOPTED this 25th day of June, 2020.
Submitted by: Jaime Waltz, Interim Director, Department of Community Services
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Deborah Kafoury, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
