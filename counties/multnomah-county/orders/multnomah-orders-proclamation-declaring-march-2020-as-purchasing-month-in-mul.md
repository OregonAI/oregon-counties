## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2020-015
Declaring March 2020 as Purchasing Month in Multnomah County, Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Purchasing and materials management professionals play a significant role in the
efficiency and effectiveness of both government and business.
b. Purchasing and materials management professionals, through their combined purchasing
power, leverage billions of dollars every year and therefore have a significant influence
upon economic conditions throughout the world.
c. Public procurement, contracting .and materials management professionals commit
themselves to providing the best value for every taxpayer dollar, and advance their
knowledge, skills and abilities toward that end.
d. The Oregon Public Purchasing Association (OPPA) and the National Institute of
Governmental Purchasing (NIGP), along with other purchasing associations throughout
the world, celebrate Purchasing Month by engaging in special efforts during the month of
March to inform the public about the contributions of purchasing professionals in
business, industry and government.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
1. March 2020 is proclaimed to be Purchasing Month in Multnomah County, Oregon.
ADOPTED this 5th day of March, 2020.
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
