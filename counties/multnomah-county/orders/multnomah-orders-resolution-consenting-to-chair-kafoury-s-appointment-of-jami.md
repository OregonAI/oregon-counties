## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO.  2020-081
Consenting to Chair Kafoury’s Appointment of Jamie Waltz as the Director of the Department
of Community Services.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3) provides that the Chair’s appointment of
department heads (directors) is subject to consent of a majority of the Board.
b. The Chair appointed Jamie Waltz as the Director of the Department of Community
Services beginning September 24, 2020.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
In accordance with Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3), the Board consents to the
appointment of Jamie Waltz as the Director of the Department of Community Services.
ADOPTED this 24th day of September, 2020.
SUBMITTED BY: Peggy Brey, Interim COO.
     Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Deborah Kafoury, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
