## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2023-055
Confirming Interim Designation for Multnomah County Commissioner, District 3.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Multnomah County Charter Section 4.50(3) and Multnomah County Code 5.003 require
that elected officials designate an interim occupant to serve until a vacancy is filled.
b. Charter Section 4.10 states the  qualifications for County officers and Code Section
5.003(B)(4) requires that the designee meet the Charter qualifications for appointees of such
offices.
c. Julia Brim-Edwards, Multnomah County Commissioner, District 3, designates Annette
Mattson as interim occupant of that office in the event of a vacancy.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
The Board confirms Annette Mattson to serve as interim occupant for the Multnomah
County Commissioner, District 3 in the event of a vacancy in that office.
ADOPTED this 15th day of June, 2023.
SUBMITTED BY: Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY , OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
By
 Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
