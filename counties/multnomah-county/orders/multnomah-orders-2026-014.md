## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2026-14
Requesting approval of a resolution to amend contract DCS-CNSTR-17767-2025
with Hamilton Construction Co. for the Morrison Strengthening Project by an
additional $2,100,000.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. The Board, acting as the Multnomah County Public Contract Review Board
(PCRB) and pursuant to PCRB Rule 49-0910(3), must approve contract
price increases beyond 20 percent of the original contract price for
contracts greater than $150,000;
B. The Morrison Strengthening Project (the “Project”) has been awarded grant
revenue that exceeds the current estimated completion cost of the Project.
In order to maximize use of the available grant funds, additional
strengthening and rehabilitation work will be added to the current contract
up to the value of the grant;
C. This amendment (the “Amendment”) will compensate the contractor for the
additional construction work, which includes installation of structural
bracing, joint repairs, and resurfacing of a failed portion of the driving
surface; and
D. The added work is within the general scope of the original contract, and
this Amendment is in accordance with the requirements of Public Contract
Review Board Rule 49-0910.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
To amend and increase the total amount of contract DCS-CNSTR-17767-2025
with Hamilton Construction Co. for the Morrison Strengthening Project by an
additional $2,100,000 for a total contract value of $3,694,850 for the Project.
ADOPTED this12th day of March, 2026.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
 SUBMITTED BY: Margi Bradway, Director
