# Proclamation 40-Mile Loop. (Proclamation 40-Mile Loop.)

## At a glance

## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY , OREGON
______PROCLAMATION NO.
40-Mile Loop Proclamation
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. At the request of the 40-Mile Loop Land Trust, the 40-Mile Loop Master Plan was developed
by David Evans & Associates 40 years ago and was published in May, 1983;
B. WHEREAS, 40 years ago the 40-Mile Loop Master Plan called for the development of a
system of trails and pathways throughout urbanized Multnomah County, connecting parks,
natural areas, commercial and employment centers, and neighborhoods;
C. WHEREAS, the name “40-Mile Loop” honors a vision of interconnected parks articulated 80
years earlier by renowned landscape architect John Charles Olmsted;
D. WHEREAS, 40 years ago the rebirth of an effort to provide a connected system of trails and
pathways has served our community members well by providing safe places for people to walk,
roll, and bicycle separated from traffic;
E. WHEREAS, the 40-Mile Loop has inspired an expanded vision of a regional system of trails
and pathways connecting all cities in the Portland metropolitan area and beyond;
F . WHEREAS, the 40-Mile Loop Land Trust acknowledges and thanks local governments and
non-profit partners, including Multnomah County, for their willingness to adopt trail and path
alignments called for by the 40-Mile Loop Master Plan, for their commitment to design, fund,
construct and maintain segments of the 40-Mile Loop; and
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
1. That, in honor of the 40th anniversary of the 40-Mile Loop Master Plan , the 40-MILE
LOOP and the REGIONAL TRAIL SYSTEM are integral parts of a safe, healthy, and
sustainable urban ecosystem for all community members and shall continue to be
recognized as such going forward.
ADOPTED this 31st day of August, 2023.
2023-074 BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
By
Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
