## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2020-038
Proclaiming June 15, 2020 as Elder Abuse Awareness Day in
Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Elder abuse is an issue that affects millions of older adults each year.
It takes many forms, including physical abuse, verbal abuse, neglect,
exploitation, as well as issues of self-neglect.
b. People 60 years and older have been asked to take extreme caution
because of COVID-19, including limiting contact with people outside of
their own household.   In some situations, this increased isolation and
reduced contact with caregivers, friends, family or medical providers
may result in additional risk for older adults.
c. Recognizing and reporting  abuse or concerns for the well -being of
older adults in our community is up to all of us.   Despite the need to
stay physically apart, we must ensure that strong community support
and social connections exist in order to reduce the likelihood of abuse
and self-neglect.
d. Providing education around recogni zing the signs of abuse or self -
neglect and encouraging the public to engage in intentional and
creative outreach to older adults is imp ortant to supporting their well -
being and safety.
e. Multnomah County recognizes the important link between social
connections and overall physical and mental health.  During this time,
the encouragement and promotion of safe social connections will help
to ensure older adults continue to be thriving members of our
communities.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
June 15, 2020 as Elder Abuse Awareness Day in Multnomah County. We
urge all agencies, advocates and citizens to commit to building safer
communities for our el der residents.  We ask everyone to reach out and
connect with an older adult in your life.  Connection through phone, email,
letter or virtual visiting can have a critical impact to ensure people stay
socially connected and able to access needed support for their physical and
emotional well-being.
ADOPTED this 4th day of June, 2020.
SUBMITTED BY:  Mohammad Bader, Director
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
  Deborah Kafoury, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
