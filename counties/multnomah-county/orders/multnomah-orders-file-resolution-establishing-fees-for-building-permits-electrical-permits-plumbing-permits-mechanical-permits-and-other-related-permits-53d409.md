BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-071
Establishing Fees for Building Permits, Electrical Permits, Plumbing Permits,
Mechanical Permits, and Other Related Permits pursuant to Chapter 29 of the
Multnomah County Code and Repealing Resolution No. 2023-056.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
 a.  Chapter  29,  Building  Regulations,  of  the  Multnomah  County  Code  (MCC)
 provides that the Board shall establish certain fees by resolution.
 b.  On  June  15,  2023,  the  Board  adopted  Resolution  No.  2023-056  establishing
 MCC Chapter 29 fees applying to building permits and other related permits.
 c.  Multnomah  County  has  entered  into  intergovernmental  agreements  with  the  cities
 of  Portland,  Gresham,  and  Troutdale  to  administer  and  enforce  portions  of  MCC
 Chapter 29 within unincorporated Multnomah County.
 d.  Portland  proposes  the  fees  for  its  administration  and  enforcement  of  MCC
 Chapter  29  within  the  Portland  Permit  Services  Area  which  are  then  set  by
 Multnomah  County  under  the  terms  of  the  intergovernmental  agreement  (IGA).
 Most  fees  within  the  Portland  Services  Boundary  are  increasing  in  this  Resolution
 by  an  estimated  8%.  Other  fees  relating  to  the  City’s  Field  Issuance  Remodel
 Program  increasing  roughly  15%.  The  City  of  Portland  has  approved  increased
 fees  effective  July  1,  2024,  under  the  Oregon  Structural  Specialty  Code  in
 accordance with OAR 918-020-0220 and ORS 455.210.
 e.  Fees  for  building  services  provided  by  the  City  of  Gresham  under  the  IGA  are  as
 set by the City of Gresham and need not be adopted by the County.
 f.  Fees  for  building  services  provided  by  the  City  of  Troutdale  under  the  IGA  are  as
 set by the City of Troutdale and need not be adopted by the County.

 39.1245 are set out in the attached Exhibit A (Building and Other;
