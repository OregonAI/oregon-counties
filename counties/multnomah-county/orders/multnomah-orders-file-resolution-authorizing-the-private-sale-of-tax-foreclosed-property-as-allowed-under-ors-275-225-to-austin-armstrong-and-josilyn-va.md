BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-064
Authorizing the Private Sale of Tax Foreclosed Property as Allowed Under ORS 275.225 to Austin Armstrong
and Josilyn Vankat and Execution of Deed and Closing Documents
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Multnomah County acquired through the foreclosure of liens for delinquent real property taxes certain
real property located in Multnomah County on June 3, 1971, more particularly described in a copy of a
proposed deed, attached as Exhibit A (“Property”).
b. ORS 275.225 allows the County to sell County owned property, including property acquired through tax
foreclosure, at a private sale if that property meets certain conditions with respect to value and the
applicable zoning and building codes.
c. The Property has a real market value of less than $15,000.00 on each constituent account on the
assessment roll prepared for the County, consistent with the requirement of ORS 275.225(1)(a).
d. The zoning jurisdiction and record and current use indicates that the Property is currently unsuitable for
the construction or placement of a dwelling thereon under applicable zoning ordinances and building
codes, as provided under ORS 275.225(1)(b).
e. The County has received payment in the amount of $110.00 from Austin Armstrong and Josilyn Vankat,
(“Purchasers”), an amount the Board finds to be a reasonable price for the Property in conformity with
ORS 275.225.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The County Chair is authorized to execute a deed, in substantial conformance with the deed attached as
Exhibit A, conveying the Property to Purchasers, in consideration of $110.00.
ADOPTED the 15th day of August, 2024.
