# Proclamation Proclaiming June 15Th, 2024 As Elder Abuse Awareness Day In Multnomah County, Oregon. (Proclamation Proclaiming June 15Th, 2024 As Elder Abuse Awareness Day In Multnomah County, Oregon.)

## Full text

Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2024-051
Proclaiming June 15th, 2024 as Elder Abuse Awareness Day in Multnomah County, Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Older adults deserve to be safe and should be treated with respect and dignity to enable
them to serve as leaders, mentors, volunteers and thriving members of our communities.
B. Elder Abuse Awareness Day is an opportunity for our community to raise awareness and
promote a better understanding of the issue of abuse, neglect and self -neglect of older
persons.
C. Elder abuse is an issue that affects millions of seniors every year. It takes many forms,
including physical abuse, verbal abuse, neglect, financial exploitation, and issues of self-
neglect. Elder abuse may be perpetrated by family members, friends, caregi vers and
strangers.
D. Recognizing and reporting abuse or concerns for the well being of older adults in our
community is a responsibility shared by all of us. Providing education around recognizing
the signs of abuse or self neglect and encouraging the public to share informati on with
older adults is important to supporting their well being and safety.
E. Effectively responding to elder abuse requires a connected and coordinated local services
system. Collaboration throughout Multnomah County and the community are an important
partnership that supports the well-being, safety and independence of our most vulnerable
citizens.
SUBMITTED BY: Mohammad Bader, DCHS Director
Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
By
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
June 15, 2024 as Elder Abuse Awareness Day in Multnomah County. We encourage all agencies,
advocates and citizens to recognize the importance of building a safe and inclusive community
for older adults.
ADOPTED this 13th day of June, 2024.
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
