## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2021-006
Acknowledging Receipt of the Multnomah County Comprehensive Annual Financial Report and
Schedule of Expenditures of Federal Awards for Year Ended June 30, 2020.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The County’s audit f or the year ending June 30, 2020  has been completed and filed
with the appropriate authorities within prescribed deadlines.
b. The County received an unqualified opinion on its financial statements from its external
auditors.
c. The audit identified two instances where a “Significant Deficiency in Internal Controls”
exists and management has resolved one and is developing a correction action plan
for the second. The two instances are in connection with the audit of the County’s
expenditures of Federal awards (Single Audit).
d. There were no significant deficiencies with the three districts  (Dunthorpe-Riverdale
Service District, Mid County Service District, and Library District)  that have their own
financial report (CUFR).
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The Comprehensive Annual Financial Report for the year ended June 30, 20 20 and the
related audit reports and recommendations are accepted.
2. The Board directs the Department of County Management to implement  the
recommendations made by the County’s external auditors.
ADOPTED this 4th day of February, 2021.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Deborah Kafoury, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
