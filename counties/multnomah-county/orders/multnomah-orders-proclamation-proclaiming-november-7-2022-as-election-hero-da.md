## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2022-095
Proclaiming November 7, 2022, as Election Hero Day in Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Election Hero Day is an opportunity to honor and show our appreciation for the
essential contributions our Election Workers make to our democracy and electoral
process here in Multnomah County.
B. This day also gives us an opportunity to bring awareness to the intimidation,
harassment, and attacks that have occurred towards Election Workers around the
country for the last few years. We acknowledge these attacks are harmful and
create undue stress and trauma for all Election Workers.
C. We acknowledge that our ability to provide fair, accessible, and accurate elections
depends on the service of these dedicated Election Workers. They truly are
upholding our democracy with their service to our Elections Division.
D. Today, we honor and affirm our commitment to providing a respectful and safe
workplace for our Election Workers, while also working alongside our partners and
other jurisdictions to ensure our workers will be protected from intimidation and
harassment while performing their essential Elections Administration duties.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
Monday, November 7, 2022, as Election Hero Day in Multnomah County and encourages
everyone to thank an Election Worker!
ADOPTED this 3
rd day of November, 2022.
SUBMITTED BY: Jamie Waltz, Department of Community Services Department Director
     Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Deborah Kafoury, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
