## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2023-067
Declaring July 16th through July 22nd, 2023 as Pretrial, Probation and Parole Supervision
Week in Multnomah County, Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Employees of the Department of Community Justice (DCJ) possess a wide variety
of skills and experience that helps us attain the vision of Community Safety through
Positive Change.
b. Annually, DCJ supervises over 7,500 adult probationers and parolees, processes
over 13,000 cases in our Recognizance unit, receives over 2,200 youth referrals,
serves approximately 1,010 youth and their families which includes diversion,
informal and formal supervision, and detention, provides victim advocacy services
to over 1,800 victims and survivors, and completes over 1,000 mediations between
parents involved in custody disputes.
c. DCJ staff treats justice-involved adults, youth, and their families with dignity, while
recognizing the right of the public to be safeguarded from criminal activity.
d. DCJ inclusively leads with race by recognizing and addressing the history of
systemic inequities to become an equitable, inclusive, and racially just
organization.
e. DCJ works in partnership with community agencies and law enforcement towards
a shared vision of a safer community and participates in critical reform efforts
addressing disparities in our system.
f. DCJ staff use best practices when holding justice-involved individuals accountable
as they deliver supervision, sanctions, and treatment resources to improve
community safety and address the factors that drive crime.
g. DCJ is dedicated to providing services and protection for crime victims and
survivors.
h. DCJ staff recognizes that their public safety impact is long term, helping individuals
to change their behavior, restore their families and build stronger and safer
communities.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
July 16th through July 22nd, 2023 is declared PRETRIAL, PROBATION and PAROLE
SUPERVISION WEEK in Multnomah County, Oregon, in honor, recognition and respect
for the dedication and contributions of the County’s community justice staff.
ADOPTED this 20th day of July, 2023.
 SUBMITTED BY: Erika Preuitt, Director, Department of Community Justice
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
By
Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
