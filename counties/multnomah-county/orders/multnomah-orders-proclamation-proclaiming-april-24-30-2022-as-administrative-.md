## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2022-034
Proclaiming April 24-30, 2022 as Administrative Professionals Week.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Administrative professionals play an essential role in coordinating the office operations of
businesses, government, educational institutions, and other organizations.
b. Administrative professionals are vital contributors in today's team -oriented work environment and
are key front-line public relations ambassadors for their organizations.
c. Administrative Professionals Week is observed annually in workplaces nationwide to recognize
the important contributions of administrative professionals.
d. The work of administrat ive professionals requires advanced knowledge and expertise in
communications, computer software, data management, research, office technology, project
management, organization, relationship building, and customer service.
e. The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners recognizes the importance and contributions of
highly skilled administrative professionals to the success of the County and our service to the
community.
f. The Board is extremely proud of the County’s talented and highly skilled Administrative
Professional staff and officially recognizes and appreciates their hard work and supports their
continued professional growth.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
The week of April 24 through 30, 2022 as Administrative Professionals Week.
ADOPTED this 28th day of April, 2022.
SUBMITTED BY: Serena Cruz, Chief Operating Officer
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Deborah Kafoury, Chair
REVIEWED:
JE
NNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
By
     Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
