BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY , OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-063
Homelessness Response System Progress Resolution.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. The United States, the State of Oregon, the City of Portland, and Multnomah
County are all experiencing a homelessness crisis, primarily caused by a severe
shortage of affordable housing with chronic homelessness compounded by
severe persistent mental illness and addiction with severely limited access to
treatment and housing that has resulted in unprecedented numbers of people
living and dying unsheltered.
B. As of January 2024, there were 11,153 people identified who were
experiencing all forms of literal homelessness in Multnomah County.
Compared to the overall local population, a disproportionate number
identified as disabled, Black, Native American, and/or Native Hawaiian/
Pacific Islander.
C. The continuation of the coordinated development and delivery of services
supporting and otherwise geared to individuals struggling with homelessness and
housing insecurity, as well as substance use and behavioral health issues, will
improve the delivery of services and enhance outcomes for those receiving such
services and for the region as a whole.
D. On July 3, 2024, Portland City Council adopted Ordinance 191810 authorizing
three-year Homelessness Response System Intergovernmental Agreement
(HRS IGA) between Multnomah County and the City of Portland.
E. On July 11, 2024, the Multnomah County Board of Commissioner adopted the
HRS IGA and related Homelessness Response Action Plan (HRAP).
F . The HRS IGA includes the related HRAP , a written plan developed by Multnomah
County, the City of Portland, and community leaders for implementing the
Homelessness Response System. The HRAP is a two-year roadmap that
commits to pursuing detailed goals and metrics; more transparent budgeting,
data sharing and financial reporting; and a new governance structure that
broadens and unifies the work of addressing homelessness.
G. Through the HRS IGA, the City of Portland and Multnomah County have agree
to develop a collaborative and mutually accountable governance structure
overseeing the work of the HRAP , formalizing collaboration between healthcare
partners, community members, the justice system, housing providers, service
providers, treatment providers and government partners at all levels.
H. TheHRAPincludesmorethan120individualactionitems,eachwithtime-boundgoalsandassignedresponsibleparties,includingclearlydefinedrolesfortheCityofPortlandandMultnomahCounty.
I. TheHRSIGAcommitsboththeCityofPortlandandMultnomahCountytoprovidingAnnualProgramPlansandAnnualSpendingPlansthatdetailannualbudgets,contractedproviders,anticipatedoutcomes,andstrategyadjustmentstoimproveoutcomes;AnnualProgramReportsthatdetailactualspendingandoutcomes;andQuarterlyProgressReportsthatdetailprogresstowardkeyperformanceindicators,annualspendingplans,andanymaterialchangesinprogrammingorstrategiestobettermeetsharedHRAPgoals.
J. ContinuingthespiritofpartnershipandmutualaccountabilityarticulatedbetweentheCityofPortlandandMultnomahCountythroughtheHRSIGAandHRAP, thisResolutionseekstoaffirmandclarifyreportingandaccountabilitymeasuresincludedintheCityofPortland’sOrdinance191810,theHRSIGA,andtheHRAP.
TheMultnomahCountyBoardofCommissionersResolvesthatMultnomahCountyshallbe:
1. Acommunitywherehomelessnessisrareandbrief.
2. Acommunitywherepeopletakecareofoneanother, fosteringopportunityandsupportinglivability.
3. Acommunitywhereservicesareequitable,coordinated,effective,justandaccessible.
TheMultnomahCountyBoardofCommissionersResolves:
1. ToestablishthefollowingBoardpoliciesandsupportactionsregardingreportingandaccountabilitymeasuresbyOctober15,2024,including:
a. Creatingandpublishingpublicly-facingdashboardstomonitorprogresstowardsgoalsandoutcomesandinvestments.b. HoldingquarterlyjointpublicworksessionsbetweentheCityCouncilandCountyCommission.c. TheJointOfficeofHomelessServiceslaunchingashelteravailabilityapplicationpilottoreportavailableaccessandappropriateplacementspotsavailable,tobetrackedinrealtime.d. TheJointOfficeofHomelessServicesprovidingadetailedplanonthe555bedsofadditionaladultshelterthatisplannedtoopenbyDecember2024.e. TheCityofPortlandwillopenTASSsite#2and#3bySeptember30,2024.
f. The Joint Office of Homeless Services launching a pilot to secure up to
200 block/primarily leased units to support rapid rehousing placement.
g. Multnomah County completing an analysis of current supportive housing
providers and developing a six-month plan to bill for Medicaid eligible
services.
h. The Homelessness Response System Steering and Oversight
Committee will have a policy discussion regarding the distribution of
tents and tarps by Multnomah County and its service providers within
the City of Portland and inform the City of Portland of Multnomah
County’s policy.
i. The Joint Office of Homeless Services finalizing an outreach and
engagement strategy workgroup and steering committee, including City of

j. The City of Portland will report on their identified ways to support the

and implementation.
