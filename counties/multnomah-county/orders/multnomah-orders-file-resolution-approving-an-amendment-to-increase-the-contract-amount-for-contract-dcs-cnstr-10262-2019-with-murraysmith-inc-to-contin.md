BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2020-060
Approving an Amendment to Increase the Contract Amount for Contract DCS-CNSTR-10262-2019
with Murraysmith,  Inc.,  to continue  construction engineering  support services  for  the  NW Cornelius
Pass Road project.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The Board, acting as the Multnomah County Public Contract Review Bo ard (PCRB) and pursuant
to PCRB R ule 48-0320(5), must approve contract price increases beyond 33 percent of the
original contract price.
b. The County awarded a contract to Murraysmith, Inc. (Consultant) for $173,564 on February 12,
2019 for construction support services for the NW Cornelius Pass  Road (US30 – NW Kaiser
Road) Improvement Project (Project) . Amendment 1 executed on May 6, 2020 increased the
maximum amount payable to $225,879. A second amendment will increase the maximum amount
payable to $399,490. Board approval is requ ired because the maximum payable now exceeds the
originally approved contract amount by more than 33%.
c. This increase is necessary because construction delays during 2019 make a second season of
construction necessary, which in turn requires continued construction engineering support by
Murraysmith.
d. This Amendment will compensate the Consultant for work remaining to complete the Project.
e. This Amendment is in accordance with the requirements of Public Contract Review Board Rule
48-0320(5).
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners , acting as the Multnomah County Public
Contract Review Board, hereby Resolves:
1. To amend and  increase the total amount of Contract Number DCS-CNSTR-10262-2019 with
Murraysmith, Inc., by an additional $ 225,926 for a total contract amount of $399,490 for the NW
Cornelius Pass Road Project.
ADOPTED this 2nd day of July, 2020.

Task 1.6 Consultation During Construction
Consultant shall provide consultation and technical services regarding design issues raised during
construction of the Project. Consultant shall clarify construction contract documents and provide
written responses to Requests for Information (RFIs). In addition, Consultant shall provide support
services to facilitate electronic data transfer to the CC.  Support will depend on the CC’s needs for
construction staking layout, but is anticipated to include generation of electronic grade reports from the
design surface at the frequency/location requested and/or review of files provided by the CC for
machine control grading. The design consultation will occur only as required and may be ongoing
throughout the CA/CEI Services and the Project.

Upon request of the CC or Agency during construction, Consultant shall:
• Clarify construction contract documents.
• Respond to field inquiries.
• Engage the services of the Professional Of Record (“POR”) on all matters involving design
changes.
• Provide support to electronic data translation to the field for use in construction.
• Provide supplemental field inspection services at the request of the Agency.

Consultant shall also coordinate with Agency on up to one (1) minor Federal Aid Highway Programmatic
Biological Opinion (FAHP) change that shall not require a FAHP Change Request Form during
construction. These changes will be incorporated into the FAHP Project Completion Report in Task
1.10.

Assumptions: For budgetary purposes, assume up to thirty (30) RFI’s and thirty (30) informal (phone
call or email) inquiries.  Also, assume up to ten (10) field trips.

Deliverables:
Consultant shall provide:
• Verbal response to phone or in person inquiries from the Agency.  E-mail or written documentation
(electronic and one (1) hard copy) of responses to CC inquiries to Agency.
• Attendance at field visits as requested by Agency and follow up Site Visit Reports (electronic or
one (1) hard copy); and
• Written notes (electronic) regarding minor FAHP change communication with Agency.

Schedule:
• Verbal responses – within one (1) day, unless agreed to by both Parties.
• E-mail or written responses - courtesy response within one (1) day to acknowledge receipt of
request, then full response within three (3) business days, or as agreed to by both Parties.
• Field visits – as determined by Agency.
• Site Visit Reports – within one (1) day of the Field Visit or a timeframe agreed to by both Parties.
• Written notes regarding minor FAHP change within three (3) business days of communication and
resolution with Agency.
