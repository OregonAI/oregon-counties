## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2025-059
Consenting to Chair Vega Pederson’s Appointment of Stacy Cowan as the Director of
Government Relations for Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3) and Code Section 25.610 provide
that the Chair’s appointment of the Director of Government Relations is subject to
consent of a majority of the Board.
B. The Chair appoints Stacy Cowan as the Director of Government Relations
beginning October 10, 2025.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
The Board consents to the appointment of Stacy Cowan as the Director of Government
Relations.
ADOPTED this 9th day of October, 2025.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
By:
Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
SUBMITTED BY: Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
