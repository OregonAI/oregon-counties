## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2021-087
Authorizing the Annual Designation of a Portion of Compensation as a Housing Allowance
for Chaplains Serving Inmates and Employees of the Multnomah County Sheriff’s Office.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The Multnomah County Sheriff’s Office employs  Lewis Kyle as chaplain serving
adults in custody and employees of Multnomah County Sheriff’s Office.
b. The Sheriff’s Office does not provide housing to Chaplain Kyle.
c. 26 USC § 107(2) allows clergy to exclude from the calculation of their gross income the
housing allowance paid as part of their compensat ion, to the extent used by them to rent
or provide a home.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
The following chaplain  is allowed to designate the following amounts of his
compensation as housing allowance for calendar year 20 22, subject to the
requirements and limitations set forth in the Internal Revenue Code and all other
applicable regulations:
Housing Allowance
Chaplain Amount Per Annum
Lewis Kyle $40,000
ADOPTED this 2nd day of December, 2021.
Deborah Kafoury, Chair
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Carlos J. Calandriello, Assistant County Attorney
