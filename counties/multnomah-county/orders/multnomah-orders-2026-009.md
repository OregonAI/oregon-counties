BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2026-009
Establishing Fees and Charges for Chapter 13 of the Multnomah County Code Relating
to Animal Control and Repealing Resolution No. 2019-067.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Chapter 13 of the Multnomah County Code (“MCC”) informs Animal Control and
provides that the Board will establish certain fees and charges by resolution.
b. On June 27, 2019, the Board adopted Resolution No. 2019-067 establishing fees
for MCC Chapter 13.
c. The Board wishes to maintain current licensing and adoption fees and to
eliminate impoundment, boarding, and other specific fees, as shown in the chart
below.
d. Multnomah County Animal Services (“MCAS”) Director has discretion and
authority to allow fee waivers or reduction of any fee with the intent to not create
undue hardship on low-income people and to create incentives for adoption or
reunification, when necessary.
e. MCAS conducted a fee study, wherein the current fee structure was assessed,
determining the percentage of operations costs recovered through fees and
comparing some fees with other jurisdictions.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The fees and charges for MCC Chapter 13 are set as follows:
Section 13.004: SPAYING AND NEUTERING ENCOURAGED.
The amount set aside by the Board from revenue generated from pet licensing to
cover the cost of the county’s public educational, spaying and neutering
programs is $25,000.
Sections 13.501 and 13.508: APPEALS.
Appeal fee of a notice of infraction or decision under this chapter is: $ 25.00
Section 13.511: IMPOUNDMENT PENDING APPEAL.
(A) Notice of infraction appeal impoundment deposit $100.00
(B) Writ of review sheltering deposit $500.00
