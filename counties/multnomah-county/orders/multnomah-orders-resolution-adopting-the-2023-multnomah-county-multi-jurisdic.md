# Resolution Adopting The 2023 Multnomah County Multi-Jurisdictional Natural Hazards Mitigation Plan. (Resolution Adopting The 2023 Multnomah County Multi-Jurisdictional Natural Hazards Mitigation Plan.)

## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY , OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2023-086
Adopting the 2023 Multnomah County Multi-Jurisdictional Natural Hazards Mitigation Plan.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The Federal Stafford Act requires the creation of a local natural hazard mitigation plan.
b. The plan describes people and places within the County and recognizes people and
places are differently situated and therefore are differently impacted by hazards.
c. The plan analyzes groups of natural hazards that have historically occurred in the
County and makes a projection of where such hazards may occur in the future, including
projections based on climate change science.
d. The plan takes into consideration County activity in the areas of land use planning,
transportation planning, facilities and property management, infrastructure planning, equity
and sustainability.
e. The plan determines the magnitude of damage that such future hazards may inflict.
f. The plan determines what infrastructure(s) are at risk of such hazards.
g. The plan describes mitigation projects which, when carried out, will lessen the chance
for damage or destruction due to natural disaster or emergency.
h. The plan allows the County to apply for Federal pre- and post-disaster natural hazard
mitigation funding.
i. This plan updates the plan previously adopted by Resolution 2017-091.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. T o adopt the attached 2023 Multnomah County Multi-Jurisdictional Natural Hazard
Mitigation Plan. The plan has been approved by FEMA pending local adoption, so will
become effective immediately.
ADOPTED this 12th day of October, 2023.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY , OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR
 MULTNOMAH COUNTY , OREGON

 BY: Chris Voss, Director
