# Resolution Approving The Fac-1 Project Plan For Construction Of The Montavilla Community Village Alternative Shelter Site. (Resolution Approving The Fac-1 Project Plan For Construction Of The Montavilla Community Village Alternative Shelter Site.)

## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO.  2024-023
Approving the FAC-1 Project Plan for Construction of the Montavilla Community Village
Alternative Shelter Site.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The Montavilla Community Village is an owned property of Multnomah County
located at 333 SE 82nd Ave Portland, OR 97216.
b. In August 2022 the County acquired what is now known as the Montavilla
Community Village, a former commercial retail space in South East Portland, for
$2.25 million. The renovation will provide shelter and social services for those
experiencing vehicular homelessness in Multnomah County.
c. Based on planning and design collaboration between the Joint Office of
Homeless Services, the anticipated shelter service provider, and Multnomah
County Facilities & Property Management, construction is anticipated to begin in
spring of 2024 and complete in winter of 2024/2025.
d. The current planning is for the Montavilla Community Village to serve up to 40
people in 29 individual sleeping units and site amenities that include 29
participant parking spaces, and 4 staff parking spaces. This site will be a gender
inclusive facility.
e. Pursuant to FAC-1, leadership in the Department of County Assets and the Joint
Office of Homeless Services (the “Project Team”) have prepared, and recommend
approval of, the Project Plan for the Montavilla Community Village at a total project
budget of $2,333,611.
f. Approval of the Project Plan is in the best interests of Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
The Project Plan is approved and the Project Team is authorized to proceed with
construction of the Project, including the solicitation of bids or proposals for construction
of the Project.
ADOPTED this 11th day of April, 2024.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
________________________________
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON

SUBMITTED BY: Tracey Massey, Director, Department of County Assets
