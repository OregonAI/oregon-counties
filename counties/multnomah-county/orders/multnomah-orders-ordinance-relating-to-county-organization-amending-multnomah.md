BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
ORDINANCE NO.
An Ordinance Relating to County Organization: Amending Multnomah County Code Chapter 25
and Creating a Chapter 22 for the Joint Office of Homeless Services.
(Language stricken is deleted; underlined language is new.)
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The Office was created as a Non-Departmental unit on June 23, 2016, when the Board
of County Commissioners amended Multnomah County Code Chapter 25.
b. Inclusion of the Office as a Non-Departmental unit was deemed necessary following the
approval of an Intergovernmental Agreement between Multnomah County and the City
of Portland that centralized the homeless services funding, policy, and planning efforts of
both jurisdictions.
c. The Office was situated within Non-Departmental to provide adequate administrative
infrastructure and ensure successful development of the new office.
d. Since incorporation within Non-Departmental, the Office has relied on central County
administrative functions, including human resources and other operational support,
provided by the Department of County Assets and the Department of County
Management.
e. In May of 2020, Metro district services area voters passed Metro Measure 26-210 (the
“Supportive Housing Services Measure”), establishing two new taxes, a business income
tax and a personal income tax, to fund supportive housing services for people
experiencing homelessness or at risk of experiencing homelessness.
f. The Metro district service area includes portions of Multnomah, Clackamas, and
Washington counties (the “Counties”). Revenues from the Supportive Housing Services
Measure will be distributed to the Counties to fund new programs and services for
people experiencing homelessness or at risk of experiencing homelessness (the
“Programs and Services”). Metro estimates that Multnomah County will receive $52.1
million in Supportive Housing Services revenue in Fiscal Year 2022 and approximately
$100.0 million per year at full revenue collection.
g. The Office will be responsible for the ongoing planning, contracting, financial
administration, reporting, and regional coordination required to carry out the provision
and delivery of the Programs and Services.
h. Supporting the provision and delivery of the Programs and Services, in addition to the
existing homeless services programs and services funded by Multnomah County, the
City of Portland, and the state and federal governments, will more than double the
Office’s budget to over $170 million per year and require substantial organizational
growth, including in administrative infrastructure.
i. Withtheongoingandplannedfuturegrowthof theOffice, theadministrativeneedsofasignificantly expanded Office cannot be effectively accommodated by the centralizedNon-Departmental services. That system is designed to serve multiple much smaller,less complex entities, rather than the needsof anorganizationthat isat thescaleandcomplexity of a department.
j. Multnomah County, the Office, andthepublic’sinterest invisibilitywill bebetter servedby the Office being moved from Non-Departmental andincorporatedasaDepartmentwithin the organizational structure.
Multnomah County Ordains as Follows:
Section 1. MCC 25.710 is deleted as follows:
§ 25.710 OFFICE ESTABLISHED.
AMultnomahCountyJoint Officeof HomelessServicesisestablished. TheOfficeshallberesponsibletoprocureandadminister contractsfor direct servicestosingleadults,families,youthandvictimsof domesticviolenceinneedofhousingsupport. TheDirectoroftheOfficeisappointed by the Chair and reports directly to theChair.
Section 2. MCC 25.720 is deleted as follows:
§ 25.720 DUTIES.
The Director will:
(A) Administer all aspects of the JointOffice of Homeless Services;
(B) Advise the Board of County Commissioners and the Portland City Council onmatters of concern to the Joint Office;
(C) Manage Joint Office staff, budget andcompliance; and
(D) Convene the Policy & Budget Alignment Committee (PBAC) established byIntergovernmental Agreement with the City of Portland.
Section 3. MCC Chapter 22, Joint Office of HomelessServices is created as follows:
§ 22.100 OFFICE ESTABLISHED.
A MultnomahCountyJoint Officeof HomelessServicesisestablished. TheOfficeshallberesponsibletoprocureandadminister contractsfor direct servicestosingleadults,families,youth and victims of domestic violence in need of housing support. The Office shall leadcommunity-based planning and oversight and serve as the Lead Agency for the localContinuumof Care. TheDirector of theOfficeisappointedbytheChair andreportsdirectlytothe Chair.
§ 22.110 DUTIES.
The Director will:
(A) Administer all aspects of the Joint Office of Homeless Services;
(B) Advise the Board of County Commissioners and the Portland City Council on
matters of concern to the Joint Office;
(C) Manage Joint Office staff, budget and compliance; and
(D) Convene the Policy & Budget Alignment Committee (PBAC) established by
Intergovernmental Agreement with the City of Portland.
