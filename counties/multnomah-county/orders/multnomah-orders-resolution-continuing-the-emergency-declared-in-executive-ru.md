BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON

RESOLUTION NO. 2020-019

Continuing the Emergency Declared in Executive Rule 388 and its Addendum and other
necessary measures.

The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:

A. On March 11, 2020, Executive Rule 388 was adopted Declaring an Emergency
due to imminent threat to the County presented by the COVID-19 outbreak.

B. On March 17, 2020, an Addendum to Executive Rule 388 was adopted adding
additional measures to further limit the spread among people experiencing homelessness
or at risk of homelessness from eviction.

C. Executive Rule 388 is set to expire on April 10, 2020, unless rescinded or extended
further.

D.  COVID-19 continues to p ose a significant and potentially catastrophic threat to
every resident of Multnomah County, requiring resources to be deployed swiftly, and for
County government to respond to emerging community needs with haste.

E. Multnomah County Code 25.450(B) states that the initial declaration of emergency
may not last for more than 30 days. A declaration of emergency may be extended or
terminated by the Board.

F. It is unlawful for any person or organization to fail to comply with any provisions of
Executive Rule 388, the Addendum to Executive Rule 388, or any other measure taken
under authority of Multnomah County Code (“MCC”) Chapter 25, and violations are
subject to fines and penalties.

The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:

1. To adopt and e xtend the emergency declared in Executive Rule 388 and its
Addendum for an additional ninety (90) days or until rescinded.

2. That any violation of any measure taken under authority of MCC Chapter 25 during
the Emergency is subject to civil fine of up to $1,000 for each offense per day, in addition
to penalties listed in MCC 25.480. Process and rules will be established by Administrative
Procedure.
