## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2022-001
Confirming Interim Designation for Multnomah County Commissioner, District 2
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Multnomah County Charter Section 4.50(3) and Multnomah County Code 5.003
require that elected officials designate an interim occupant to serve until a
vacancy is filled by election or appointment.
B. Charter Section 4.10 states the qualifications for County officers and Code Section
5.003(B)(4) requires that the designee meet the Charter qualifications for
appointees of such offices.
C. Susheela Jayapal, Multnomah County Commissioner, District 2, designates
Jesse Beason as interim occupant of that office in the event of a vacancy.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
The Board confirms Jesse Beason to serve as interim occupant for Multnomah County
Commissioner District 2 in the event of a vacancy in that office.
ADOPTED this 6th day of January, 2022.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
By
 Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
