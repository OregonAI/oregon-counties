## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-090
Resolution Consenting to Chair Vega Pederson’s Appointment of Annie Lewis as the
Multnomah County Libraries Director.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3) provides that the Chair’s appointment of
department heads (Directors) shall be subject to consent of a majority of the Board of
Commissioners.
b. The Chair appointed Annie Lewis as the Multnomah County Libraries Director with an
expected start date beginning October 17, 2024.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
In accordance with Multnomah County Charter Section 6.10(3), the Board consents to the
appointment of Annie Lewis as the Multnomah County Libraries Director.
ADOPTED this 17th day of October, 2024.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
By:
      Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
SUBMITTED BY: Travis Graves, Interim Chief Operating Officer and DCM Director
