BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2022-064
Establishing Fees for Building Permits, Electrical Permits, Plumbing Permits, Mechanical
Permits, and Other Related Permits pursuant to Chapter 29 of the Multnomah County Code
and Repealing Resolution No. 2021-054.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Chapter 29, Building Regulations, of the Multnomah County Code (MCC) provides
that the Board shall establish certain fees by resolution.
b. Multnomah County has entered into intergovernmental agreements with the cities of
Portland, Gresham, and Troutdale to administer and enforce  portions of MCC
Chapter 29 within unincorporated Multnomah County.
c. Portland proposes the fees for its administration and enforcement of MCC Chapter
29 within the Portland Permit Services Area which are then set by Multnomah County
under the terms of the intergovernmental agreement (IGA).
d. Pursuant to the Gresham IGA, fees for building services provided by the City of
Gresham under the IGA are as set by the City of Gresham and need not be adopted
by the County.
e. The City of Troutdale imposes the fees set by Multnomah County (“County Fees”)
for administration and enforcement of MCC Chapter 29.
f. On June 17, 2021, the Board adopted Resolution No. 2021-054 establishing MCC
Chapter 29 fees applying to building permits and other related permits.
g. None of the County Fees are increasing in this Resolution.  Fees within the Portland
Urban Services Boundary are increasing in this Resolution by an estimated 5%. The
City of Portland has recently approved increased fees effective July 1, 2022, under
the Oregon Structural Specialty Code in accordance with OAR 918-020-0220 and
ORS 455.210.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The fees for Chapter 29, Building Regulations, of the Multnomah County Code are
set as follows:
A. For the areas of unincorporated Multnomah County within the Portland
Permit Services Area, pursuant to MCC 29.010 and MCC 39.1245 (Building
and Other, Electrical, Enforcement, Mechanical, On-Site Sewage Disposal,
Plumbing, Sign & Awning, Site Development), the fees are set out in the
attached Exhibit A.
B. Pursuant to the Gresham IGA, fees for building services provided by the City
of Gresham under the IGA are as set by the City of Gresham and need not
be adopted by the County.
C. For the areas of unincorporated Multnomah County within the Troutdale
Permit Services Area, pursuant to MCC 29.010 (Building Code), MCC 29.106
(Electrical Code), and MCC 29.207 (Plumbing Code), the fees are as set out
in the attached Exhibits:
Schedule Fee
Building Code Fees See Exhibit B attached
Electrical Code Fees See Exhibit C attached
Plumbing Code Fees See Exhibit D attached
2. On July 1, 2022, Resolution No. 2021-054 is repealed and this Resolution takes
effect.
