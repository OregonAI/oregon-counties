BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2024-077
Proclaiming September 15 through October 15, 2024, as Hispanic and Latiné Heritage Month in
Multnomah County, Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Multnomah County observes and recognizes “Mes de Nuestra Herencia Hispana /
Latina,” also known as National “Hispanic and Latiné Heritage Month,” celebrated
nationwide from September 15th through October 15th. This annual celebration seeks to
increase awareness of the many culturally diverse contributions that Hispanic, Latino,
Latina, and Latiné communities have made and continue to make in our community.
B. This year’s theme, “Raíces de Esperanza: Roots of Hope and Resilience,” invites us to
dig deep into our cultural roots, celebrating the strength, perseverance, and boundless
contributions of Hispanic and Latiné communities in driving the prosperity, power, and
progress of Multnomah County and beyond.
C. September 15 is significant as it marks the anniversary of independence for countries
such as Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, and Nicaragua, while Mexico
celebrates its independence on September 16th and Chile on September 18th.
D. During this “Mes de Nuestra Herencia Hispana,” there is much to recognize and
celebrate. We honor our Familia, Culture, Tradition, and History, which have shaped us
into the most diverse community. We are mestizos, somos del maíz, somos del sol; we
are Black, Brown, Native Indigenous communities from many nations. Our differences
are our strengths in unity, with over 400 different languages spoken. In Multnomah
County alone, our growing population of 103,753 individuals proudly identifies as Latiné,
Hispanic, Latinx, Salvadoreños, Mexicanos, Caribbeans, Central and South Americans
— we are Americans!
E. Con mucho orgullo, we recognize this month as "Herencia Hispana / Latina." We
celebrate the countless contributions made to this country in areas such as business,
workforce development, entrepreneurship, athletics, arts, entertainment, and science.
We also uplift marginalized groups, including women, Black, and Indigenous Latiné
leaders. We honor those who labor in agriculture under the most challenging conditions,
ensuring that the families in our community are provided with basic needs. We recognize
the leaders, teachers, advocates, and mission driven leaders dedicated to seeking social
justice and equality for all.
F. In recognition of Herencia Hispana / Latina, we recognize the contributions to build
community and the great pride carried out in danzas, canciones, customs, and in the
honoring of ancestors. The pride through the stories that are told year after year to keep
history alive and pass on through generations, ensuring they are never forgotten.
G. As we come together, we honor the countless ways in which Hispanic and Latiné
communities have woven their stories into the fabric of our society, bringing color,
passion, and a deep sense of comunidad. Yet, we also recognize the challenges that
remain and recommit ourselves to breaking down barriers and ensuring that every door
of opportunity swings open for all. The Hispanic and Latiné communities in Multnomah
County are a kaleidoscope of nationalities and backgrounds, each adding their unique
flavor to our shared experience. From education to business, from non-profits to
government, their impact is felt in every corner of our county. Our Hispanic and Latiné
employees are the heartbeat of Multnomah County, leading with passion, dedication,
and a spirit of service that uplifts us all. We celebrate their achievements, honor their
hard work, and recognize the incredible legacy they are building.
H. Hispanic and Latiné Heritage Month is a time for all of us to unite our voices, to
recognize the essential role Hispanic and Latiné communities play in shaping the future
of our county, our country, and our world. It’s a time to celebrate Latinos, Latinas, and
Latinés as leaders, creators, visionaries, and community builders. We commit to seeing,
hearing, and valuing the contributions of our Hispanic and Latiné communities,
acknowledging that they are the driving force behind our shared prosperity, power, and
progress. We proudly celebrate Hispanic and Latiné Heritage Month, and we encourage
every resident to join in the celebration, to honor the roots of hope and resilience that
define our Hispanic and Latiné communities, and embrace the joy, culture, and unity that
this vibrant month brings to us all.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
September 15 through October 15, 2024, as Hispanic and Latiné Heritage Month in Multnomah
County, Oregon, in recognition and celebration of the many contributions of Latiné residents to
the County and our community.
ADOPTED this 19th day of September, 2024.
 SUBMITTED BY: Chair Jessica Vega Pederson
 Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
