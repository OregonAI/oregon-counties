## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2022-012
Proclaiming February 2022 as Black History and Future Month in Multnomah County,
Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Black History Month is observed every February throughout the United States to
celebrate and recognize the vital role and contributions of Black and African Americans
in our history and culture. Black History Month was first celebrated in 1976.
b. Beginning in 2016, following engagement with community members and leaders,
the Multnomah County Board of Commissioners has recognized February as Black
History and Black Future Month. By acknowledging the importance of the future state,
Multnomah County seeks to not only honor Black history in America, but also to  recognize
that racial injustices persist for Black communities in Multnomah County and across the
country, and to honor the people and organizations that are working to create a better
and more just future for Black and African American communities.
c. In 2022, Multnomah County recognizes the importance of celebrating Black health
and healing. That healing can come in many different forms - including at the ballot box,
the courtroom and the church; through cultu ral traditions, entrepreneurship and
community connection; in art, history, music, prose and poetry; through equitable and
responsive healthcare and true self-determination.
d. Multnomah County acknowledges and rejects the long history of institutional and
structural racism toward African Americans and in the United States and this observance
of Black History and Future Month calls our attention to the continued need to confront
racism and build a society that lives up to its democratic values.
e. Multnomah Co unty recognizes that racial and ethnic diversity enriches our
community.
f. Multnomah County recognizes and endeavors to dismantle the systemic barriers
set up to hinder Black and African Americans people’s ability to achieve equity,
opportunity, and social mobility. Multnomah County further recognizes the resulting and
immense economic impacts that racism and these systemic barriers have had for our
Black and African American community members.
g. Multnomah County honors the rich heritage, extraordinary legaci es, and ongoing
accomplishments of Black and African Americans, including successful entrepreneurial
endeavors, thriving local businesses, and cultural contributions.
h. Multnomah County honors and celebrates our Black and African American
employees working towards eliminating disparities in their communities and creating a
better future for all community members.
i. Multnomah County honors the workforce equity contributions of Employees of
Color (EOC), Managers of Color (MOC) and Black and African American employees that
ensure safety, trust and belonging for all in our community.
j. Multnomah County joins the chorus of other local, state and national leaders who
celebrate Black and African American excellence and lift up the importance and power of
Black and African American health, healing, justice and hope. We celebrate and
recognize Black and African American communities not only this month, but 365 days a
year.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaim:
February 2022 as Black History Month and Black History and Future Month in Multnomah
County, Oregon, in recognition and celebration of the contributions of Black and African
Americans to our community.
ADOPTED this 24th day of February, 2022.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
By
     Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
SUBMITTED BY: Chair Deborah Kafoury
Deborah Kafoury, Chair
