## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO.  2021-019
Proclaiming April 18 through April 24, 2021 as Volunteer Week in Multnomah County, Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. This year, National Volunteer Week takes place from April 18th to April 24 th and provides an
opportunity to recognize the invaluable contributions of volunteers to supporting safe, healthy and
equitable communities.
b. During the unprecedented crisis of t he COVID -19 pandemic, Multnomah County volunteers
stepped up to support the activities of our Emergency Operations Center, and continued to participate in
volunteer programs across departments.
c. From March 2020 to March 2021, volunteers contributed over 11,500 hours to Multnomah
County’s COVID-19 response efforts.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
The week of April 18 through April 24, 2021 as Volunteer Week in Multnomah County, Oregon. We take
great pleasure in hon oring all volunteers with our sincere gratitude and appreciation for their selfless
efforts.
ADOPTED this 15th day of April, 2021.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Deborah Kafoury, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
By
     Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
SUBMITTED BY: Dani Bernstein, Director, Office of Community Involvement
