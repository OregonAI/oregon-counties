BEFORE  THE  BOARD  OF  COUNTY  COMMISSIONERS   FOR  MULTNOMAH  COUNTY,  OREGON
RESOLUTION  NO.  2026-017
Scheduling  a  Public  Hearing  and  Directing  Notice  Thereof  for  the  Consideration  of  the
Legalization

of

a

Portion

of

NW

Kaiser

Road,

County

Road

No.

107.

The  Multnomah  County  Board  of  Commissioners  Finds:
a)  NW  Kaiser  Road  was  established  in  1878  as  County  Road  No.  107,  and  for  portions
of

said

road,

from

its

intersection

with

NW

Germantown

Road,

southeasterly

to

the

Washington

County

line,

in

the

SW1/4

of

Section

8,

T.

1N.,

R.

1W.,

W.M.

(hereafter

referred

to

as

“the

portion

of

NW

Kaiser

Road”),

the

road

as

traveled

and

used

for

at

least

the

past

ten

years

does

not

conform

to

the

existing

records

for

this

county

road;

b)  On  August  21,  2025,  the  Board  of  Commissioners  adopted  resolution  No.
2025-051,

initiating

proceedings

for

the

legalization

of

this

portion

of

NW

Kaiser

Road

and

directed

the

County

Surveyor

to

conduct

a

survey

of

the

road,

pursuant

to

ORS

368.201

to

368.221;

c)  As  required  under  ORS  368.206(1),  the  County  Surveyor  has  completed  the
survey

of

the

road,

attached

here

as

Exhibit

A,

and

prepared

a

report

to

the

Board

with

the

proposed

new

County

Road

Number

“5032”;

and

d)  The  above  referenced  statutes  require  the  County  to  hold  a  public  hearing  to
consider

legalization

of

NW

Kaiser

Road,

and

provide

notice

thereof

by

service

to

the

abutting

property

owners

and

by

posting

pursuant

to

ORS

368.206(1)(c).

The  Multnomah  County  Board  of  Commissioners  Resolves:
1.  The  Board  of  County  Commissioner  will  hold  a  hearing  on  April  30,  2026  at  9:30  am
in

the

Multnomah

Building,

First

Floor

Commissioners

Boardroom

100,

501

SE

Hawthorne

Boulevard,

Portland,

Oregon.

2.  The  purpose  of  the  hearing  is  to  determine  whether  the  legalization  of  a  portion  of  NW
Kaiser

Road,

as

County

Road

No.

5032,

is

in

the

public’s

interest.

The

hearing

will

concern

NW

Kaiser

Road

from

its

intersection

with

NW

Germantown

Road,

southeasterly

to

the

Washington

County

line,

in

the

SW1/4

of

Section

8,

T.

1N.,

R.

1W.,

W.M.

3.  The  County  Surveyor  is  directed  to  provide  notice  of  the  hearing  to  abutting
property owners by service and by posting in a manner consistent with ORS
368.401 to 368.426.
