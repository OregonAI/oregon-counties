## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2023-039
Appointment of Robert Liberty to the Columbia River Gorge Commission.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
1. The Columbia River Gorge National Scenic Area Act, 16 U.S.C. §§ 544 –544p,
created the National Scenic Area, authorized the states to enter into a compact
creating the Gorge Commission, required the CRGC a nd U.S. Forest Service to
adopt a regional management plan, and required counties to adopt land use
ordinances consistent with the management plan.
2. The Columbia River Gorge Compact is the agreement between Oregon and
Washington establishing the Gorge Commission. It is codified at Revised Code of
Washington (RCW) 43.97.015 and Oregon Revised Statutes (ORS) 196.150.
3. The Board of County Commissioners has the authority under Section
544c(a)(1)(C)(i) of the Columbia River Gorge National Scenic Area Act and unde r
ORS 196.150 to appoint a commissioner to the CRGC.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. Robert Liberty is appointed as Multnomah County’s representative to the Columbia
River Gorge Commission for a four-year term that shall conclude on June 8, 2027.
ADOPTED this 8th day of June 2023.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY , OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
By
 Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
