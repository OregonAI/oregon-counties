## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON

PROCLAMATION NO. 2024-076

Proclaiming September 2024 as Recovery Month in Multnomah County, Oregon.

The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:

A. “Join the voices of recovery: Together we are stronger.” Multnomah County
observes September as Recovery Month. Recovery Month is meant to increase
awareness about substance use disorders, decrease the stigma surrounding
addiction and amplify the voices of those with lived experience. We recognize the
complexities and challenges that individuals may face at any phase in their
journey. We also want to acknowledge there are both external and internal
barriers that people are facing.

B. Connection to community is more critical than ever. We ask that all of the
community join us in the fight against addiction and support those at all stages of
recovery. That is why this year's theme couldn't be more fitting (Join the voices of
recovery: Together we are stronger).

C. Often the community at large may feel that our current crisis is not their problem
to solve or may not know what they can do to help. Sometimes it can be easier to
look the other way until the problem is knocking at your front door. The truth is
this problem has affected all of our lives in one form or another. No one is
exempt!  It has impacted our business community, our local and state politicians,
the behavioral health workforce, first responders, families of all colors and
everyone in between, but most importantly, the people who have lost their battle
to this disease that will never get to join our fight. This is a call to action! We are
asking all to Join the voices of recovery because we cannot do this alone. Yes,
people are dying and that should not go unheard but neither should the voices of
all the people who are recovering from addiction. There is hope, people are living
full and wholesome lives.

D. From the 2021 National Survey on Drug Use and Health, for substance use
specifically, of the 29.0 million adults who perceived that they ever had a
substance use problem, 72.2% (or 20.9 million) considered themselves to be in
recovery or to have recovered from their drug or alcohol use problem. Fentanyl
and poly-substance use is still devastating our community and more, has had an
unpredictable, life-threatening impact on our community. Although individuals are
suffering, effective recovery requires engagement from the community around
them. Our proclamation celebrates hope and aims to reduce stigma surrounding
individuals with substance use issues.

E. We will highlight successes in recovery and in advocacy. Multnomah County is
still managing the impacts of COVID, including the isolation that has contributed
to an increase in the acuity of issues related to substance use. Multnomah
County Behavioral Health Division is eager to move toward a more hopeful future
in partnership with community treatment and recovery support service providers.
We want to effectively reach Multnomah County Community members in need of
services and support to overcome substance use challenges.
F. Sharing inspiring stories of recovery builds understanding and promotes wellness
for all Multnomah County residents. Recovery Month allows us an opportunity to
center around this growing need, and engage our community in awareness and
support of this work. Recovery is not only possible but it is probable with the right
supports in place.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
The month of September 2024 is proclaimed to be Recovery Month in Multnomah County,
Oregon. All county residents are invited to commit to continuing support of recovery for
those impacted by substance use disorders. This month, we invite you to celebrate those
who are achieving their goals for recovery and wellness, and encourage you to share
information about programs, resources and services to help others take their first steps
toward a healthier life.
ADOPTED this 12th day of September, 2024
SUBMITTED BY: Heather Mirasol
 Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair

JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
