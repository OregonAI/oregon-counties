## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2023-024
Proclaiming April 28, 2023 as Workers’ Memorial Day in Multnomah County, Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Workers’ Memorial Day is a day to remember those who have suffered and died
on the job and rededicate ourselves to the fight for safe workplaces.
B. We remember those who have died in workplace catastrophes, suffered diseases
due to exposure to toxic substances or been injured because of dangerous
conditions.
C. The core theme for Workers’ Memorial Day has been “Mourn for the dead, fight for
the living.”
D. April 28th has been chosen as Workers’ Memori al Day in 2023 because it is the
anniversary of when the Occupational Safety and Health Act went into effect.
E. The Northwest Oregon Labor Council will have a remembrance ceremony at 9:30
am at Fire Station 21.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
April 28, 2023 as Workers’ Memorial Day in Multnomah County.
ADOPTED this 27th day of April, 2023.
SUBMITTED BY: Chair Jessica Vega Pederson
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
By:
      Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
