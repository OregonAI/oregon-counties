## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO.  2022-046
Approving the FAC -1 Acquisition Proposal for the Real Property Located at 801 SW
Harvey Milk Street, Portland, Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. From 2019 to 2021, the Multnomah County Board of Commissioners approved
plans and measures to establish the Behavioral Health Resources Center at 333
SW Park Avenue, Portland, Oregon (“BHRC”). See Resolutions, 2019-007, 2019-
011, 2019-083, and 2021-032.
b. As set forth in Resolution 2021 -032, the BHRC will address an identified need in
our community for a centrally located, dedicated behavioral health resource center
as one means of addressing a resource gap in facilities and services av ailable to
serve those in our community experiencing homelessness together with disabling
conditions, including behavioral health issues such as mental illness and
substance use disorders.
c. To date, as identified in Resolutions 2019 -007 and 2019-011 the BHRC is being
established on two contiguous parcels, one bearing a building that is now being
renovated into the BHRC and one bearing a surface parking lot that is now being
renovated as a courtyard and outbuildings for the BHRC. A third contiguous parcel
to the south of the building (also bearing a surface parking lot) was not previously
available to the County, but the opportunity to acquire this parcel, located at 801
SW Harvey Milk Street, Portland, Oregon, has arisen (“Property”).
d. As set forth in the FAC -1 Acquisition Proposal for the Real Property Located at
801 SW Harvey Milk Street, Portland, Oregon, enclosed herewith (“FAC -1
Proposal”), the Property presents an excellent opportunity to further the BHRC
Program and/or support the provision of other comm unity services as well as
existing downtown County and community-partner programs and services.
e. The Director of the Multnomah County Facilities & Property Management Division
and the Interim Director of the Multnomah County Behavioral Health Division
recommend approval of the FAC-1 Proposal.
f. Approval of the FAC-1 Proposal is in the best interests of Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
The FAC-1 Proposal is approved.
ADOPTED this 19th day of May, 2022.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Deborah Kafoury, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
SUBMITTED BY:  Tracey Massey, Director, Department of County Assets
