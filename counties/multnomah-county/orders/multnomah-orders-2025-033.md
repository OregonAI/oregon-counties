BEFORE THE MULTNOMAH COUNTY LIBRARY DISTRICT BOARD
RESOLUTION NO.
Adopting the Fiscal Year 2026 Budget for the Multnomah County Library District and Making
Appropriations Thereunder, Pursuant to ORS 294.456.
The Multnomah County Library District (MCLD) Board Finds:
A. The MCLD budget, as prepared by the duly appointed MCLD Director/Budget Officer,
has been considered and approved by the MCLD Board acting as the Budget
Committee.
B. A public hearing on this budget was held before the Multnomah County Tax
Supervising and Conservation Commission on the 4th day of June, 2025.
C. The budget is now on file in the Multnomah County Library District’s Administrative
Office.
D. The MCLD Board has made one amendment to the above described budget and that
amendment is attached to this Resolution as Attachment A.
E. The appropriation authorized is as follows:
GENERAL FUND (1520)
Library Services $ 125,188,589
Cash Transfers $  4,850,050
Operating Contingency $  1,000,000
Total Appropriation $ 131,038,639
Reserved for Future Expenditure $  6,253,645
Unappropriated Balance  $  22,038,379
Total Requirements $ 159,330,663
CAPITAL FUND (2514)
Library Services  $  3,530,000
Operating Contingency $  73,161,085
Total Appropriation $   76,691,085
Total Requirements $   76,691,085
F. The Tax Supervising and Conservation Commission has certified the budget and there
are no recommendations and no objections.
