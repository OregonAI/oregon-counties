BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2020-087
Scheduling a Publi c Hearing and Directing Notice Thereof fo r the Consideration of the
Legalization of a Portion of NW Rocky Point Road, County Road No. 445.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. NW Rocky Point  Road was  establish as County Road No. 445 in 1888 and for
portions of said road, between Engineer’s Stations 75+91.54 and 84+40.70, the
road as traveled and used for at least the past ten years does not conform to the
existing records for this county road.
b. On June 25, 2020, the Board of Commissioners adopted Resolution No. 2020-055,
initiating proceedings for the legalization of this portion of NW Rocky Point Road
and directed the County Surveyor to conduct a survey of the road , pursuant to
ORS 368.201 to 368.221.
c. As required under ORS 368.206(1), the County Surveyor has completed the
survey of the road, attached here as Exhibit A, and prepared a report to the Board
with the proposed new County Road Number “5031”.
d. The above referenced statutes require the County to hold a public hearing to
consider legalization of NW Rocky Point Road, and pr ovide notice thereof by
service to the abutting property owners and by posting  pursuant to ORS
368.206(1)(c).
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The Board of County Commissioner will hold a hearing on Thursday, December
10, 2020 at 9:30 am.  Due to ongoing Covid -19 pandemic, Board meetings are
being held virtually and are accessible to the public via telephone or electronic
device.  Information about providing testimony and obtaining access can be found
at https://multco.us/multnomah-county/about-board-meetings.
2. The purpose of the hearing is to determine whether the legalization of a portion of
NW Rocky Point Road, as County Road No. 5031, is in the public’s interest.  The
hearing will concern NW Rocky Point Road from Engineer’s Station 75+9 1.54 to
Engineer’s Station 84+40.70
3. The County Surveyor is directed to provide notice of the hearing to abutting
property owners by service and by posting in a manner cons istent with ORS
368.401 to 368.426.
SUBMITTED BY: Jamie Waltz, Director, Department of Community Services
ADOPTED this 1st day of October, 2020.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Deborah Kafoury, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY MADOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
