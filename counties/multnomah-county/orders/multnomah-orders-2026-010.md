BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY
COMMISSIONERS FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY,
OREGON
RESOLUTION NO.
Declaring the East County Office Building, Located at 18600 SE Stark St, Gresham,
Oregon as Surplus; and Approving the Terms of a Lease.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. This matter comes to the Board pursuant to administrative procedure FAC-3,
which establishes the County's policy for declaring property surplus, and leasing
surplus County-owned real property to a non-County tenant.
B. Facilities & Property management (FPM) has previously assessed potential uses
for the East County Office Building, the most recent being for the Department of
Community Justice’s Community Services program. The estimated cost of
construction, if performed by the County, has been cost prohibitive to programs
inquiring about potential use of the building. At this time, there is no County use
identified for this property.
C. The Rockwood Common Lease (Lease) will be for a term of 60 months with a
gratis rental rate. The tenant will be responsible for all operating expenses,
preventative maintenance and repairs during the term of tenancy.  Any tenant
improvement requirements, and associated costs, will be the responsibility of the
Rockwood Common.
The tenant will have the option to renew the Lease for three consecutive terms of
60 months each.
The tenant holds a one-time option to terminate the Lease at the end of the ninth
month, provided they give three months' prior written notice. Following the initial
60 month term, the County reserves the right to terminate the Lease at any time
by providing 120 days' written notice.
D. Following administrative procedure FAC-3, Multnomah County Facilities &
Property Management (FPM) has determined that the East County Office
Building is not needed for use by Multnomah County and is appropriate to be
declared surplus.
E. It is in the best interests of the County to act on FPM’s determination and
recommendation.
______ 2026-010
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The East County Office Building is declared surplus and the building will be
leased on terms substantially similar to those described in this Resolution.
2. The Board Chair is authorized to execute such Lease and any ancillary
instruments and to execute renewals or amendments without further Board
action.
ADOPTED this _19_ day of _February, 2026.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY
ATTORNEY FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY,
OREGON
