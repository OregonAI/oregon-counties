## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO.  2021-027
Approving the Southeast Health Center Structural Repairs FAC-1 Project Construction Plan.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Multnomah County owns the Southeast Health Center building located at 3653 SE 34th
Ave., Portland, Oregon.
b. In January of 2018, the Health Department notified Facilities that there was a gap
between the floor and the exterior wall in the NW corner of the building.  Investigation
into this gap determined that there appeared to be issues with the structure in the NW
corner of the building.  Further investigation found that there were numerous areas of
water damage to the structure and a project was needed to engage professional
engineers to assess the structure and develop a remedy for the situation.
c. In March of 2018, emergency funding was secured to do further investigations and
analysis by an architect and structural engineer. The Architect and Engineer’s
assessment, completed in early May of 2018, recommended shoring of the beams
until permanent replacements of the beams could be completed. Further investigation
revealed that the Southeast Health Center structural system supporting the first floor
west wings, as well as the north and south exterior walls, have deteriorated due to
moisture damage from years of water infiltration through the building exterior siding.
d. The Project Management Team developed a project composed of (1) the removal and
replacement of the deteriorated structural system supporting the West Wing of the
Southeast Health Center; (2) replacement of the failed exterior siding and original
building windows; and (3) interior renovation of the two reception areas into a single
Universal Reception Area to promote patient care and accessibility (collectively, the
“Project”).
e. On May 14, 2020, through Resolution 2020-028, the Multnomah County Board of
Commissioners approved the Southeast Health Center Structural Repair Fac-1
Preliminary Planning Proposal and Project Plan for the Project (“Project Plan”).
f. Consistent with Resolution 2020-028, Project design drawings as well as procurement
of the construction contractor have been completed and the Project construction cost
estimate has been updated, all as further set forth in the Multnomah County Southeast
Health Center Structural Repair Fac -1 Project Construction Plan enclosed with the
present Resolution (“Construction Plan”). As procured, the Project included two
Alternate (i.e., potential additional) scopes of work, Alternate #1 (replacement of the
existing flooring in the Primary Care & Dental Clini cs) and Alternate #2 (addition of an
ADA automatic door operator for the Primary care clinic). The low bid, received by
Swinerton Construction, for the work without the Alternates is $858,858.00; the
combined bid for Alternates #1 and #2 is an additional $103,185.00, for a combined total
Project construct hard cost estimate of $962,043.00. The Project Management Team
recommends Board approval of the entire proposal, including Alternates # 1 and # 2, as
set forth in the enclosed Construction Plan.
g. Approval of the Construction Plan is in the best interests of Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
The Construction Plan i s approved, including implementation of the Next Step s Activities
specified therein.
ADOPTED this  22nd day of APRIL 2021.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
