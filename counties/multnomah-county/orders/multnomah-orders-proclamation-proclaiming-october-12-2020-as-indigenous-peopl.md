# Proclamation Proclaiming October 12, 2020 As Indigenous Peoples’ Day In Multnomah County, Oregon. (Proclamation Proclaiming October 12, 2020 As Indigenous Peoples’ Day In Multnomah County, Oregon.)

## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
PROCLAMATION NO. 2020-089
Proclaiming October 12, 2020 as Indigenous Peoples’ Day in Multnomah County,
Oregon.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners finds:
a. The American Indian, Alaskan Native and Indigenous heritage of the United States
extends since time immemorial and is a vital influence in our country’s prosperity.
b. The United States rests on the ceded territory of the Indigenous Tribal Nations of this
region and their homelands and villages; and we acknowledge the loss of life when
European explorers came to this country.
c. Our name, “Multnomah,” is derived from an Indigenous word and we conduct daily
business on Indigenous soil. We recognize that we are guests and stewards of this
land.
d. We value the many contributions made to our community by Tribes and Indigenous
Peoples: their knowledge, labor, environmental advocacy, science, philosophy, arts
and the deep cultural contribution that has substantially shaped the character of our
region.
e. That Indigenous Peoples' Day shall be an opportunity to celebrate the thrivin g
traditions and deeply rooted values of the Indigenous Peoples who reside in
Multnomah County and their ancestors before them.
f. By honoring and celebrating our region’s first peoples, we are reclaiming a piece of
history and choosing to no longer observe a time of genocide.
g. Today Multnomah County recognizes the more than 70,000 American Indian and
Alaska Natives living in our region, and honors the important  role that Tribal Leaders
play in sustaining our culture, promoting community health and making positive
impacts every day in the lives of the people of Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Proclaims:
October 12th, 2020 as Indigenous Peoples’ Day in Multnomah County, Oregon, to honor
and celebrate the many contributions made by the Indigenous peoples throughout our
vibrant community.  We  encourage  other  businesses,  organizations,  public  institutions
and community members to recognize Indigenous Peoples' Day, and to take time to learn
and teach others around the history and cultural significance of our county and Indigenous
community.
ADOPTED this 8th day of October, 2020.
SUBMITTED BY:
Anna Marie Allen, Policy & Engagement Advisor, Multnomah County Chair’s Office
     Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Deborah Kafoury, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
