BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO.  2026-044
Resolution to amend contract  number  DCS-SVCSGEN-175-1 2-2025 for the
Zoning Code Improvement Project, increasing the contract price by an additional
$73,505.00.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. T
he Board, acting as the Multnomah County Public Contract Review Board
(PCRB) and pursuant to PCRB Rule 49 -0910(3), must approve contract
price increases beyond 33 percent of the original contract price for contracts
greater than $150,000.
B. T
he first two phases of the multi-year Zoning Code Improvement  Projec t
(“Pro
ject”) have been  completed. Phases one and two  included a zoning
code audit and public outreach. The Project is now entering the code
amendment phase (phases three  and four ), resulting in the need  for
additional funding and to increase the contract price by more than 33 percent
of the original contract price. The total contract cost to date is $240,820.00.
C. This ame
ndment (“Amendment”) will compensate the M oore Iacofano
Goltsman Inc. (MIG) consultant s for code amendment and public meeting
services required to support completion of the Project.
D. T
he added work is within the general scope of the original contract, and this
Amendment is in accordance with the requirements of Public Contract
Review Board Rule 49-0910.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
To amend and increase the total amount of contract number DCS-SVCSGEN-175-
12-2025 with MIG by an additional $73,505.00  for a new  total  contract price  of
$314,325.00 for the Project.
ADOPTED this 18th day of June, 2026.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
By:
June Bradley, Assistant County Attorney
