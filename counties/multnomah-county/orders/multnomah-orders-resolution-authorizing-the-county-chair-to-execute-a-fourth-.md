BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2022-083
Authorizing the County Chair to Execute a Fourth Amendment to an Intergovernmental
Agreement with the Portland Development Commission, doing business as Prosper
Portland, for the Administration of the PropertyFit Program.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. In 2015, Multnomah County (“County”) established the Commercial Property
Assessed Clean Energy (“CPACE”) program in Resolution 2015-097. The CPACE
program facilitates private loans to commercial, industrial, and certain multifamily
residential building owners and developers to assist with financing energy -
efficiency upgrades, renewable energy development such as roof -top solar,
energy storage, electric vehicle charging, water efficiency and seismic
rehabilitation projects. Those private loans are secured by a lien on the property
that has the same priority as a lien for assessments of local improvement districts.
B. “PropertyFit” is the branded name for the CPACE program.
C. In Resolution 2015-098, the Board of County Commissioners authorized the
County Chair to execute an Intergovernmental Agreement (“IGA”) with the Portland
Development Commission, now doing business as Prosper Portland (“Prospe r
Portland”), to serve as the PropertyFit program administrator. The IGA is set to
expire in September, 2022.
D. In 2016, the County and Prosper Portland executed the First Amendment to the
IGA to, among other things, recognize statutory changes that include d seismic
upgrades among the eligible uses for PropertyFit.
E. In January 2020, the County and Prosper Portland executed the Second
Amendment to the IGA to reflect the transition to the enforcement mechanism
described in the local improvement district statutes (ORS 223.505 to 223.650), as
authorized by ORS 223.680(7)(c) and ORS 223.685(6)(c).
F. In September 2020, the County and Prosper Portland executed the Third
Amendment to the IGA to extend the IGA for an additional two years.
G. Prosper Portland and the Count y agree on the need to develop a new IGA that
better reflects current program administration realities.
H. To continue the partnership between Proper Portland and the County that has
given rise to this growing PropertyFit Program, and to allow for the time needed to
work on a new IGA, Prosper Portland and the County will extend the IGA for six
months.

results in a material increase or decrease in the obligations of, or benefits to, the County
under the IGA or the Fourth Amendment.
