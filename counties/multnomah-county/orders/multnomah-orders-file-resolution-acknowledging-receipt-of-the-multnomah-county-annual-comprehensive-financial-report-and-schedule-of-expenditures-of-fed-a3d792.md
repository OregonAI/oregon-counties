## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-004
Acknowledging Receipt of the Multnomah County Annual Comprehensive Financial Report and Schedule
of Expenditures of Federal Awards for Year Ended June 30, 2023.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The County’s audit f or the year ending June 30, 2023  has been completed and filed with t he
appropriate authorities within prescribed deadlines.
b. The County received an unmodified opinion on its financial statements from its external auditors.
Financial statements are presented fairly in accordance with US GAAP.
c. The audit identified one compliance finding, the County had one over expenditure in the Faciliti es
Management Fund.
d. Th
e County had no control or compliance findings with audit of federal expenditures “Single Audit”.
e. There were no significant deficiencies with the three districts  (Dunthorpe-Riverdale Service District,
Mid County Service District, and Library District) that have their own financial report (CUFR).
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The Annual Comprehensive Financial Report for the year ended June 30, 2023 and the related audit
reports and recommendations are accepted.
2. Th e Board directs the Department of County Management to implement  the recommendations made
by the County’s external auditors and resolve compliance finding.
ADOPTED this 18th day of January, 2024.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Jessica Vega Peterson, Chair
SUBMITTED BY: Eric Arellano, Chief Financial Officer, Department of County Management
 REVIEWED:
 JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
