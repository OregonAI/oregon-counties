# Resolution Declaring A Portion Of The Multnomah Building (Tmb) Temporary Surplus. (Resolution Declaring A Portion Of The Multnomah Building (Tmb) Temporary Surplus.)

## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-036
Declaring a Portion of the Multnomah Building (TMB) Temporary Surplus.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Approximately 500 square feet of space (the “Premises”) within the County owned Multnomah
Building located at 501 SE Hawthorne Boulevard, Portland, Oregon is not needed for any County
use.
b. The Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) is interested in leasing space in the
southeast corner of the second floor for one year, an approximate 500 square foot area that
includes ten cubicle workstations. They are willing to pay $25,000 for a one year lease, an
amount determined by Facilities and Property Management sufficient to cover County facility
costs.
c. Multnomah County’s Emergency Management (EM) is located on the second floor of TMB and
would like to lease the space to FEMA as they believe the close physical proximity will foster a
strong relationship with FEMA. They believe that the relationship may help EM build its capacity
to support future inclement weather events which rise to the level of a federal disaster
declaration.
d. It is in the best interest of the County to declare the Premises temporary surplus for up to two
years, allowing for a lease extension with FEMA of up to one year, and to execute a lease with
FEMA for an initial term of one year.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The Premises is declared temporary surplus for up to two years.
ADOPTED this 23rd day of May, 2024.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON

SUBMITTED BY: Tracey Massey, Director, DCA and CIO
By
     Andrew MacKendrick, Sr. Asst. County Attorney
