## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-014
Authorizing approval of leases of real property by Multnomah County exceeding $200,000
in annual base rent and repealing Resolution 99-157.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Multnomah County occasionally obtains space for its operations and programs
through leasing real property.
B. It is often necessary to act expeditiously to obtain such space economically and
most suited to the need.
C. The County Charter Section 6.10 states that the Chair is the Chief Executive
Officer of the County. County Administrative Procedure, FAC-3 sets forth the County
process by which the County identifies, negotiates, and executes leases for real property.
D. In the interest of public transparency and good governance, the Board requests
that leases in excess of $200,000 annual base rent in their first year for County operations
and programs be brought to the Board for approval.
E. Adjustments to the settlement thresholds listed in section D of this resolution will
require adjustment over time to reflect inflation or other factors.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. Leases of real property exceeding $200,000 in annual base rent in their first year
for the County’s operations and programs may be brought to the Board for approval.
2. The settlement thresholds listed in this resolution may be adjusted annually by the
Chief Financial Officer to reflect inflation or other factors.
3. Resolution Number 99-157 is repealed.
ADOPTED this 7th day of March, 2024.
SUBMITTED BY:  Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
  Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
