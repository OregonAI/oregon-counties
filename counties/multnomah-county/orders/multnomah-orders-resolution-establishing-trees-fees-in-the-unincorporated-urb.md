BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2021-002
Establishing Trees Fees  in the Unincorporated Urban Areas  Located Within the City of
Portland’s Urban Services Boundary.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. MCC 39.1245 provides that the Multnomah County Board of County
Commissioners will set fees by resolution for services provided pursuant to
Multnomah County Code (“MCC”) Chapter 39.
b. Pursuant to an intergovernmental agreement executed in 2002, and
subsequently amended, (the “IGA”), the City of Portland, Oregon (“City”),
provides, with certain exceptions, land use planning services and services
related to land divisions, signs, erosion control and stormwater disposal, grading,
floodplain review, and requirements for trees in development situations for those
areas of unincorporated Multnomah County located within the City’s Urban
Services Boundary (the “Unincorporated Urban Areas”). The County adopts code
provisions relating to those services as amendments to MCC Chapter 39.
c. Under the IGA, the County agreed to adopt the City’s fee schedule for services
provided pursuant to the IGA.
d. Through Ordinance No.190200 (effective December 12, 2020), the Portland City
Council amended the Title 11 Trees Fee Schedule. Because the City administers
requirements for trees in development situations in the Unincorporated Urban
Areas under the IGA, the City has asked the County to adopt the associated Title
11 Trees Fee Schedule for the Unincorporated Urban Areas.
