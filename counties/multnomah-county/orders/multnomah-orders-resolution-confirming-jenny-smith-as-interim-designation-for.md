BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-084
Confirming Jenny Smith as Interim Designation for Multnomah County Chair.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
A. Multnomah County Charter Section 4.50(3) and Multnomah County Code 5.005 require
that elected officials designate an interim occupant to serve until a vacancy is filled by election
or appointment.
B. Charter Section 4.10 states the qualifications for County officers and Code Section
5.005(B)(4) requires that the designee meet the Charter qualifications for appointees of such
offices.
C. Jessica Vega Pederson, Multnomah County Chair, designates Jenny Smith as interim
occupant of that office in the event of a vacancy.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
The Board confirms Jenny Smith to serve as interim occupant for Multnomah County Chair in
the event of a vacancy in that office.
ADOPTED this 3rd day of October, 2024.
SUBMITTED BY: Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
 Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
