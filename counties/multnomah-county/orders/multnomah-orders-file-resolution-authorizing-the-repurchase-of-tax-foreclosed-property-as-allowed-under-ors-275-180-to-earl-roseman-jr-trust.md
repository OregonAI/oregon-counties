BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2023-072
Authorizing the Repurchase of Tax Foreclosed Property as Allowed Under ORS 275.180 to Earl
Roseman Jr. Trust.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The Earl Roseman Jr. Trust is the former owner of certain real property, more particularly
described in the proposed deed to Earl Roseman Jr. Trust (“Former Owner”), att ached
and identified as Exhibit A (“Property”).
b. On or about October 28, 2020, judgment was entered in Multnomah County Circuit Court
foreclosing the delinquent taxes levied against the Property.
c. On October 31, 2022, the County Tax Collector deeded all right, title and interest in the
Property to Multnomah County as authorized under ORS 312.200.
d. Staff have worked with the Former Owner as established residents of the Property and
have identified an opportunity for family housing retention.  A County program has capacit
y
to fund the repurchase price of $32,848.17, an amount consistent with ORS 275.180 and
MCC Subsection 7.402(B).
e. It is in the best public interest that the Property be sold to the Former Owner as described
above in Paragraph d.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
The County Chair is authorized to execute a deed, in substantial conformance with the attached
deed, conveying the Property to Earl Roseman Jr. Trust.
ADOPTED this 24th day of August, 2023.
.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
