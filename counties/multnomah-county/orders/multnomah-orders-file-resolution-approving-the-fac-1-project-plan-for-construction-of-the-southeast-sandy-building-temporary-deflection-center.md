# Resolution Approving The Fac-1 Project Plan For Construction Of The Southeast Sandy Building Temporary Deflection Center. (Resolution Approving The Fac-1 Project Plan For Construction Of The Southeast Sandy Building Temporary Deflection Center.)

## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY , OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-061
Approving the FAC-1 Project Plan for Construction of the Southeast Sandy Building
T emporary Deflection Center.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The Southeast Sandy Building T emporary Deflection Center is a leased property of
Multnomah County located at 900 SE Sandy Blvd Portland, OR 97214.
b. In June of 2024 the County leased this location, which is known as the Southeast
Sandy Building, a former commercial retail space in Southeast Portland, for two years
to serve as a temporary deflection center. The renovation will provide social services
for those experiencing addiction challenges within Multnomah County. This site will
connect individuals who would otherwise be arrested for possession of a controlled
substance to a behavioral health pathway toward recovery.
c. Based on planning and design collaboration between the Health Department
(Behavioral Health), Department of Community Justice, representatives from the
Chair’s office, design team, and Multnomah County Facilities & Property Management,
construction is anticipated to begin this July and opening Phase 1 of 2 on September
1st, 2024.
d. The Southeast Sandy Building has the capacity to serve up to 150 participants in both
Phase 1 and 2. Phase 1 will include the creation of a triage and interview areas as
well as hygiene services with waiting areas. Phase 2 will include a build-out to serve
up to 16 additional participants in need of sobering services.
e. Pursuant to FAC-1, leadership in the Department of County Assets and the Health
Department (the “Project T eam”) have prepared, and recommend approval of, the
Project Plan for Construction for the Southeast Sandy Building with a price range of
$1.5M- $2.0M.
f. Approval of the FAC-1 Project Plan for Construction is in the best interests of
Multnomah County.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
A draft operational project plan will be brought to the Multnomah County Commission by
August 15th for deflection, treatment, and recovery services, which outlines timelines,
deliverables, policy, standard operating procedures, requirements of those being deflected,
goals, reporting, and a Good Neighborhood framework by September 1, 2024, as well as the
direct line of accountability for the work to implement and operate our deflection program.
The Project Plan is approved and the Project T eam is authorized to proceed with construction
of the Project.
ADOPTED this 25th day of July, 2024.
SUBMITTED BY: Kerensa Mauck, Deputy Director, Department of County Assets
