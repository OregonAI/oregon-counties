## Full text

BEFORE THE MULTNOMAH COUNTY LIBRARY DISTRICT BOARD
RESOLUTION NO.  2024-089
Appointing the District Librarian for the Multnomah County Library District:
The Multnomah County Library District Board Finds:
a. Multnomah County Charter Section 9.10(2) provides that the Multnomah County Library
District Board will appoint a district librarian.
b. Annie Lewis is qualified to serve as the MCLD district librarian.
The Multnomah County Library District Board Resolves:
To appoint Annie Lewis as the district librarian for the Multnomah County Library District.
ADOPTED this 17th day of October, 2024.
LIBRARY DISTRICT BOARD
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
Jessica Vega Pederson, Chair
REVIEWED:
JENNY M. MADKOUR, COUNTY ATTORNEY
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
By:
 Jenny M. Madkour, County Attorney
SUBMITTED BY: Travis Graves, Interim Chief Operating Officer and DCM Director
