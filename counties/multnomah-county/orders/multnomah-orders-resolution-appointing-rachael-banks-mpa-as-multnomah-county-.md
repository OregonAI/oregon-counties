## Full text

BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-085
Appointing Rachael Banks, MPA as Multnomah County’s Local Public Health Administrator.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. ORS 431.418 requires the local public health authority to appoint a qualified local public
health administrator.
b. Per ORS 431.003, the Board of County Commissioners is the governing body of the local
public health authority with the right and obligation to appoint the local public health
administrator.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves as Follows:
1. The Board of County Commissioners appoints Rachael Banks, MPA as the Multnomah
County Local Public Health Administrator, effective immediately.
2. The Board has considered standards for the selection of Multnomah County’s Local Public
Health Administrator as prescribed by the Oregon Health Authority. These standards
include: (a) a bachelor’s degree; and (b) public health work experience and education that
demonstrates competencies in the foundational programs as defined by ORS 431.141
and foundational capabilities as defined by ORS 431.131.
3. This action follows procedures outlined in OAR 333-014-0520. Upon appointment of
Rachael Banks, MPA as the Local Public Health Administrator, Multnomah County will
provide notice of the appointment to the Oregon Health Authority.
ADOPTED this 3rd day of October, 2024.
BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
 Jessica Vega Pederson, County Chair
