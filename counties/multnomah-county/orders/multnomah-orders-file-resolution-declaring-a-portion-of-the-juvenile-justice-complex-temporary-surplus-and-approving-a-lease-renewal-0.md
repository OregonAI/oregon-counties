BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2023-062
Declaring a Portion of the Juvenile Justice Complex Temporary Surplus; and Approving
a Lease Renewal.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a.       Approximately 824 square feet of space (the “Premises”) within County owned
Juvenile Justice Complex ("JJC") located at 1401 NE 68th Avenue, Portland,
Oregon is not needed for any County use and is presently being used by The State
of Oregon for office and  administrative functions associated with the Oregon
Department of Human Services (“ODHS”). Pursuant to FAC -3, Sec. IV, it is
appropriate to declare the Premises temporarily surplus in order to continue to
lease the space.
b. ODHS’s original lease is set t o expire on June 30, 2023, and all renewal options
have been exercised. There is mutual desire between the County’s Department of
Community Justice and ODHS to continue to lease the Premises at JJC for its
youth program for an additional five year term com mencing on July 1, 2023 and
expiring June 30, 2028 with no other options to extend the lease. Base rent shall
continue as GRATIS.
c. The continued lease of the Premises will allow The ODHS to remain in close
proximity with State Court Offices and Multnomah  County programs provided at
the JJC. The County’s Department of Community Justice believes that this is a
productive and appropriate co-tenancy and has program benefits.
d. It is in the best interests of the County to declare the Premises temporary surplu s
and to execute a lease renewal substantially in conformance with the terms set
forth in Exhibit 1 (the Lease Renewal) to this Resolution.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1.    The Premises is declared temporary surplus for the term of a lease (as extended
by renewal or amendment) executed in substantial conformance with the terms set
forth in Exhibit 1 (Lease Renewal) to this Resolution.

2. The Chair is authorized to execute such lease and any ancillary instruments and
to execute renewals or amendments of the same without further Board action.
ADOPTED this 22nd day of June, 2023.

RECITALS
TENANT
A. Landlord and Tenant are parties to a Lease commencing April 20, 2016, for space located
in the building known as the "Juvenile Justice Complex" at 1401 NE 68th Avenue,
Portland, Oregon ("Lease").
B. The Lease is set to expire on June 30, 2023, and all renewal options have been exercised.
C. Tenant desires to renew and extend the Lease for a five (5) year period commencing July 1, 2023
through June 30, 2028.
AGREEMENT
1. The parties agree to renew and amend the Lease as follows:
a. Section 1.3 of the Lease is amended such that Tenant and Landlord hereby agree
to a five (5) year extension commencing July 1, 2023 and expiring June 30, 2028.
b. Tenant shall have no other options to extend the Term of the Lease.
c. Base rent shall continue as GRATIS.
2. Except as expressly provided herein, all other terms of the Lease shall remain in full
force and effect.
