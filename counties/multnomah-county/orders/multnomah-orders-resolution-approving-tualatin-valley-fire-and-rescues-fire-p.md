BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2021-011
Approving Tualatin Valley Fire and Rescue¶s Fire Prevention Code, as set forth in Fire
Code Ordinance 2020-01, Pursuant to the Requirements of ORS 478.924, and Repealing
Resolution No. 2014-044.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. Tualatin Valley Fire & Rescue (the “Fire District”), a rural fire district, provides fire and
life safety services to portions of western unincorporated Multnomah County (Exhibit
B).
b. ORS 478.924 requires that the Multnomah County Board of County Commissioners
approve the Fire District¶s fire prevention code before that code can be applied by
the Fire District within the boundaries of Multnomah County.
c. On April 24, 2014, by Resolution No. 2014 -044, the Multnomah County Board of
County Commissioners approved the Fire District¶s fire prevention code, as set forth
in Fire Code Ordinance 14-01.
d. On February 25, 2020 , pursuant to ORS 478, the Board of Directors of the Fire
District adopted a new fire prevention code, set forth in Fire Code Ordinance 2020-
01 (Exhibit A).
e. The general purpose of Fire Code Ordinance 2020-01, as was the purpose of Fire
Code Ordinance 14-01, is to establish the minimum requirements consistent with
nationally recognized good practice for providing a reasonable level of life safety and
property protection from the hazards of fire, explosion or dangerous conditions in new
and existing buildings, structures and premises and to provide for the safety of
firefighters and emergency responders during emergency operations.

forth in Fire Code Ordinance 2020-01, Pursuant to the Requirements of

The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
The Board approves the Tualatin Valley Fire and Rescue Fire Prevention Code Ordinance
2020-01, attached as Exhibit A, and Resolution No. 2014-044 is repealed.
ADOPTED this 11th day of March, 2021.
