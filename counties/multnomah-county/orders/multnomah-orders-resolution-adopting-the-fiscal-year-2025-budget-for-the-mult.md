   BEFORE THE MULTNOMAH COUNTY LIBRARY DISTRICT BOARD  RESOLUTION NO. 2024-047  Adopting the Fiscal Year 2025 Budget for the Multnomah County Library District and Making Appropriations Thereunder, Pursuant to ORS 294.456.    The Multnomah County Library District (MCLD) Board Finds:  a. The MCLD budget, as prepared by the duly appointed MCLD Director/Budget Officer, has been considered and approved by the MCLD Board acting as the Budget Committee.    b. A public hearing on this budget was held before the Multnomah County Tax          Supervising and Conservation Commission on the 29th day of May, 2024.  c. The budget is now on file in the Multnomah County Library’s Administrative Office.  d. The MCLD Board has made one amendment to the above described budget and that amendment is attached to this Resolution as Attachment A.  e.        The appropriation authorized is as follows:      GENERAL FUND (1520)    Library Services    $ 118,492,915 Cash Transfers    $   16,141,902 Operating Contingency    $     1,000,000 Total Appropriation    $ 135,634,817  Reserved for Future Expenditure   $     4,254,267 Unappropriated Balance   $   18,508,553 Total Requirements    $ 158,397,637   CAPITAL FUND (2514) Library Services     $     3,800,000   Operating Contingency   $   68,420,410 Total Appropriation              $   72,220,410                                                                                          Total Requirements              $   72,220,410  f.        The Tax Supervising and Conservation Commission has certified the budget and there are no recommendations and no objections.
