BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2026-004
Approving the Oregon Historical Society Levy Renewal – Explanatory Statement and
Ballot Title.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The Oregon Historical Society houses and preserves Oregon's and Multnomah
County’s collection of historical documents, photos, books, maps and artifacts.
The Museum and Library provide public access to research, study and interact
with these materials to learn Oregon and Multnomah County’s history.
b. In 2021 Multnomah County residents passed a five-year levy of $.05 per $1,000
assessed valuation to support the Oregon Historical Society, including the
Oregon History Library and Museum.
c. A renewal of the five-year levy of $.05 per $1,000 assessed valuation will provide
free admission to all Multnomah County residents and school groups to the
Oregon Historical Society Museum and Research Library, as well as providing
support to the Gresham Historical Society, the Troutdale Historical Society, the
Crown Point Country Historical Society, and the East County History
Organization. An independent citizen oversight committee created to review all
expenditures and ensure dollars are spent as promised will continue to provide
accountability of the funds raised by the levy.
d. The ballot title and explanatory statement for renewal of a five-year levy of $0.05
per $1,000 of assessed property value have been prepared and are attached to
this Resolution as Exhibit A.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
1. The attached ballot title and explanatory statement are approved and certified to
the Director of the Multnomah County Division of Elections.
2. The Board Clerk shall submit this resolution, ballot title and explanatory
statement to the Multnomah County Elections Director for further action as
required by law.
ADOPTED this 15th day of January, 2026.
