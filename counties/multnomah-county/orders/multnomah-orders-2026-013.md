BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
FOR MULTNOMAH COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. 2026-013
Establishing Fees and Charges for Chapters 27 and 29, Community Services, of the Multnomah
County Code and amending Resolution 2020-077.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Finds:
a. The Multnomah County Code provides that the Board shall establish certain fees and
charges by resolution;
b. The Transportation Division collects fees for permits related to the use or modification of
the County’s rights of way and investigates and processes petitions for road vacations.
The user fees that fund these services are not adequate to recover the operational and
administration costs necessary to provide these services;
c. Fees for right of way permitting and road vacation were last increased 5 years ago
(Resolution 2020-077). Costs of providing these services have increased during this
time. It is necessary to increase these fees to sustain the County’s right of way
permitting program;
d. From FY 2027 until FY 2031 there will be an automatic fee increase in the amount of the
lesser of 5% or CPI; and
e. All other County fees and charges established by Resolution 2020-077 are intended to
remain in effect.
The Multnomah County Board of Commissioners Resolves:
The following fees are amended as follows:
