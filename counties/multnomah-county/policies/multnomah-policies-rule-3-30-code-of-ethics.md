A  public  office  is  a  public  trust,  and  all  County  employees,  as  defined  below,  are  public  officials  under  ORS  Chapter  244 and  must  conform  to  this  rule  and  all  relevant  provisions  of  ORS  Chapter  244.

guidelines.

outlined

in

Human

C.  Suspected  ethical  violations  as  outlined  in  this  policy  may  be  reported  to:
1.  Any  manager;

3.  Multnomah  County  Chief  Human  Resources  Officer  (CHRO);
4.  Multnomah  County  Auditor;

6.  State  Code  of  Ethics  (ORS  Chapter  244)  to  the  Oregon  Government  Ethics  Commission.

be

maintained

to

extent

permitted

by
