sexual

or

B.  The  County  provides  leave  to  its  employees  who  are  victims,  or  the  parents  or

C.  The  County  offers  reasonable  safety  accommodation  to  employees  who  are  victims  of
domestic

violence,

harassment,

sexual

assault,

or

stalking.

D.  Requests  for  leave  and  reasonable  safety  accommodation  may  only  be  limited  or

when

E.  Employees  involved  in  domestic  or  non-work  related  situations  that  may  pose  a  risk  to

§  2-65-020   Eligibility  for  Leave  Under  State  Law  A.  County  employees  are  entitled  to  take  a  reasonable  amount  of  paid  or  unpaid  leave  to
address

domestic

violence,

harassment,

sexual

assault

or

in

with
 the  provisions  of  ORS  659A.272.
B.  An  employee  may  take  leave  for  the  following:
1.  To  seek  legal  or  law  enforcement  assistance  or  remedies  to  ensure  the  health
and

safety

of

the

employee

or

the

employee’s

minor

child

or

dependent,

including

preparing

for

and

participating

in

protective

order

proceedings

or

other

civil

or

criminal

to

domestic

violence,

harassment,

sexual

assault,

or

stalking.

2.  To  seek  medical  treatment  for,  or  to  recover  from,  injuries  caused  by  domestic
violence,

harassment,

sexual

assault,

or

stalking

of

the

eligible

employee

or

the

employee’s

minor

child

or

dependent.

3.  To  obtain,  or  to  assist  a  minor  child  or  dependent  as  a  parent  or  guardian ,  in
obtaining,

of

4.  To  obtain  services  from  a  victim  services  provider  for  the  eligible  employee  or  the
employee’s

minor

child

or

5.  To  relocate  or  take  steps  to  secure  an  existing  home  to  ensure  the  health  and
safety

of

the

eligible

employee

or

the

employee’s

minor

child

or

C.  Employees  eligible  for  Safe  Leave  under  Paid  Leave  Oregon  (PLO)  may  be  entitled  to  up  to  twelve  (12)  weeks  of  paid  leave  in  accordance  with  the  provisions  of  ORS  657B.

PLO

 c.  Culturally  specific  circumstances  where  disclosure  may  be  a  risk.
