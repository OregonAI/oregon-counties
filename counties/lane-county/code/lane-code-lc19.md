Chapter 19 – LANE COUNTY DESIGNATED FREE SPEECH AREA TIME, MANNER AND
PLACE PROVISIONS

19.100 – FREE SPEECH AREA PROVISIONS
19.100.005 - Purpose and Findings.
The purpose of this chapter is to establish the Lane County Designated Free Speech Area in which
residents and visitors to Lane County may engage in free speech as guaranteed by the Constitutions of
the State of Oregon and the United States. Lane County has long recognized freedom of speech and
honors the memory of Wayne Morse through the 1984 naming of a public plaza immediately adjacent to
the Lane County Courthouse as the “Wayne Morse Terrace.” Previous Administrative actions recognized
a specific area within the Plaza as the “actual free speech area.”
(Ordinance 13-4, 9.17.13)

19.100.110 - Definitions.
For purpose of this chapter, the following words and phrases have the meanings ascribed:
 “Actual Free Speech Area” means an area within the Wayne Morse Terrace, beginning at the
podium west of Harris Hall, a semi-oval area contained by the Wayne Morse statue on the south and the
planter areas to the west and north, and measuring approximately 71’ x 73’.
 “Camp” means to set up or to remain in or at a campsite.
 “Campsite: means any place where any bedding, sleeping bag, or other material used for bedding is
placed to maintain a temporary place to live, whether or not such place incorporates the use of any tent,
lean-to, shack, or any other structure, or any vehicle or part thereof.
 “Organized user” means an individual or group of individuals whose intent is to utilize the free
speech area to exercise their free speech rights.
 “Wayne Morse Terrace” means the front plaza of the Lane County Courthouse, abutted by W 8th
Avenue and Oak Street, so designated by Lane County Board Order No. 84-6-19-2.
(Ordinance 13-4, 9.17.13)

19.100.115 - Limits on Use.
A. The Designated Free Speech Area is open from 6AM - 11PM.
B. Users of the Free Speech Area may not create a nuisance as otherwise defined in Lane Code or
Eugene Municipal Code.
(Ordinance 13-4, 9.17.13)

19.100.120 - Permitted Use Required.
A. A utility permit must be acquired for any use of the Designated Free Speech Area that requires
electricity.

B. The County Administrator or designee will establish procedures for the actual issuance of permits
consistent with Chapter 60 of the Lane Manual.
C. Fees. Utility permit fees shall be established by separate Order of the Board.
(Ordinance 13-4, 9.17.13)

19.100.125 - Enforcement.
Unauthorized use of the Designated Free Speech Area may be punishable as trespass under Lane Code
§6.025 and the City of Eugene Municipal Code §4.815.
(Ordinance 13-4, 9.17.13)
