Klamath County Human Resources

ELECTED OFFICIALS
 Salary Schedule

Effective July 1, 2026

In accordance with the Cost of Living Adjustment for County Employees, the following salaries will
become effective on July 1, 2026 for the County's Elected Officials:

    ANNUAL   MONTHLY

Assessor   $102,259.22   $8,521.60

Clerk    $98,078.51   $8,173.21

Commissioner  $95,090.91             $7,924.24

District Attorney*  $53,580.60   $4,465.05

Justice of the Peace  $55,487.82   $4,623.99

Sheriff**   $137,894.28   $11,491.19

Surveyor   $59,657.27   $4,971.44

Treasurer   $23,480.83       $1,956.74

Notes:
*This accounts only for compensation paid by Klamath County and does not include compensation paid to the
District Attorney by the State of Oregon.

**Sheriff Salary based on ORS
