Economic Development
Whether it’s a Business Relocation or New Startup – It’s Just Better Here.
You're ready to move an existing business or start a new business and you are wondering if
Klamath County could be the place? Well, you've come to the right spot. Klamath Falls is the
ideal place to relocate your existing business or start one from the ground up.

The atmosphere is definitely pro-business and there are many resources here to support you. Our
"Team Klamath" approach has been in operation for more than 20 years and is a successful
model that ensures you have a partner every step of the way as you move your business – and
your life – to Klamath.
