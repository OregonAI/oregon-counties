ARTICLE TWO

DIVISION TWELVE             PARK MODEL RV INSTALLATION

SECTION 2.12.010            FINDINGS

The Board of Curry County Commissioners finds that because of the high winds and
seismic activity in the county, it is in the County’s best interest to adopt installation
standards for park model recreational vehicles.

SECTION 2.12.020            AUTHORITY

This division is authorized under OAR 918-530-005(1) which allows counties and cities
to adopt installation standards for park model recreation vehicles.

SECTION 2.12.030            PARK MODEL RV INSTALLATION STANDARDS

The attached requirements of OAR 918-530-005 through 918-530-120 and Tables 1,
2, and 3 and OAR 918-535-010 through 918-535-070, which are incorporated by
reference, are adopted as requirements in the unincorporated areas of Curry County, as
well as in the incorporated cities of Port Orford and Gold Beach.
