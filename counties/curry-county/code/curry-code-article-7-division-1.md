             ARTICLE SEVEN — DISTRICTS AND ELECTIONS

DIVISION ONE                    TAXING DISTRICT ASSISTANCE

SECTION 7.01.010                RESERVED

SECTION 7.01.020                RESERVED

SECTION 7.01.030                PURPOSE STATEMENT

The purpose of this division is to allow the County to provide assistance to Taxing
Districts within Curry County through the lending of funds, thereby promoting
public health, safety, convenience and general welfare, and to include in allowed
funds federally sourced road funds as authorized by 2017 House Bill 3435 codified
as ORS 368.709.

SECTION 7.01.040                DEFINITIONS

The following words and phrases shall mean:

(1) Road Funds: As used herein, Road Funds shall mean moneys from federal
sources in the county Road Capital Improvement Fund.

(2)Taxing Districts. As used in this ordinance except as otherwise provided,
“Taxing District” means:

      a. Any governmental unit in Curry County that has the right to receive ad
      valorem real property taxes.

SECTION 7.01.060                FILING APPLICATION AND SETTING
                                HEARING DATE

Upon receiving an application for assistance, and upon determining that it meets
the requirements of SECTION 7.01.050, the County Administrator shall set a date
for a public hearing. The hearing shall be set not less than 15 days nor more than
60 days from the date of filing, but may be continued by the Board for good cause
shown for an additional 60 days. All information in the application will be subject
to public disclosure and exemptions therefrom under Oregon Public Records Law.

SECTION 7.01.070                NOTICE OF HEARING

The Board shall give notice by posting the time, place and reason for the hearing in
the County Courthouse and the County Annex Administrative Office Building; by
publishing once in a newspaper of general circulation in the County at least one
week prior to the hearing date; and by First Class U.S. mail letter to the office of
the Taxing District. The County may use any other reasonable method of notice,
including but not limited to posting on the County website and email.

SECTION 7.01.080                PUBLIC HEARING

On the date set, a public hearing shall be held. The hearing shall be held in an
orderly manner with all interested persons being given an opportunity to be heard.
The Board Chair or a person he/she designates shall preside over the hearing. All
relevant testimony and evidence shall be received and considered by the Board.
SECTION 7.01.090                  DECISION ON APPLICATION

Within 30 days of the hearing the Board shall either (a) deny the application, (b)
grant the application in whole or in part, or (c) continue the hearing to a later date
if the Board is of the opinion that additional relevant information may become
available. The total of any continuations shall not be more than 60 days.

SECTION 7.01.100                  DENIAL OF THE APPLICATION

If the Board is of the opinion that the granting of the assistance requested would
not be in the public interest or that the assistance is not available, the Board shall
deny the application and notify the Taxing District of its decision in writing by
First Class US mail, not later than 30 days from a decision.

When an application for assistance has been denied, the Taxing District shall not
be allowed to make another application until the expiration of six (6) months from
the date the notification of denial is mailed.

SECTION 7.01.110                  GRANTING AN APPLICATION

If the Board is of the opinion that the granting of the application for assistance
would be in the public interest and that the assistance requested is available from
Taxing District Assistance Fund described herein the Board may grant the
application for assistance in whole or in part.

When the application is so granted the Board and the governing body of the Taxing
District shall enter into a written agreement providing among other things for the
disbursement and repayment of the assistance and the use or uses for which the
funds are authorized.

The loan agreement will be an intergovernmental agreement but does not create
any partnership or joint venture or separate government entity under ORS Chapter
190 or otherwise.
SECTION 7.01.120                        LOAN TERMS

Curry County intends to responsibly manage the road funds available for lending
under this Ordinance by considering liquidity, security and rate of return. Those
considerations will be addressed by the terms of any given loan. Such terms shall
include but are not limited to:

A. Interest Rate: Minimum of 4% above the annual interest the road fund receives
from its investment pool as defined in ORS 294.805.

B. Loan Term in Years:     Loans shall not exceed 5 years in duration.

C. Collateral and Security: Applicant shall be required to give County a security
interest in any capital asset, including real estate.

      i. Loan will not exceed 40% of entire project cost or real property value or
      capital value.

      ii. Borrower must have equity of at least 20% in capital or real property
      subject to loan.

      iii. Borrower will maintain insurance on any project or capital

D. Project Related Requirement: As applicable, the loan document shall have
project-specific requirements including but not limited to:

             i.   Abide by all laws

             ii. Borrower shall not allow liens on property

             iii. Borrower shall obtain certificate of satisfactory completion

             iv. Borrower shall allow inspections by county agents

             v. Borrower shall allow County to audit finances and records

             vi. Project loan funds may be disbursed in progress payments

E. Default

      Any payment delinquent more than 10 days late past due date

F. County is not required to lend funds if not appropriated in budget

G. Borrower will pay all closing costs on any real property purchase

H. Payments commence one month after funds issued

I. Application becomes part of agreement. Any loan will be subject to the
provisions of this Ordinance in general, and will include specific terms, as
applicable. The County reserves the right to include any reasonable terms in its
loan agreement.

SECTION 7.01.130                 COUNTY REMEDIES FOR DEFAULT

If an applicant under a loan agreement is in default, the County may invoke any
remedy allowed by law, including but not limited to:

      a. revoke the loan agreement

      b. require repayment to county of any unexpended funds

      c. withhold further distributions under the agreement

      d. offset future tax distributions to borrower in amount in default

      e. bar applicant from applying for future loans

Prior to invoking any remedy above, the County shall notify the borrower and
allow at least 30 days for borrow to cure the default.

SECTION 7.01.140                 TAXING DISTRICT LOAN FUND

There shall be created a Taxing District Loan Fund for the purpose of
implementing this ordinance. The fund shall consist of monies appropriated by the
Curry County Budget Committee at their regular annual sessions or special
meeting called for that purpose; monies appropriated by the Board of Curry
County Commissioners at supplemental budget hearing; and monies repaid to the
County by Taxing Districts as provided in the respective written agreements.

The amount of monies in this fund shall determine the amount of assistance
available to grant applications. In no event shall the Taxing District Assistance
Fund — including outstanding loans — contain more than 10% (ten per-cent) of the
Road Funds described herein.
