 ### ARTICLE 13.00 - I-2 HEAVY INDUSTRIAL USE ZONE

 ### 13.01 - PURPOSE

The Heavy Industrial Zone is intended to provide for new or continued industrial development
utilizing large amounts of labor, raw materials or energy, and possibly creating smoke,
odor, vibration, noise, or other conditions not attracted to urban areas. Items manufactured,
processed or produced in this zone shall be primarily for wholesale.

 ### 13.02 - PERMITTED USES

The following uses are permitted outright in an I-2 Zone.
1.
All uses involving manufacturing, processing or storage of materials except explosives
or chemicals.
2.
Freight terminal warehouses.
3.
Wholesale operations.
4.
Animal hospitals.
5.
Storage facilities.
6.
Lumber milling and wood processing.
7.
Stockyard, feedlot or slaughter houses.
8.
Petroleum storage and distribution.
9.
Farm use.
(Ord. No. 2026-01, Art. 13, 3-18-2026)

 ### 13.03 - CONDITIONAL USES

In an I-2 Zone the following uses and their accessory uses may be established as conditional
uses subject to the approval of the Planning Commission when authorized in accordance
with the requirements of Article 21.00 .
1.
Eating and drinking establishments and service stations.
2.
Governmental use or activity.
3.
Airports and heliports.
4.
Manufacturing, processing, or storage of explosives or chemicals.
5.
Junkyards.
6.
Waste disposal, sorting, and recycling sites.
7.
Aggregate and mineral exploration and processing.
8.
Concrete batching plants.
9.
Asphalt plants.
10.
Outdoor amusement or recreational use including, but not limited to, golf course,
race track, rodeo arena or amusement park.
11.
Telecommunications towers.
12.
Utility facilities necessary for public service including, but not limited to, substations,
power generating facilities for public and private use, and/or other related structural
uses.
13.
Boarding of dogs for profit.
14.
Other uses per criteria in subsection 21.06.2.
15.
Temporary workforce housing.
(Ord. No. 2026-01, Art. 13, 3-18-2026)

 ### 13.04 - DIMENSIONAL STANDARDS

Standards in the I-2 Zone shall be the same as in the I-1 Zone.
(Ord. No. 2026-01, Art. 13, 3-18-2026)
Editor's note—
Ord. No. 2026-01, Art. 13, adopted March 18, 2026, repealed § 13.04, which pertained
to prohibited uses and derived from the original Code. Subsequently, Ord. No. 2026-01,
Art. 13, adopted March 18, 2026, renumbered the former §§ 13.05, 13.06 as §§ 13.04,
13.05. The historical notation has been retained with the amended provisions for reference
purposes.

 ### 13.05 - DEVELOPMENT STANDARDS

1.
Standards in the I-2 Zone shall be the same as in the I-1 Zone.
2.
Development is also subject to the Baum Industrial Park Development, Performance and
Maintenance Standards Overlay if property is located within that overlay. The most
restrictive requirements apply.
(Ord. No. 2026-01, Art. 13, 3-18-2026)
Editor's note—
Ord. No. 2026-01, Art. 13, adopted March 18, 2026, renumbered the former § 13.06 as
§ 13.05. The historical notation has been retained with the amended provisions for
