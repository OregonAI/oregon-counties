 ### ARTICLE 40.00 - SURFACE MINING IMPACT AREA (SMIA) OVERLAY ZONE

 ### 40.01 - PURPOSE

The purpose of the SMIA Overlay Zone is to protect designated surface mining resources
in Surface Mining Zones from new development which conflicts with the removal and/or
processing of a mineral or aggregate resource while allowing property owners near
surface mining sites reasonable use of their property.

 ### 40.02 - LOCATION

The SMIA Overlay Zone shall apply to all property located within one-quarter mile
of the boundary of a Surface Mining Zone, except property located within an urban
growth boundary, a city limits or another county.

 ### 40.03 - APPLICATION OF PROVISIONS

The SMIA Overlay Zone and its provisions shall be applied in combination with each
Surface Mining Zone.
The SMIA Overlay Zone standards and provisions shall apply in addition to those specified
in the underlying zone. If a conflict occurs between the provisions of the SMIA Overlay
Zone and the provisions of the underlying zone, the provisions of the SMIA Overlay
Zone shall govern.

 ### 40.04 - USES PERMITTED OUTRIGHT

Uses permitted outright shall be those identified in the underlying zone(s), except
noise or dust sensitive uses or structures as defined in Section 1.08 shall be identified as conditional uses subject to Section 40.05 provisions.

 ### 40.05 - CONDITIONAL USES

The following uses may be established as conditional uses subject to Planning Commission
review and approval:
1.
Conditional uses in the underlying zone(s) that are not noise or dust sensitive uses
or structures shall be reviewed as conditional uses subject to the standards and provisions
of the underlying zone.
2.
Noise or dust sensitive uses or structures shall be reviewed as conditional uses subject
to standards and provisions in Section 40.06 and site plan review and approval requirements in Section 40.07 .

 ### 40.06 - DEVELOPMENT STANDARDS

The following standards shall apply to all dwellings in a SMIA Overlay Zone:
1.
Setbacks.
The setbacks shall be the same as those prescribed in the underlying zone, except
as follows:
A.
No noise or dust-sensitive use or structure established or constructed after the designation
of the SMIA Overlay Zone shall be located within 250 feet of any Surface Mining Zone,
except as provided in Subsection 15.05 A.4.
B.
No noise or dust-sensitive use or structures established or constructed after the
designation of the SMIA Overlay Zone shall be located within one-quarter mile of any
existing or proposed surface mining processing or storage site, unless the applicant
demonstrates that the proposed use will n to prevent the adjacent surface mining operation
from meeting the setbacks, standards and conditions set forth in Section 15.05 .
2.
Minimum Lot Size.
The lot size in a SMIA Overlay Zone shall be that prescribed in the underlying zone.

 ### 40.07 - SITE PLAN REQUIREMENTS

1.
Applicability.
A.
New dwellings, now noise and dust sensitive uses or structures, or additions exceeding
10% of the size of the existing dwellings or noise and dust-sensitive uses or structures
shall be subject to site plan review and approval.
B.
Waiver of Remonstrance. The applicant for site plan approval in the SMIA Overlay Zone
shall sign and record with the Union County Clerk a statement declaring that the applicant
and his successors will not now or in the future complain about the allowed surface
mining activities on the adjacent surface mining site.

 ### 40.08 - DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT AND PERFORMANCE BOND

As a condition of site plan approval, the applicant may be required to execute a development
agreement with the county and performance bond or with other form of security approved
by the county to ensure full and faithful performance of any improvements required
to meet the setbacks, standards and conditions set forth above. Any bond shall be
for 110% of the dollar amount of the improvement costs.

 ### 40.09 - EXEMPTIONS

The following shall be exempt from this section:
A.
Uses in the SMIA Overlay Zone which are not within one-quarter mile of any identified
resource in the Surface Mining Zone after all reclamation has occurred.
B.
Continuation and maintenance of a conforming or nonconforming use established prior
to the effective date of this Ordinance.
C.
The employment of land for farm and forest use.
D.
Additions to a dwelling existing on the effective date of this Ordinance which are
completely screened from the surface mining site by the existing dwelling.
