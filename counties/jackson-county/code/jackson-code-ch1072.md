1072.01 EMERGENCY MEDICAL CARE FOR PRISONERS.

(a) Treatment Required.  No person in the custody of any law enforcement officer shall
be accepted for custody at the County Correctional Facility until any readily apparent
condition requiring medical services has been treated.

(b) Liability for Expenses.  Liability for the expenses of providing emergency medical
services is the responsibility of the recipient of such services and the public agency
employing the  law enforcement officer having custody of such person, as provided in
ORS 169.166.

(c) Authority and Scope.  Subsections (c) through (e) hereof are adopted pursuant to the
provisions of ORS 203.035 and apply to justice courts within Jackson County.

(d) Purpose.  The purpose of subsections (c) through (e) hereof is to ensure that
sufficient funds will exist in the Oregon State Law Enforcement Medical Liability
Account (LEMLA) to pay prisoner medical expenses at the time that the LEMLA
becomes liable for such costs on July 1, 1992.  Adequate funds will accrue only if justice
courts collect and deposit designated County corrections assessment fees into the
LEMLA, as circuit, district and municipal courts are required to do.

1072.02 PAYMENT OF PRISONERS FOR SERVICES.

Inmates in the County Jail will be reimbursed at the rate of one dollar ($1.00) per day for
services performed as trusties on County work crews, which compensation shall be paid
out of the County Sheriff's budget.

(Order 123-77. Passed 4-8-77.)
