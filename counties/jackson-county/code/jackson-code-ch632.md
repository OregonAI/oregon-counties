632.01 DEFINITIONS.

Unless the context or subject matter otherwise requires, terms, defined here shall have the
following meaning when used in this Chapter.

(a) Alarm System - means any assembly of equipment and devices installed on
premises, buildings or structures for the purpose of signaling the presence of an
emergency or hazard requiring urgent attention, including burglar and robbery alarm
systems, excepting any system, device, or mechanism primarily protecting a motor
vehicle.

(b) Alarm System User - means the person who owns the premises upon which an
alarm system is installed, the tenant of the premises upon which an alarm system is
installed, or the subscriber, client, or customer of an alarm company operator.

(c) Emergency - means the commission or attempted commission of a robbery,
burglary, or other criminal activity.

(d) False Alarm - means the activation of an alarm system resulting in a response by the
Jackson County Sheriff’s Office when an emergency of the kind for which the alarm
system was designed does not exist.

(Ord. 2017-16. Passed 11-08-17.)

632.02 FALSE ALARMS - VIOLATION.

It shall be a violation of this Chapter for any alarm system user to allow or fail to prevent
their alarm system from signaling a false alarm.

(Ord. 2017-16. Passed 11-08-17.)

632.03 WARNING.

Notwithstanding any provision of Chapter 203 of these Codified Ordinances, prior to the
issuance of any citation for a false alarm, an employee of the Jackson County Sheriff’s
Office shall issue the alarm system user a warning of violation upon the signaling of the
first false alarm within the past 365 days and upon the signaling of a second false alarm
within 365 days of the first false alarm.  The warnings of violation may be made either
orally or in writing and should be reas onably calculated to apprise the alarm system user
that any additional false alarms may result in monetary penalties.

(Ord. 2017-16. Passed 11-08-17.)

632.04 ENFORCEMENT.

Upon the signaling of a third false alarm within 365 days of the second false alarm or upon
the signaling of any additional false alarm within 365 days of the prior false alarm, this
Chapter may be enforced by code enforcement officers in accordance with Chapter 203 of
these Codified Ordinances.

(Ord. 2017-16. Passed 11-08-17.)

632.99 PENALTY.

The penalties for any violations of this Chapter shall be as set forth in Chapter 202.99 of
these Codified Ordinances.

(Ord. 2017-16. Passed 11-08-17.)
