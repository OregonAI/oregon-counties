1448.02 REMISSION TO STATE OF SURCHARGES.

The State Building Codes Agency levies surcharges on fees collected by the County for
building permits. Jackson County shall remit to the State Building Codes Agency an
amount equal to the surcharge fees established in ORS 455.210(4), 455.210(5) and
455.220(1). Such remittances shall be on affidavits filed by the Building Official.

(Ord. 92-7. Passed 7-1-92; P. Ord. 93-5. Passed 3-10-93; E. Ord. 93-6. Passed 2-17-93.)
