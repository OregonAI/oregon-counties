420.01 PERMIT REQUIRED.

No person, group of persons or organization shall conduct any event or activity on a
County road, which event or activity requires traffic control measures, without first
obtaining a permit therefor as provided in this chapter.

(Ord. 83-1. Passed 2-16-83; E. Ord. 2021-5. Passed 6-9-21; Ord. 2021-4. Passed 6-23-21)

420.02 PERMIT APPLICATION.

A person wishing to conduct an organized event or activity on a County road, which
event or activity requires traffic control measures, shall submit an application for a
special event permit to the County Administrator not later than thirty days prior to the
proposed event or activity. Such application shall include at least the following
information:

(a) The date, time, duration and general description of the event or activity;

(b) A description or illustration of the portion of the County road desired to be used; and

420.03 REVIEW OF APPLICATION BY SHERIFF AND ROADS AND PARKS
DIRECTOR.

The County Administrator shall transmit the appropriate information, submitted under
Section 420.02, to the Sheriff and the Roads and Parks Director for their
recommendations regarding the feasibility of conducting the event or activity and control
measures necessary to ensure public safety.

(Ord. 83-1. Passed 2-16-83; Ord. 2003-4. Passed 1-22-03; Ord. 2007-2. Passed 1-31-07;

420.04 INSURANCE.

A person wishing to conduct an organized event or activity on a County road, which
event or activity requires traffic control measures, shall provide, in conjunction with the
application submitted under Section 420.02, a certificate of insurance and endorsement
which verifies that the County and its officers, agents, employees, boards and
commissions are named as additional insureds on policies of insurance for public liability
for the organized event or activity. These policies of insurance shall extend coverage for
the risk referred to in the recitals in an amount of not less than the limitation on awards
for the liability of the county, and the liability of the County’s officers, employees, and
agents acting within the scope of their employment or duties as set forth in the ORS
30.272 or any successor statute.

(Ord. 83-1. Passed 2-16-83; E. Ord. 2021-5. Passed 6-9-21; Ord. 2021-4. Passed 6-23-21)

420.05 ISSUANCE OF PERMIT; CONDITIONS.

The County Administrator may issue a special event permit to the applicant to use a
County road for conducting an organized event or activity. Such permit may include
reasonable conditions regarding the use of the public facilities specified, such as
limitations upon times of use, signs and directional markings, and the requirement that
public facilities be left in a good and clean condition after the event or activity.

(Ord. 83-1. Passed 2-16-83; E. Ord. 2021-5. Passed 6-9-21; Ord. 2021-4. Passed 6-23-21)

420.06 DENIAL OF PERMIT.

The County Administrator may deny permission to use a County road for an event or
activity if the applicant fails to comply with this chapter or if the County Administrator
determines, following input from the Sheriff and the Roads and Parks Director, that the
conduct of the event or activity would not be in the public interest. Such denial shall
occur not less than ten days before the scheduled event or activity.

(Ord. 83-1. Passed 2-16-83; Ord. 2003-4. Passed 1-22-03; Ord. 2007-2. Passed 1-31-07;
E. Ord. 2021-5. Passed 6-9-21; Ord. 2021-4. Passed 6-23-21)

420.07 APPEALS.

Any decision of the County Administrator may be appealed to the Board of County
Commissioners. The Board shall hear the appeal at the earliest possible meeting date
following receipt of the notice of appeal.

(Ord. 83-1. Passed 2-16-83.)
