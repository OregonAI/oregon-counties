296.01 NAME CHANGE.

“Justice Court District of Jackson County”.

(Ord. 2005-22, Passed 11-23-05.)

296.02 BOUNDARIES.

The boundaries of the new Justice Court District of Jackson County are hereby set off
and established as follows:
296.02 Justice of the Peace 201
2019 Replacement

JUSTICE COURT DISTRICT OF JACKSON COUNTY

The North, East, South and West boundaries of the Justice Court District of Jackson
County shall be co-extensive with the boundaries of the County of Jackson, State of
Oregon; EXCEPTI NG HOWEVER, all lands situate and being within the corporate
limits of the City of Medford, Oregon.

(Ord. 2005-22, Passed 11-23-05)
