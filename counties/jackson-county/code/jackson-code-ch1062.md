1062.01 PREVIOUS AND SPECIAL REGULATIONS.

(a) Previous Regulations. The regulations set forth in this chapter supersede and cancel
all other previous regulations as set forth by the County.

(b) Special Regulations, Notices or Directives. Special regulations, notices, memoranda
or directives of an operational nature, of interest to persons engaged in business with the
Rogue Valley International-Medford Airport, shall be issued under the authority of this
chapter.

(c) Any violation of any federal, state or local government laws, statutes, codes,
regulations, ordinances, or administrative rules of the Federal Aviation Administration,
Transportation Security Administration, and other applicable federal agencies or state
agencies constitutes a violation of this chapter and may be enforced by authority of this
chapter.

(Order 24-74. Passed 3-13-74; Order 94-95. Passed 3-15-95; Ord. 2002-8. Passed 2-13-
02; Ord. 2015-13. Passed 10-07-15.)

1062.02 DEFINITIONS.

As used in this chapter:

(a) Air Operations Area - means all areas of the Airport in which aircraft might move in
a normal manner, including, but not limited to, the landing field, all runways, taxiways,
aprons or ramps and hangar areas.

(b) Aircraft - means any contrivance, now known or hereafter invented, used or
designed for navigation of or flight in air.

(c) Airport - means the Rogue Valley International- Medford Airport and all
improvements and appurtenances contained thereon.

located at the Rogue Valley International-Medford Airport.

(f) Airport Departm ent - means the department of Jackson County that operates the

1062.04 BUILDING REQUIREMENTS AND GROUND RENTALS.

(a) Any person desiring to erect or construct a building on the Rogue Valley
International-Medford Airport shall be required to submit plans and specifications for the
same to the office of the Airport Director. The plans shall include a general layout, drawn
to scale, showing the desired amount of ground actually required for the operation of
such building, in addition to the portion occupied by the building proper.

(b) All buildings erected upon the Airport shall conform to the Fire Building Code
requirements of the City of Medford.

(c) All persons desiring to erect or construct on the Rogue Valley International-
Medford Airport, who have approved plans, must pull all required permits with the
appropriate jurisdictional authority prior to construction and must file a copy of those
permits with the Airport Director.

(Order 24-74. Passed 3-13-74; Order 94-95. Passed 3-15-95; Ord. 2002-8. Passed 2-13-

1062.05 NONLIABILITY OF COUNTY.

The permission granted by the County to use the Rogue Valley International- Medford
Airport and its facilities, or to fly to, from or over the same, shall be, at all times,
conditioned upon the assumption of full responsibility therefor by every person
exercising or taking advantage of such permission. Further, it shall be a condition thereof
that each person, as a consideration for the use of the Airport and its facilities, shall, at all
times, release, hold harml ess and indemnify the County, the Airport Director and their
agents and employees from any and all responsibility, liability, loss or damage resulting
to any such person, or caused by or on his or her behalf, and incident to the manner in
which the Airport is operated, constructed or maintained, served from within or without,
or used from without. The use of the Airport by any person  for  any  purpose,  the
paying of fees therefor  or  the  taking  off  or  landing  of  aircraft thereon, shall be itself
an acknowledgment that such person accepts such privileges with the conditions set forth
in this section.

(Order 24-74. Passed 3-13-74; Order 94-95. Passed 3-15-95; Ord. 2002-8. Passed 2-13-

and all facilities and equipment in connection therewith and for the enforcement of this
chapter.

1062.07 ACCIDENT REPORTS.

All persons involved in any accident, personal, aircraft, or automotive, outside individual
work areas, occurring on the premises of the Rogue Valley International -Medford
Airport, shall notify the Airport Director and make a report to the Medford Police
Department as soon as possible, giving all pertinent information as reques ted by the
officer in charge, and provide the Airport with a copy of the report.

(Order 24-74. Passed 3-13-74; Order 94-95. Passed 3-15-95; Ord. 2002-8. Passed 2-13-

1062.08 PARADES, DEMONSTRATIONS AND OBSTRUCTIONS.

No person shall conduct or participate in any parade, march, patrol, demonstration, sit-
down, assemblage or distribution of pamphlets or other materials, or carry or display
signs or placards in or upon, or in any manner obstruct buildings, grounds, roads, walks,
approaches or any of the property of, the Rogue Valley International-Medford Airport,
without the written permission of the Airport Director therefor.

(Order 24-74. Passed 3-13-74; Order 94-95. Passed 3-15-95.)

1062.09 SOUND-AMPLIFYING DEVICES.

Sound trucks and amplified record-playing machines are prohibited on the Rogue Valley
International-Medford Airport, except as authorized by appropriate County authority.
Only such public address systems as are commonly employed, announcing the arrival and
departure of scheduled airline aircraft, shall be permitted on the Airport.

(Order 24-74. Passed 3-13-74; Order 94-95. Passed 3-15-95.)

1062.10 USE OF STREETS AND SIDEWALKS.

Airport highways may be used as a means of ingress and egress by highway vehicles to,
from and between th e city streets with which such highways connect and various
buildings and land areas at the Rogue Valley International -Medford Airport abutting
upon such highways. Sidewalks along such highways (and other portions of such
highways when designated for that purpose) may be used by pedestrians as a means of
ingress and egress to, from and between various portions of the Airport.

(Order 24-74. Passed 3-13-74; Order 94-95. Passed 3-15-95.)

1062.11 RESTRICTED AREAS.

All areas of the Rogue Valley International- Medford Airport, except public areas, are
restricted, and no person shall enter upon the landing field, runways, taxiways, airline
communications offices of the Terminal Building, control tower, utility and service
rooms or areas, hangars or aprons of the Airport, or any other areas that may be
“RESTRICTED”, except:

(a) Persons assigned to duty therein, who are badged to be in that area or appropriately
escorted;

(b) Pilots with a valid pilot’s license going to or from their aircraft or hangar on general
aviation business;

(c) Authorized representatives of the FAA, with the appropriate Airport authorized
badges;

(d) Persons authorized by the Airport Director;

(e) Passengers under appropriate supervision entering the apron or private hangars for
the purpose of enplaning and deplaning;

(f) Business representatives in the conduct of their affairs with the airlines or other
tenants who are badged or escorted; or

(g) Authorized badged tenants.

(h) Temporary access by any other person will be allowed with the express consent of
the Airport Director, and such persons will be under supervision or escort.

(Order 24-74. Passed 3-13-74; Order 94-95. Passed 3-15-95; Ord. 2002-8. Passed 2-13-

1062.13 LICENSING AND REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND AIRCRAFT.

(a) No person shall operate motorized ground equipment of any kind on the Rogue
Valley International-Medford Airport without a valid State operator's license.

(b) No person shall operate any motor vehicle in the air operations area without first
registering the same with the Airport Director and obtaining a permit for the operation of
such equipment.  Such permit shall be issued upon payment of any fee, if required, as
established from time to time by the Board of County Commissioners and upon such
terms as may be prescribed by the Airport Director.

(c) No person shall operate from the Airport any aircraft that is not fully certificated by
the Federal Aviation Administration, registered with the Administration and carrying a
current certificate of airworthiness, with the exception of government-owned aircraft.

(Order 24-74. Passed 3-13-74; Order 94-95. Passed 3-15-95; Ord. 2002-8. Passed 2-13-
02.)

1062.14 RULES OF OPERATION GENERALLY.

(a) No person shall operate a motor vehicle of any kind on the Rogue Valley
International-Medford Airport in a reckless manner or in excess of the speed limits
prescribed by the Airport Director.

(b) Pedestrians and aircraft shall at all times have the right of way over vehicular traffic.
All vehicles shall pass to the rear of taxiing aircraft and on the field side of parked
aircraft and shall pass not nearer than a twenty -foot horizontal distance from any wing or
tail section of a parked aircraft.

(c) No person operating a motor vehicle on the Airport shall fail to give proper signals
or fail to observe the directions of posted traffic signs.

(d) No person under the influence of intoxicating liquor or narcotic or dangerous drugs
shall operate a motor vehicle or aircraft on the Airport.

(e) No person shall operate any motor vehicle on the Airport that is overloaded or
carrying more passengers than that for which the vehicle was designed. No person shall
ride on the running board, stand up in the body of or ride with arms or legs protruding
from the body of a motor vehicle.

(f) No vehicle shall be operated on the Airport if it is so constructed, equipped or
loaded as to endanger persons or property.

(g) No person shall operate a motorcycle, truck or other motor vehicle without exhausts
protected by screens or baffles to prevent the escape of sparks or the propagation of flame
in any hangar on the Airport.

(h) When parking adjacent to a runway, all vehicles must be parked parallel to the
runway and at least 200 feet to the outside of the runway lights.

(i) Two-way radio communications with the tower will be required of all authorized
vehicles traversing on the air oper ations area (excluding resident general aviation tie-
down apron and private hangar areas) and such vehicles shall display and fly on the rear
thereof an all-yellow or checkered flag, or light as required under Federal regulations, of
a size not less than t hree feet square.  All vehicles operating on usable runways or
taxiways used by air carriers without radio equipment shall be escorted by a vehicle
having two- way radio communication. The escort vehicle must be in continuous
communication with the control tower.

(j) No vehicle shall cross a runway or active area unless specific clearance is obtained
by the operator thereof from the traffic control tower by radio communications.

(k) Emergency conditions existing at the time on the Airport will not suspend these
regulations or excuse their violation. During such conditions, the driver of any vehicle,
civilian or military, shall make certain that he or she does not move his or her vehicle in
any direction, unless specifically cleared by the traffic control tower to do so. Permits
issued under Section 1062.13 shall be rendered invalid until the emergency situation has
been alleviated. The Airport Director shall determine when normal operations may be
resumed.

1062.15 SPEED LIMITS.

(a) No person shall drive a motor vehicle or a motor bicycle upon any public street or
road within the territorial limits of the Rogue Valley International- Medford Airport at a
speed greater than is reasonable and prudent or posted, having regard to the traffic,
surface and width of the street or road, hazard at intersections, other conditions then
existing and use of the street and road.

(b) Any speed in excess of posted and authorized speed limit shall be prima facie
evidence of a violation of subsection (a) hereof.

1062.16 CARELESS OPERATION.

No person shall operate a motor vehicle or motor bicycle upon any street or road within
the territorial limits of the Rogue Valley International -Medford Airport in a careless
manner. As used in this section, "careless manner" means in a manner that endangers or
would be likely to endanger any person or property.

(Order 24-74. Passed 3-13-74; Order 94-95. Passed 3-15-95.)

1062.17 PARKING.

(a) No person shall park a motor vehicle for loading, unloading or any other purpose on
the Rogue Valley International- Medford Airport other than in the areas specifically
designated for parking and in the manner prescribed by signs, lines or other means. No
person shall abandon any motor vehicle on the Airport. No person shall park a motor
vehicle in a manner as to obstruct roadways, or in aircraft parking areas or on grassed
areas.

(b) No person shall park a vehicle in any space marked for parking in such a manner as
to occupy part of another marked space, nor shall any person park, stand or stop any
vehicle in any reserved or restricted area so marked.

(c) Parking in designated public parking areas is open to all members of the public
using the Airport. An employee parking lot is provided for the exclusive use of Airport
employees working in the Terminal area of the Airport.

(d) All emplo yees of organizations and agencies having tenancy in the Terminal
Building area shall park private vehicles in the employee parking lot, as may be
designated by the Airport Director. Only those vehicles bearing an authorized parking
permit shall be permitted in the employee parking lot. Registration of vehicles for permits
shall be made in the office of the Airport Director. The quarterly fee, if required, for such
permit shall be as established from time to time by the Board of County Commissioners.
All expiring employee parking permits shall be replaced by a new permit issued by the
Airport Director's office. There shall be a charge as established from time to time by the
Board of County Commissioners for lost and misplaced keycards.

(e) All service vehicles, including utility company trucks, government-owned vehicles,
delivery trucks, etc., shall be parked in specially reserved and marked areas as may be
designated by the Airport Director.

(f) Limousine and taxi waiting and standing areas shall be those specifically reserved
and marked as may be designated by the Airport Director.

1062.18 CLEANING AND REPAIR OF VEHICLES; TAMPERING.

No person shall clean or repair vehicles anywhere within the Rogue Valley International-
Medford Airport boundary, other than in shops or other areas designated for such
purposes, except minor repairs necessary to remove such vehicle from the Airport. No
person shall move, interfere with or tamper with any vehicle, put the engine in motion or
take or use any vehicle part, instrument or tool thereof without permission of the owner
or satisfactory evidence of the right to do so presented to the Airport Director.

(Order 24-74. Passed 3-13-74; Order 94-95. Passed 3-15-95.)

1062.19 TAXICABS, LIMOUSINES AND BUSES.

All individuals, partnerships and corporations operating taxicabs, limousines or buses on
the Rogue Valley International-Medford Airport for the purpose of transporting persons
or passengers for hire shall comply with the following rules and regulations:

(a) Pay County a shuttle fee for operating at the Airport.  The monthly fee shall be
established from time to time by the Board of Commissioners.

(b) All taxicabs, limousines and buses shall be parked in such manner and in such areas
as may be designated from time to time by the Airport Director.

(c) All vehicles operated by taxicab or limousine companies shall be kept in good
operating condition and appearance, and the Airport Director is authorized to order the
immediate removal from service of vehicles that are deemed by him or her to be unsafe
or are continuously unkempt in appearance.

(d) Charges made by any taxicab, limousine or bus owner or operator for the
conveyance of persons in such vehicles shall be posted in an appropriate place in the
vehicle and made available to the Airport Director.

(e) Provide Airport Department with a valid copy of insurance certificate in not less
than an amount to be established and may be changed from time to time.

(f) Any company not in full compliance may be denied the ability to operate at the

1062.20 RADIO EQUIPMENT.

(a) All vehicles operating in the critical air operations area shall be equipped with a
two-way radio and shall be in continuous communication with the control tower, with the
exception of the following equipment, which shall be accompanied by an escort vehicle
when operating or working on or within 150 feet of usable runways or taxiways:

(1) Crash equipment while attending an accident; and

(2) Operational, agricultural and maintenance equipment not engaged in working
on runways proper.

(b) The installation of two -way radios does not permit the operation of vehicles on the
Rogue Valley International-Medford Airport without a vehicle operation permit issued by
the Airport Director.

(c) The operator of any vehicle that has been permitted to operate on the Airport shall
not proceed closer than 200 feet from the edge of the runways nor across any of the
runways prior to being cleared by the control tower. All vehicle operators shall be
conversant with the standard Airport light signal, regardless of whether or not the vehicle
is radio equipped.

(Order 24-74. Passed 3-13-74; Order 94-95. Passed 3-15-95.)

1062.21 OPERATION OF AIRCRAFT.

(a) All aircraft in flight within the Rogue Valley International-Medford Airport control
zone or in motion or parked on runways, taxiways, aprons, hardstands or ramps of the
Airport shall be governed by the current Federal Aviation Administration rules and
regulations concerning flight and all written or oral instructions of the air traffic
controller. All aircraft shall follow the appropriate taxiway and runway guidance lines
when operating on the Airport.

(b) No aircraft shall be operated on the surface of any public landing area, public
aircraft ramp and apron area, public passenger ramp and apron area, public cargo ramp
and apron area or public aircraft parking and storage area in a careless or negligent
manner, in disregard of the rights and safety of others, without due caution and
circumspection, at a speed or in a manner which unreasonably endangers persons or

1062.67 FINDINGS AND PURPOSE.

(a) The County finds that:

(1) The County owns and controls that certain Airport and air navigation facility
located in Jackson County, State of Oregon, and known as the Rogue Valley
International-Medford Airport ("Airport"); and

(2) The Airport promotes a strong economic base for the community, assists and
encourages world trade opportunities, and is of vital importance to the health,
safety, and welfare of the State of Oregon; and

(3) The Airport is a commercial service airport as that phrase is defined in 14
C.F.R. Part 158, as adopted by FAA, being a public airport enplaning 2,500 or
more scheduled air passengers per year; and

(4) The deregulation of the airline industry, the restructuring of airline ownerships,
and fluctuating market changes in the field of commercial aviation have placed
new financial challenges on the County; and

(5) The operation of the Airport as a public facility attracting scheduled airline
passenger service by airline carriers at the Airport imposes financial
responsibility on the County for airport facilities and operations; and
1062.68 Rogue Valley International Medford Airport 97

(6) The County will require substantial expenditure for capital investment,
operation, maintenance, and development of the Airport facilities to meet the
future demand for passenger air travel; and

(7) The Congress of the United States has authorized the adoption of a Passenger
Facility Charge program by local airports pursuant to the Aviation Safety and
Capacity Expansion Act of 1990 (pub. L. 101-508, Title IX Subtitle B,
November 5, 1990) (hereinafter the "Act"); and

(8) It is in the County's best economic interest to continue the Passenger Facility
Charge program and in the interest of airline passengers that the County
continue a Passenger Facility Charge program as identified in Sections 1062.66
through 1062.71 to maintain and further expand the transportation facilities of
the Community; and

(9) In establishing and implementing the Passenger Facility Charge program, the
passengers using the Airport should contribute to a greater degree toward the
development of Airport facilities used by passengers and continued
development thereof; and

(10) The fees implemented by Section 1062.68 are reasonable for the use of the
Airport and aviation facilities by the general public.

(b) The purpose of Sections 1062.66 through 1062.71 is to enact a Passenger Facility
Charge program consistent with the above findings and Sections 1062.66 through
1062.71 and the regulations published pursuant thereto shall be liberally construed to
effectuate the purposes expressed.

(Ord. 95-15. Passed 4-12-95; Order 94-95. Passed 3-15-95.)
