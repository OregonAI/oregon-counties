Medical Marijuana Dispensaries

640.01 Moratorium on Operation of Marijuana Dispensary.
640.02 Definitions.

CROSS REFERENCES

Medical marijuana facility registration-see ORS 475.314

640.01 MORATORIUM ON OPERATION OF MARIJUANA DISPENSARY.

(a) There is a moratorium on the operation of a marijuana dispensary in any area
subject to the jurisdiction of Jackson County from the effective date of this Ordinance
through May 1, 2015.

(b) No person shall operate a marijuana dispensary in any area subject to the
jurisdiction of Jackson County during the moratorium period.

(E. Ord. 2014-2. Passed 3-19-14.; P. Ord. 2014-3. Passed 4-02-14)

640.02 DEFINITIONS.

(a) As used in this Ordinance, “marijuana dispensary” includes any facility that
dispenses marijuana pursuant to ORS 475.314, or any other provision of Oregon law.

(E. Ord. 2014-2. Passed 3-19-14.; P. Ord. 2014-3. Passed 4-02-14)
