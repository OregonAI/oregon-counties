260.07 ESTABLISHMENT OF ADVISORY COMMITTEES; DUTIES.

There are hereby established in and for the County the following County Health and
Human Services advisory committees:

(a) The Jackson County Local Alcohol and Drug Planning Committee which shall serve
as the local alcohol and drug planning committee for the County, as specified in ORS
430.342;

(b) The Jackson County Mental Health and Development Disabilities Advisory
Committee which shall serve as the local mental health and developmental disabilities
advisory committee, as specified in ORS 430.630; and

(c) The Jackson County Veterans Advisory Committee which shall serve as the local
veteran’s advisory committee providing recommendations and advice on current and
future issues that could improve the Veteran Service Office.

260.08 JACKSON COUNTY LOCAL ALCOHOL AND DRUG PLANNING
COMMITTEE COMPOSITION; TERMS OF OFFICE, FUNCTIONS
AND RESPONSIBILITIES.

(a) The Jackson County Local Alcohol and Drug Planning Committee shall consist of a
minimum of nine and a maximum of thirteen members. Appointments shall be for a term
of three years, commencing on June 30. Members shall:

(1) Be representative of the geographic area and be persons with interest or
experience in developing programs dealing with alcohol and substance abuse
problems;

(2) Include minority members that reasonably reflect the proportion of the need for
alcoholism and substance abuse treatment and rehabilitation services of
minorities in the community;

(3) Include, one member of the Jackson County Mental Health and Developmental
Disabilities Advisory Committee; and

(4) If possible, include one representative from the following:  County Community
Justice Department’s Juvenile Program, local schools, a medical practitioner,
and a non-medical psychotherapist.

(b) If a member fails to attend more than three regular meetings, unexcused, in any one
fiscal year, the Committee may, upon a majority vote, recommend to the Board that the
office held by that member be declared vacant and that a replacement be appointed to
serve the remainder of the unexpired term.

(c) The Committee shall identify needs and establish priorities for alcohol and drug
prevention and treatment services that best suit the needs and values of the community

260.09 JACKSON COUNTY MENTAL HEALTH AND DEVELOPMENTAL
DISABILITIES ADVISORY COMMITTEE COMPOSITION; TERMS
OF OFFICE, FUNCTIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES.

(a) The Jackson County Mental Health and Developmental Disabilities Advisory
Committee membership shall consist of a minimum of nine and a maximum of fifteen
members. Appointments shall be for a term of three years, commencing on June 30.
Members shall:

(1) Be broadly representative of the community with the balance of age, sex,
ethnic, socioeconomic, geographic, professional and consumer interests
represented, including the consumer interests of alcoholism, alcohol abuse,
emotional disturbances, drug abuse, mental retardation and other
developmental disabilities; and

(2) For at least three of the members, have experience receiving mental health
services themselves or members of their families.

(b) If a member fails to attend more than three regular meetings, unexcused, in any one
fiscal year, the Committee may, upon a majority vote, recommend to the Board that the
office held by that member be declared vacant and that a replacement be appointed to
serve the remainder of the unexpired term.

(c) The Committee shall advise the Board of County Commissioners on matters of
public concern relating the mental health and developmental disabilities services offered
in the County.

(d) The Committee shall hold regular meetings at least once quarterly, with the
frequency, time and place to be established by the Co mmittee members in accordance
with needs of each Committee and at the request of the Board of County Commissioners.

260.10 JACKSON COUNTY VETERANS ADVISORY COMMITTEE
COMPOSITION; TERMS OF OFFICE; FUNCTIONS AND
RESPONSIBILITIES.

(a) The Jackson County Veterans Advisory Committee membership shall consist of a
minimum of nine and a maximum of eleven members. Appointments shall be for a term
of 2 years.  Members be veterans, representatives of veteran organizations, civic leaders,
dependents of veterans, and survivors of veterans.

(b) If a member fails to attend more than three regular meetings, unexcused, in any one
fiscal year, the Committee may, upon a majority vote, recommend to the Board that the
office held by that member be declared vacant and that a replacement be appointed to
serve the remainder of the unexpired term.

(c) The Committee shall provide the Board of County Commissioners, the County
Administrator, and the Director of County Health and Human Services with
recommendations and advice on issues concerning services to veterans, and dependents
and survivors of veterans.

(d) The Committee shall hold regular meetings at least once quarterly, with the
frequency, time and place to be established by the Committee members in accordance
with needs of each Committee and at the request of the Board of County Commissioners.

(e) The Committee shall organize and select officers.

(Ord. 2015-19. Passed 11-12-15.)
