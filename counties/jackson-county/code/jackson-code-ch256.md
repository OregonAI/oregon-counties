CHAPTER 256
Boards of Property Tax Appeals

EDITOR
'S NOTE:  This chapter, formerly titled "Boards of Ratio Review and
Equalization," was re -titled "Boards of Property Tax Appeals" as part of the 2000
updating and revision of these Codified Ordinances. Therefore, any reference in these
Codified Ordinances to "Boards of Ratio Review and Equalization” shall be deemed to
mean “Boards of Property Tax Appeals.”

There are no sections in Chapter 256. This chapter has been established to provide a place
for cross references and any future legislation.
