1070.04 OPERATION.

(a) Except as otherwise provided in subsection (b), the Law Library shall be operated
under the direction and control of the County Administrator. The Law Library may be
open to the public at such hours and on such days as may be established by the County
Administrator

(b) The County may enter into a contract with another organization or unit of local
government for the operation of the Law Library of the provision of Law Library
services.

1070.06 LIBRARY RULES.

Unless the County has entered into a contract with another organization or nit of local
government for the operation of the Law Library or the provision of Law Library
services, the County Administrator is authorized to establish by written regulation other
rules regarding the conduct of persons on and within the premises. All rules adopted by
the County under Section 1070.06 shall not become effective until they are signed by the
County Administrator and filed with the Board of Commissioners.

(E. Ord. 87-6. Passed 6 -3-87; P. Ord. 87-7. Passed 7-1-87; Ord 2017- 4. Passed 03-01-17;
Ord 2018-4. Passed 7-18-18; E. Ord 2018-5. Passed 6-06-18.)

1070.07 VIOLATIONS.

Any person violating any regulation duly adopted and filed under this chapter shall be
guilty of a violation, which may be cited and prosecuted, and punished by civil fine, in
the manner prescribed by Section 202.99 of the Codified Ordinances, except as below-
provided. If the violator, having been warned by the County personnel in charge of the
Law Library premises and asked to comply with the rule, persists in a continuing course
of conduct in violation of the rule, then each such incident shall be deemed a separate
violation punishable by fine as above-provided. If the County personnel in charge of the
Law Library premises finds that the conduct of a person on the Law Library premises
violates any of the rules or is disruptive and interferes with the use of the Law Library by
others, or if an unauthorized person is on premises at a time when the Law Library is not
open to public use and declines to depart on request by County personnel then the
violator shall be guilty of a violation under this chapter, and, in addition, any peace
officer is authorized, at the request of County  personnel, to cause the violator to be
evicted physically from the Law Library premises, and any person who resists, or
interferes with, the performance of this duty by the peace officer shall be punishable for
such violation as may be prescribed by State law.
