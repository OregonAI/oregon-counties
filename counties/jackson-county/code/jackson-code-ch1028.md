1028.01 MAINTENANCE OF DITCHES ADJACENT TO COUNTY ROADS.

The County shall, unless reimbursed, maintain ditches adjacent to County roads only in
those situations where such maintenance will prevent the deterioration of the County
road.

(Order 442-83. Passed 11-9-83.)
