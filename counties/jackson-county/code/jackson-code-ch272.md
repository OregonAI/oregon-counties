272.01 ESTABLISHMENT; COMPOSITION; COMPENSATION.

(a) If the County operates a Law Library, there is hereby established in and for the County
a Law Library Advisory Committee.

(b) The Committee shall consist of the County Administrator or his or her designee, as a
voting ex-officio member, and four additional members appointed by the Board of County
Commissioners, as follows:

(1) Two members recommended by the Jackson County Bar Association;

(2) One resident Judge of the First Judicial District recommended by the Presiding
Judge; and

(3) One lay member intended to represent the general public and who may be
recommended by the Committee.

(c) Appointments to the Committee shall be for terms of four years. Members may be
reappointed.  All terms shall expire on June 30 of the appropriate year.

(d) Members of the Committee shall serve without compensation, subject to budget
limitations for incurred expenses.

(Order 6-83. Passed 1-5-83; Ord. 6-83. Passed 1-5-83; Ord. 87-5. Passed 6-17-87; Ord.

(b) Formulate and recommend operational policies to the Board of County
