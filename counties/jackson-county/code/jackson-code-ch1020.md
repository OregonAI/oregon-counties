1020.01 CONSTRUCTION, RECONSTRUCTION, MINOR BETTERMENT
AND MAINTENANCE POLICIES.

The new construction and major reconstruction policy shall be to perform construction by
the contract method when economically feasible as determined by the Board of County
Commissioners through the local contract review process. Minor betterment and
maintenance shall be by the use of County forces with staff and equipment provided in
the annual budget. Other forces or equipment needed for additional work in emergencies
and beyond the capability of the County shall be drawn from private industry.
Engineering design, inspection and contract administration shall be by the staff of the
Roads and Parks Department, except for special project design, such as a complex bridge
or a State-Federal aid project, which may require supplemental engineering and
administration.

(Order 104-77. Passed 3-30-77; Ord. 2003 -4. Passed 1-22-03; Ord. 2007-2. Passed 1-31 -
07.)

1020.02 CONSTRUCTION POLICIES BY CLASS OF CONSTRUCTION.

(a) New construction and major reconstruction, to be done by contact when
economically feasible, shall meet the following criteria:

(1) Roads and S treets - Improvements, including pavement, which involve
substantial upgrading of an existing facility or the construction of a new high-
standard facility, where detailed plans, specifications and quality control are
necessary;

(2) Bridges and Culverts:

A. Bridge replacement involving structures having a span length greater than
thirty feet and a travel width of twenty-eight feet or more;

B. Reinforced concrete box culverts where the improvement is part of a
major improvement, as described in paragraph (a)(1) hereof;

C. Circular culverts where the required culvert diameter is greater than
eighty-four inches; and

D. Circular culverts within the major improvement, as described in
paragraph (a)(1) hereof; and

(3) Pavement Overlays  - Asphalt pavement overlays on arterial type roads. The
annual paving program shall be examined for economical accomplishment and
the method of construction shall be approved by the Board of County
Commissioners prior to the inclusion of the project in a construction budget.

(b) Minor betterment and spot traffic safety projects, to be done by County forces, shall
meet the following criteria:

(1) Roads and Streets:

A. Minor improvements involving widening shoulders, adding base rock and
oiling substantial sections of existing roads, where such improvement
does not necessitate significant grade changes or detailed plans and
specifications;

B. Improvement of isolated sections of roads to correct vertical and
horizontal curve problems and to provide additional width for safety;

C. Bridge approaches and/or detours when they are not included as part of a
contract;

D. Random drainage improvements;

E. Fencing projects which are part of a larger force account job or a right-of -
way agreement which is accomplished prior to a new construction or
major reconstruction contract;

F. Right of way projects; and

G. Other work clearly related to minor betterment; and

(2) Bridges and Culverts - Bridge replacement or new bridges involving structures
having a span length less than thirty feet and a travel width less than twenty -
eight feet.

(Order 104-77. Passed 3-30-77.)

1020.03 ROAD CONSTRUCTION STANDARDS; NUMBERING AND MILE
POSTING.

To improve the information available to the Board of County Commissioners relating to
the maintenance functions set forth in Section 1020.02, the Roads and Parks Department
shall provide data which indicate standards for work to be performed, equipment required
and personnel required for the following types of roads:

(a) Primitive (Type A) - An unimproved route on which there is no public maintenance;

(b) Unimproved (Type B) - Open for travel, generally with minimum maintenance, for
use by a limited number of four-wheel vehicles;

(c) Graded and Drained (Type C) - Graded and with no imported material for surface;
drained by longitudinal and/or transverse drainage system;

(d) Soil, Gravel or Stone (Types D and E) - Improved by importation of surface
material, such as gravel, stone, sand-clay or admixtures of stabilizing material;

(e) Low-Type Bituminous (Types F and G) - A bituminous surface-treated road with a
surface of less than one inch in compacted thickness, regardless of base thickness;

(f) High-Type Bituminous (Types H and I)  - A bituminous concrete surface of one inch
or more and a base and surface of seven inches or more in compacted thickness; and

(g) Cement, Brick, Block (Types J, K and L) - A surface type consisting of Portland
cement concrete, brick or block.

Road numbering and mile posting shall be undertaken to take advantage of traffic safety
data reports made by the Sheriff's Department and the State Police.

(Order 104-77. Passed 3-30-77; Ord. 2003 -4. Passed 1-22-03; Ord. 2007-2. Passed 1-31 -

1020.04 PURCHASE OF CRUSHED AGGREGATE.

Funds for the purchase of crushed aggregate required by the Roads and Parks Department
shall be included in the budget of the Department for specified contract locations and
quantities based on forecasted needs.

The County shall make its County-owned quarries available for use by bidders on County
road projects.

(Order 104-77. Passed 3-30-77; Ord. 2003 -4. Passed 1-22-03; Ord. 2007-2. Passed 1-31 -
07.)

1020.05 PLANNING.

(a) Planning for new construction and major reconstruction shall be done in three
phases, as follows:

(1) Preliminary design;

(2) Final design and right of way acquisition; and

(3) Contract administration.

(b) The planning and right of way phases shall be reflected in at least two annual budgets

(c) The Roads and Parks Department shall prepare and work from a five- year plan for

1020.07 GENERAL POLICY CONDITIONS.

The Roads and Parks Department shall prepare and test personnel and equipment
requirements to perform minor betterment and maintenance functions as set forth in this
chapter, as follows:

(a) Necessary personnel requirements on a crew size basis as may be required to
implement and carry out this chapter;

(b) Adjustments to the equipment division as may be required to implement and carry
out this chapter;

(c) Identification of equipment units which may be phased out to implement this
chapter, and;

(d) Identification of equipment units which may be needed as additions to current
ownership to implement and carry out this chapter.

(Order 104-77. Passed 3-30-77; Ord. 2003 -4. Passed 1-22-03; Ord. 2007-2. Passed 1-31 -
07.)
