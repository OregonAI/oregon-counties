 ### CHAPTER 29 - POLICY-MAKING AUTHORITY FOR BENTON COUNTY CORRECTION FACILITY

 ### 29.000 - Purpose.

The purpose of this ordinance is to expressly delegate policy-making authority to
the Benton County Sheriff's Office over matters concerning the Benton County Correction
Facility.

 ### 29.005 - Delegation of Policy-Making Authority.

(1)
The Benton County Board of Commissioners delegate to the Benton County Sheriff authority
to prepare and implement policies and procedures relating to the Benton County Correctional
Facility as follows:
(a)
Inmate property control;
(b)
Population control; and
(c)
Inmate intake and release.
(2)
The Board of Commissioners acknowledge and reaffirm any and all constitutional, statutory
or regulatory authority given to the Sheriff for the operation, maintenance and control
of the Benton County Correction Facility.

 ### 29.010 - Review of Policies.

The Sheriff shall bring all Correction Facility policies and procedures to the Benton
County Board of Commissions for review no less than 1 time every 3 years.
