 ### CHAPTER 3 - CITIZEN COMMITTEES

 ### 3.005 - Definitions.

As used in BCC Chapter 3 :
(1)
"Committee" means any citizen committee, commission, or board to which appointments are made
by the Board of Commissioners.
[Ord. 85-0024]

 ### 3.010 - Applicability.

This section shall apply to all advisory boards, commissions, and committees that
are created solely by the Benton County Board of Commissioners or for which Benton
County is legally responsible, unless to do so would cause County to be in violation
of state or federal law.
[Ord. 2016-0273]

 ### 3.015 - Appointments.

(1)
The Board of Commissioners shall select and appoint members of County advisory, boards,
commissions, and committees. The Board may establish procedures for member selection
and appointment to supplement the provisions of this chapter.
(2)
Applications for appointment to County boards, commissions, and committees shall be
filed with the Board of Commissioners. The Board of Commissioners may limit the times
when it will accept applications. The Board shall review submitted applications and
may refer applications to advisory boards, commissions, and committees for screening
and recommendations.
(3)
All persons appointed to serve on County advisory boards, commissions and committees
shall serve on such advisory boards, commissions, and committees at the pleasure of
the Board of Commissioners. Appointments shall be made for such terms as designated
by the Board of Commissioners.
(4)
The Board of Commissioners reserves the right to appoint or not appoint a person to
fill any position vacated by a member of an advisory board, commission or committee.
[Ord. 85-0024; Ord. 2016-0273]

 ### 3.020 - By-Laws.

(1)
By-Laws may be drafted, reviewed, and recommended to the Board of Commissioners by
any advisory board, commission, or committee. By-Laws may also be prepared by the
Board of Commissioners.
(2)
The Board of Commissioners shall have the sole authority to adopt, amend, or repeal
by-laws for County advisory boards, commissions, and committees.
(3)
By-Laws shall govern the conduct of the County advisory board, commission, or committee
but shall not, in any way, bind the Board of County Commissioners. By-Laws may contain
more stringent requirements or procedures than those specified in this chapter.
[Ord. 2016-0273]

 ### 3.025 - Conflicts of Interest.

(1)
All members of County advisory boards, commissions, or committees shall comply with
all State laws relating to conflicts of interest, financial interest, and ethics laws.

 ### 3.030 - Quorum.

A quorum is required before a County advisory board, commission, or committee or any
of its members may decide any matter relating to the advisory board, commission, or
committee's business. A quorum shall be a majority of the persons then appointed by
the County to serve on the advisory board, commission, or committee, excluding vacant
positions.
[Ord. 2016-0273]

 ### 3.035 - Removal; Grounds, Procedures.

(1)
Removal. Removal of a member of a County advisory board, commission, or committee may be made
or approved by the Board of Commissioners, without cause, at any time.
(2)
Opportunity to Address Board of Commissioners. Whenever practicable, the Board of Commissioners may provide the member being removed
from an advisory board, commission or committee position with an opportunity to address
the Board of Commissioners concerning that member's removal prior to removal from
the advisory board, commission or committee.
[Last Modified 3/9/90, Ord. 90-0067 3-2; Ord. 2016-0273]
