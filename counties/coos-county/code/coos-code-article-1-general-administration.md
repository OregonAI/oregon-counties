 ARTICLE ONE -- GENERAL ADMINISTRATION

DIVISION ONE - BOARD PROCEDURES
[Extensively revised by Ordinance 21-01-001L; Effective May
17, 2021]

SECTION 01.01.010  MEETINGS OF THE BOARD OF COUNTY
     COMMISSIONERS

The Board of Commissioners shall meet for the transaction of
County business at such days and times as may be set by the
Board.  All agreements, contracts, real property
transactions, legislative and quasi- judicial decisions and
other formal documents will not be deemed final and binding
on the County until reduced to writing, and formally approved
and signed by the Board.  For purposes of this section
"signed by the Board" means signed by at least two (2)
members of the Board or, after approval by the Board, signed
by the Chairperson, or in the absence of the Chair, by the
Vice Chairperson.  Board actions other than those listed
above will be deemed final upon approval by the Board. The
Board may, at any time and in accordance with applicable law,
delegate the authority to approve agreements, contracts, real
property transactions, and other formal documents. [09-10-

DIVISION TWO - HEALTH AND LIFE INSURANCE BENEFITS

SECTION 01.02.010  DEFINITIONS

As used in this division, unless the context requires
otherwise:
 (1) "Board" means the Coos County Board of
Commissioners.
 (2) "Health and Life Insurance Benefits" means those
benefits provided to officers and employees and their
dependents and domestic partners, including but not limited
to health insurance, life insurance, and any and all other
insurance designed to protect the health and the person of
County offices and employees and their dependents and
domestic partners.

SECTION 01.02.020  FINDINGS

It is found that state law does not adequately provide for
health and life insurance coverage for officers and employees
and their dependents, and it is necessary as a matter of
County concern to enact an ordinance authorizing such
benefits.

SECTION 01.02.030  AUTHORIZATION

Whenever it is deemed necessary and in the best interest of
Coos County and its officers and employees, the Board may
agree by order or contract or otherwise to provide health and
life insurance benefits not otherwise expressly authorized or
necessarily implied by state law.

DIVISION THREE - CONTRACT REVIEW BOARD
[Extensively revised by Ordinance 05-01-003L, effective March
1, 2005]

SECTION 01.03.010  CONTRACT REVIEW BOARD

The Board of Commissioners for the County of Coos is hereby
designated as the local Contract Review Board and it shall
have all the powers granted the State Public Contract Review
Board relative to contract concerns of Coos County.

SECTION 01.03.020  RULES GOVERNING PUBLIC CONTRACTS

The Contract Review Board shall adopt rules governing public
contracts by Board Resolution.

SECTION 01.03.030  AGREEMENTS PURSUANT TO BID;
     PROPOSAL; QUOTE

No agreement or contract shall be entered into by Coos County
with any party pursuant to bids, proposals or quotes under
ORS Chapters 279A, 279B and 279C unless the review procedures
as adopted by resolution of the Coos County Board of
Commissioners shall have been completed prior to the award.
The procedures shall include legal review of the bid or
proposal documents by the Coos County Office of Legal Counsel
and review of the availability of budgeted funds by Board of
Commissioners, unless the County otherwise delegated contract
authority.

SECTION 01.03.040  LONG-TERM PURCHASE AGREEMENTS FOR
     PERSONAL PROPERTY

Pursuant to the authority granted to the Board by ORS
203.035, the Board may, by order, authorize the County to
enter into long-term installment purchase agreements, lease
purchase agreements, or other agreements to acquire personal
property, if all payments due under such agreements are
subject to the appropriation of funds.  Any such agreements
previously approved by the Board are hereby ratified and
confirmed.

SECTION 01.03.050  PROTESTS AND JUDICIAL REVIEW

All protests and judicial review relating to contracts
pursuant to ORS Chapters 279A, 279B and 279C shall be
governed by the rules adopted by the Board.

DIVISION FOUR - CLOSURE OF COUNTY OFFICES

SECTION 01.04.010  PURPOSE

The purpose of this Division is to authorize the Coos County
Board of Commissioners to order the closure of County offices
on days other than legal holidays, and to limit the hours of
public access to County offices.  This Division is
necessitated by the County's lack of resources to staff its
offices during all normal working days.

SECTION 01.04.020  CLOSURE OF OFFICES

The Coos County Board of Commissioners shall have authority
to close one or more County departments for the transaction
of business on any given day, when the Board determines that
such closure is necessary due to limited staff resources.
This authority may be exercised either by Order or by Resolu-
tion.

SECTION 01.04.030  PUBLIC ACCESS

The Coos County Board of Commissioners is authorized to limit
public access to certain offices during the work day.  This
authority may be exercised either by Order or by Resolution.

DIVISION FIVE – NONPARTISAN OFFICE OF COUNTY COMMISSIONER
   [Adopted as Division Five of Article
    One on August 25, 2004]

SECTION 01.05.010  POLICY AND PURPOSE

Beginning with filings for election to the office of Coos
County Commission for the primary election of 2006, all
offices of Coos County Commissioner shall thereafter be
“nonpartisan” within the meaning of ORS Chapter 249 and other
state election laws.

SECTION 01.05.020  REFERRAL TO VOTERS IN NOVEMBER 2004
     GENERAL ELECTION; EFFECTIVE DATE

This ordinance shall be referred to the electors as a county
measure in the general election of November 2004.  This
ordinance shall become effective only if approved by a
majority of those electors voting on the proposed ordinance.
If approved by the electors, this ordinance shall take effect
on the 90
th day after the date of the election.

DIVISION SIX - MAINTENANCE OF THE COUNTY CODE
[Adopted as Division Six of Article One on
May 17, 2021]

SECTION 01.06.010  Purpose

The purpose of this Section is to define the procedures for
amending and organizing the Coos County Code (Code). This
Section also authorizes routine corrections to the Code
without requiring the passage of additional ordinances,
which represent a needless burden on time and financial
resources.

SECTION 01.06.020  Amendment and Maintenance of Code

(1) The Code may only be substantively amended by
ordinance. The Office of Legal Counsel shall assist
in assigning the proper Code chapter and section
numbers to any proposed amendment.
(2) The Office of Legal Counsel is hereby assigned the
duties of codification of general County ordinances
within the County Code. Legal Counsel shall keep the
Code and its copies as current as reasonably
possible, and in a format conducive to public use.

SECTION 01.06.030  Authorized County Code Corrections

The Office of Legal Counsel is authorized to make formatting
changes and corrections to the Coos County Code, provided
that such changes do not alter the sense, meaning, effect,
or substance of the altered provision. Such changes and
corrections may include the following:
a. Designating or re-designating by number or letter
sections or parts of sections;
b. Rearrangement of sections;
c. Inserting table of content information or other
internal references to aid in the use of the Code;
d. Conformance of textual references to agree with
renumbered or otherwise re-designated chapters of
the County Code, state statutes, administrative
rules, or any other rule or law referenced in the
County Code;
e. Substitution of the proper subsection, section,
chapter, or other division number or letters;
f. Omission of repetitious words;
g. Changes of capitalization and punctuation for the
purposes of uniformity; and
h. Correction of manifest clerical or typographical
errors.
