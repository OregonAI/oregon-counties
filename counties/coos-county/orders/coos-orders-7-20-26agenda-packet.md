           This position is responsible for coordinating daily park maintenance operations, including
           planning, prioritizing, and directing assigned work activities related to the construction,

           public facilities.

           identifies training needs of staff.

           complex or escalated issues to the Parks Director.
           Attend meetings and seminars as requested; coordinates projects with various utilities
