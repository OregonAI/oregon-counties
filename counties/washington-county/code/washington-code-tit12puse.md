 ### Title 12 - PUBLIC SERVICES

 ### Chapter 12.04 - WATER FACILITIES MASTER PLAN

 ### 12.04.010 - Adoption.

Pursuant to ORS 451.010 to 451.140, that portion of the document entitled Tualatin
Basin Water and Sewerage Master Plan as prepared by the engineering firm of Stevens,
Thompson and Runyan, Inc., January, 1969, more specifically known as the Water Facilities
Plan, pages 41 through 75, marked Exhibit A, by this reference made a part hereof
as if set out in full, is adopted as the master plan for water facilities for the
unincorporated areas within the county and any incorporated cities within the county
agreeing to be bound by said water master plan.
(Ord. 89 § 1, 1971)

 ### 12.04.020 - Definitions.

As used in this chapter, unless the context or a specially applicable definition requires
otherwise:
A.
"Board" means the board of county commissioners for the county.
B.
"Construction" means construction, reconstruction, modification, expansion, addition,
extension or installation.
C.
"Person" includes individuals, corporations, associations, firms, partnerships, joint
stock companies, special districts having authority to construct sewerage works, and
any other agency or entity having said authority.
D.
"Water facilities" includes any and all water supply works, including all facilities
necessary for tapping natural sources of domestic and industrial water, treating and
protecting the quality of the water and transmitting it to the point of sale to any
city, domestic water supply corporation or other public or private agency for ultimate
distribution by the city, corporation or agency to water users.
(Ord. 89 § 2, 1971)

 ### 12.04.030 - Promulgation of rules and regulations.

The board may adopt reasonable rules and regulations pursuant to this chapter and
may also delegate technical review functions to staff personnel.
(Ord. 89 § 4, 1971)

 ### 12.04.040 - Interim measures.

A.
The board may, in its discretion, approve proposed water facilities construction or
operation for interim solutions of water problems which is not in conformity with
the master plan on a temporary basis in emergency situations, or where said proposal
is compatible with the long-term goals of the master plan.
B.
The board may apply such conditions to its approval of interim measures to ensure
compatibility with long-term goals of the master plan.
(Ord. 89 § 6, 1971)

 ### 12.04.050 - Contracts with outside agencies.

The board may enter into contracts or agreements with cities, counties, state agencies
or special districts for the approval and enforcement of the master plan.
(Ord. 89 § 7, 1971)

 ### 12.04.060 - Advisory committee.

The board may, by resolution and order, appoint and define the responsibilities of
an advisory committee to advise the board as to any phase of the implementation and
enforcement of this master plan and as to recommended revisions of this chapter.
(Ord. 89 § 8, 1971)

 ### 12.04.070 - Filing of plans.

Any person proposing construction of water facilities within the master plan boundaries
of the county shall, before undertaking any work on the ground other than making surveys
and preliminary plans, file with the board full plans and specifications for approval
and determination that such proposed construction conforms to the master plan.
(Ord. 89 § 3(A), 1971)

 ### 12.04.080 - Commencement of construction—Plan approval required.

No construction shall be commenced until the plans and specifications filed with the
board are approved by the board. Any construction or operation must then be in accordance
with the plans and specifications, either as originally proposed or modified as approved,
by the board. The board or its representatives shall have access at reasonable times
to any construction site for water facilities for inspection purposes.
(Ord. 89 § 3(B)(part), 1971)

 ### 12.04.090 - Temporary approval.

In the event of failure of the board to act upon any plans and specifications filed
with the board within sixty days after filing, the person proposing construction shall
be deemed to have received temporary approval, such approval to expire upon final
action by the board.
(Ord. 89 § 3(B)(1), 1971)

 ### 12.04.100 - Notice of board action.

The board shall give written notice of its intention to deny, suspend, modify or revoke
any approval issued pursuant to Sections 12.04.070 through 12.04.090 and 12.04.110 through 12.04.150 . Such notice shall be sent by registered mail to the last known address of the person
proposing construction. The person to whom the notice is addressed shall have twenty
days from the date of mailing of such notice in which to demand a hearing by the board
or its authorized representative. Such hearing shall be recorded and must be held
within twenty days from the date of receipt of notice demanding said hearing.
(Ord. 89 § 3(B)(2), 1971)

 ### 12.04.110 - Order of board—Appeal.

The board shall, within thirty days from the date of hearing enter its order showing
final action on the proposed construction and state its reasons therein. The order
of the board shall be final and conclusive on all parties unless an appeal is taken
therefrom as provided by Section 12.04.160 of this chapter.
(Ord. 89 § 3(B)(3), 1971)

 ### 12.04.120 - Hearing procedures—Authority.

The board may prescribe procedures for the conduct of said hearing.
(Ord. 89 § 3(B)(4), 1971)

 ### 12.04.130 - Notice of intention deemed final—When.

If no hearing is requested within the time prescribed by this chapter, the notice
of intention of the board shall be deemed the final action of the board and shall
be final and conclusive on all parties.
(Ord. 89 § 3(B)(5), 1971)

 ### 12.04.140 - Bond—Filing.

Every person proposing water facilities construction shall file with the board a surety
bond or other security as may be acceptable to the board, unless waived by the board,
in an amount not to exceed the estimated cost of construction, executed in favor of
the board, to assure full and faithful conformance with this master plan.
(Ord. 89 § 3(C)(part), 1971)

 ### 12.04.150 - Bond—Forfeiture.

The bond or other security shall be forfeited in whole or in part to the board by
failure to follow the plans and specifications approved by the board. The bond or
other security shall be forfeited only to the extent necessary to secure compliance
with the approved plans and specifications. The board may expend the amount forfeited
to secure compliance with the approved plans and specifications.
(Ord. 89 § 3(C)(1), 1971)

 ### 12.04.160 - Appeal.

Any person may appeal final action of the board within thirty days of the entering
of the order provided by Section 12.04.110 of this chapter, to a court of competent jurisdiction pursuant to ORS 34.010 to 34.100.
(Ord. 89 § 10, 1971)

 ### 12.04.170 - Variances.

The board may grant specific variances from the particular requirements of the master
plan to such persons proposing water facilities construction or operation upon such
conditions as it may deem necessary if it finds that strict compliance is inappropriate
because of conditions beyond the control of said persons, or the course of special
circumstances which would render strict compliance unreasonable, burdensome or impractical
due to special physical conditions or cause or because strict compliance would result
in substantial curtailment or closure of existing water facilities, or because no
other alternative water facility is available. Such variances may be limited in time.
(Ord. 89 § 5, 1971)

 ### 12.04.180 - Conformance with state law.

This chapter shall in no way be a substitute for nor eliminate the necessity of conforming
with any and all state laws, rules and regulations which are now or may in the future
be in effect which relate to the construction of water facilities. The chapter is
in addition to said state laws, rules and regulations.
(Ord. 89 § 9, 1971)

 ### 12.04.190 - Violation—Penalty.

Construction or operation of water facilities by any person without approval of the
board, or construction or operation not in conformity with the plans and specifications
approved by the board is deemed a nuisance and the board, the district attorney of
the county, or the county counsel may, in addition to other remedies provided by law,
institute injunction, mandamus, abatement or any other appropriate proceeding to temporarily
or permanently enjoin, abate or remove unapproved construction.
(Ord. 89 § 11, 1971)

 ### Chapter 12.12 - SUBSURFACE SEWAGE DISPOSAL SYSTEMS

 ### 12.12.010 - Short title.

The ordinance codified in this chapter shall be known as the "sewage disposal and
water systems ordinance" and may be so pleaded and cited herein as "this chapter."
(Ord. 173 § 4(Exhibit A § 101), 1975)

 ### 12.12.020 - Definitions.

A.
General Provision. Unless otherwise specified in this chapter, the present tense includes
the future; the singular number includes the plural number and the plural number the
singular; the word "shall" is mandatory and not directory and the term "this chapter"
is deemed to include all amendments hereinafter made to this chapter and all rules
and regulations adopted pursuant thereto.
B.
Specific Definitions. Unless the context otherwise requires, the following words and
their derivations shall be utilized in this chapter:
1.
"Approved" means approved by the county department of public health.
2.
"Board" means the board of county commissioners for the county.
3.
"Building" means any structure erected for the support, shelter and enclosure of person,
animals, chattels or movable property of any kind.
4.
"Building code" means the Washington County Building Code, Ordinance 159, as a supplement
to the Uniform Building Code, enacted by the board of county commissioners on November
24, 1970, and as amended.
5.
"County" means Washington County, Oregon.
6.
"Department" means the department of public health of the county.
7.
"Health officer" means the director of health services of the county, or his authorized
delegate.
8.
"Individual water supply system" means a water supply source other than a public water
supply source.
9.
"Person" means and includes individuals, domestic or foreign corporations, societies,
joint ventures, associations, firms, partnerships, joint stock companies, clubs or
any legal entity whatsoever.
10.
"Potable water" means water which is satisfactory for drinking, culinary and domestic
purposes and meets the requirements of the county department of public health.
11.
"Private water supply" means water supply facilities which include a water supply
source other than a public water supply source.
12.
"Public water supply" means facilities of a water utility system operated by a water
supply agency for use by the public.
13.
"Rules and regulations" means:
a.
The rules and regulations adopted by the board of county commissioners for the county
pursuant to this chapter; or
b.
Duly adopted and filed Administrative Rules by any agency of the state of Oregon,
as the context requires.
14.
"Septic tank" means a watertight receptacle which receives the discharge of a drainage
system, designed and constructed so as to retain solids, digest organic matter through
a period detention, and allow the liquids, waste or effluent to discharge into the
soil outside of the tank through a system of open joint piping.
15.
"Sewage" means any combination of water-borne or liquid-borne wastes, which include
human, animal or vegetable matter, or other wastes from any building, structure, floating
structure or place, and which may include other wastes or wastewater when present,
such as chemical wastes, industrial wastes or infiltrating groundwater, surface water
or stormwater.
16.
"Sewage, domestic" means the water-borne wastes derived from the ordinary living processes
and of such character as to permit satisfactory disposal, without special treatment,
into the public sewer system or by means of a private disposal system.
(Ord. 173 § 4(Exhibit A Ch. 2), 1975)

 ### 12.12.030 - Compliance required.

A.
All waste removal facilities, as set forth in Section 12.12.040 , outside the boundaries of incorporated cities, except as provided by this chapter,
and all sewage disposal system repairs, alterations, renovations or installations
in and for such buildings or structures, except as otherwise herein provided, shall
conform with the provisions of this chapter and the rules and regulations adopted
pursuant thereto.
B.
All sewage disposal system work shall meet the requirements of this chapter and the
rules and regulations adopted pursuant thereto, and no sewage disposal system shall
be cut into, disturbed, added to, or moved other than in conformity with this chapter,
nor shall any person place in use or maintain a sewage disposal system which has been
installed, repaired, or renovated in violation of this chapter or rules and regulations
adopted pursuant thereto.
(Ord. 173 § 4(Exhibit A § 102), 1975)

 ### 12.12.040 - Applicability.

The provisions of this chapter shall also apply to individual water supply facilities
and systems and individual liquid and liquid-borne waste removal and sewage disposal
facilities whether or not adjacent to or serving buildings in parks, trailer parks,
picnic areas, campgrounds, farm labor camps, parking lots, gas or service stations,
or other individual areas on which facilities are provided. Such water facilities
include, but are not limited to:
A.
Drinking or domestic water distribution systems, except water distribution systems
which are owned and maintained by a water supply agency, as defined in this chapter;
B.
Facilities developed or constructed for a water supply source, except facilities owned
and maintained by a water supply agency, as defined in this chapter;
C.
Any water distribution system or facility serving less than fifty people or less than
eleven individual residences, except as a water supply agency, as defined in this
chapter;
D.
Any water distribution system or facility serving a subdivision comprising less than
fifty people or less than eleven individual residences, except a water supply agency,
as defined by this chapter;
E.
Any water distribution system or facility serving any apartment or multiple dwelling,
except a water supply agency, as defined by this chapter.
(Ord. 173 § 4(Exhibit A § 103), 1975)

 ### 12.12.050 - Administration—Director responsibility.

The director of health, subject to the supervision of the board, shall be responsible
for the administration and enforcement of this chapter.
(Ord. 173 § 4(Exhibit A § 301(A)), 1975)

 ### 12.12.060 - Administration—Authority.

In order to carry out the duties imposed by this chapter, the director of health shall
have the authority to administer oaths, certify to all official acts, subpoena and
require the attendance of witnesses at public hearings before the board; require production
of relevant documents at public hearings; swear witnesses, take testimony of any person
by deposition; enter, or authorize personnel to enter, upon the premises of any person
regulated by this chapter to determine compliance with this chapter and the rules
and regulations promulgated by the board pursuant thereto.
(Ord. 173 § 4(Exhibit A § 301(B)), 1975)

 ### 12.12.070 - Administration—Duties.

Administration of this chapter by the director of health shall include, but not be
limited to:
A.
The collection of fees and other charges imposed pursuant to this chapter;
B.
The examination and approval or disapproval of all plans pertaining to permits issued;
C.
The inspection of sewage disposal system work performed pursuant to a validly issued
permit pursuant to this chapter;
D.
Observation of all necessary tests of sewage disposal system repairs, alterations,
renovations, and installations;
E.
The issuance of certificates of inspection approval;
F.
All necessary acts involving the prosecution of all persons in violation of this chapter;
G.
Recommendation to the board concerning amendments to this chapter;
H.
A right of entry at all reasonable times to enter any building, premises or land for
the purpose of inspecting the sewage disposal system work.
(Ord. 173 § 4(Exhibit A § 301(C)), 1975)

 ### 12.12.080 - Administration—Inspections.

Inspections which are performed outside buildings or structures or other functions
which pertain to sewage disposal may be conducted by qualified public health sanitarians,
sanitarian trainees of the county department of public health, or the persons designated
by the director of health.
(Ord. 173 § 4(Exhibit A § 301(D)), 1975)

 ### 12.12.090 - Certification.

No person shall engage in the business of furnishing labor and material, or labor
only, to repair, alter, renovate, or install sewage disposal system unless he is the
holder of a current certificate issued pursuant to ORS 454.695, 454.705 or the Department
of Environmental Quality administrative rules and regulations pursuant thereto.
(Ord. 173 § 4(Exhibit A § 302(A)), 1975)

 ### 12.12.100 - Registration.

In addition to the license required pursuant to ORS 454.695, all persons engaged in
sewage disposal system work or sewage cesspool work shall be registered with the department
prior to the performance of any work in the county.
(Ord. 173 § 4(Exhibit A § 302(B)), 1975)

 ### 12.12.110 - Permit—Required.

No person shall perform repair, alteration, renovation or installation of any sewage
disposal system until a permit is obtained by the person who performs such work.
(Ord. 173 § 4(Exhibit A § 303), 1975)

 ### 12.12.120 - Permit—Application.

Permit applications pursuant to this chapter shall be made in such a manner as prescribed
by the board.
(Ord. 173 § 4(Exhibit A § 304), 1975)

 ### 12.12.130 - Permit—Approval or disapproval.

The department of public health shall, without undue delay, examine any application
for permit requested pursuant to Section 12.12.120 of this chapter and shall either approve or disapprove the same as presented.
(Ord. 173 § 4(Exhibit A § 305(A)), 1975)

 ### 12.12.140 - Permit—Statement of disapproval.

If an application for permit is disapproved, the department of public health shall
furnish a written statement to the applicant setting forth the reason or reasons for
disapproval and, where feasible, instructions as to what changes shall be necessary
prior to the issuance of a permit.
(Ord. 173 § 4(Exhibit A § 305(B)), 1975)

 ### Chapter 12.16 - ON-SITE SEWAGE DISPOSAL

 ### 12.16.010 - Short title.

This chapter shall be known and pled as the "on-site sewage disposal ordinance" of
the county.
(Ord. 254 § 1, 1981)

 ### 12.16.020 - Purpose.

The board recognizes that regulation of on-site disposal of sewage is necessary to
protect the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and natural environment of
the county. Accordingly, the county has acted as an agent of the state in performing
the regulatory duties set forth in ORS Chapter 454, as authorized by ORS 454.725.
The board further recognizes, however, that the statutory enforcement procedures heretofore
available have proven inadequate. As authorized by 1981 Oregon Laws, Chapter 147 §§
1 and 2, therefore, the board finds that enactment of a county enforcement mechanism
to be used in conjunction with its regulatory duties is necessary to ensure that the
county can effectively carry out its regulatory program and enforce the standards
for sewage disposal found in ORS Chapter 454 and the rules of the Environmental Quality
Commission.
(Ord. 254 § 2, 1981)

 ### 12.16.030 - Definitions.

Except as specifically provided below, all terms in this chapter shall be defined
as provided in Oregon Revised Statutes Chapter 454 (1979 Replacement Part) and in
the rules adopted herein:
A.
"Board" means the board of county commissioners of the county.
B.
"County health director" means the director of the department of public health of
the county or the director's designated representative.
C.
"Person" means and includes individuals, corporations, societies, associations, joint
ventures, firms, partnerships, joint stock companies, clubs or any other legal entity.
D.
"Rules" means the standards and regulations duly existing and enacted by the Environmental
Quality Commission and Department of Environmental Quality on the date the ordinance
codified in this chapter is enacted relating to sewage disposal as authorized by ORS
454.504 through 454.780 by reference incorporated herein.
E.
"System" means any proposed or installed on-site sewage disposal system, including
but not limited to, standard subsurface, septic, alternative, experimental or nonwater
carried sewage disposal systems.
(Ord. 254 § 4, 1981)

 ### 12.16.040 - Administrative rules adopted.

Except as specifically provided herein, all administrative rules which are duly existing
and adopted by the Environmental Quality Commission of the state of Oregon, and the
Department of Environmental Quality on the date of enactment of the ordinance codified
in this chapter, pursuant to ORS 454.605 through 454.780, including appendices, are
adopted as administrative rules of the board and shall have full force and effect
as law as if fully set forth herein. Violations of this chapter shall be measured
by the technical and other standards found in said rules. Said rules currently are
compiled in the Department of Environmental Quality publication entitled "On-Site
Sewage Disposal Rules," Oregon Administrative Rules Chapter 340 through Div. 71, said
publication being current as of March 31, 1981. Copies of these rules shall be made
available for public inspection at the offices of the county department of public
health, office of county counsel, county law library and such other locations as the
county health director deems necessary.
(Ord. 254 § 3, 1981)

 ### 12.16.050 - Administrative rules—Amendment.

The board may adopt new rules or amend the rules herein adopted by reference, through
adoption of a resolution and order of the board as provided under the Charter and
ordinances of the county. Said amendments shall have the full force and effect of
law, as provided herein, immediately upon board adoption of the resolution and order.
Copies of the rules as amended shall be made available, as provided in Section 12.16.040 , immediately upon adoption.
(Ord. 254 § 12, 1981)

 ### 12.16.060 - Permits required.

Except as specifically provided in the rules, it is unlawful and a violation of this
chapter to:
A.
Begin construction, installation or development of any system without first obtaining
a construction installation permit from the county health director;
B.
Place into service, change the use of, or increase the projected daily sewage flow
into an existing system without obtaining either an authorization notice or alteration
permit, as appropriate, from the county health director;
C.
Repair a system without first obtaining a repair permit from the county health director,
except that emergency repairs may be made when sewage is backing up into a dwelling
or commercial facility, or when there is a broken sewer pipe and immediate action
is necessary provided that a permit is obtained within three days after the emergency
repairs are begun.
(Ord. 254 § 5, 1981)

 ### 12.16.070 - Prohibitions generally.

It is unlawful and a violation of this chapter for any person to:
A.
Fail to treat or dispose of any sewage as required by this chapter, including the
rules adopted and by reference incorporated herein;
B.
Discharge untreated or partially treated sewage or septic tank effluent directly or
indirectly onto the ground surface or into any public waters;
C.
Discharge cooling water, air-conditioning water, water softener brine, groundwater,
soil or roof drainage into any system;
D.
Connect any plumbing fixture from which sewage is or may be discharged into any sewage
disposal system that has not been approved by the county health director;
E.
Obstruct, cover, modify the soil covering or otherwise affect a system replacement
area without first obtaining approval from the county health director;
F.
Fail to abandon an on-site system, including a septic tank and system, when required
to under the rules or fail to comply with the procedures and requirements for proper
abandonment as provided in the rules;
G.
Backfill or cover, connect to or use, any system without first obtaining a certificate
of satisfactory completion of construction, installation, repair or alteration unless
issuance of the certificate has been waived by operation of law or otherwise;
H.
Fail to meet requirements for satisfactorily complying with any correction notice
within thirty days from receipt or posting of the notice;
I.
Use any materials that do not comply with standards for on-site systems set forth
in the rules;
J.
Falsify or fail to provide any information requested by the county of any applicant
for a permit, variance or hardship relief with the intent to evade or circumvent the
procedures or standards established for regulation of on-site systems;
K.
Fail to comply with the terms or conditions of any permit including the duties imposed
on permit holders by the rules.
(Ord. 491 § 2(B) Exh. M (part), 1997: Ord. 254 § 6, 1981)
