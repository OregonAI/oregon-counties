ADMINISTRATIVE POLICY & PROCEDURE

CLATSOP COUNTY ADMINISTRATIVE POLICY AND PROCEDURE NO. A-17

SUBJECT: VISITOR/VENDOR/COMMUNITY SERVICE WORKER ID BADGES

DATE:  March 6, 2003   Revised January 2017

I. POLICY

Clatsop County requires all visitors and vendors to register and obtain a county-issued
visitor badge when entering an employee-only designated area for any length of time.
Policies and procedures are established for the issuance of “permanent” vendor badges
and for temporary visitor badges.

Community service workers with the Jail or Community Corrections shall be required to
wear an identification badge approved by the County Manager’s office when performing
service on county property. The service worker must sign in and out with the department
to which that person is assigned.

II.       VISITOR BADGES

A. Information Systems will provide all departments with visitor badges
preconfigured for access into their work areas.
B. Each department is responsible for signing in the visitor/vendor and issuing the
visitor badge and at the end of the visit, collecting the badge.
C. Each department shall only use the badge and sign-in system approved by the
County Manager’s Office to provide countywide uniformity.
D. Visitors are required to wear the badge in an easily visible location when in
employee-only designated areas.
E. Visitors may be required to show identification in order to sign in and obtain a
visitor badge at the discretion of the department head.
F. During a declared emergency or alert situation as declared by the sheriff
and/or County Manager, all visitors must sign in and wear visitor badges when
on any County premise.
G. During a declared emergency or alert situation as declared by the Sheriff,
County Commissioners and/or the County Manager, visitors must sign out with
the department where it was obtained before departing.
H. Departments may choose, at their expense, to issue special photo ID to regular
standardized vendors per policy A16.

III. COMMUNITY SERVICE WORKERS

A. Community Service workers will be issued badges that must be worn in a
highly visible location on their person at all times while performing community
service for the County. The Jail and Community Corrections is responsible for
issuing the badges and collecting the badges at the end of the individual’s work
assignment on a daily basis.
B. The department to which the individual is assigned shall sign that person in at
the start of their work and out at the end of their work.

IV.  USE OF VISITOR, VENDOR AND COMMUNITY SERVICE BADGES

Acceptable Use Standards: Acceptable uses of the visitor badges will include,
but not be limited to, the following:
(a) To identify the individual having authorized access to a specific
employee-only designated area.

Unacceptable Use Standards: Unacceptable uses of the visitor badge will
include, but not be limited to, the following:
(a) Unlawful Access: Efforts to gain unlawful access to a facility or
resource.
(b) No County Affiliation: Illegal, fraudulent or malicious activity; political
activity; religious promotion; or activity on behalf of organizations or
individuals having no affiliation with the County.

V.  PROCEDURES

Each department is responsible for distributing and collecting visitor and vendor
badges.

Lost or stolen badges must be immediately reported to Administrative Services
Information Systems.  Information Systems can be reached at 503-325-8662
during working hours or by contacting the Jail control room after hours.

Each department must use logs approved by the County Manager. Each
department shall maintain the registration sheets on file as required by the
County records officer.

Each department is responsible for registering and issuing the badges used by
its visitors and vendors.

The Jail and Community Corrections Department will issue community service
worker badges.

Public Works will issue vendor badges for its vendors.
