CLATSOP COUNTY ADMINISTRATIVE
POLICY & PROCEDURE
May 2007  Policy A-15 Page 1

SUBJECT: Third Party Incident Reporting

DATE:  July 3, 2003      REVISED: May 2017
Nov 2025

I. POLICY
Whenever an incident/accident involving damage to a third party’s personal property; when a third party
has been injured; or when County-owned property has been damaged, details must be gathered and
provided to the County’s insurance agent and County Counsel.  In some cases, the insurance agent or
County Counsel will need to take immediate action.  Therefore, it is critical that they have an opportunity
to review incident details as soon as possible.

This policy sets forth a procedure for gathering and submitting incident information for both third parties
and County employees.

A. Definition
For the purpose of this policy, the definition of incident/accident is an unplanned event that results in:

• damage to personal property;
• bodily injury to a third party; or
• damage to County-owned property.

If this incident/accident resulted in an employee injury, please complete the Employee
Incident/Investigation Report form that is included in the County’s Safety Manual.

B. Objectives
1. To assure that information is gathered as quickly and completely as possible and is provided to
the County’s insurance agent and County Counsel;
2. To provide the public with a method for submitting third party claims related to such incidents; and
3. To provide County departments with a procedure for providing information about such incidents.

II. PROCEDURE
1. Immediately following an incident as defined above, departments should have the third party
involved complete a Third-Party Incident Report form (see attached).  Please include any relevant
documents, i.e. police reports, photos, diagrams, etc.
2. After the County employee certifies the completed Third Party Incident Report form, this shall be
routed to the Risk Management Officer and Human Resources for review.  If appropriate, the Risk
Management Officer will forward the completed Third Party Incident Report form to County
Counsel and the County’s Insurance Agent of Record for processing.
3. If the department is contacted by a person who claims to have sustained either injuries or
property damage as a result of an incident involving County employees or equipment,
departments should either provide the person with a Third-Party Incident Report form to complete
or the County employee may complete this form over the telephone.  Any additional information
or documentation a person wishes to provide should be attached to the form.
