Agenda Item 6.1

AGENDA ITEM BRIEFING
Gilliam County Court

MEETING DATE: April 2, 2025

FROM: Brooklynn Griffith, Finance Director

TITLE OF AGENDA ITEM: Consider Adoption of Resolution R2025-04 Amending Position
Schedule for Non-Represented Employees from Hourly to Salary
___________________________________________________________________________

SUMMARY: The AFSCME local 3326 union has proposed a Letter of Agreement for the
purpose of updating the wage schedule outlined in the Collective Bargaining Agreement. The
County would like to make the same updates to the non-represented employee wage
schedule.

The proposed resolution will amend the position schedule for non-represented from hourly to
salary.

ALIGNMENT WITH STRATEGIC PLAN GOAL:

SUGGESTED MOTION LANGUAGE: (if applicable)
“I move to approve Resolution R2025-04 as {presented/amended}”

ATTACHMENTS: (Please list supporting documents. All attachments should be submitted with
this brief)

- Resolution R2025-04 with Exhibit A

IN THE COUNTY COURT FOR GILLIAM COUNTY, OREGON

RESOLUTION NO. R2025-04

A RESOLUTION AMENDING THE EMPLOYMENT TYPE FOR
NON-REPRESENTED EMPLOYEES

WHEREAS, Gilliam County (“County”) and the AFSCME local 3326 (“Union”) have
entered into a Letter of Agreement for the purpose of updating the wage schedule
outlined in the Collective Bargaining Agreement; and

WHEREAS, the County finds it necessary for the updates made by the Union to apply to
non-represented employees as well;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Gilliam County Court hereby resolves as follows:

1. The employment type for non-represented employees shall be amended as follows:

a. All non-represented employee positions currently classified as non-exempt
full-time hourly positions shall now be classified as non-exempt full-time
salaried positions. These positions remain eligible for overtime pay,
premium pay, and benefits.

b. Other employees will continue to be classified as hourly positions.

c. Employee position designations are outlined on Exhibit A to this
resolution.

Adopted by the Gilliam County Court this _____ day of April 2025.

___________________________________
Cris Patnode, County Judge
