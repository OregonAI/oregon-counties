## Full text

IN THE COUNTY COURT FOR GILLIAM COUNTY, OREGON

RESOLUTION NO. R2025-16

A RESOLUTION IN THE MATTER OF TERMINATING THE DISSOLUTION PROCESS
FOR THE PORT OF ARLINGTON

WHEREAS, on October 15, 2025, the Gilliam County Court adopted Resolution R2025-
13 which initiated the dissolution process for the Port of Arlington, special district; and

WHEREAS, on October 31, 2025, the Port of Arlington submitted its fiscal year 2021-
2022 audit to the Oregon Secretary of State’s office, bringing the Port into compliance
with ORS Chapter 297; and

WHEREAS, on November 4, 2025, the County Court moved to terminate the dissolution
process initiated by Resolution R2025-13;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Gilliam County Court hereby resolves as follows:

1. The dissolution process for the Port of Arlington initiated on October 15, 2025, by
Resolution R2025-13 is hereby terminated.

Adopted by the Gilliam County Court this ___ day of December 2025.

___________________________________
Cris Patnode, County Judge
