AGENDA ITEM BRIEFING
Gilliam County Court

TODAY’S DATE:  April 29, 2026

MEETING DATE:  May 6, 2026

FROM:  Brooklynn Griffith, Finance Director

AGENDA ITEM: Consider Approval of Resolution No. R2026-04; Adopting FY 2026-27 Salary Schedules
___________________________________________________________________________________________

SUMMARY
Each year, the County Court is responsible for adopting salary schedules for Represented Employees, Non-
Represented Employees, Sheriff’s Office Employees, and Elected Officials for the upcoming fiscal year.

This year, the Court is asked to consider an increase of 3.5% for Non-Represented and Sheriff’s Office
employees. In addition, the Gilliam County Compensation Board has recommended a 3.5% cost of living increase
for Elected Officials.

With the Emergency Managers’ retirement anticipated within the upcoming fiscal year, the Sheriff’s Office has
proposed the addition of a Sheriff Office Manager. A salary range has been identified; however, no salary
comparison or supporting documentation has been provided to the County Court for review.

Note: In a Letter of Agreement with AFSCME, proposed to the Court during this May 6th, meeting, the County
Court will consider the approval of a 3.5% cost of living adjustment for Represented Employees which will take
effect on July 1, 2026.

RECOMMENDATION
I recommend the County Court adopt Resolution No. R2026-04 as presented.

SUGGESTED MOTION LANGUAGE (If applicable)
“I move to adopt Resolution No. R2026-04 {as presented/as amended}.”

ATTACHMENTS
Resolution No. R2026-04 (5 pages)
Letter from Gilliam County Compensation Board (1 page)

1 – RESOLUTION NO. R2026-04
IN THE COUNTY COURT FOR GILLIAM COUNTY, OREGON

RESOLUTION NO. R2026-04

A RESOLUTION ADOPTING FISCAL YEAR 2026-2027 NON-REPRESENTED,
SHERIFF’S OFFICE, and ELECTED OFFICIAL SALARY SCHEDULES

WHEREAS, Gilliam County (“County”) desires to provide fair and equitable
compensation for its employees; and

WHEREAS, the County regularly provides annual cost of living increases to
employees based on the change in the West-Size class B/C of the CPI-W measured
from December to December each year; and

WHEREAS, the calculated cost of living is equal to 3.5%; and

WHEREAS, the Gilliam County Compensation Board recommends County
Elected Officials receive a 3.5% cost of living adjustment; and

WHEREAS, represented employees in the AFSCME Council 75 received a 3.5%
cost of living adjustment per the Letter of Agreement signed May 6, 2026.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Gilliam County Court resolves as follows:

1. Findings; Adoption.  The above-stated findings contained in this
Resolution No. R2025-06 (this “Resolution”) are hereby adopted. The Gilliam County
Court (the “Court”) hereby approves and adopts the Fiscal Year 2026-2027 Salary
Schedules for Non-Represented Employees, Sheriff’s Office Employees, and Elected
Officials attached hereto as Exhibit A (the “Schedules”) effective July 1, 2026.

2. Miscellaneous.  This Resolution (which includes the Schedules) replaces
and supersedes any existing and/or previously adopted salary schedules in their
entirety.  This Resolution may be corrected by order of the Court to cure editorial and/or
clerical errors.

ADOPTED by the Gilliam County Court this 6th day of May, 2026.

___________________________________
Cris Patnode, County Judge
