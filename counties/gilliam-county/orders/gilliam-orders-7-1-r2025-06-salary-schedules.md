Schedules
__________________________________________________________________________________
SUMMARY
Each year, the County Court is responsible for adopting salary schedules for Represented Employees,
Non-Represented Employees, Sheriff’s Office Employees, and Elected Officials for the upcoming fiscal
year.
This year, the Court is asked to consider an increase of 3.5% for Non-Represented and Sheriff’s Office
employees. In addition, the Gilliam County Compensation Board has recommended a 3.5% cost of
living increase for Elected Officials.
Note: In a Letter of Agreement with AFSCME, approved during the April 7, 2025, Meeting, the County
Court approved a 3. 5% cost of living adjustment for Represented Employees which will take effect on
July 1, 2025.
RECOMMENDATION
I recommend the County Court adopt Resolution No. R2025-06 as presented.

IN THE COUNTY COURT FOR GILLIAM COUNTY, OREGON

RESOLUTION NO. R2025-06

A RESOLUTION ADOPTING FISCAL YEAR 2025-2026 SALARY SCHEDULES

WHEREAS, Gilliam County (“County”) desires to provide fair and equitable
compensation for its employees; and

WHEREAS, the County regularly provides annual cost of living increases to
employees based on the change in the West-Size class B/C of the CPI-W measured
from December to December each year; and

WHEREAS, the calculated cost of living is equal to 3.5%; and

WHEREAS, the Gilliam County Compensation Board recommends County
Elected Officials receive a 3.5% cost of living adjustment; and

WHEREAS, represented employees in the AFSCME Council 75 received a 3.5%
cost of living adjustment per the Letter of Agreement signed April 7, 2025.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Gilliam County Court resolves as follows:

1. Findings; Adoption.  The above-st ated findings contained in this
Resolution No. R2025-06 (this “Resolution”) are hereby adopted. The Gilliam County
Court (the “Court”) hereby approves and adopts the Fiscal Year 2025-2026 Salary
Schedules for Non-Represented Employees, Sheriff’s Office Employees, and Elected
Officials attached hereto as Exhibit A (the “Schedules”) effective July 1, 2025.

2. Miscellaneous.  This Resolution (w hich includes the Schedules) replaces
and supersedes any existing and/or previously adopted salary schedules in their
entirety.  This Resolution may be corrected by order of the Court to cure editorial and/or
clerical errors.

ADOPTED by the Gilliam County Court this 7th day of May, 2025.

Cris Patnode, County Judge

Re: Gilliam County Compensation Board Report to the Gilliam County
Court for the 2025-2026 Budget

Dear Court Members,

The Compensation Board thanks the Court for allowing us the
opportunity to review and make recommendations concerning the
salaries of the Gilliam County Elected Officials.

Recommendation:

The Gilliam County Compensation Board recommends the following
changes to the Elected Official salary schedule:
• Apply a COLA pay increase of 3.5% (CPI Indexed)

• Increase the base pay of the elected officials (excluding the Judge
and Sheriff’s positions) to align with the base pay of the top paid
non-elected official (currently the Undersheriff).

• Set the base pay for the Judge position to be 15% higher than the
other elected officials (with the exception of the Sheriff).

Further, we recommend moving Judge Patnode to Step E of the salary
schedule to honor her previous experience with the County government.

Sincerely,

Nichole Schott
2025-26 Gilliam County Compensation Board Chair
