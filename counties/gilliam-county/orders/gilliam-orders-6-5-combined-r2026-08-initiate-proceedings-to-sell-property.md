AGENDA ITEM BRIEFING
Gilliam County Court

MEETING DATE: July 15, 2026

FROM: Judge Patnode

TITLE OF AGENDA ITEM: Consider adoption of Resolution 2026-08 initiating proceedings for
the sale of foreclosed property under House Bill 2089
___________________________________________________________________________

SUMMARY: The Oregon Legislature passed House Bill 2089 during its 2025 legislative
session. HB 2089 changes the way counties dispose of foreclosed property, and how the
proceeds of the sale are handled. The County has one property to be disposed of and would
like to initiate proceedings to do so.

SUGGESTED MOTION LANGUAGE: (if applicable)
“I move to approve Resolution 2026-08 initiating proceedings for the sale of foreclosed
property under HB 2089 as {presented/amended}”

ATTACHMENTS: (Please list supporting documents. All attachments should be submitted with
this briefing)

- Resolution 2026-08

IN THE COUNTY COURT FOR GILLIAM COUNTY, OREGON

RESOLUTION NO. 2026-08

A RESOLUTION INITIATING PROCEEDINGS FOR THE SALE OF FORECLOSED
PROPERTY UNDER HOUSE BILL 2089

WHEREAS, the Oregon Legislature passed House Bill 2089 during its 2025 legislative session;
and

WHEREAS, HB 2089 changes the way counties dispose of foreclosed property, and how the
proceeds of such sale are handled; and

WHEREAS, the County would like to initiate proceedings for the sale of one foreclosed property.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Gilliam County Court hereby resolves as follows:

1. Gilliam County would like to initiate proceedings for the sale of the following property
pursuant to HB 2089:

Address Map # Tax ID #
403 N. Lincoln
Condon OR 97823
4S21E10BA004300 0001-135

2. The property has a real market assessed value of less than $250,000.

3. The property, and the auction for the sale of these properties will be advertised on
websites.

4. The County will conduct a public, high-bid auction for the sale of the property, and each
property on the auction list will have a starting minimum bid.
