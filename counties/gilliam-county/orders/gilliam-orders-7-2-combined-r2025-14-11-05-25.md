AGENDA ITEM BRIEFING
Gilliam County Court

MEETING DATE: November 5, 2025

FROM: Joely Jaeger, Road Department Office Manager

TITLE OF AGENDA ITEM: Consider Approval of Resolution No. R2025-14 Declaring a
County Vehicle As Surplus Equipment
______________________________________________________________________

SUMMARY:
The Gilliam County Maintenance Department is the County Court to declare the the
following vehicle as surplus:

2011 Chevrolet Equinox AWD
LT Sport Utility 4D
Vin #2CNFLEEC5B6224792
Mileage: 143,514

The vehicle runs but does not drive. When the accelerator is pressed, the vehicle shuts
off.

SUGGESTED MOTION LANGUAGE: (If applicable)
“I move to adopt Resolution No. R2025-14 declaring a County vehicle as surplus
equipment as {amended/presented}”

ATTACHMENTS:
R2025-14 (1 page)

IN THE COUNTY COURT FOR GILLIAM COUNTY, OREGON
RESOLUTION NO. R2025-14

A RESOLUTION DECLARING COUNTY VEHICLE AS SURPLUS EQUIPMENT

Whereas, the County Court for Gilliam County sitting as the County governing
body at a regularly scheduled meeting on this 5
th day of November 2025; and

Whereas, the Gilliam County Maintenance Department has requested the
County Court declare the vehicle listed below as surplus equipment; and

Whereas, the vehicle listed below is no longer being used by the Gilliam County
Maintenance Department as it has served its useful life.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Gilliam County Court resolves as follows:

1. The Gilliam County Court declares the following vehicle as surplus equipment:

2011 Chevrolet Equinox AWD
LT Sport Utility 4D
Vin #2CNFLEEC5B6224792
Mileage: 143,514

2. The Gilliam County Court directs the vehicle to be sold through an online public
auction site.

ADOPTED this 5
th day of November 2025, by the Gilliam County Court.

 _________________________________
Cris Patnode, County Judge

ATTEST:

_________________________________
Recording Secretary
