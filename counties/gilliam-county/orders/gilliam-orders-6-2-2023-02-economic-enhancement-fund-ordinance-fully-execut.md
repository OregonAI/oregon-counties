                          Section 5:_    Approval or Denial of an Application
(1) Any application for a rebate under this Article shall be approved, or disapproved, by the Gilliam County
Assessor. If the application is approved, the property shall be considered a Homestead.

(2) If the application is disapproved, or if there is a failure to reapply for a Homestead classification under
Section 4.3, or if the property is disqualified, the Assessor shall notify the Owner in writing. The notice
shall describe the property in general terms and require the Owner to respond within 30 calendar days
after the postmark of the notice. If the Owner does not respond, or fails to show good and sufficient cause
why the application should be approved, the Assessor shall proceed to disqualify the property.

(3) An Owner whose application filed under this Article has been denied, or whose Homestead has been
disqualified, may appeal to the Gilliam County Court. The deadline for filing an appeal with the County
Court is 30 calendar days after the postmark of the denial letter from the Assessor. A prior year rebate
cannot be appealed. The ruling of the Gilliam County Court shall be final. Gilliam County will not be liable
to an Owner for any court costs or attorney fees associated with such an appeal.

(1) The Gilliam County Assessor shall determine the amount of rebate to be paid by Gilliam County on
