       NOW ON THIS DAY, the above-entitled matter having come on regularly for

         WHEREAS: On June 26, 1983, Wasco County adopted by ordinance the City of

with the City, which requires that plans and implementing ordinances be coordinated

to the existing UGB/City Limits, and to change the designation of the approximately 45
acres from Wasco County "R-R" Rural Residential to City "R-1" Urban Residential, pursuant
to the City of The Dalles/Wasco County Joint Management Agreement on property

September 9, 1991, Planning Commission meeting; and

      WHEREAS:      On September 18, 1991, at 10:30 a.m. in the Courtroom of this Court
in the Wasco County Courthouse, a review to consider the aforementioned application to
