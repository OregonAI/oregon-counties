# Ordinance 26-001Repeal & Replace Ordinance.rev2 signed (Ordinance 26-001Repeal & Replace Ordinance.rev2 signed)

## Full text

IN THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF THE STATE OF OREGON IN AND FOR THE COUNTY OF WASCO
IN THE MATTER OF REPEALING AND REPLACING ORDINANCE #94-376 LAND CORNER PRESERVATION FUND
Ordinance #26-001

WHEREAS, Ordinance No. 94-376, (Land Corner Preservation Fund) as amended , established a fund within the
Wasco County General Fund known as the Public Land Corner Preservation Fund, and set a fee at the maximum
statutory rate of $10.00 to be collected by the Wasco County Clerk upon recording of certain instruments as set
forth in ORS 205.130 and ORS 205.320; and
WHEREAS, all fees collected are deposited with the Finance Department and credited  to the Public Land Corner
Preservation Account, to be used only to pay expenses incurred or authorized by the County Surveyor in establishing
and maintaining all corners created by government surveys; and
WHEREAS, enrolled House Bill 3175, relating to county recording fees, amends ORS 203.148 to remove the statutory
limit of $10.00, and authorizes the county governing body to establish  the fee for recording all instruments under
ORS 205.130(2) after providing public notice and holding a public hearing.
WHEREAS, enrolled House Bill 3175 further requires all such moneys collected by the County Clerk to be deposited
with the County Treasurer at least once a month and credited to the Publ ic Land Corner Preservation Fund; and
whereas in Wasco County those duties are administered by the Finance Department; and
WHEREAS, adjusting the fee will offset inflationary increases in program costs, provide adequate revenue to
maintain a reserve fund balance, and continue to provide the necessary level of service to establish an d maintain
the several thousand government corners in Wasco County.
NOW, THEREFORE, the Wasco County Board of Commissioners ORDAINS as follows:
1. Wasco County Ordinance No. 94-376 is hereby repealed and replaced as set forth by the Wasco County Land
Corner Preservation Fund Ordinance attached as Exhibit A and incorporated herein by this reference.
2. If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, phrase or portion of this Ordinance is for any reason held invalid
or unconstitutional by any court of competent jur isdiction, such portion constitutes a separate, distinct and
independent provision, and such holding does not affect the validity of the remaining portions  hereof.
3. Nothing herein is intended to, nor acts herein to amend, replace, or otherwise conflict with any other
Ordinances of Wasco County, or any other code or statutory provisions, unless expressly so  stated.
4. The County Counsel and Administrator are authorized to format this Ordinance, including deleting and adding
textural material, renumbering sections or pages, and making any technical change not affecting  the
substances of this Ordinance.
DATED this 18 day of March 2026.

Exhibit A

Section 1. PURPOSE
State law authorizes the use of funds from this account for the establishment, re -establishment and
maintenance of all corners created by government surveys and provides for the creation of a user fee to be
collected at the time of recording certain instruments conveying an interest in real property, pursuant
to ORS 205.130. The County Board recognizes this fund to be a great public benefit, with several
thousand government corners in Wasco County needing to be established, re-established and maintained.
Section 2. DEFINITIONS
1) "Corner" means point determined by the surveying process.
2) "County Surveyor'' means the Wasco County Surveyor or the Surveyor’s  designee.
3) "Existent Corner'' means one whose position can be identified by verifying the evidence of the
monument or its accessories, by reference to the description in the field notes, or locate d by an
accepted supplemental survey record, some physical evidence, or testimony.
4) "Government Corners" includes all corners and boundary lines as accepted in the U.S. Department
of Interior (Bureau of Land Management) Manual of Surveying Instructions and its supplements,
such as section, quarter section, donation land claim, meander, closing, witness, State boundary and
line trees.
5) "Lost Corner'' means a point of a survey whose position cannot be determined beyond
reasonable doubt, either from traces of the original marks or from acceptable evidence or
testimony that bears upon the original position, and whose location can be restored only by
reference to one or more interdependent corners.
6) "Monument" means the object or the physical structure which marks the corner point.
7) "Obliterated Corner'' means one at whose point there are no remaining traces of the
monument, or its accessories, but whose location has been perpetuated, or the point for which
may be recovered beyond reasonable doubt by the acts and testimony of the interested
landowners, competent surveyors, other qualified local authorities, or witnesses, or by some
acceptable record evidence.
Section 3. FEES.
1) Collection of Fees. The Wasco County Clerk shall collect, pursuant to ORS 203.148, 205.130 and ORS
205.320, in addition to any other fees, a fee ( the “Monument Fee”) for and upon the recording of:
a. Deeds and mortgages of real property, powers of attorney and contracts affecting the title
of real property, authorized by law to be recorded, assignments thereof and of any interest
therein when properly acknowledged or proved;
b. Certificates of sale of real property under execution or order of court, or assignments
thereof; and
c. Any other instrument presented for recording that affects title to or an interest in real
property.

2) Treatment of Death Certificates.
a. Where a death certificated is submitted as an attachment or supporting exhibit to a
recordable instrument, the Monument Fee shall apply to the instrument as a whole.
Where a death certificated is recorded as a standalone documents, the Monument Fee shall
not be assessed against the death certificate.
b. Nothing in this section shall be construed to waive or exempt any fee required to be collected
under state law for recordable instruments.
3) Deposit of Funds. All funds collected under this s ection shall be deposited with the County Finance
Department at least once a month and credited to the Public Land Corner Preservation Account.
Section 4. ESTABLISHMENT OF ACCOUNT, USE OF FUNDS.
1) Pursuant to ORS 203.148, Wasco County establishes a Public Land Corner Preservation Account.
2) Monies in the Public Land Corner Preservation Account shall be used only to pay expenses
incurred or authorized by the County Surveyor in the establishment, re-establishment and
maintenance of corners of government surveys under ORS 209.070 (5) and (6).
Section 5.  EFFECTIVE DATE
This Ordinance shall take effect 90 days after the second reading and adoption by the Wasco
County Board of Commissioners.
