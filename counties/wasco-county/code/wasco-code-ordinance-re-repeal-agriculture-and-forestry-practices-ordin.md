# Ordinance re Repeal Agriculture and Forestry Practices Ordinance (Ordinance re Repeal Agriculture and Forestry Practices Ordinance)

## At a glance

Ordinance re Repeal Agriculture and Forestry Practices Ordinance — code of Wasco County. 2 pages.

## Full text

NOW ON THIS DAY, the above-entitled matter having come on regularly for consideration, said day being one duly
set in term for the transaction of public business and a majority of the Board of Commissioners  being present; and
THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF WASCO COUNTY OREGON DOES ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE
This ordinance may be cited as the Wasco County Farming and Forestry Practices Protection and Compliant
Mediation Ordinance.
SECTION 2. AUTHORITY
This Ordinance is adopted pursuant to ORS 203.035.
SECTION 3. PURPOSE
The purpose of this Ordinance is to repeal the Wasco County Farming and Forestry Practices Protection and
Compliant Mediation Ordinance in its entirely.
SECTION 4. FINDINGS
The Board of County Commissioners adopts the findings of the fact and conclusions of law contained in the Staff
Report of the Planning Division date March 21, 2025, a copy of which is attached hereto as Attachment A, and is
incorporated herein by this reference.
SECTION 5. HISTORY
The current Wasco County Farming and Forestry Practices Protection and Compliant Mediation Ordinance was
adopted by Ordinance on September 3, 2003.

IN THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF THE STATE OF OREGON
IN AND FOR THE COUNTY OF WASCO
IN THE MATTER REPEALING THE ORDINANCE PROVIDING PROTECTION FOR GENERALLY ACCEPTED FARMING AND
FORESTRY PRACTICES AND ESTABLISHING A COMPLAINT MEDIATION PROCESS.
ORDINANCE #25-002
ORDINANCE#25-002
SECTION 6 REPEALED
The Wasco County Farming and Forestry Practices Protection and Compliant Mediation Ordinance as attached, is
hereby repealed.
SECTION 7. Effective Date
The first reading of this Ordinance occurred on April 2, 2025; the second reading on April 16, 2025. The effective
Date of this ordinance shall be July 16, 2025.
DATED this 16th day of April, 2025.
